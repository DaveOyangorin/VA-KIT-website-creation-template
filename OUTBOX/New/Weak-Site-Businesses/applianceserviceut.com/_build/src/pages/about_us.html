About Us

Servicing Appliances Across Salt Lake City

Locally owned ASI Appliance Service Inc boasts not only more than 25 years of continued professional service, but nearly 40 years of experience in the industry. Our friendly, expert technicians, drug-free and background approved, will be on site, promptly, using the latest tools and technology to ensure that your appliance is running like new again in no time flat.

There's no need to spend money on a new appliance. Whether you're a homeowner, landlord or apartment complex, we will be there for you to repair all major appliances in most brand names.

We are a fully licensed, registered and insured. We guarantee/warranty all work.
