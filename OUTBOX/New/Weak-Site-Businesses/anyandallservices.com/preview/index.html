What the audit found

  • Mobile speed now: 62/100. Aim for 90+.
  • Home page words: 157. Our standard is 800+.
  • Pages in the sitemap: 6.
  • Meta description: present.
  • H1 headings on the home page: 2 (should be 1).
  • Tap-to-call phone link: no.
  • Business schema: no.
  • Images without alt text: 5 of 6.
  • Pages that name Salt Lake City: 0.

What to do

  • Speed: 62/100 now. Compress images, lazy-load, remove unneeded scripts. Target 90+.
  • Home page has 157 words. Write 800+ useful words (services, area, process, FAQ).
  • Use exactly one H1 per page (home page has 2).
  • Add a tap-to-call phone link in the header.
  • Add LocalBusiness schema with the exact name, address and phone.
  • Add alt text to images (5 of 6 have none).
  • Name Salt Lake City in the title, H1 and first paragraph of every service page.
