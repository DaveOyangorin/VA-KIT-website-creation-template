When to call

Six signs not to ignore.

From the National Fire Protection Association’s electrical safety tip sheet: call a qualified electrician if you have any of these.

01

Frequent problems with blowing fuses or tripping circuit breakers

02

A tingling feeling when you touch an electrical appliance

03

Discolored or warm wall outlets

04

A burning or rubbery smell coming from an appliance

05

Flickering or dimming lights

06

Sparks from an outlet

Source: NFPA Electrical Safety tip sheet. More in our post on signs you need an electrician.
