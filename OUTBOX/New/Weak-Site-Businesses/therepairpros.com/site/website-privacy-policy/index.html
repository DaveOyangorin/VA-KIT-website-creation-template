We collect various types of information from and about users of our website, including:

  • Personal Information: Information such as your name, phone number, email address, and other details that can directly identify you.
  • Technical Information: Information about your device, browsing actions, and patterns, such as your IP address, browser type, and operating system.
  • SMS Data: Information related to SMS messages you receive or send to us, including phone numbers, message content, and delivery status.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our services, including SMS communications.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To comply with legal obligations and industry standards, including TCR requirements.
  • To improve our website and services based on user feedback and preferences.
  • To send you promotional messages, offers, and updates (with your consent).

When you provide us with your phone number and consent to receive SMS messages, you agree to the following:

  • SMS messaging and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier's plan.

We may share your information with third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: To third-party vendors who assist us in providing our services, such as SMS delivery platforms, in compliance with TCR guidelines.
