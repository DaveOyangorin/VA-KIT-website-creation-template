About Affordable Heating & Cooling

Family owned. Third generation. All makes and models.

Free Estimate 801.261.0924

Servicing the Salt Lake Valley for more than two decades and with over 50 years of experience, Affordable Heating & Cooling, Inc. is a family owned and operated company that offers affordable products and honest services.

In our third generation of family operation, we promise to offer the best quality in high-efficiency products for all your heating, ventilation and air conditioning needs. We offer sales, installations and service on all makes and models.

Why choose Affordable

We recognize there are a variety of good and honest competitors in our industry. Our goal is to earn a relationship the good-old-fashioned way and build your trust with service that is professional, reliable and, most of all, affordable. Because we strive to be better each time, we won’t be a low-cost leader by furnishing substandard work, craftsmanship or discounted parts. We will promise to do our best and create a plan and service that fits within your budget.
