About Proformance Heating and Air

Draper heating and air since 2015.

Get a free quote 385-226-7667

Since 2015 we have become a well-known air conditioning and furnace repair service in the Draper area.

We were established in 2015 with one mission: to bring a reliable, high quality furnace and air conditioning repair service to the Salt Lake City area. Our passion for excellence is what inspired us to open up our shop, and continues to drive us to this day. All our repairs are completed with meticulous detail, resulting in a product that is as good as new.

This is our story

What started out as a repair service has now become our passion, and we’re glad to share it with you. We’re a team of dedicated repair technicians with a genuine desire to make sure that your experience with us is easy, convenient and satisfying. Restoring heating and cooling to your home!

Where we work

We provide HVAC repair and installation services to Bluffdale, Riverton, South Jordan, Sandy, Herriman and Cottonwood Heights, from our base in Draper.
