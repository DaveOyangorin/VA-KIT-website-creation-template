What we work on

Every box that heats or cools

We service all major brands of furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, air handlers, space heaters, fireplaces and rooftop equipment.

Heat

Furnaces

Repair, yearly maintenance and replacement. On the heating side, a tune-up includes checking gas connections, gas pressure, burner combustion and the heat exchanger.

Cool

Air conditioners

Repair, maintenance and new installs. Cooling maintenance covers cleaning the evaporator and condenser coils, checking refrigerant and adjusting the blower for proper airflow.

Our history

Since 2015, one mission

We were established in 2015 with one mission: to bring a reliable, high quality furnace and air conditioning repair service to the Salt Lake City area. Our passion for excellence is what inspired us to open up our shop, and continues to drive us to this day.

What started out as a repair service has become our passion. We’re a team of dedicated technicians with a genuine desire to make sure your experience with us is easy, convenient and satisfying.
