Maintenance service

Keep your equipment running like new for years to come. Regular maintenance prevents premature parts failures. ENERGY STAR recommends a yearly tune-up, and its pre-season checklist is a good picture of what a visit covers:

  • Checking thermostat settings
  • Tightening electrical connections and measuring motor voltage and current
  • Lubricating moving parts
  • Checking the condensate drain
  • Checking the system’s controls, startup cycle and shutoff
  • For cooling: cleaning the evaporator and condenser coils, checking the refrigerant level, and cleaning and adjusting blower components
  • For heating: checking gas connections, gas pressure, burner combustion and the heat exchanger

Between visits, ENERGY STAR suggests checking your filter every month. See our guide to furnace filters and MERV ratings.
