01

Outdoor Lighting

The right outdoor lighting does more than boost curb appeal—it enhances safety, security, and functionality. We design and install landscape, pathway, security, and patio lighting to highlight features, improve visibility, and create inviting outdoor spaces. From concept to installation, we’ll help you choose lighting that fits your style, budget, and long-term goals.

02

Outdoor Outlets & Power

Need more power outside? Whether it’s for seasonal decorations, electric tools, or powering up your patio, we install outdoor-rated outlets and circuits that are weather-resistant and safely grounded. We also offer GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) outlets for added protection against electrical shock in damp or wet areas.

03

Spa, Hot Tub & Pool Wiring

Are you installing a new spa or pool? These features require specialized wiring and grounding to ensure safe operation. We handle everything from dedicated circuits to bonding and grounding systems, ensuring your setup meets local codes and manufacturer specifications. If you already have a spa or hot tub that’s giving you issues, we also provide troubleshooting and repairs to keep things running smoothly and safely.

04

Generators

Don’t get left in the dark when the power goes out. We install and service backup generators to keep essential systems like refrigerators, lights, and medical devices running when the grid goes down. We’ll help you choose the right size and type of generator for your home, install it professionally, and make sure it's ready when you need it most.

05

EV Charger Installation

If you’ve made the switch to an electric vehicle, we make charging at home simple and efficient. We install all levels of EV chargers for faster charging and long-term convenience. Whether it’s in your garage, driveway, or a custom location, we’ll ensure the electrical system is prepared to handle the load safely and reliably. We also handle panel upgrades if your current electrical system doesn’t have the capacity to support an EV charger.

06

Panel & Meter Upgrades

Your electrical panel is the heart of your home’s power system, and if you’re adding high-demand features like a pool, hot tub, or EV charger, your panel may need an upgrade. From there, we recommend and install panels and meters that provide the capacity and safety your system requires. A panel upgrade is a smart way to future-proof your home, reduce the risk of overloads, and ensure everything runs without a hitch.
