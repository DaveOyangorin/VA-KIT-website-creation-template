01

Electrical Repairs & Installations

Does your home have an outlet that’s not working or a breaker that keeps tripping? Have you noticed wires or outlets that are warmer than they should be? These can be signs of electrical problems within your home. Our skilled electricians diagnose and repair all kinds of electrical issues, ensuring your home stays safe and functional. If you need something new installed, we make sure it’s done right the first time. Whether it’s ceiling fans, new outlets, or high-tech home theater wiring, our licensed electricians are here to help.

02

Electrical Remodels & Whole-Home Rewiring

Remodeling your home can be a stressful process, but when it comes to the electrical system, you don’t have to worry. Are you adding a room or even finishing off the basement? Don’t forget about the electrical work that’s required, including new outlets, lighting, and even system upgrades. We’ll work with you to ensure that you have the power you need and that it’s safe and up to code. Do you need to rewire an older system? From smaller areas to whole-home rewiring, our residential electricians ensure that the wiring is up-to-date, efficient, and fully functional. We have the expertise to get the job done safely.

03

Lighting & Landscape Electrical

Brighten up your outdoor space with professional lighting installations. From energy-efficient LED upgrades to stunning landscape lighting that enhances your home’s curb appeal, our team has the expertise and experience you can rely on. If you’re considering new lighting inside the home, look no further than EZ Electric for your lighting needs. We can install under-cabinet lighting, recessed lighting, security lighting, and more. When considering the comfort of your home, the right lighting makes all the difference.

04

Electrical Panels & Meters

Your electrical panel is the heart of your home’s power system. If your home still has an outdated panel or you’re experiencing frequent power surges, it might be time for an upgrade. We install and replace electrical panels and meters to ensure your home has the power it needs for modern appliances and electronics. We’ll even work with the city to ensure that everything is up to code and follows all local ordinances.

05

EV Chargers – Charge Up at Home

Do you drive an electric vehicle, or are you considering getting one? Having the right power supply to keep you going is a crucial part of the equation. At EZ Electric, we install EV chargers to keep your car powered properly, giving you faster and more convenient charging right in your own garage. No more waiting in line at public charging stations; just plug in and power up from home!

06

Hot Tubs, Pools & Spas

Adding a little luxury to your backyard? We safely wire hot tubs, pools, and spas so you can enjoy your oasis worry-free. We’ll make sure your setup meets safety codes and is ready for relaxation. From hookups and panels to mood lighting and more, we help you enjoy your downtime in style.

07

Generators – Stay Powered During Outages

Don’t let a power outage leave you in the dark. A whole-home generator provides a reliable backup power source, keeping your essential appliances, lights, and HVAC system running when the grid goes down. Having a generator ensures your home stays comfortable and functional. We install and service a variety of emergency generators that automatically kick in when the power goes out, so you never have to worry about losing electricity.

08

Smoke & Carbon Monoxide Detectors

Safety first! Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors are essential for protecting your home and family from fire and deadly gas leaks. Our electricians make sure your detectors are properly wired, interconnected, and up to code. Whether you're upgrading to the latest technology or ensuring your existing system is fully functional, we’ll handle everything so you can rest easy knowing your home is safeguarded 24/7.
