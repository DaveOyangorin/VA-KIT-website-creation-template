Commercial Services

Running a business comes with plenty of challenges—your electrical system shouldn’tbe one of them. We minimize downtime and ensure your systems are efficient and dependable.

Reliable Electrical Solutions for Local Utah Businesses

Whether you’re expanding your space, upgrading outdated wiring, or need a quick repair to keep things running, we’re here to make sure your electrical needs are handled safely, efficiently, and with minimal disruption to your operations. Our licensed and experienced electricians specialize in commercial electrical work, serving businesses of all sizes with dependable, code-compliant solutions. From retail storefronts to warehouses, office buildings to industrial sites, we keep your business powered and productive.

Electrical Repairs

Have you had a power outage in the building? Is something regularly tripping breakers? Are there flickering lights all over the office? We offer fast, reliable repair services to diagnose and fix electrical issues before they interrupt your business or become safety hazards. We respond quickly and work efficiently to get you back up and running.

Installations

Whether you’re adding a new wing or upgrading your existing setup, our team installs everything from outlets and lighting to large-scale commercial equipment. We ensure each component is installed to meet your unique needs and the latest safety standards.

Electrical Remodels

If your business is undergoing a remodel or renovation, our commercial electricians work closely with your general contractor to update or redesign your electrical layout. We’ll make sure everything from lighting placement to panel capacity is optimized for your space and operations.

Lighting

Let there be light, exactly where and how you need it. We handle all types of commercial lighting, including energy-efficient upgrades, LED retrofits, emergency lighting, motion sensors, and custom lighting designs for both interiors and exteriors. Whether you need new lighting solutions or you simply want to make sure you’re getting the best lighting options possible, we’re here to help.

Electrical Inspections &
Code Violation Correction

Need to assess your system’s safety or prepare for a sale or expansion? Are you concerned about electrical code violations that easily become a headache? Our thorough electrical inspections identify potential hazards and inefficiencies before they turn into costly problems. We’ll give you a clear, actionable report to keep your system up to par. Avoid fines and ensure a safe environment for your team and customers with EZ Electric.

Panels & Meters

If your electrical panel is outdated or you’re adding more load to your system, we offer panel upgrades and new meter installations to ensure your system is equipped to handle your current and future needs.

EV Chargers

With more companies switching to electric vehicle fleets or offering EV charging as an employee perk, we understand that your business may need a little help. At EZ Electric, we install commercial-grade EV charging stations with scalable options for growth. Whether you need a single charging station or several, give us a call!

New Construction

If you’re building from the ground up, you’ll need an electrical contractor or licensed electrician to ensure that you have the power you need, both for now and for future growth. We provide complete electrical solutions for new commercial construction, working directly with your builder to ensure everything is installed efficiently, up to code, and ready for inspection.

Smoke & Carbon Monoxide Detectors

Safety first. We install and maintain smoke and CO detectors that comply with commercial safety regulations and keep your staff and property protected.

Landscape Lighting & Electrical

Create a welcoming and secure environment with custom landscape lighting and outdoor electrical systems. Whether it’s accent lighting for a storefront or power for signage, we’ll design and install your outdoor and landscape lighting to fit your needs.

Generators

Don’t let a power outage take your business offline. We install and service commercial backup generators to keep your essential systems up and running no matter what the weather, or the grid, is doing.

Commercial Machine Power

If you’re running heavy machinery or specialized commercial equipment, we’ll ensure you have the correct power supply and infrastructure to support your operations without overload or downtime.

Tenant Improvement

Need to customize a leased space for your business? Our tenant improvement services provide targeted electrical upgrades to meet the unique demands of your industry, on time and on budget.
