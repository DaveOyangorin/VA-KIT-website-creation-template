What the audit found

  • The home page has 6 H1 headings, some empty.

  • Pages in the sitemap: 16.

  • Tap-to-call phone link: yes.
  • Business schema: no.
  • Images without alt text: 29 of 49.
  • Pages that name Midvale: 0.

What to do

  • Speed: 35/100 now. Compress images, lazy-load, remove unneeded scripts. Target 90+.
  • Home page has 379 words. Write 800+ useful words (services, area, process, FAQ).
  • Add a unique meta description to every page.
  • Use exactly one H1 per page (home page has 6).
  • Add LocalBusiness schema with the exact name, address and phone.
  • Add alt text to images (29 of 49 have none).
  • Name Midvale in the title, H1 and first paragraph of every service page.
