Professional Rat Control Services

Rats are highly destructive pests that pose serious risks to both property and health. Once established, they chew through wiring, insulation, and structural materials, creating fire hazards and costly damage. Rat urine and droppings contaminate surfaces, spread harmful bacteria, and leave scent trails that attract additional rodents if not properly addressed.

Unlike some pests, rats remain active year-round. They commonly nest under concrete slabs, sheds, decks, crawlspaces, and within walls or attics, often staying hidden while damage and contamination continue to build. Without professional intervention, rat problems tend to worsen over time and can spread throughout a structure or neighboring properties.

Rat Trapping and Baiting Programs

Control methods may include professional baiting programs using tamper-resistant stations placed strategically along exterior foundations, fences, and pressure points to reduce populations quickly. Safe and effective trapping is used in indoor areas or locations where baiting is not appropriate, ensuring targeted control without unnecessary risk.

Rat Exclusion and Seal-Up Services

Exclusion is essential for permanent rat control. Once activity is reduced, I perform full seal-up and exclusion work using durable, chew-resistant materials designed to block access and withstand continued rodent pressure. Proper exclusion prevents re-entry and stops new rats from moving in after removal.

Professional Rat Control Services

I provide comprehensive rat control services for both residential and commercial properties, combining aggressive removal with long-term prevention. Effective rat control requires identifying where rats are nesting, how they are accessing the structure, and what conditions are allowing them to survive and reproduce.

What Damage Do Rats Cause?

Rats can cause extensive property damage by chewing through wiring, insulation, and structural materials, which increases the risk of fire hazards and expensive repairs. They also create serious contamination concerns, as urine and droppings spread harmful bacteria and can impact indoor air quality and surface cleanliness. Scent trails left behind can attract additional rodents, making infestations harder to eliminate if not addressed properly.

Inspection and Customized Rat Control Plans

Each rat service begins with a detailed inspection of the entire property, including structures, foundations, crawlspaces, attics, sheds, and surrounding grounds. This inspection identifies active and potential entry points, nesting zones, burrows, and travel routes. Based on these findings, I design a customized removal and prevention plan tailored to the property and severity of activity.
