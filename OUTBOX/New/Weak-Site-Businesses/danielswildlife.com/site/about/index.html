Why I Started Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest

Before starting my own company, I helped build and scale established pest and wildlife operations from the ground up. I was involved in developing service systems, training standards, and operational processes that supported their growth and reputation.

As those companies expanded, quality declined. Services became rushed, problems were patched instead of properly solved, and customers were left dealing with repeat issues that should have been prevented the first time.

Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest was created to return to high-standard, detail-driven work where accountability and results come first. When you hire me, you are not dealing with rotating technicians, sales-driven service plans, or shortcuts. You are working directly with the person responsible for the outcome of the job.

Locally Owned and Owner-Operated

I am the owner and the technician on every job. With over eight years of hands-on experience throughout the region, I have personally handled thousands of pest and wildlife cases across both residential and commercial properties. My work includes single-family homes, multi-unit buildings, property-managed communities, retail locations, offices, and specialty commercial sites.

Licensed, Insured, and Professional

I am fully licensed and insured, and all work is performed in accordance with applicable state and local requirements. This is especially important for commercial and managed properties where liability, documentation, and compliance matter.

Built on Consistency, Transparency, and Results

Homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients choose Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest because they want consistency, transparency, and results. Commercial clients in particular value reliability, communication, and accountability, and those expectations are built into how I operate from scheduling and access coordination to follow-through and long-term prevention.

When you work with me, you are getting experienced, professional service from someone who takes personal responsibility for every job from start to finish. No shortcuts. No guesswork. Just clean, effective solutions designed to last.

A Thorough Inspection Comes First

Every service begins with a thorough inspection and an honest assessment of what is actually happening on the property. I evaluate animal behavior, insect activity, access points, structural conditions, sanitation issues, and environmental factors before recommending a plan. The goal is not just removal, but prevention that holds up over time.

Trusted by Homeowners and Commercial Properties

I am fully licensed and insured, and all work is performed in accordance with applicable state and local requirements. This is especially important for commercial and managed properties where liability, documentation, and compliance matter.

Integrated Pest Management Approach

My approach is based on integrated pest management principles. This means identifying why the problem started, what conditions allowed it to persist, and what needs to change to prevent future activity. Treatments are applied responsibly and strategically, with a focus on long-term effectiveness rather than unnecessary product use.

No Quick Fixes. Real Solutions That Last.

I do not believe in quick fixes, rushed services, or one-size-fits-all treatment plans. Effective pest and wildlife control requires strategy, experience, and attention to detail. Every property is different, and I design customized solutions that fully remove the issue while focusing on long-term prevention rather than temporary relief.

Bird Control and Exclusion Specialists

Bird control is a major area of specialization within my work. I provide pigeon control, solar panel bird exclusion, custom bird netting installations, and the safe removal of birds from vents and ventilation systems.

These services require precise planning, proper materials, and correct installation to be effective long-term, and they are frequently mishandled by companies that do not specialize in bird work.

Cleanup, Sanitation, and Damage Repair

In addition to removal and exclusion, I provide cleanup, sanitation, and minor damage repair services. Wildlife and bird activity often leaves behind droppings, nesting material, odors, and harmful pathogens that must be addressed for a job to be truly complete.

Structural sealing and exclusion work is performed using durable materials designed to prevent future entry.
