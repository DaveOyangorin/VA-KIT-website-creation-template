Bird Removal & Exclusion Services

Northern Utah & the Salt Lake Valley

Call or text 24/7 (801) 783-9638

Birds nesting in or around your home can lead to serious health risks, property damage, and persistent noise issues. Whether they’re in your attic, soffits, vents, or wall voids, birds leave behind droppings, nesting material, and parasites that contaminate air and damage structures.

I offer safe, humane bird removal and exclusion services designed to fully eliminate the problem and stop it from coming back. A major focus of my bird work involves solar panel cleanouts, where pigeons and other birds often build dense nests underneath the panels. Over time, these areas fill with droppings, feathers, and waste. Bird pheromones left behind can continue to attract more birds, growing the infestation. In many cases, gutters become completely clogged, making them ineffective and leading to water damage, overflow, and staining near siding and entryways. With over 8 years of experience handling both nuisance birds and protected species, I do the job right—clean, legal, and built to last.

Call Daniel’s Wildlife to schedule a full bird and solar panel inspection today.

I provide full solar panel bird services including:

  • Removal of all birds and nesting materials from under and around panels
  • Elimination of pheromone traces to stop re-attraction
  • Roof, gutter, and ground-level cleanup of droppings and debris
  • Gutter clearing and sanitation to restore proper water drainage
  • Full sealing of solar panel edges to prevent access
  • Installation of bird deterrents to make rooftops less appealing

In addition to rooftop bird work, I also:

  • Humanely remove birds from attics, soffits, vents, and walls
  • Remove hazardous nests located in areas that threaten human health
  • Replace soft ventilation passages (such as flex ducting and dryer vent hoses) that have been packed with nesting materials, restoring safe and proper airflow
  • Offer complete cleanup, sanitation, and exclusion to keep birds out for good
