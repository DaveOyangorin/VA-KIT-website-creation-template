Professional Insect Control Services

Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest provides professional, full-service insect control for residential and commercial properties. Unlike companies that rely on routine spraying alone, I take a comprehensive approach that focuses on identifying the source of the problem, correcting conditions that allow insects to thrive, and preventing repeat activity over time.

Specialized Insect Treatment Services

I also provide specialized treatments for more challenging insect problems. Bed bug treatments are offered using chemical-only applications with proven effectiveness. Cockroach control services are available for light to severe infestations and may include targeted baiting, sanitation guidance, and ongoing monitoring to achieve lasting results.

Wasp and Stinging Insect Control

Wasp and stinging insect control is a frequent concern, especially when nests are located in elevated or hard-to-reach areas. I safely remove nests from eaves, rooflines, second stories, attics, walls, and crawlspaces, addressing both visible nests and hidden activity to reduce the risk of stings and repeat nesting.

Exclusion and Sealing Services

Exclusion and sealing services are available as an add-on and are highly recommended for properties experiencing recurring insect issues. I identify where insects are gaining access and offer durable sealing solutions using crack and gap sealing, screening, and appropriate materials designed to provide long-term protection.

Integrated Pest Management Approach

All insect services are performed using an Integrated Pest Management approach. Every situation is evaluated individually and treated with a customized strategy based on pest type, activity level, structure layout, and environmental factors. The goal is effective elimination and long-term control rather than temporary surface-level relief.

Seasonal Insect Problems in Utah

Seasonal nuisance insects such as box elder bugs, elm seed bugs, flies, moths, and earwigs are especially common in Utah homes as temperatures change. These pests often invade in large numbers and can be difficult to control without addressing entry points and exterior pressure zones. I focus on reducing interior activity while also managing the exterior conditions that drive infestations indoors.
