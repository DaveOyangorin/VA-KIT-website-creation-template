Professional Squirrel Removal Services

I provide professional, humane squirrel removal, trapping, and exclusion services focused on eliminating active infestations and stopping repeat entry. Squirrel problems are rarely isolated to a single hole or location, and effective control requires addressing how and why they continue to access the structure.

Cleanup and Sanitation Services

Squirrel infestations frequently leave behind nesting material, droppings, urine, and scent trails that can attract continued wildlife interest if not addressed. Cleanup and sanitation services are available to remove contamination, reduce odors, and restore affected areas as part of the overall solution.

What Damage Do Squirrels Cause?

Squirrel activity commonly results in damaged rooflines, compromised soffits, torn insulation, and chewed electrical wiring. Their persistent gnawing increases the risk of electrical fires and leads to hidden structural deterioration inside attics and wall cavities. Because much of this damage occurs out of sight, squirrel problems often worsen over time if not addressed properly.

Stop Squirrel Damage and Fire Risk Before It Gets Worse

If squirrels are active inside your attic or structure, professional trapping, removal, and exclusion are critical to preventing further damage and fire risk. Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest provides squirrel control solutions designed for long-term protection and structural safety.

Inspection and Customized Removal Plans

Each squirrel service begins with a comprehensive inspection of the roofline, attic, and surrounding areas to identify entry points, nesting locations, and damage patterns. Based on these findings, I develop a customized removal plan specific to the structure and level of activity. Humane trapping is used when appropriate, along with other removal measures designed to resolve the issue without creating ongoing pressure points.

Addressing Breeding Activity and Repeat Entry

Squirrel activity often intensifies during breeding seasons, and mothers may establish multiple nesting areas within a structure. These behaviors are taken into account when determining the proper approach so the problem is resolved completely rather than partially. Once the squirrels are removed, vulnerable areas are secured using durable materials intended to withstand continued chewing attempts.

With over eight years of hands-on wildlife and pest control experience, I provide honest assessments and effective squirrel control solutions based on what the property actually needs. My recommendations are straightforward and focused on results, not unnecessary treatments.
