Dead Animal Removal & Sanitation Services

Northern Utah & the Salt Lake Valley

Call or text 24/7 (801) 783-9638

A deceased animal in or around a home or building is more than an unpleasant situation. It is a serious health and sanitation concern that can quickly lead to strong odors, insect activity, and the spread of bacteria. Dead animals left inside walls, attics, crawlspaces, or on the property can contaminate surrounding materials and make living spaces unsafe if not handled properly.

Professional Dead Animal Removal Services

Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest provides fast, professional dead animal removal services with full cleanup and sanitation. Whether the animal is visible or you are only noticing an odor, I can locate the source, remove it safely, and address the contamination left behind.

Locating Hidden Animals

Dead animal situations often involve hidden locations such as wall voids, attics, crawlspaces, under decks, sheds, or landscaped areas. I use proven methods to locate carcasses efficiently and remove them with care and discretion. Proper handling and disposal are critical to preventing further health risks and pest activity.

Common Dead Animal Removal Situations

Common dead animal removals include raccoons, skunks, squirrels, rats, mice, birds, and snakes. These situations may occur naturally or as a result of previous pest activity, exclusion work, or environmental factors. Regardless of the cause, prompt removal is essential to limit damage and health concerns.

Preventing Future Issues

As part of the service, I inspect the surrounding area to determine how the animal gained access and whether additional prevention or exclusion work is recommended. Addressing entry points helps reduce the likelihood of future incidents and recurring odor issues.

Restore Safety and Comfort Quickly

If you are dealing with a dead animal or unexplained odor on your property, professional removal and sanitation are the safest and most effective solution. Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest provides prompt service focused on cleanliness, safety, and restoring your space as quickly as possible.

Cleanup, Sanitation, and Deodorization Services

Once the animal is removed, cleanup and sanitation services are performed to address bodily fluids, insects, nesting material, and contaminated surfaces. Disinfection and deodorization are used to eliminate bacteria and lingering odors rather than simply masking the smell.

Why Choose Daniel’s Wildlife and Pest?

With over eight years of hands-on wildlife and pest control experience, I provide effective dead animal removal and sanitation services based on what the property actually needs. My recommendations are straightforward and focused on results, not unnecessary treatments.
