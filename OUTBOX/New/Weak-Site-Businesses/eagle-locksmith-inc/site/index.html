How locksmith scams work

Borrowed names

BBB says scammers often advertise using the name and logo of a reputable locksmith.

02

Generic greetings

Callers may hear “locksmith services” instead of a business name.

03

Lowball quotes

A very low phone price, then reasons on site to charge much more.

04

Needless drilling

BBB: it isn’t routine for a locksmith to insist on drilling a lock to open it.
