The northern Utah yard calendar

When the work should happen.

Timing from USU Extension’s Basic Turfgrass Care for the northern region, and the Action services that line up with it.

MowingMarch – November

Mow at about 3 inches; never cut more than a third of the blade.

Action: Weekly lawn mowing with string trimming & edging
IrrigationApril – October

Water in the early morning, ½ to 1 inch per irrigation.

Action: Sprinkler start up in spring, shut down in fall, check ups and repair
AerationMarch – May · Sept – Oct

Aerate to improve water infiltration and reduce compaction and thatch.

Action: Aeration
FertilizingApril · June · October

Typical home lawns need only 2–3 lbs of nitrogen per 1,000 sq ft a year.

Action: Fertilizing and lawn treatment programs
SeedingSept – Oct (best) · April – May

Fall is the best window for seeding and overseeding.

Action: Sod and new landscaping & renovation

Source: Kelly Kopp and Paul Johnson, USU Extension, Basic Turfgrass Care (2011), northern Utah region.
