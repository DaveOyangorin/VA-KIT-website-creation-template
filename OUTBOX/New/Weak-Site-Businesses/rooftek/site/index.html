How steep is your roof?

Roofers describe slope as rise over a 12-inch run: a 6/12 roof climbs 6 inches for every foot across. Set yours, add the home’s footprint, and see the angle and roughly how much roof that really is.

What makes a roof stronger

Four upgrades from IBHS research

What a FORTIFIED roof is
01

Stronger edges

The FORTIFIED Roof standard calls for a wider drip edge and a fully adhered starter strip, to help keep the edge of the roof covering from peeling up.

02

A sealed roof deck

Sealing the seams of the roof deck can reduce water intrusion by up to 95% if the shingles blow off, according to IBHS.

03

Ring-shank nails

IBHS says ring-shank nails in an enhanced pattern nearly double a roof’s strength against wind compared with smooth nails.

04

Impact-rated shingles

FORTIFIED’s optional hail supplement requires asphalt shingles rated Good or Excellent in IBHS’s hail impact ratings.

Source: Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety, FORTIFIED Roof (fortifiedhome.org). Ask any roofer whether they are FORTIFIED-trained.
