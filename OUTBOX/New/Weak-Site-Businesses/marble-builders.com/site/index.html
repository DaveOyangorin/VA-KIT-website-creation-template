After the damage

Mitigation dries it out. We put it back.

After a burst pipe or a fire, two companies usually touch your home. Here’s who does what, and where Marble Builders takes over.

Restoration companyMarble Builders

01 · Restoration company

Emergency & mitigation

The restoration company stops the damage: water extraction, drying and dehumidification, moisture mapping, removal of soaked drywall, flooring and insulation; soot, smoke and odor removal; mold containment.

02 · Restoration company

Documentation

Photos, moisture readings and a mitigation scope go to your insurance adjuster.

03 · Where most homeowners stall

The gap

Restoration companies don’t typically rebuild what was removed. This is where many homeowners get stuck, left to find, hire and coordinate a contractor on their own.

04 · Marble Builders

Rebuild scope

Marble reviews the restoration scope, coordinates with the project manager and plans the rebuild, knowing which items need supplements and how to document the work for the carrier.

05 · Marble Builders

Rebuild

Drywall, flooring, cabinetry, kitchens and bathrooms put back, with clear communication to you and your adjuster from demo to finish.

02Do restoration companies rebuild homes?

Not always. Many handle mitigation (drying, demo, cleanup) but don’t rebuild what was removed, so you may need a separate contractor. That’s where Marble Builders steps in.
