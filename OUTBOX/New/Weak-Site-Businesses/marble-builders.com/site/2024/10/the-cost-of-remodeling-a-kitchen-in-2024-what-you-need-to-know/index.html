The Cost of Remodeling a Kitchen in 2024: What You Need to Know

Marble Builders blog · 2024

Get a quote 801-828-5540

Remodeling a kitchen is one of the most exciting (and sometimes stressful) home improvement projects you can undertake. In 2024, the cost of giving your kitchen a makeover has shifted slightly due to changing trends, fluctuating material prices, and labor costs. Whether you’re planning a total overhaul or a modest upgrade, knowing what to expect in terms of budget will help you navigate the process smoothly and get the most value for your money.

Let’s break down the costs of a kitchen remodel in 2024 so you can plan your dream kitchen without breaking the bank.

The Average Cost of a Kitchen Remodel in 2024

How much does it actually cost to remodel a kitchen in 2024? The answer depends largely on the scope of your project. On average, a kitchen remodel can cost anywhere from $15,000 to $75,000. However, luxury renovations in high-end areas can easily exceed that range, while minor updates can cost considerably less.

  • Minor remodel: If you’re just updating your cabinets, swapping out old appliances, or installing new countertops, expect to spend around $10,000 to $25,000. This is the perfect budget range for a small kitchen or a basic upgrade.
  • Mid-range remodel: A more comprehensive renovation involving new cabinetry, countertops, and appliances can cost between $30,000 and $50,000.
  • High-end remodel: For a full kitchen transformation with custom cabinetry, high-end appliances, luxury finishes, and structural changes, you could be looking at upwards of $60,000 to $100,000 or more.

Factors That Impact Kitchen Remodel Costs

Several factors play into how much you’ll spend on your kitchen remodel in 2024. Here are some key elements that will affect your budget:

Materials

The materials you choose for your kitchen will have a significant impact on your total cost. For example, custom cabinetry is far more expensive than stock or semi-custom options. Similarly, natural stone countertops like marble or granite are pricier than laminate or butcher block alternatives.

If you’re on a budget, opting for mid-range materials like quartz or engineered wood can give you the high-end look without the high-end price tag.

Appliances

Upgrading to modern, energy-efficient appliances can quickly add to your budget. A basic appliance package (fridge, oven, microwave, and dishwasher) can cost between $3,000 to $8,000, but high-end brands like Sub-Zero or Wolf can push that number much higher. If you’re aiming for a luxurious kitchen, the cost of top-of-the-line appliances could easily exceed $15,000.

Labor Costs

Labor costs can account for a significant portion of your remodeling budget. From plumbing and electrical work to flooring installation and cabinetry, you’ll likely be paying a skilled crew to bring your vision to life. In 2024, expect to spend around 20-30% of your total budget on labor. Hiring reputable contractors can increase costs, but it’s worth investing in experienced professionals to ensure your remodel is done right.

Structural Changes

If you’re planning to knock down walls, move plumbing, or adjust the layout of your kitchen, prepare for additional expenses. Structural changes often require permits, inspections, and professional work that can increase your costs by $5,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on the complexity of the project.

Where to Splurge and Where to Save

While it’s tempting to go all out on every aspect of your kitchen remodel, balancing splurges with savings is the key to staying on budget. Here’s where you should consider spending more and where you can cut costs without sacrificing style.

Splurge: Countertops and Appliances

Your countertops and appliances are the heart of the kitchen, both functionally and aesthetically. Investing in durable, high-quality countertops like quartz or granite will not only elevate the look of your kitchen but also stand the test of time. The same goes for appliances—energy-efficient, reliable models will save you money on utility bills and repairs in the long run.

Save: Cabinets and Lighting

You don’t have to invest in custom cabinetry to achieve a high-end look. Stock or semi-custom cabinets offer plenty of design options at a fraction of the cost. You can even paint or refinish existing cabinets for an affordable facelift.

For lighting, consider stylish but affordable fixtures that brighten your space without busting your budget. Pendant lights and under-cabinet lighting can make a big impact without a hefty price tag.

Tips for Keeping Costs Down

Remodeling a kitchen doesn’t have to drain your bank account. Here are a few tips to keep costs under control while still achieving your dream kitchen:

  • 1. Plan ahead: Having a detailed plan and budget in place will help you avoid costly changes mid-project.
  • 2. DIY where possible: If you’re handy, consider tackling small tasks like painting or installing light fixtures yourself.
  • 3. Shop sales: Look for appliance sales, especially around holidays, to score major savings on big-ticket items.
  • 4. Reuse and repurpose: If your cabinets or appliances are in good shape, consider refinishing or refacing them instead of replacing them entirely.

Is a Kitchen Remodel Worth the Investment?

A kitchen remodel is one of the most valuable home improvements you can make. On average, homeowners can expect to recoup 60-80% of their kitchen remodel costs in added home value, making it a smart investment whether you plan to stay in your home for years or sell in the near future.

Conclusion

Remodeling your kitchen in 2024 offers endless possibilities, from sleek modern designs to timeless traditional styles. While the costs can vary widely, with careful planning and strategic choices, you can create a stunning kitchen within your budget. Whether you choose to splurge on luxury finishes or opt for affordable alternatives, the key is to design a space that feels functional, stylish, and personal to your needs. So, take your time, explore your options, and turn your kitchen into the heart of your home.
