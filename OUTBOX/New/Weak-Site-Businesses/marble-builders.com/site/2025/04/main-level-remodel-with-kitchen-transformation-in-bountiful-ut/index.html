Main Level Remodel with Kitchen Transformation in Bountiful, UT

Marble Builders blog · 2025

Get a quote 801-828-5540

Project: Macha Home Remodel Location: Bountiful, Utah

This main-level remodel brought new life and functionality to a beautiful home in Bountiful. Our team at Marble Builders completed a comprehensive renovation, with standout improvements to the kitchen, bathrooms, and common areas—all designed to support both everyday living and entertaining in style.

Project Highlights:

  • Custom kitchen overhaul featuring sleek new cabinetry with soft-close doors and drawers, a large island with a 12” overhang for seating, and stunning quartz countertops with a matching backsplash and windowsill.
  • Wood flooring updates including refinishing throughout and seamless patchwork where the kitchen wall was removed.
  • Wall removal between kitchen and family room replaced with a hidden structural beam to open up the main living space.
  • New recessed lighting throughout the home, including family room, office, bedrooms, and both bathrooms.
  • Fresh two-tone paint and upgraded baseboards and trim add polish across the entire main level.

Bathroom Upgrades:

  • The main bath was reimagined with a new layout, now accessed directly from the primary bedroom. A frameless Euro shower with tile to the ceiling, new vanity, and lighting upgrades bring a modern, spa-like feel.
  • The guest bath was redesigned for better access and updated with a new vanity, stone countertops, and tile flooring.

Additional Updates Include:

  • A fully reworked primary bedroom closet with an open design.
  • Updated window sills in every room.
  • Crown and chair molding removal in the office and guest bedroom for a cleaner look.
  • Electrical panel relocation for better accessibility.

This transformation not only modernized the home but made it more livable and luxurious. The warm wood tones, rich cabinetry, and polished stonework reflect the thoughtful attention to detail Marble Builders brings to every space.
