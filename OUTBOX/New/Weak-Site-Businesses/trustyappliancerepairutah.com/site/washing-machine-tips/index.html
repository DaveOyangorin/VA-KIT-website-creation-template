Making Your Washing Machine Last: Essential Tips

From the blog · Sep 2, 2023

Schedule a Repair (801) 255-9063

Your washing machine is a household workhorse, handling loads of laundry day in and day out. To get the most out of this valuable appliance, it’s crucial to implement proper care and maintenance practices. By giving your washing machine the attention it deserves, you can extend its lifespan and keep it running smoothly for years to come. Here’s a comprehensive guide on how to do just that.

Mindful Loading: Don't Overload

Resist the urge to stuff your washing machine with more clothes than it can handle. Overloading not only affects the quality of your wash but also puts unnecessary strain on the machine’s components. Refer to your washing machine’s manual for recommended load capacities and stick to them to prevent premature wear and tear.

Use the Right Detergent and Amount

Using the correct type and amount of detergent is essential for both your laundry and your washing machine. High-efficiency (HE) detergents are designed for modern machines and are more efficient in cleaning while producing fewer suds. Excess suds can lead to residue buildup and strain the machine’s motor and pump.

Clean the Drum and Dispensers

Regular cleaning of the washing machine’s drum and detergent dispensers is crucial. Over time, soap scum, lint, and dirt can accumulate, leading to unpleasant odors and reduced efficiency.

Choose Lower Temperatures

Washing clothes in hot water might seem effective, but it can wear out your washing machine faster and lead to higher energy consumption. Modern detergents are designed to work well even in cooler water, and this practice will help extend the life of your machine’s heating elements.

Inspect and Clean Hoses

Regularly check the hoses that supply water to your washing machine. Look for any signs of cracks, bulges, or leaks. Replace hoses if you notice any issues, to prevent water damage to your machine and surrounding areas. Additionally, clean the inlet screens to prevent debris from entering the machine.

Balance and Leveling

A washing machine that isn’t level can cause excessive vibrations during the spin cycle, potentially leading to damage. Use a leveling tool to ensure your machine is evenly balanced. Adjust the machine’s feet, if needed, to eliminate wobbling and reduce strain on internal components.

Leave the Door Open

After completing a load, leave the washing machine door slightly open to allow air circulation. This prevents mold and mildew growth and helps keep the machine’s interior fresh.

Clean the Lint Filter

Lint and debris can accumulate in your washing machine’s lint filter. Regularly clean this filter to ensure proper drainage and prevent clogs.

Address Issues Promptly

If you notice unusual noises, leaks, or any other irregularities, address them promptly. Ignoring these signs can lead to more significant problems down the line. Call Trusty Appliance Repair to get issues addressed.

Professional Maintenance

Consider scheduling annual professional maintenance for your washing machine. Trained technicians at Trusty Appliance Repair can perform thorough checks and identify potential issues that might not be visible to the naked eye.

In conclusion, your washing machine is a valuable investment, and taking proper care of it can help you get the most out of its service life. By following these essential tips, you can ensure that your washing machine continues to provide efficient and reliable performance, making laundry a breeze for years to come.
