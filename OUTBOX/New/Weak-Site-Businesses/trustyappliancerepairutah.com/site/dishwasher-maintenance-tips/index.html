Dishwasher Longevity: Practical Tips for Extending its Lifespan

From the blog · Sep 2, 2023

Schedule a Repair (801) 255-9063

Your dishwasher is a true time-saver, ensuring your dishes come out sparkling clean with minimal effort. To make the most of this modern convenience, it’s important to maintain and care for your dishwasher properly. With the right practices, you can significantly prolong its lifespan and keep it running smoothly for years. In this blog, we’ll delve into some valuable tips to help you achieve just that.

Loading Smartly: Avoid Overloading

While it might be tempting to cram as many dishes as possible into your dishwasher, overloading can hinder its efficiency and strain its components. Follow the manufacturer’s guidelines for load capacity and distribute dishes evenly to ensure proper water circulation and cleaning.

Pre-Rinse Wisely

Modern dishwashers are designed to handle most food residues, so extensive pre-rinsing isn’t necessary. Scrape off large food particles and avoid placing dishes with sticky or heavy residues directly into the dishwasher. However, don’t overload the filter with excess food debris either, as it can affect drainage.

Use the Right Detergent

Selecting the right dishwasher detergent is crucial for both your dishes and the appliance itself. Use detergents specifically formulated for dishwashers to avoid excessive suds, which can damage the dishwasher’s pump and other components.

Keep Sprayer Arms Clean

Sprayer arms distribute water to clean your dishes effectively. Over time, these arms can become clogged with food particles and mineral deposits. Regularly inspect and clean the sprayer arms to ensure proper water flow and optimal cleaning performance.

Clean the Filter

The dishwasher filter prevents debris from entering the drain pump and causing clogs. Clean the filter regularly to maintain efficient drainage and prevent foul odors. Refer to your dishwasher’s manual for instructions on how to access and clean the filter.

Use Hot Water

Using hot water for your dishwasher cycle aids in dissolving detergent and effectively cleaning your dishes. Make sure your water heater is set to an appropriate temperature (around 120°F or 49°C) for optimal dishwasher performance.

Maintain the Door Seal

A proper door seal ensures a watertight enclosure during the wash cycle. Regularly clean and inspect the door seal for any signs of mold, cracks, or wear. Keeping the seal in good condition prevents water leaks and maintains the dishwasher’s efficiency.

Use Soft Water If Possible

If your area has hard water, consider using a water softener. Hard water minerals can build up in your dishwasher, affecting its performance over time. Softened water reduces the likelihood of mineral deposits and helps maintain optimal functioning.

Avoid Overuse of Rinse Aid

Rinse aid helps prevent water spots and aids in drying your dishes. However, excessive use can lead to a sticky residue buildup. Follow the manufacturer’s recommendations for the proper amount of rinse aid to use.

Regular Maintenance Checks

Periodically inspect your dishwasher for signs of wear, leaks, or unusual noises. Address any issues promptly to prevent further damage and extend the appliance’s lifespan.

In conclusion, your dishwasher is a valuable asset in your kitchen, saving you time and effort in the daily chore of dishwashing. By following these practical tips and maintaining regular care, you can ensure that your dishwasher continues to serve you efficiently for years to come. Proper loading, using the right detergent, and keeping its essential components clean are key to maintaining optimal performance and prolonging the life of this indispensable appliance.
