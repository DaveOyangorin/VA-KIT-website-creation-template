Microwave Lifespan: Tips to Make Your Appliance Last Longer

From the blog · Sep 2, 2023

Schedule a Repair (801) 255-9063

The microwave oven has revolutionized modern cooking, making meal preparation quicker and more convenient. With its indispensable role in the kitchen, it’s only natural to want your microwave to last as long as possible. Fortunately, a few simple steps can go a long way in prolonging the lifespan of this essential appliance. In this blog, we’ll explore some valuable tips to help you achieve just that.

Proper Placement Matters

Where you place your microwave can impact its performance and longevity. Ensure that it’s placed on a flat, stable surface with ample space around it for proper ventilation. Avoid placing it near heat sources like stovetops or ovens, as excess heat can strain its components.

Mindful Usage: Follow Guidelines

Respect the manufacturer’s guidelines and usage recommendations. Microwaves are designed for specific tasks, so avoid using them for purposes they’re not intended for, such as heating metal or cooking foods in non-microwave-safe containers. Over time, improper usage can lead to damage and decrease the appliance’s lifespan.

Regular Cleaning: Inside and Out

Keeping your microwave clean not only ensures proper hygiene, but also contributes to its longevity. Clean spills, splatters, and stains as soon as they occur to prevent them from becoming stubborn and harder to remove. Regularly wipe down the interior and exterior surfaces with a damp cloth. For thorough cleaning, microwave a bowl of water mixed with vinegar or lemon juice to steam away stubborn residues.

Use Microwave-Safe Containers

Using microwave-safe containers is essential to prevent potential damage and hazards. Containers not labeled as microwave-safe can release harmful chemicals into your food and damage the microwave’s interior. Stick to glass, ceramic, or microwave-safe plastic containers to ensure safe and efficient heating.

Avoid Empty Heating

Running a microwave without anything inside it can damage the appliance’s magnetron, which generates the microwave radiation. Always place a microwave-safe container with water or food inside the microwave when operating it.

Regular Maintenance Checks

Periodic maintenance checks can help catch potential issues early on. Inspect the microwave’s door seals for any damage or wear, as faulty seals can lead to energy wastage and uneven heating. If you notice any unusual noises, odors, or malfunctions, consider seeking professional help for repairs.

Limit Overheating

Microwaving food for extended periods or at high power levels can cause overheating. To prevent this, use the microwave’s timer wisely and choose appropriate power settings for your needs. Overheating not only affects food quality, but also puts undue stress on the microwave’s components.

Mind the Electronics

Avoid placing electronic devices like smartphones, tablets, or other gadgets on top of your microwave. Microwaves emit electromagnetic radiation, which can potentially interfere with electronic devices and reduce their lifespan.

Power Surges Protection

Power surges can damage sensitive electronics, including your microwave. Consider using a surge protector or unplugging the microwave during thunderstorms or other situations where power fluctuations are likely.

Upgrade Wisely

If your microwave is showing signs of significant wear and tear or has persistent issues, it might be time to consider an upgrade. Modern microwaves often come with advanced features and improved energy efficiency that can enhance your cooking experience.

In conclusion, a few mindful practices can significantly extend the life of your microwave oven. By placing it correctly, using it appropriately, cleaning it regularly, and addressing issues promptly, you can ensure that this indispensable kitchen appliance remains a reliable companion for years to come.
