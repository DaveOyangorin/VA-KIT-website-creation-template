Guided by Purpose, Built on Principles

At Abode, we pride ourselves on our commitment to our mission and three core principles that guide everything we do:

“Our mission is to build small businesses one 5-star service at a time. Abode works with incredible small businesses close to your neighborhood. These small businesses are insured, vetted with the abode quality process, and have been in business for more than 3 years. Over the past several years we have had the pleasure to connect hundreds of wonderful cleaners to neighbors just like you.”

Keep It Simple

Our services are designed to simplify your life, offering a straightforward and hassle-free experience. From booking to cleaning, we keep things simple, ensuring a seamless process for our customers.

Be Transparent

Transparency is the foundation of trust. We are dedicated to being open and honest in all our interactions. You can expect clear communication, detailed information about our services, pricing, and there are no hidden fees. At Abode, we want to build lasting relationships.

Keep Pricing Low

Quality cleaning services shouldn't come with a hefty price tag. We are committed to offering competitive and affordable pricing without compromising on the experience of our work. Our goal is to make professional house cleaning accessible to everyone, ensuring a clean and comfortable home for you without breaking the bank.
