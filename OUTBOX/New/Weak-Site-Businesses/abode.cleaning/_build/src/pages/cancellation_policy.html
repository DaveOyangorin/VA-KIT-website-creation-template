Cancellation Policy

If an appointment needs to be rescheduled or canceled please make sure to do it 24-48 hours prior to the appointment. If an appointment is canceled less than 24-48 hours or same day there will be a $25 cancellation fee. Appointments canceled up to 8 hours prior to the appointment are subject to a $65 cancellation fee.
