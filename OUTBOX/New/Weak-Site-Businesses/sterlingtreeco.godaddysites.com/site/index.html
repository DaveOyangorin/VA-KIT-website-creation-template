The tree check

Is your tree trying to tell you something?

Walk around one tree and answer ISA’s eleven questions. The parts of the tree you flag light up.

0 yes · 0 of 11 answered

  1. Are there large dead branches?
  2. Are there detached, hanging branches?
  3. Have any branches fallen from the tree?
  4. Is there loose bark on the trunk?
  5. Are there cracks or splits in the trunk or where branches are attached?
  6. Has the trunk developed unusually?
  7. Are there cavities or rotten wood along the trunk or in major branches?
  8. Are mushrooms present at the base of or under the tree?
  9. Has the area recently been altered by construction, changes in soil level, or installations of lawns or pavement?
  10. Have the leaves developed an unusual yellow color or do they seem smaller in size?
  11. Has the tree been topped or heavily pruned?

02What does an ISA Certified Arborist do differently?

According to the International Society of Arboriculture, ISA Certified Arborists have proven a level of knowledge in the art and science of tree care through experience and a comprehensive exam, and must continue their education to keep the certification.

03Do you give free estimates?

04Can a weak tree be saved without removing it?

Sometimes. ISA notes that an arborist may suggest pruning out defective branches or cabling and bracing weak branches and stems; such supports increase strength and stability but are not guarantees against failure. Some trees with unacceptable risk are best removed.
