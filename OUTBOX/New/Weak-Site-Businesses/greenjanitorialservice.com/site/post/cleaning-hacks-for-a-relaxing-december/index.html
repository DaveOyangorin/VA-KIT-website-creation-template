Don’t Let the Holidays Overwhelm You: Cleaning Hacks for a Relaxing December

By Claudia Hinojosa · Nov 14, 2025

Get a quote +1 801-577-8703

“Finally, it's December.”

Don’t we all love December? It's the perfect holiday season, a time for family, friends, and celebrations. Most of us have family and friends coming over, which brings joy and excitement and a whirlwind of stress. This is, especially true when it comes to keeping your home clean and presentable.

At Green Janitorial Services, we know how overwhelming December can get, so we’ve compiled a list of cleaning hacks to help you stay ahead and truly enjoy the festive season.

We aim to simplify your cleaning routine, allowing you to focus on what truly matters—creating cherished memories with your loved ones and enjoying the magic of the festive season. With these tips, you can tackle tasks efficiently and keep your home inviting and sparkly clean, making your holiday celebrations even more enjoyable.

Tackle Small Chores Daily

Procrastination can be like a sneaky friend trying to lead you astray!

Start small every day instead of cleaning your entire house at the last minute. Spend half an hour cleaning daily. For example, focus on the kitchen one day; clear out old food and rotten vegetables from the fridge, wipe down surfaces, and sweep the floor to keep the area tidy. On another day, you might tackle the living room—dusting shelves, vacuuming carpets, and organizing clutter. This will help keep the messes as manageable as possible.

You don't have to shoulder all the responsibilities alone. It's important to delegate some tasks to your family members. Assign tasks to your partner—one way is to help with grocery shopping or meal prep. Involve your children as well; they can take on tasks, such as setting the dinner table or tidying up their play area. By working together as a family, you get the house cleaner every day and teach your children valuable life lessons.

Declutter

I am sure you are ready to take out the stunning Christmas trees, twinling lights, and charming vintage decorations from your storage. But, before you dive into your festive spirit and bring anything seasonal out, STOP and take a moment to declutter. Decluttering is the first step to a clean and organized living.

Any non-seasonal decor and items can be kept back in storage. Go through your stuff and donate any items that you won’t be using anymore. This can include clothing that doesn’t fit or you haven’t worn in a while, toys that your children have outgrown, or household items that is no longer used. This not only helps clear up space in your home but also gives your unused items a new life, helping those who may be in need.

High-Traffic Areas

The living room, kitchen, and bathroom are the main hubs for holiday gatherings and activities, so it's essential to keep these areas in good shape. Thoroughly wipe down all surfaces, including coffee tables, countertops, and kitchen islands. Vacuum the carpets and rugs and mop the hardwood floors. Fluff the cushions on sofas and chairs to create a cozy and welcoming atmosphere for guests.

Ensure you have a backup supply of essential items readily available. This supply can include hand soap, toilet paper, and a stack of clean towels readily accessible. Also, you can consider adding extra items such as napkins, paper towels, and glasses.

Deep Clean

Ensure your home sparkles for holiday gatherings by tackling these tasks ahead of time.

  • Clean the oven by removing any burnt-on residue or spills from the it’s interior
  • Wipe down cabinets and clean kitchen surfaces. Pay special attention to handles and knobs, as they often collect grime
  • Remove stains or buildup from your sinks. Disinfect toilets for a fresh, sanitizing finish.
  • Sweep to gather any loose dirt and debris, then mop with a cleaning solution to leave your floors spotless.
