Deep Cleaning Essentials for Hot Weather Home Maintenance: Outdoor Spaces

By Claudia Hinojosa · Jul 1, 2025

Get a quote +1 801-577-8703

We’re in the heat of summer now, and one of the key parts of summer is outdoor parties. Whether you're celebrating the 4th of July or Pioneer Day or enjoying being able to gather with friends on a summer evening, you want to make sure that your home is prepared for the festivities.

That’s why we want to give you some ideas surrounding deep cleaning essentials hot weather home maintenance.

Some key elements of home maintenance are best done during the summer months to ensure that your home is in the best shape possible, especially when preparing to welcome friends and family over for summer gatherings.

Check your Roof, Gutters and Siding

Do a thorough examination of your home. Check for missing, cracked or loose shingles on your roof, check to make sure that no cracks or damage in your siding, stucco, or brickwork need repairs while the weather is still warm.

Ensure that your gutters and downspouts are secure, in good condition, and clear of any debris that could cause damage during the next storm system too.

Windows

Check for any air leaks around your windows and doors to ensure your heating and cooling systems aren’t working overtime. Then, we can help your windows become streak-free and sparkling with our window-cleaning service. We’ll ensure your windows are shining and ready to be the talk of your next gathering.

Patios & Decks

For patios and decks, check to make sure that all cracks, splinters, and any damage is repaired. Then, make sure your patios and decks are ready to go is with a power wash. First, remove all furniture, plants, and decor items, then one of our service team members will come in to offer power washing with soap, water, brush, and vacuum, tackling even the toughest cleaning challenges.

Outdoor Furniture

Wipe down any outdoor furniture with a mild soap solution and microfiber clot. For any cushions, remove and wash covers if possible, and spot-clean removable cushions with a mix of warm water and a mild, biodegradable detergent. Rinse all furniture well and let them fully dry in the sun. Following these steps, you can apply a protective coating to outdoor furniture to prevent UV damage.

Walkways & Driveways

Power washing is also a great way to keep your driveway and sidewalk looking their best. For tough stains, a biodegradable degreaser or concrete cleaner is a good option. If you have oil stains, use baking soda to pull up the oil before washing.

Landscaping

For your landscaping, make sure that you or your landscaper trim and prune any trees and bushes, and overgrown plants. Pull any weeds and replace your old mulch with fresh, vibrant options to make your garden beds look their best. Next, clean your fences, garden statues, and trellises with a mild, biodegradable and plant-safe solution.

Cleaning outdoor areas is an essential part of keeping your home well-maintained, and ready for any summer get-togethers and parties that may arise. Plus, we’re here to help you get your home in top-notch shape for your next party. Book your power-wash and deep clean today.
