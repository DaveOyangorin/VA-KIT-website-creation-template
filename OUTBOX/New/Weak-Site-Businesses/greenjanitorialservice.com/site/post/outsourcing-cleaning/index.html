Outsourcing Cleaning During Times of Transition

By Claudia Hinojosa · Aug 5, 2025

Get a quote +1 801-577-8703

Change is inevitable. It is a constant and ever-present part of our world. The thing is, it’s also immensely difficult. Even a small change in our lives can cause stress. Whether it’s positive or negative, expected or unexpected, transitions impact our daily lives and can cause stress to our minds and our bodies.

While the list of transitions that may cause stress or disruption is never-ending, a some major life-altering transitions are as follows:

  • Marriage
  • The birth of a child
  • A new school year beginning
  • Moving
  • A new job
  • Changing romantic relationships
  • Retirement

During these times of transition, you may find yourself experiencing a wide range of emotions and experiences. Transitions frequently bring changes in scheduling and routines and focus and attention are pulled in different directions than you are used to. It is a good time to reassess your current habits and practices to see if there is something that you can change.

Finding effective ways to cope with change can look different for everyone, but maintaining a good support system is one key aspect of successful transitions. For some, this may mean having your partner pick up the kids from school on Thursday afternoons because of a recurring appointment. For others, it may mean hiring professionals to take care of tasks like cleaning the home.

Benefits of Outsourcing Cleaning

1. Time-Saving

Hiring us to clean your home will help save you time, allowing you to take care of other things during your transition or maybe take a moment for self-care. We want you to enjoy your home without worrying about the details. Our services aim to enable you to reclaim time and relax in a clean environment.

2. Consistent, High Quality Cleaning

We provide consistent cleaning on a monthly, bi-weekly, or monthly basis following our initial deep cleaning. With a regular cadence and uncompromising quality, we deliver a superior clean that is safe for your family and the environment. Our rigorous cleaning process includes disinfecting surfaces, thorough washing, and precise dusting to eliminate residue or allergens. We go beyond basic cleaning to provide a luxurious, healthy living space.

3. Improved Health and Hygiene

Our professional cleaning services use premium, eco-friendly products and the most up-to-date techniques to keep your home clean. Traditional cleaning chemicals can harm health and damage furniture and fixtures. At Green Janitorial Services, we use only 100% environmentally friendly, biodegradable products to free your home from harmful toxins. Our green cleaning methods ensure safety for people, pets, and plants without compromising cleanliness.

4. Customizable Services

We can tailor our cleaning services to fit your specific needs. If there is a specific area of your home that needs special attention, let us know ahead of time and we can make sure that your cleaning team is ready to spend some extra time to make sure that area is spic and span. From immaculate kitchens and sparkling bathrooms to pristine living areas and bedrooms, we ensure every corner of your home is flawlessly clean.

5. Reduced Mental Load

Our services allow you the convenience of a perfectly maintained home without lifting a finger. Our services allow you the relief of knowing that your home will be clean without the extra mental load of knowing that cleaning is still on your to-do list and all of the little additional tasks that come with it.

Embrace the luxury of a pristine home and more easily navigate the times of transition with Green Janitorial Services.
