Our Services

Specialized Cleaning Services

Embrace the luxury of a pristine home and the freedom of time well spent with a deep cleaning followed by weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly maintenance.

Carpet Cleaning

We use advanced techniques and equipment to deep clean and remove stains from carpets, ensuring a fresh and revitalized appearance.

COVID-19 Disinfection Services

Utilizing state-of-the-art electro sprayers, we provide thorough disinfection to combat the spread of pathogens, ensuring a safe and healthy environment for your premises.

Floor Scrubbing

We clean and restore various floor surfaces with our industrial-grade scrubbers, rejuvenating tired floors and maintaining a polished appearance.

Power Washing

We offer power washing services to keep your outdoor spaces spic and span. From storefront windows to decks and patios, our power washing service will keep your outdoor spaces ready for anything.

High Dusting

Effectively remove dust and debris from high and hard-to-reach areas using specialized equipment and techniques, ensuring a thorough clean.

Strip

Our professional stripping and waxing services transform dull and worn-out floors into vibrant, polished surfaces, revitalizing the look of your space.

Window Cleaning

Achieve streak-free, sparkling windows in both commercial and residential settings, enhancing the appearance and ambiance of your space.
