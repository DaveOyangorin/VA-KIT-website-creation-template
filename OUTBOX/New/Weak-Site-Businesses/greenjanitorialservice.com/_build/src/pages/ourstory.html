Our Story

Our journey began 11 years ago when our family moved from Mexico to the United States, driven by a dream and a desire to make a difference. We started working in the cleaning industry to support our family. In 2018, I founded Green Janitorial Services, combining my passion for the environment with my love for meticulous cleaning.

What began as a small endeavor has grown into a respected family-owned business known for its dedication to luxury cleaning and eco-friendly practices. We use innovative techniques and biodegradable products to provide exceptional cleaning services while caring for the planet.

Our commitment goes beyond just cleaning; we believe in supporting our community. We offer opportunities to those facing language barriers, single parents seeking flexible employment, and individuals seeking a supportive and rewarding work environment. We believe in creating a workplace where everyone can thrive and contribute to our mission of excellence.

Caring for others and the environment is at the heart of everything we do. At Green Janitorial Services, we are not just cleaning spaces but enhancing lives, one clean space at a time. Join us to create a cleaner, greener, and more caring world.
