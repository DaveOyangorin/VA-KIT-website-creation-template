Join Our Team

At Green Janitorial Services, we are more than just a cleaning company. Our family-owned business is committed to excellence, sustainability, and community support. Our mission is to provide luxury cleaning services while caring for our environment and supporting our team members.

What we're looking for:

  • A Passion for Cleaning: If you love cleaning and take pride in your work, you will find a welcoming home with us

  • Positive Environment: We foster a positive and supportive work environment where every team member is valued.

  • Growth Mindset: We provide comprehensive training and continuous learning opportunities to help you grow and excel in your role.

  • Eco-Friendly Practices: We prioritize environmentally friendly cleaning techniques and products, ensuring a safe and healthy environment for our clients and staff.

  • Dedication: We offer opportunities to allow our communities to thrive. From individuals overcoming language barriers, single parents seeking flexible work, and anyone in our community dedicated to doing their best.

Job Openings

Residential and Commercial Cleaner

General Details

Compensation: $13 During 15-day training period, $14 Regular Pay

Hours: Part Time (0-30 hours/week) and Full Time (31-30 hours/week)

Schedule: Flexible, between 8 a.m. - 9 p.m.

Transportation: GJS provides transportation to worksites from a scheduled meeting location and time.

Responsibilities

  • Detailed cleaning and sanitizing of residential, commercial, and industrial sites using eco-friendly products.

  • Adhering to GJS Standard Operating Procedures.

  • Working in teams of 2-4 to ensure a thorough clean.

  • Attending mandatory paid training every three months.

What we Expect

  • Enjoyment in cleaning and a positive attitude.

  • Detail-oriented and teachable individuals.

  • Ability to work well within a team.

  • Commitment to safety policies and procedures.

Ready to get started?

  1. Filut the Interview Request Form: Provide your availability for an interview and answer a few questions to help us determine if you would be a good fit.

  2. References: Be prepared to provide a few references.

  3. Background Check: Understand that a background check will be required.

  4. Payment: Note that we pay by direct deposit.
