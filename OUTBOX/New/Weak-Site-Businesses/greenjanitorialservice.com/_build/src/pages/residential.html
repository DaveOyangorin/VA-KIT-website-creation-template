Our Services

Residential Cleaning Services

Embrace the luxury of a pristine home and the freedom of time well spent with a deep cleaning followed by weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly maintenance.

Rediscover Your Time

We understand the value of your time. Our comprehensive cleaning solutions allow you to enjoy your home without worrying about the stress of keeping your home tidy. Our luxury cleaning services will enable you to reclaim time and relax in a clean environment.

Tailored to Perfection

Our expert team offers a luxury, detailed cleaning experience tailored to your needs. From immaculate kitchens and sparkling bathrooms to pristine living areas and bedrooms, we ensure every corner of your home is flawlessly clean. We clean the oven and refrigerator, baseboard, window cleaning, and more.

Uncompromising Quality

Using premium, eco-friendly products, we deliver a superior clean that is safe for your family and the environment. Our rigorous cleaning process includes disinfecting surfaces, thorough washing, and precise dusting to eliminate residue or allergens. We go beyond basic cleaning to provide a luxurious, healthy living space.

Health & Safety

Traditional cleaning chemicals can harm health and damage furniture and fixtures. At Green Janitorial Services, we use only 100% environmentally friendly, biodegradable products to free your home from harmful toxins. Our green cleaning methods ensure safety for people, pets, and plants without compromising cleanliness.

Effortless Comfort

Experience the convenience of a perfectly maintained home without lifting a finger. Our services include bedmaking, vacuuming carpets, mopping floors, dusting blinds, removing garbage, and washing cans. Whether you prefer weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly maintenance, we ensure your home always exudes comfort and cleanliness.

Move-In/Move-Out

Our detailed cleaning services prepare residential and commercial properties for new occupants, ensuring a fresh start for new tenants, to stage your home for sale, or to give your home that final clean to make sure you get your deposit back.
