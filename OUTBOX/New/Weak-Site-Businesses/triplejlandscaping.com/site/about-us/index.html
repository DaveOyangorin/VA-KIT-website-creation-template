About Triple J Landscaping

Local experts creating outdoor spaces in Utah.

Free Quote 385-227-5475

Triple J Landscaping is a locally owned and operated landscaping company serving homeowners and businesses throughout Salt Lake County.

From custom landscape design and irrigation installation to lawn maintenance and hardscape construction, we bring craftsmanship, care and reliable results to every project. Our goal is simple: make outdoor spaces more beautiful, functional and enjoyable, without headaches for you.

Our services

Complete landscaping solutions, from custom design and installation to lawn maintenance, irrigation systems and hardscape features. Whether you need a small upgrade or a full outdoor transformation, we tailor each project to your space, style and long-term goals.

Experience

With years of hands-on experience in outdoor construction and property care, our team brings skill and efficiency to every job. We’ve refined our process so projects stay organized, on schedule and built to last, and we apply the same precision to simple lawn improvements and complete renovations.

Accountability you can count on

Strong communication and reliability are as important as quality workmanship. From the first consultation through completion, we keep you informed and involved at every stage so there are no surprises. We respect your property and stand behind our work.
