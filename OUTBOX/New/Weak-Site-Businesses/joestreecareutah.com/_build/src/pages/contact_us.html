Frequently Asked Questions

1. Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — fully licensed and insured for your peace of mind.

2. How can I get the most work done at the best price?

The best way is to bundle services. When we can handle everything in one visit with all the right equipment on-site, it saves time and lowers your overall cost.

3. When is the best time to fertilize trees?

Fertilizer is most effective from April through September, but earlier in the season (spring and early summer) tends to get the best results.

4. When is the best time to use Dormant Oil Sprays?

Dormant oil sprays work best in April and early May — right before buds open and insects start moving in.

5.  Is your machinery yard friendly?

Yes — we use yard-safe machinery. Some circumstances require us to put mats and protective barriers up for safety.

6.  What form of payments do you take?

We accept Venmo, cash, check, credit card, and Apple Pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should I schedule lawn care services?

The frequency depends on your lawn’s condition and the season. Generally, every 2-4 weeks is recommended for regular maintenance.

2. What lawn care services do you offer?

We provide mowing, fertilization, weed control, pest control, aeration, over seeding, irrigation setup, and seasonal cleanup services.

3. Are the weed and pest control products safe for pets?

Yes, we use eco-friendly and pet-safe products that effectively protect your lawn without harming your family or the environment.

4. How can I keep my lawn green and healthy all year?

Regular mowing, watering, fertilization, aeration, and seasonal treatments are key to maintaining a lush and healthy lawn.

5. Do you offer seasonal lawn care plans?

Yes! We have customized plans for spring, summer, fall, and winter to ensure your lawn stays in top condition throughout the year.
