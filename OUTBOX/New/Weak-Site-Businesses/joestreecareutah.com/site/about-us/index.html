About Joe's Tree Care

Built on trust. Backed by experience.

What started as a small, family-run operation in 2018 has grown into a trusted name in tree care across northern Utah.

Joe’s Tree Care was founded with one goal in mind: to deliver reliable, affordable and safe tree services backed by real expertise. From residential backyards to commercial properties and HOA landscapes, we’ve built our reputation through honest work, top-notch equipment and a deep respect for every property we service.

Over the years, we’ve expanded our services while staying true to our values: integrity, precision and putting the customer first.
