03When is the best time to fertilize trees?

Fertilizer is most effective from April through September, but earlier in the season (spring and early summer) tends to get the best results.

04When is the best time for dormant oil sprays?

Dormant oil sprays work best in April and early May, right before buds open and insects start moving in.
