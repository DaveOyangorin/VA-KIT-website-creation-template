Gift Cards to Support Local Restaurants

April 21st, 2020 | by Cary Taylor | Posted in Uncategorized

COVID-19 has changed the way we live our day to day lives here in Utah and around the world. With social distancing guidelines and restaurants having to close their indoor dining, it’s causing financial challenges for many small businesses.

RWK wants to help: when you choose us to finish your basement or remodeling project, we will give you a $125 gift card for a local restaurant of your choice.
