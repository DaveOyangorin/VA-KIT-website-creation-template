About Us

Since 2001, RWK Construction’s mission has been to make your vision a reality. RWK Construction works with passionate experts and excellent communication skills to fulfill this mission. With over 20 years of experience, you can trust RWK to create a space you are excited to be in through an honest and timely process. RWK Construction is proud to be a Utah-born business and serve the Salt Lake, Utah, Davis, and Tooele Counties.
