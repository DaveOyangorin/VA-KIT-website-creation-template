Basement Finishing, Remodels & Home Theaters

Designing and finishing basements in Utah since 2001.

RWK Construction designs and finishes basements, remodels homes, builds home theaters and builds custom homes across Salt Lake, Utah, Davis and Tooele Counties. Every project starts with a free consultation and estimate at your home.

Basement finishing

Since 2001 RWK Construction has been designing and finishing basements. We are renovation and design experts specializing in basement refinishing and basement remodeling. Not only will you save money, but your project will move quickly and efficiently with less stress and hassle.

With your input and our experience and design expertise, we will create a plan, which not only meets your needs, but also utilizes the space in creative ways. No matter what your finish basement project entails, let us show you how we can help make your dream space a reality. Contact us to set up a free consultation now!

Home renovations

Whether you need a bigger laundry room, a larger kitchen, or you want to expand your whole house, we can help you update your space. Whatever your vision RWK Construction is here to help you plan out your idea and make it happen. As a whole home remodeling company we know the ins and outs when it comes to remodeling any room.

RWK Construction will work with you, your ideas and your existing budget to build the home you really want. If you live in or around the Salt Lake and Utah County area and are ready to create your dream space give us a call!

Home theaters

Nothing brings family and friends together better than a night at the movies. A giant, wall-sized screen. Rows of fully reclining, theater-style seating.

A state-of-the-art surround sound system that puts you right in the middle of the action. We'll start with a customized design that reflects your personality and décor. Next, we'll select from the finest audio and video components, cabinetry, acoustical treatments, lighting, control systems, and accessories.

Finally, we'll meticulously install everything to perfection. With the right planning, investing in a home theater can be one of the best enhancements to your home and lifestyle.

Before you call

  • Basements must be at least 500 square feet.
  • We’re not currently taking on houses older than 1975.
  • We only build basement walkouts when we’re also finishing the whole basement.

Common questions

01What size basement do you take on?

Basements need to be at least 500 square feet. Below that, we’re not the right fit for the project.

02Can you add a walkout to my basement?

Yes, when we’re also finishing the whole basement. We don’t build walkouts as a stand-alone project.

03Do you work on older homes?

We’re not currently taking on houses older than 1975.
