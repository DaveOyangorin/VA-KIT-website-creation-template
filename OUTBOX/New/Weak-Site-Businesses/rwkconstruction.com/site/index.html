Process

Six steps, and you can see every one.

Every RWK client gets a login to our Homeowner Portal: the schedule, progress photos, requests and payments, in one place.

The full process
  1. 01Consultation

    We meet with you in person to talk through your ideas and needs. Bring your photos, floor plans, Pinterest boards and wish list. We get to know each other, discuss pricing and see if we’re the right fit.

  2. 02Design

    Already have a design? Great. If not, we’ll sit down with you, go through your plans and help you make the most of the space. We’ve done numerous projects of all styles.

  3. 03Communication

    Once you decide to move forward, you get a login to our online Homeowner Portal: the schedule, progress photos, new requests, expenses and when payments are due, all in one place.

  4. 04Construction

    With the design squared away and your portal set up, construction starts. Our team keeps communication open and transparent with everyone involved.

  5. 05Finishing up

    As we near the end we meet once more to answer any questions or concerns, and walk you through our warranty process in case anything comes up after we leave.

  6. 06Post-completion

    Your space is ready to enjoy, show off, and make memories in for years to come.

Questions

01What size basement do you take on?

Basements need to be at least 500 square feet. Below that, we’re not the right fit for the project.

02Can you add a walkout to my basement?

Yes, when we’re also finishing the whole basement. We don’t build walkouts as a stand-alone project.

03Do you work on older homes?

We’re not currently taking on houses older than 1975.

04How will I know what’s happening on my project?

Every client gets a login to our online Homeowner Portal: the schedule, progress photos, requests, expenses and payment dates, plus a direct line to us.

05Where do you work?

Salt Lake, Utah, Davis and Tooele Counties. Our office is in Bluffdale, open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm.
