How we work

  1. Consultation. We meet with you in person to talk through your ideas and needs. Bring your photos, floor plans, Pinterest boards and wish list. We get to know each other, discuss pricing and see if we’re the right fit.
  2. Design. Already have a design? Great. If not, we’ll sit down with you, go through your plans and help you make the most of the space. We’ve done numerous projects of all styles.
  3. Communication. Once you decide to move forward, you get a login to our online Homeowner Portal: the schedule, progress photos, new requests, expenses and when payments are due, all in one place.
  4. Construction. With the design squared away and your portal set up, construction starts. Our team keeps communication open and transparent with everyone involved.
  5. Finishing up. As we near the end we meet once more to answer any questions or concerns, and walk you through our warranty process in case anything comes up after we leave.
  6. Post-completion. Your space is ready to enjoy, show off, and make memories in for years to come.
