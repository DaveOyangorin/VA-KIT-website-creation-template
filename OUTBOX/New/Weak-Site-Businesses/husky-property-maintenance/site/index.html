Smoke alarms

Four rules from the U.S. Fire Administration

Smoke and CO alarm basics
01

Every level, every bedroom

USFA: put smoke alarms inside and outside each bedroom and sleeping area, and on every level of the home.

02

Test monthly

Press the test button once a month.

03

Batteries yearly

Replace 9-volt batteries, or the backup battery in hardwired alarms, at least once a year.

04

Replace at 10 years

Replace the entire smoke alarm every 10 years, and choose interconnected alarms so when one sounds, they all sound.

Source: U.S. Fire Administration, “Smoke alarms” (last reviewed October 13, 2026).
