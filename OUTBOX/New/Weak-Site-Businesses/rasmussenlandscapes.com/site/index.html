The yard year

What your yard needs, month by month.

Utah is the second-driest state, with hot summers and hard-freezing winters, so timing is everything. Turn the year to see what we do in your yard each month.

  • Sprinkler turn-onMostly in the months of April and May, our sprinkler teams are busy starting sprinkler systems back up, with a top-to-bottom check of every zone.
  • Spring aerationThe Rasmussen Way of aerating is once in the spring (March through April)…
  • Late-summer aeration…and again before the fall growth spurt (late August into September) for maximum results.
  • Early-spring hedge trimThe first time to trim hedges is in early spring, right before the first big spring growth.
  • Summer hedge trimThe second time is in the summer, after the spring growth spurt, so hedges look tidy all summer.
  • Fall hedge trimThe last trim is in the fall, right as the final growth spurt happens, to prepare for heavy snow.
  • Summer sprinkler tune-upBeginning in July and sometimes through September, the temperatures get very hot and the watering needs of your plants change.
  • Sprinkler maintenanceWhen it’s growing season (May to October) our beloved plants, flowers, and vegetables need enough water not only to survive, but to thrive.
  • Winter shut-offStarting in October, our sprinkler teams begin shutting off sprinkler systems and evacuating any water left in the system.
  • Spring cleanupIn the spring, debris needs cleaning up, annuals need planting, and preparing lawns for the growing season is at the top of everyone’s list.
  • Fall cleanupIn the fall, leaves need cleaning up, perennials need cutting back, bulbs should be planted, and coniferous plants need to be prepped for winter.
  • Plant bed weedingWe insist that weeding be done often, so that yards look great every day of the week for the entire spring, summer, and fall.
  • Snow removalWhen it’s time to put our mowers and trimmers away, we dust off our winter boots and snow blowers and stand at the ready.

Fertilizing runs across the whole growing season in six targeted rounds: weed pre-emergent, weed & feed, turf builder & iron, soil conditioner, grub & insect control and a lawn winterizer. How we feed Utah lawns
