It's Time To Dream

"Dream no small dreams for they have no power to move the hearts of men."

If You Can Dream It...

Dream big! This is the time to go on drives and walks, take photos of things that inspire you, look at old photos from places you have been, and save ideas on Pinterest. Try to identify what you really like, what brings you peace and leaves you awestruck. Our designers can then use these specifics to harmonize your dream with the style of your home and what will work within the physical space in your yard.

Purpose

What do you envision as the purpose of your new yard? Will you be entertaining guests? Do you need a place for the kids to play? Will it be a beautiful sanctuary? We want to understand what you find important.

Style

What look and feel will your yard have? What is the design style of your home? Do you prefer curved or straight lines? Do you like perfect symmetry or things more asymmetrical? Will the yard be formal or informal? Gather inspirational images in print or from Pinterest to help you develop your style. And then our designers can help pull it all together to make it work.

Special Features

What unique elements do you want to see in your yard? Is there a pool, water features, vegetable garden, patio, fire pit, structures, play areas, courts or other special features? Can you see a space for these?

Budget

What are you planning to spend on your project? It is imperative to have a number in mind to inform design. A basic full landscape should cost between 15% and 20% of the cost of your home. Let's put a plan together. We can even do it in phases if that's easier.
