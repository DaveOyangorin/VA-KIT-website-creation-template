Water & Fire Features

The Oasis of Any Yard

The look and sound of flowing water is therapeutic, especially here in the desert. Water features add a natural element that can be seen, heard, and felt.

From Vision to Reality

When we are on your team to put in a new water feature, you are hiring a design team, an installation team, and a maintenance team. We cover every detail to ensure that your water feature is designed to fit your yard aesthetic, is installed properly, and has the maintenance it needs for years of beauty, function, and enjoyment.

Your only job is to tell us where you want your new water feature, what type of water feature you want, and when you would like it done. Leave the rest to us.

Gentle & Subtle

Streams set the mood and embody motion through space and time. We design our streams to sing and have personality. They might energize the atmosphere with a quicker tempo near your center of entertainment. Or they can calm the senses with their soft, burbly cadence.

Typically, streams cover more area than other types of water features.

Make a Statement

It's all about the drama with falls. The rushing water and verticality of the feature beg for attention! Falls are great when there is not a large footprint to work with.

In Utah, along the Wasatch bench, falls are particularly suited to the many yards that back up against mountainsides. Using the verticality of the steep grade, a waterfall is perfect for using that space to create interest and beauty.

Calm

Ponds provide a reflection that creates openness while attracting wildlife and aquatic plant life.

Any water feature, and especially ponds, can be combined with other water features to create variety. Mixing falls and fountains with ponds is a great way to develop a small, self-contained ecosystem in your landscape.

Drama is Key

There is only one reason that fountains exist - to delight and captivate! The water never moves the same way twice and is always in motion. For much the same reason that we are mesmerized by natural fountains and geysers, yard fountains bring the magic of natural fountains into your yards!

Fountains come in all shapes and sizes. If you can dream it, we can build it.

From Dream to Installation

Every yard is unique. Our team can help in the design process to ensure that the water feature matches the space and style of yard, and once the design is finalized, our installation team takes over. We have all the equipment, crews, and knowledge to do it right. We ensure proper function, performance, and maximum enjoyment from every water feature that we build.

Fireplace

A fireplace is typically designated by its location. Commonly found on the perimeter of a patio or formal outdoor area, an outdoor fireplace adds so much interest and vibrancy to the space. We know how to run and hook up a gas line, and we're equipped to build either a gas or wood-burning fireplace.

Firepit

The all-time best place to gather around! A firepit needs a dedicated space with enough room for seating. Fire pits can be built to run on natural gas or on firewood, and we can design it to match the style and materials used elsewhere in the landscape. It should look like it was put there by nature!
