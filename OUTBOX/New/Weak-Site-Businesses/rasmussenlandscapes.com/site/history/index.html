History in a snapshot...

Back in 1971, lawns were cut in much the same way as they are today. One lawn at a time with great care, precision, and attention to detail. We worked hard for the best customer experience possible. I worked through the ranks cutting lawns on my own at age 7 and working alongside my brothers. Our reputation for honesty, dependability and quality grew and our budding business took us through college. Around the turn of the century my last brother left for medical school and I realized Rasmussen Landscapes had become my passion.

Mission Statement﻿

﻿Rasmussen Landscapes' mission is to CHANGE LIVES. We work everyday to change the lives of our employees and our customers, through which we change the life of our company, and in turn, change the world we live in. We do this one step at a time, one day at a time, one landscape at a time. We feel that if we hire and train on these values, we are helping build people with good character. When our employees live by our values, we hope we improve in every facet of our lives, which ultimately spreads through the service we provide and hopefully changes the lives of our customers.
