Water heater season is upon us!

By Bj Zelch · Oct 1, 2026

Book service (801) 433-7354

As temperatures begin to drop across Utah, your water heater starts working harder to keep up with the demand for hot water. Fall and winter are some of the most common times for homeowners to notice problems with an aging or struggling water heater. From longer recovery times to strange noises, leaks, or running out of hot water altogether, these warning signs shouldn’t be ignored.

At Emet Plumbing Heating & Air, we want homeowners to know what to look for before a small water heater issue turns into an unexpected cold shower—or a costly leak.

1. Colder incoming water. During fall and winter, the water entering your home is colder. Your water heater has to work harder and longer to bring that water up to your desired temperature.

2. Increased hot water use. Colder weather often means longer hot showers, warmer baths, more laundry, holiday guests, and greater overall hot water demand.

3. Older water heaters may start showing their age. A water heater that seemed fine during the summer may have trouble keeping up once the workload increases. Older tanks can experience slower recovery times, reduced efficiency, corrosion, or leaks.

4. Sediment buildup can affect performance. Minerals and sediment collect at the bottom of traditional tank water heaters over time. A popping or rumbling noise can be a sign of sediment buildup, which may reduce efficiency and make the unit work harder.

5. Small problems can become bigger problems. Corrosion, leaking connections, worn components, pressure issues, or a failing tank can become more noticeable when the water heater is operating more frequently.

6. Holiday guests can put your water heater to the test. Extra showers, dishes, laundry, and cooking can quickly expose a water heater that is undersized or no longer performing like it should.

Don't Wait Until You're Taking a Cold Shower

If your water heater has been making strange noises, isn't keeping up with your family's hot water needs, or you're unsure how much life it has left, Emet Plumbing Heating & Air can help.

Our experienced technicians can inspect your system, identify potential problems, and help you determine whether maintenance, repair, or replacement makes the most sense for your home.
