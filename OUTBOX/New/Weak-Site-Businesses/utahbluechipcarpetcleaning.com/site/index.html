Our four-step process

Clean to the backing. Nothing left behind.

We use the hot-water method recommended by carpet manufacturers and taught by the IICRC, with a non-residue organic process that restores your carpet to its natural beauty.

  1. 1Pre-treat

    We pre-treat all the carpet to break up spots, stains and heavily soiled areas.

  2. 2Organic enzyme

    A non-residue, organic enzyme with a little hot water cleans all the way down to the backing.

  3. 3No soap, no soak

    No soaps, chemicals or harsh detergents, and no over-soaking.

  4. 4Dry in 1–3 hours

    Safe for kids, pets and anyone with allergies or breathing problems.
