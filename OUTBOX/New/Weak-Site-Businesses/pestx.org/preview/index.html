What the audit found

  • Mobile speed now: 70/100. Aim for 90+.
  • Pages in the sitemap: 18.
  • Meta description: present.
  • H1 headings on the home page: 3 (should be 1).
  • Tap-to-call phone link: no.
  • Business schema: no.
  • Images without alt text: 57 of 57.
  • Pages that name Midvale: 0.

What to do

  • Speed: 70/100 now. Compress images, lazy-load, remove unneeded scripts. Target 90+.
  • Use exactly one H1 per page (home page has 3).
  • Add a tap-to-call phone link in the header.
  • Add LocalBusiness schema with the exact name, address and phone.
  • Add alt text to images (57 of 57 have none).
  • Name Midvale in the title, H1 and first paragraph of every service page.
