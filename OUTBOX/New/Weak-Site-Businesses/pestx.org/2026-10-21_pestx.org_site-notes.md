## Concept: "A field guide to the Utah home"
Pestx's own site already reads like a naturalist's notebook. It covers ten Utah pests, each with Identification, Elimination and Prevention, all drawn in ink wash. It also has a "Full home coverage" diagram that letters seven parts of a house, A Eaves to G Basement.
- **Hero:** a specimen plate you can leaf through (arrows, dots, swipe). It shows the plate number, common name, order, when each pest is seen and where.
- **Signature:** the house diagram becomes an interactive cross-section. Tap a lettered zone to see which pests Pestx's own pages place there, each with a link.
- Season chart for year-round protection, the field-guide grid, a guarantee band, Q&A and a quote form.
- **References** (screenshotted 21 Oct 2026):
  - Audubon: one specimen as the hero.
  - iNaturalist: identification cards and mono captions.
  - Gardenista: editorial serif and white space.
  - NHM London, Aesop and Kew were blocked by Cloudflare.
- **Brand:** Pestx plum #82234C, the round X mark, fog #F2F3F0, and their own fonts (Libre Baskerville, Manrope and Fragment Mono).
