Common Utah pests

Wasps

Aggressive and territorial, wasps can turn outdoor spaces into danger zones. Learn how to identify their nests, safely remove them, and prevent them from coming back.

Identification

Common Utah wasps include paper wasps, yellow jackets, and hornets. They build nests under eaves, in trees, and even inside wall voids. Wasps are aggressive when their nest is disturbed and can deliver painful stings.

Elimination

If a nest is small and easily accessible, you can use a wasp spray at night when they are less active. For large or hard-to-reach nests, professional removal is recommended. Avoid swatting wasps, as this can provoke them.

Prevention

Seal cracks and gaps around your home to prevent nesting inside walls. Keep outdoor food and drinks covered, and regularly check for early signs of nest-building in sheltered areas.
