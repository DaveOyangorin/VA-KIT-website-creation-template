Common Utah pests

Ants

Tiny but persistent, ants can invade Utah homes in search of food and water. Learn how to identify common species, eliminate infestations, and keep them from coming back.

Identification

Ants are one of the most common household pests in Utah. The most frequently seen species include pavement ants, carpenter ants, and odorous house ants. They typically enter homes in search of food and moisture, often forming long trails along counters and floors.

Elimination

To get rid of ants, start by cleaning up food crumbs and spills immediately. Bait stations can help eliminate entire colonies, as worker ants carry poison back to their nests. Avoid spraying repellent sprays directly on trails, as this can scatter the colony instead of eradicating it.

Prevention

Seal entry points around windows, doors, and cracks in your home’s foundation. Store food in airtight containers and regularly take out the trash to remove attractants. Keep outdoor vegetation trimmed away from your home to limit ant access.
