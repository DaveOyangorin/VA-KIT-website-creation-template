Air Conditioning Repair & Installs

Central air and mini splits, without the upsell.

Free Estimate (385) 352-3285

AC repair, routine inspections, new central air and ductless mini splits. We’re up-front about which option is right for you, and we won’t push the most expensive equipment.

AC leaks need immediate attention

Water on the floor may simply be condensation, but depending on your unit it can be a sign of a bigger problem. If you smell rotten eggs, or a carbon monoxide alarm goes off when your AC kicks on, shut it off and call us right away. Ignoring leaks leads to bigger, more expensive problems.

Routine AC inspections

Routine maintenance prolongs the life of your system. Our technician inspects your central air, cleans and lubricates the parts, and identifies worn parts. We recommend seasonal tune-ups for furnaces and air conditioners.

New central air installation

We’ve helped hundreds of Utah homeowners choose the right central air system for their household and budget, with many units and cooling options to choose from.

Mini splits

A ductless system that can be installed just about anywhere, and usually more efficient than a traditional AC. Great for hard-to-cool or hard-to-heat spaces like offices, garages and bonus rooms, on new construction, basement finishes, remodels and retrofits.
