Our pure-water system

Why our windows stay clean longer.

Utah tap water is hard. Clean glass with it and minerals dry into spots. We filter the water down to pure H₂O, so it rinses dirt away and dries clear.

  • Spot-free dryingNothing left behind on the glass to attract dust.
  • Gentle on frames and panelsNo harsh chemicals on seals, screens or solar coatings.
  • Reaches second stories safelyWater-fed poles clean high glass from the ground.
