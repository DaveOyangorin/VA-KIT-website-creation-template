Our pure-water system

Utah tap water is hard. Ours isn’t.

Clean glass with tap water and the minerals dry into spots. We filter the water down to pure H₂O, so it rinses dirt away and dries clear.

  • Spot-free drying. Nothing left behind on the glass to attract dust.
  • Gentle on frames and panels. No harsh chemicals on seals, screens or solar coatings.
  • Reaches second stories safely. Water-fed poles clean high glass from the ground.
