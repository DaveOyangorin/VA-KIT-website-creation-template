About Advanced Community Services

Professional, efficient HOA management.

Request a Bid 1-877-844-4227

Advanced Community Services (ACS) was created because of a recognized need in the property management industry for more professional and efficient service. ACS is founded on basic managerial principles that are often lost within associations.

Our mission

Our mission is to be the trusted partner every HOA deserves; driven by innovation and integrity, we communicate clearly, manage finances with expertise and transparency, complete projects in a timely manner, and serve as a strong, reliable industry expert wholly committed to increasing the value of every community we serve.

What makes us different

Five key elements keep our focus on the communities we work with: communication, transparency, enforcement, training and accounting.

  • Communication. Community calendars and newsletters kept up to date, and phone lines open.
  • Transparency. Monthly financial reports presented at every board meeting.
  • Enforcement. Consistent handling of rule and CC&R violations.
  • Training. Education and legal training for boards and committees.
  • Accounting. Separate bank accounts for each community, and dues collection with easy payment options.

Philosophy

The main idea of our philosophy is to give accurate, good information and direction to the Board of Directors and association members. After all, communication is the key to satisfied homeowners.

Why use ACS?

A third party can help resolve problems. Why take a do-it-yourself approach to non-payment problems when a trained staff of professionals can handle them for you?

Easy to do business with

We take the time to understand the community we serve. We build relationships, and that is what makes doing business together easy.
