Professional HOA Services

Exceptional professional services.

Request a Bid 1-877-844-4227

With all the demands placed on HOA boards, the obstacles can be overwhelming. ACS professionals put proven, flexible systems in place. We begin with a Reserve Analysis that makes clear the maintenance and growth issues that need planning now, and a transparent monthly reporting cycle that is presented to the board at each meeting.

Here are a few of our services

  • Process architectural compliance forms
  • Coordinate corporate reports and IRS filings
  • Resale and refinancing documents for lenders and title companies
  • Transparency and accountability for all community financials
  • Manage insurance claims
  • Review rules, regulations and CC&Rs for recommended amendments
  • Education and legal training for the board and committees
  • Coordinate legal services at a discounted rate
  • Separate bank accounts for each community
  • Reserve analysis to plan for maintenance and growth

Collections, covenants and legal issues

The most expensive and troubling challenges an HOA can face are collection matters, covenant violations and other legal issues. ACS has relationships with HOA attorneys that let us coordinate experienced legal services at better rates, and an in-house attorney who researches HOA issues affecting our communities.
