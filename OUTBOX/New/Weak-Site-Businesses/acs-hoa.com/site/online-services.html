Online Services for HOA Boards

Supporting managers and boards with online functionality.

Request a Bid 1-877-844-4227

Board members and homeowners both have ongoing questions and needs. ACS gives every client a full-service website as another line of communication, with two levels of access: board members get transparent access to financial records, activity follow-ups, meeting agendas and membership records; homeowners can pay dues, view the CC&Rs, review meeting minutes and suggest new ideas.

Here are a few of our services

  • An interactive website developed for each association
  • Board member list and board meeting minutes
  • Easy access to the association’s monthly records
  • Distribute and store important association documents
  • Board access to financial records and meeting follow-ups
  • Association emails and mailings
  • A monthly community newsletter
  • Homeowner access to CC&Rs and bylaws
  • A homeowner recommendation and complaint database
  • Online ownership records and a map of the development
  • Online payment of dues
  • Automated welcome packets for new owners and move-ins
  • Friendly late-fee notices for monthly dues

Action items between meetings

ACS also provides an online structure that organizes board members’ duties. It records action items, schedules reminders on urgent tasks and keeps property managers in sync with board members.
