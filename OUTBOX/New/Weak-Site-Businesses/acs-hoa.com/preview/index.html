What the audit found

  • Mobile speed now: 58/100. Aim for 90+.
  • Pages in the sitemap: no sitemap.
  • Meta description: present.
  • H1 headings on the home page: 3 (should be 1).
  • Tap-to-call phone link: yes.
  • Business schema: no.
  • Images without alt text: 18 of 19.
  • Pages that name Lehi: 0.

What to do

  • Speed: 58/100 now. Compress images, lazy-load, remove unneeded scripts. Target 90+.
  • Use exactly one H1 per page (home page has 3).
  • Add LocalBusiness schema with the exact name, address and phone.
  • Add alt text to images (18 of 19 have none).
  • Name Lehi in the title, H1 and first paragraph of every service page.
  • Add an XML sitemap and submit it in Search Console.
