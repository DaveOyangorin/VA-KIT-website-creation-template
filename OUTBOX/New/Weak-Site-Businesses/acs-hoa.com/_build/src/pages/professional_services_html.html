Exceptional Professional Services

With all the demands placed on HOA boards and members, the hassles and obstacles that arise can be overwhelming or at the very least frustrating. ACS professionals implement proven yet flexible systems that resolve all HOA concerns.

We begin improving your HOA with a Reserve Analysis that will make clear the maintenance and growth issues which need to be planned for right now.

ACS utilizes a transparent monthly reporting cycle that is prepared and presented to Board members at each board meeting. These reports are easily customized and readily available to all board members. Board members are truly in charge of HOA finances.

The most expensive and troubling challenges that HOAs can face are collection matters, covenant violations and other legal issues. ACS has developed relationship with HOA attorneys that allow us to coordinate experienced legal services at the best rates. In addition, ACS has an in-house attorney who sometimes researches HOA issues that are troubling our communities for free.

The bottom line is that ACS will help your HOA navigate through any obstacle and maintain its financial stability by reducing expenses and planning for the future.

HERE ARE A FEW OF OUR MANAGERIAL SERVICES

  • Process Architectural Compliance Forms
  • Coordinate Preparation of Corporate Reports and IRS Filings
  • Provide Resale and Refinancing Documents to Lenders and Title Companies
  • Provide Transparency and Accountability for all Community Financials
  • Manage any Insurance Claims
  • Conduct an Analysis of Rules & Regulations and CC&Rs for recommended Amendments
  • Provide Education and Legal Training to Board of Directors and Committees
  • Coordinate legal services at a discounted rate
  • Provide Separate Bank Accounts for each Community
