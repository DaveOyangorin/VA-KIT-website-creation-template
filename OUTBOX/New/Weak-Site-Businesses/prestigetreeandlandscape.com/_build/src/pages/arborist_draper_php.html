When to Call a Tree Doctor

If your trees and other woody plantings have been affected by bad weather, you'll know you need a tree doctor right away. However, it's important to note there are many more ways we can contribute to the success and increased value of your landscape.

Our experts recommend getting in touch with a tree doctor when:

  • Your trees are showing signs of damage or change that you've not noticed before
  • The tree is starting to lean
  • You'd like the canopy of the tree to be reduced
  • Branches need pruning
  • Insects have taken over
  • Part of a tree is broken, and careful repair work could save the limb
  • You'd like to move a tree that is encroaching on your home or another space you'd like to use
  • The tree requires cabling
  • You'd like to change the shape or height of a tree
  • You're planting trees and would like assistance with selection, placement, and installation for the best results

Our services extend far beyond what is listed above. If you have questions about an existing tree or one you'd like to add to your property in the future, don't hesitate to reach out.
