Why hire a certified arborist?

Some people wonder why they should bother to hire a certified arborist when there are general landscaping companies who are willing to do the same kind of work. The difference is that a certified arborist is trained in the art and science of tree care, from planting, to insect and disease control, to general care. When you hire a certified arborist, you are hiring someone who is guaranteed to have a high level of tree knowledge, and someone who has been required to keep up to date on the latest techniques and standards in order to maintain their certification.
