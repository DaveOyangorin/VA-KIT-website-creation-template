What hardness does

Four signs of hard water

Hard water, explained
01

Soap scum

USGS: calcium reacts with soap to form soap scum, so you need more soap and detergent for hands, hair and laundry.

02

Spots on dishes

Spots and film on glassware are hard-water residue. USGS calls them unsightly but not dangerous.

03

Scale in the water heater

Heating hard water forms calcium carbonate deposits that USGS says can shorten equipment life, raise heating costs and lower the efficiency of electric water heaters.

04

Narrowing pipes

Over time, USGS says scale can build up inside pipes, reducing water flow and pressure.

Source: U.S. Geological Survey, Water Science School, “Hardness of Water” (updated August 24, 2026). USGS: hardness is not a health concern, but it can be a nuisance.
