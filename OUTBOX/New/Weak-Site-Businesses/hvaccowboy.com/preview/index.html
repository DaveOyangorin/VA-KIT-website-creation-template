What the audit found

  • Chrome shows a one-page site (no other pages linked).
  • Mobile speed now: 89/100. Aim for 90+.
  • Home page words: 10. Our standard is 800+.
  • Pages in the sitemap: no sitemap.
  • Meta description: present.
  • H1 headings on the home page: 0 (should be 1).
  • Tap-to-call phone link: no.
  • Business schema: yes.
  • Pages that name Lehi: 0.

What to do

  • Speed: 89/100 now. Compress images, lazy-load, remove unneeded scripts. Target 90+.
  • Home page has 10 words. Write 800+ useful words (services, area, process, FAQ).
  • Use exactly one H1 per page (home page has 0).
  • Add a tap-to-call phone link in the header.
  • Name Lehi in the title, H1 and first paragraph of every service page.
  • Add an XML sitemap and submit it in Search Console.
