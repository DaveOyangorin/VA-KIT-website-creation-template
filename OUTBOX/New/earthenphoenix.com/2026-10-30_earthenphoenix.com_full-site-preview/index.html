Before and after

One service, one motion

Each animation shows what the session actually does. They run only on screen, pause in background tabs, and fall back to a still frame for visitors who turn motion off.

Pressure finderThe logo's banded circle as a depth gauge. Slide from "no pressure" (Reiki) to "deepest" (Sarga); the light sinks as far as the session goes. Reiki: on or nearHands move over a draped, clothed body. Toggle "on" or "near"; warmth gathers under the hands, then evens out (balance). Swedish: long strokesOne long stroke per breath leaves a warm oil sheen. Choose light or medium; add sound, meditation or breathwork. Drag to stroke. Deep tissue: layersA forearm sinks through skin, fascia and muscle on the exhale and eases on the inhale. Knotted fibres lengthen. You choose the depth. Thai: rhythm, then reachPalm-walking compressions on a beat, then a held leg stretch and knee to chest. Lymphatic: six steps by scrollBrushing toward the heart, flow along the lymph paths, warm wrap, hot towel, head and neck, firming cream. Sarga: foot and strapHer bare foot glides along the back while the overhead strap takes her weight; tangled fascia lines straighten. Includes an honest comfort check. Zen mountain: the river slowsA mountain river rushes, then slows while you stay. Fast movement stirs it again. Optional river sound, off by default. Yoga: flow, then holdOne line moves through a Vinyasa sequence, then holds Yin, plus the breathing circle and a "split the $140" picker. Readings & herbs (kept)Star trails, the birth-chart wheel, tarot by candle and the $2/min meter. Herbs now have their own page.

Site-wide: the five logo bands fall like strata between pages, a left-edge strata gauge tracks scroll, and the home hero's "Release" lines loosen under the cursor.

Design direction

Built on the "come back to earth" direction already approved: the page descends from sand to sage like the logo's bands, with one ember accent. Type is Instrument Serif (display) and Figtree (text). All photos are her own.

References studied (for polish only, not copied): McGarr Massage Therapy (Awwwards-listed), Mountain Rose Herbs (her herb supplier), Alo Moves. Aesop, Six Senses and Body Freedom blocked automated access.

Testing done

  • Phone header: menu panel sits exactly under the header, page locks while open, header stays pinned after scrolling
