How a session flows

Flow first, then hold.

Schole yoga uses traditional asanas guided to move with your own breath and natural abilities, for a challenging yet intentional practice. Fun Vinyasa movement, followed by slow Yin holds at the end. Tap the figure to flow again.

“This practice is to guide you into using your own intuition to get into your body and flow verse being told exactly how and when to move.”

Your breath leads

Move with your own breath.

Every movement follows your breathing, not a count from the front of the room. Try three breaths: four in as the circle fills, four out as it settles.
