Questions, answered.

How much is a deep tissue massage in Salt Lake City?

At Earthen Phoenix deep tissue massage is $150 for 60 minutes or $180 for 90 minutes.

Is deep tissue massage painful?

No. It is therapeutic and never painful. Tell Miquelle how much pressure you want and she adjusts.

What else happens besides pressure?

Deep pressure is combined with breathwork, stretching and movement to restore balance, and you get stretching guidance to take home so the results last.

Is there anything deeper?

Sarga bodywork goes deep into the fascia and suits old injuries. You need to be very comfortable with deep pressure to receive it.
