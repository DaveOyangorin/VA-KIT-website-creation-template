Before you book

Questions, answered.

How much is a massage at Earthen Phoenix?

Massage starts at $140 for 60 minutes of Swedish massage. Deep tissue is $150 / $180, Thai bodywork $150 / $170, the lymphatic firming treatment and Sarga are $180 for 90 minutes, and the Zen mountain session is $200 for 90 minutes.

Is there a first-time discount?

Yes. Mention you are a first-time client when you book for 20% off your first service.

Which session is right for me?

Use the pressure finder above as a start. For no pressure at all, Reiki. For relaxation, Swedish. For deep work, deep tissue or Sarga. If you are not sure, call or text 801-609-4263 and Miquelle will help you choose.
