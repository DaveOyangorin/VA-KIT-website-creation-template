The 90 minutes, step by step

Six steps, one slow treatment.

  1. Dry brushing

    The skin is brushed in long sweeps toward the heart.

  2. Lymphatic massage

    Light, manual lymphatic massage follows the body’s own drainage paths.

  3. A heated body wrap

    You are wrapped and warmed.

  4. A hot towel cleanse

    The wrap comes off and hot towels cleanse the skin.

  5. Head and neck

    A head and neck treatment.

  6. Firming cream

    A finish with Eminence Coconut Firming cream.
