Booking & visiting

Where is Earthen Phoenix?

Earthen Phoenix is in Salt Lake City, Utah. The Zen mountain session takes place outdoors beside a mountain river; ask Miquelle about its location and season.

How do I book?

Use the online booking page, or call or text 801-609-4263. You can also email info@earthenphoenix.com.

Is there a first-time discount?

Yes. Please mention you are a first-time client for 20% off your first service.

Which session should I choose?

Try the pressure finder. If you are still unsure, call or text and Miquelle will help you choose.

Massage & bodywork

How much does a massage cost?

Swedish $140 / $170, deep tissue $150 / $180, Thai bodywork $150 / $170 (60 / 90 minutes). The lymphatic firming treatment and Sarga are $180 for 90 minutes; the Zen mountain session is $200 for 90 minutes.

Is deep tissue painful?

No. It is therapeutic and never painful. Tell Miquelle how much pressure you want.

Is Sarga uncomfortable?

It can be somewhat uncomfortable during the session. You must be very comfortable with deep pressure to receive it.

What is in the lymphatic firming treatment?

Dry brushing, manual lymphatic massage, a heated body wrap, a hot towel cleanse and a head and neck treatment, finished with Eminence Coconut Firming cream. 90 minutes, $180.

Reiki & yoga

Do I stay clothed for Reiki?

Yes. Hands are placed gently on or near the clothed body. Reiki is $130 for 60 minutes.

Can I share a yoga session?

Yes. A 60-minute session is $140, private or split between group participants.

Readings & remedies

How are readings priced?

Astrology, tarot and oracle readings are $2 per minute.

How long do herbal remedies take?

Allow one to two weeks for herbs to arrive from Mountain Rose Herbs in Oregon. Consultations are about $40 to $100.

About Miquelle

What is Miquelle’s training?

She started with Reiki in 2011 and is now a Master Reiki practitioner. She graduated from the Utah College of Massage Therapy in 2012 and is certified in Sarga, lymphatic, Thai bodywork, cupping, cranial-sacral therapy, sound therapy, reflexology, yoga, Reiki and breath-work.
