Before you book

Asked and answered.

How much is a massage at Earthen Phoenix?

Swedish massage starts at $140 for 60 minutes. Deep tissue is $150 / $180 and Thai bodywork $150 / $170 (60 / 90 minutes). The lymphatic firming treatment and Sarga are $180 for 90 minutes, and the Zen mountain session is $200 for 90 minutes.

Is there a first-time discount?

Yes. Mention you are a first-time client when you book for 20% off your first service.

Which session is right for me?

For no pressure at all, Reiki. For relaxation, Swedish. For deep work, deep tissue or Sarga. Still unsure? Call or text 801-609-4263 and Miquelle will help you choose.
