Before you book

Asked and answered.

How much is private yoga in Salt Lake City?

$140 for 60 minutes, private or split between group participants.

What style of yoga is it?

Schole yoga: traditional asanas guided to move with your own breath and natural abilities. Vinyasa flow, then slow Yin holds at the end.

Do I need experience?

The practice guides you to use your own intuition to get into your body and flow, rather than being told exactly how and when to move. Call or text 801-609-4263 with any questions.
