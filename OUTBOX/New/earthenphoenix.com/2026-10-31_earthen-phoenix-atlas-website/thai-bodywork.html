Rhythm, then reach

Pressed on a beat, then drawn long.

Palms press on a steady beat, alternating sides, from the feet toward the hips. Then the whole body is drawn out in a long stretch and the ground widens and settles. Her booking page describes it as supporting the lymphatic, immune and nervous systems, muscles, tendons and joints. Tap the terrain to add a press.

Asked and answered.

How much is Thai massage in Salt Lake City?

Thai bodywork is $150 for 60 minutes or $170 for 90 minutes.

What happens in Thai bodywork?

Full-body stretching combined with rhythmic compressions. Miquelle is certified in Thai bodywork.

How is it different from deep tissue?

Thai is built on stretching and rhythmic compressions; deep tissue uses deep pressure with breathwork and stretching.
