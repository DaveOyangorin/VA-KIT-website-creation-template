How much is the lymphatic firming body treatment?

What is included?

Dry brushing, manual lymphatic massage, a heated body wrap, a hot towel cleanse and a head and neck treatment, finished with Eminence Coconut Firming cream.

What is it for?

The booking page describes it as a treatment for skin firming and for reducing bloating, water retention and inflammation. Choose it for a slow, full-body treatment rather than a pressure-focused massage.
