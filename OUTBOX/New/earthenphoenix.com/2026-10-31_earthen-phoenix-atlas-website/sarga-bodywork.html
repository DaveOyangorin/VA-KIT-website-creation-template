Step by step

Bare feet, deep into the fascia.

Miquelle works barefoot, holding a strap overhead so she controls exactly how much weight goes into each step. Each footprint presses a deep hollow, and the pressure spreads wider than the foot, into the fascia around it. Tap the terrain to place a step yourself.
