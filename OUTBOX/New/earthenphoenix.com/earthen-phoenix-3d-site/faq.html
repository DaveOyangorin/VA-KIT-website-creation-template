Booking and visiting

Where is Earthen Phoenix?

In Salt Lake City, Utah. The Zen mountain session takes place outdoors beside a mountain river; ask Miquelle about its location and season.

How do I book?

Use the online booking page, call or text 801-609-4263, or email info@earthenphoenix.com.

Is there a first-time discount?

Yes. Mention you are a first-time client for 20% off your first service.

Which session should I choose?

See how each one works on Sessions & prices. Still unsure? Call or text and Miquelle will help you choose.

Massage and bodywork

How much does a massage cost?

Swedish $140 / $170, deep tissue $150 / $180, Thai bodywork $150 / $170 (60 / 90 minutes). Lymphatic firming and Sarga are $180 for 90 minutes; the Zen mountain session is $200 for 90 minutes.

Is deep tissue painful?

No. It is therapeutic and never painful.

Is Sarga uncomfortable?

It can be somewhat uncomfortable. You must be very comfortable with deep pressure to receive it.

What is in the lymphatic firming treatment?

Dry brushing, manual lymphatic massage, a heated body wrap, a hot towel cleanse and a head and neck treatment, finished with Eminence Coconut Firming cream.

Reiki and yoga

Do I stay clothed for Reiki?

Yes. Hands are placed gently on or near the clothed body. $130 for 60 minutes.

Can I share a yoga session?

Yes. $140 for 60 minutes, private or split between group participants.

Readings and remedies

How are readings priced?

Astrology, tarot and oracle readings are $2 per minute.

How long do herbal remedies take?

Allow one to two weeks for herbs to arrive from Mountain Rose Herbs in Oregon. Consultations are about $40 to $100.

About Miquelle

What is Miquelle’s training?

Reiki since 2011, now a Master Reiki practitioner; Utah College of Massage Therapy, 2012; certified in Sarga, lymphatic, Thai bodywork, cupping, cranial-sacral therapy, sound therapy, reflexology, yoga, Reiki and breath-work.
