When to Replace vs Repair

When a roof has widespread wear, repeated leaks, storm damage across multiple areas, or has reached the end of its service life, replacement is often the better long-term solution. A full replacement can provide more dependable protection and help avoid ongoing repair costs from an aging system.

Asphalt Roofs

We replace asphalt shingle roofing systems for homes and businesses throughout Salt Lake City and nearby Utah areas. Asphalt shingles remain one of the most common and dependable roofing options because they offer strong protection, a clean finished look, and a wide range of style and color choices. We provide full asphalt roof replacement for aging, storm-damaged, and worn roofing systems. We offer system pledge warranties with all GAF systems.

Metal Roofs

We replace metal roofing systems for residential and commercial properties in Salt Lake City and surrounding Utah communities. Metal roofing is known for long-term durability, clean appearance, and strong performance in snow, wind, and changing weather conditions. Most fabrication happens onsite for impeccable finishes.

Flat Roofs

We replace flat and low-slope roofing systems for commercial and residential properties in Salt Lake City and surrounding Utah communities. Flat roofs require careful attention to drainage, penetrations, flashing details, and overall system layout to maintain long-term watertight performance.

Wood Shake and Wood Shingle Roofing

Wood shake and wood shingle roofs provide a timeless, upscale look that adds natural beauty and architectural character to a home. Because wood is a natural material, these roofs require regular maintenance to help manage weathering, split or curled pieces, moisture-related wear, and debris buildup.

Skylight Replacement or Installation

A well-placed skylight can add natural light, brighten interior spaces, and enhance the overall feel of a home. During roof replacement projects, we assess existing skylights and address replacement and flashing details as needed based on age, condition, and long-term performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you replace damaged roof decking if needed?

Yes. If damaged decking is found during the replacement process, it can be replaced as needed before the new roofing system is installed.

How do I know if I need roof replacement instead of repair?

If the roof has widespread wear, repeated leaks, storm damage across multiple areas, or has reached the end of its service life, replacement is often the better long-term solution. If the damage is isolated, repair may still be a good option.

Do you provide free roof replacement estimates?

Yes. Contact Exceptional Roofing to schedule a roof replacement estimate and we can help determine the condition of the roof and the best path forward.
