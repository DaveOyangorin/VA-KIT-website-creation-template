Notes on the list

  • Roof installation is the only new page needed. Keep it different from roof replacement. Suggest: installation = new builds and additions; replacement = tearing off and replacing an old roof.
  • TPO, flat and commercial roofing overlap. TPO is a type of flat roof, often used on commercial buildings. Keep each page focused (the TPO material / flat roofs in general / commercial buildings) and link them to each other so they don't compete.
  • Asphalt roofing vs. roof replacement: keep asphalt about the material (shingles, lifespan, colors) and replacement about the job.
  • Emergency roofing: only claim "24/7" or "same-day" if the client confirms it.
  • 47 pages: worth a quick check with Angelo for old or thin pages that repeat these topics. Those can be merged into the main service page (with a redirect).
