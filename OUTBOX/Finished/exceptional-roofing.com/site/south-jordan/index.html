Roof Repair and Roof Replacement

Some roofing issues can be handled with targeted repair work, especially when the damage is limited to one area and the rest of the roof is still in serviceable condition. In other cases, widespread wear, recurring leaks, storm damage, or age-related deterioration make full roof replacement the better long-term solution.

Asphalt Shingle Roofing

Asphalt shingle roofing remains one of the most dependable and widely used roofing options. Our preferred asphalt products include GAF Timberline HDZ, CertainTeed Landmark, CertainTeed Presidential TL, and GAF Grand Sequoia depending on the look, budget, and goals of the project.

Metal, Flat, and Commercial Roofing

We provide metal roof repair, replacement, and installation services including standing seam, custom corrugated metal, and CMG Metals color and finish options. We also work on TPO, PVC, GAF EverGuard TPO, flat, low-slope, and commercial roofing systems.

Common questions

01What types of roofing do you work on in South Jordan?

We work on asphalt shingle, wood shake, metal, flat, low-slope, and commercial roofing systems for residential and commercial properties.

02Do you provide roof repair in South Jordan?

Yes. We repair common roofing issues including leaks, storm damage, flashing failures, and other roof-related problem areas depending on the condition of the system.

03How do I know if I need roof repair or replacement?

If the issue is limited to a specific area, repair may be a good option. If the roof has widespread wear, recurring leaks, or age-related deterioration, replacement may be the better long-term solution.
