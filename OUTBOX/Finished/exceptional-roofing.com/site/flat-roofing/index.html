Types of Flat Roofing

Flat and low-slope roofing systems can vary depending on the design of the building, the condition of the existing roof, and the performance goals of the project. We work on flat roofing systems for both residential and commercial properties and help determine the right solution based on the roof type, age, and long-term needs of the property.

TPO Roofing

TPO roofing is a popular option for many flat and low-slope roofing projects because it offers a clean appearance, strong weather resistance, and dependable long-term performance when properly installed. It is commonly used on commercial properties and can also be a strong choice for certain residential flat roof applications.

PVC Roofing

PVC roofing is a durable option for certain flat and low-slope roofing applications where long-term performance, chemical resistance, and watertight heat-welded seams are important. It can be a strong choice for some commercial buildings and specialty applications depending on the needs of the project and the condition of the existing roof.

Drainage, Flashing, and Penetrations

Flat roofing performance depends heavily on the quality of the roof details. Proper drainage, flashing, membrane tie-ins, penetrations, and transitions all play a major role in helping the roof remain watertight over time. We focus on these critical details during both repair and replacement work.

Parapet and Edge Details

We address parapet walls and roof edge conditions with the membrane, flashing, coping, and transition details needed for a clean, secure, and watertight finished roofing system. Proper detailing at these areas is important for both long-term performance and finished appearance.

Common questions

01What types of flat roofing systems do you work on?

We work on flat and low-slope roofing systems for residential and commercial properties, including TPO, PVC, and related flat roof applications.

02Do you repair flat roofs?

Yes. We repair common flat roof issues including leaks, flashing failures, drainage-related problem areas, membrane damage, and penetrations depending on the condition of the roof.

03How do I know if my flat roof needs repair or replacement?

If the issue is isolated, repair may be a good option. If the roof has widespread wear, recurring leaks, or aging problems in multiple areas, replacement may be the better long-term solution.

04What is the difference between TPO and PVC roofing?

TPO and PVC are both popular flat roofing materials used on low-slope systems. Each has its own advantages depending on the building, the roof conditions, and the performance goals of the project.
