Regional Roofing: Utah, Southern Idaho & Wyoming

Larger and specialty roofs beyond the Salt Lake Valley.

Free Estimate 801-680-6499

Exceptional Roofing is based in the Salt Lake Valley and serves homeowners, businesses, builders, property managers, and specialty roofing projects across a broad regional service area.

Where We Work

Our core service area includes Salt Lake City, Sandy, Draper, Murray, Holladay, Cottonwood Heights, South Jordan, West Jordan, Riverton, Park City, Heber, Kamas, Oakley, Alpine, Alta, Santaquin, and nearby Wasatch Front communities.

For the right project, we also take roofing work throughout Utah and into nearby parts of southern Idaho and southwest Wyoming. Larger roof replacements, commercial roofs, metal roofing, flat roofing, wood shake, historic-style roofing, storm damage, and specialty projects are reviewed by scope, schedule, location, roof type, and travel requirements.

Need a roof outside Salt Lake County?
Send the address, roof type, project details, and photos. We will let you know quickly whether it fits our schedule and service area.

Utah Roofing Service Area

We serve the Salt Lake Valley, Wasatch Front, Summit County, Wasatch County, Utah County, Davis County, Tooele County, and qualifying roofing projects throughout Utah.

Southern Idaho Roofing Projects

For qualifying projects, we can review roofing work in southern Idaho communities where the project scope, timing, and roofing system make sense.

Southwest Wyoming Roofing Projects

We can review projects in southwest Wyoming, including communities near the Utah border, especially for larger roof replacement, metal, flat, and specialty roofing work.
