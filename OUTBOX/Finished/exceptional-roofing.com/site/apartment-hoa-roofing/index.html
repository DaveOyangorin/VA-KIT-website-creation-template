Common questions

01Do you provide free roofing estimates?

Yes. Contact Exceptional Roofing to schedule a free inspection or estimate for roof repair, replacement, flat roofing, metal roofing, or commercial roofing.

02How do I know if I need repair or replacement?

If the issue is limited to one area and the roof is otherwise serviceable, repair may be enough. If there are repeated leaks, widespread wear, storm damage across multiple areas, or aging materials, replacement may be the better long-term option.

03What roofing systems do you work on?

We work on asphalt shingle, metal, flat, TPO/PVC, low-slope, and commercial roofing systems for homes and businesses in Utah.
