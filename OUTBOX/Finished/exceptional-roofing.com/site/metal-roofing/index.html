Common questions

01What types of metal roofing do you install?

We work with standing seam, corrugated panels, custom metal details, flashing, trim, and related metal roofing systems for residential and commercial properties.

02Do you repair metal roofs?

Yes. We repair common metal roof issues including leaks, failed flashing, loose panels, damaged trim, and problem roof transitions depending on the condition of the system.

03What is the difference between standing seam and corrugated metal?

Standing seam usually has raised vertical seams and concealed fasteners for a cleaner premium look. Corrugated metal has a more exposed panel profile and can be useful for certain designs and budgets.
