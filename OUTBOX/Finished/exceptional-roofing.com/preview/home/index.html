Questions

Good to know.

01Do you provide free roofing estimates?

Yes. Free quotes on any project, and most quotes are given the same day or week. Call or text 801-680-6499, or send the estimate form.

02Should I repair or replace my roof?

If the issue is limited to one area and the rest of the roof is serviceable, repair may be enough. Repeated leaks, widespread wear, storm damage in several areas, or aging materials usually point to replacement.

03What roofing systems do you work on?

Asphalt shingle, standing seam and corrugated metal, copper, wood shake and wood shingle, flat and low-slope, TPO and PVC, for homes and businesses.

04Do you offer financing?

Yes, through Acorn Finance. You can pre-qualify online and review payment options; approval, rates and terms are set by the lenders, not by us.

05Do you work outside Salt Lake County?

Our core area runs from Salt Lake City to Park City, Heber, Kamas and Santaquin. For the right project we also take work elsewhere in Utah, southern Idaho and southwest Wyoming.
