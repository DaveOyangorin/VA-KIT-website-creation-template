Asphalt Roof Repair

We repair common asphalt shingle roof problems including leaks, missing shingles, wind damage, exposed nail heads, damaged flashing, and aging problem areas. Our goal is to stop water intrusion and extend the life of your roof where repair is still a good option.

Metal Roof Repair

We repair metal roofing issues such as leaks at fasteners, flashing failures, separated panels, penetrations, and weather-related damage. Whether your roof is standing seam or exposed fastener, we work to identify the source of the problem and make clean, durable repairs.

Flat Roof Repair

We repair flat roofing systems affected by ponding water, membrane damage, seam issues, punctures, flashing failures, and leaks around drains or penetrations. We work on common low-slope roofing systems and focus on repairs that help restore watertight performance.

Skylight Replacement

We assess existing skylights for age, leaks, flashing condition, and overall performance during the roofing process. If replacement is needed, we can remove and replace the skylight and install updated flashing details to help provide a cleaner, more watertight finished system.

Swamp Cooler/AC Unit Removal or Replacement

We remove existing swamp coolers or roof-mounted AC units during roofing work and re-roof the area so the finished shingles blend in cleanly with the surrounding roof, with no visible signs the old unit was there. This includes proper deck preparation, underlayment, shingle installation, and flashing details as needed for a watertight finish.

Emergency Leak Response

We offer emergency roofing services for unexpected disasters. Our team responds quickly to minimize damage and provide fast, reliable repairs anywhere in Utah.

When to Repair vs Replace

In many cases, a roof can be repaired if the damage is limited and the overall system is still in serviceable condition. If the roof has widespread failure, repeated leak history, major aging, or significant material breakdown, replacement may be the better long-term solution. We give straightforward recommendations based on the condition of the roof and the scope of the issue.

Common questions

01What types of roofs do you repair?

We repair asphalt shingle, metal, and flat roofing systems for both residential and commercial properties.

02How do I know if my roof needs repair or full replacement?

If the damage is limited to a specific area and the rest of the roof is still in serviceable condition, repair is often a good option. If the roof has widespread wear, repeated leaks, or major aging, replacement may be the better long-term solution.

03Do you inspect skylights during roof repair work?

Yes. We can assess skylights for leaks, flashing condition, and age-related problems, and replace them if needed as part of the roofing work.

04Can you remove an old swamp cooler and make the roof look clean again?

Yes. We can remove an existing swamp cooler or roof-mounted unit and properly re-roof the area so the shingles tie in cleanly with the surrounding roof.

05What are the most common signs my roof needs repair?

Common signs include active leaks, water stains, missing shingles, lifted materials, damaged flashing, visible wear around penetrations, and storm-related damage.
