Consulting

Repairs VS Replacing Roof Consultation

Many of our clients approach us asking for a total roof replacement. Upon inspecting the state of their roof, however, we often find that all it needs is some quality repair work to keep it going strong for at least another few years.

We could have easily told them that their roof is in a terrible condition and should be replaced. However, we at J & N Roofing Maintenance only want what is best for you. That is why we offer a free Estimate for Repairs and Maintenance VS Replacing Roof Consultation to all our clients.

On-Site Roof Inspection

What Does Our Free Repairs VS Replacing Roof Consultation Entail?

After you call us for a free estimate and book your Repairs VS Replacing Roof Consultation, we will have a chat with you over the phone to get your details and clear the basics. Don’t worry about getting everything right the first time round. We will simply ask you a few questions to give us an idea of where things stand. The main inspection will take place in person when our experts visit your home.

We will then schedule a time and date convenient for you for us to come over and have a good look at the state of your roof. You would need to give us full access to your property and the roof, but don’t worry about anything else. We will bring all the tools and equipment that we need.

After carrying out a detailed inspection of your roof, we will hand down our final verdict. If we believe that replacing it is the best solution, we will provide you with all the information you need to make an informed decision. Likewise, if we think that all your roof needs are some repairs here and there, we will let you know.

In either case, we are happy to help you get your roof back in order. However, if you prefer to work with a different contractor, that’s no problem. Our Repairs VS Replacing Roof Consultation is 100% obligations-free!
