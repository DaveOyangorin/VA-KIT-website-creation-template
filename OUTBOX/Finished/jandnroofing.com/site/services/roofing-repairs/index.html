Do You Have a Leak?

What Does Our Roofing Repairs Entail?

#1 Repair Or Replace

Not all roofing repairs are created equal. Before you start looking for a contractor, you need to examine the extent of damage on your roof.

If you’re looking at a couple of missing shingles, that’s good news, as replacing them shouldn’t be that big of a deal. But if your roof has suffered serious damage during the latest storm and is now a safety hazard, you might have to consider a complete overhaul.

#2 Cost & Affordability

Unless you’re filthy rich, the cost of unexpected roofing repairs will cause a headache or two. Much like with anything else in life, the amount of money you’ll have to spend is proportionate to the damage you have to have repaired.

Keep in mind that, aside from buying construction material, you might also have to factor in the price of permits for large-scale roofing repairs.

#3 Choice of Roofing Contractor

Although there’s a YouTube tutorial for everything, think twice before you take the DIY approach. Sure, that hole in your roof seems simple to patch up. But what if you climb the roof, only to realize the damage is much bigger than you expected?

Keep your own as well as the safety of your home in mind when deciding how to handle roof damage. A licensed expert will be best suited to assess the damage level and offer the best, most effective solutions.
