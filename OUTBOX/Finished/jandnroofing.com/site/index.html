What warranty does J & N Roofing offer?

We offer a GAF System Plus Warranty. If you have a problem with your roof, you call the GAF Warranty department; a GAF certified roofing contractor will inspect and repair the roof, and if the repair is covered there’s no charge. See warranty support.

Do I need to take off my current roof to install a new one?

Not always. A re-roof (overlay) can go over an existing roof where it qualifies. We’ll look at your roof in a free consultation and tell you whether a tear-off is needed.

What’s the difference between shingle grades?

Heavier grades of shingles offer more durability and higher wind ratings, up to 110 mph. The 30-year shingle is the most commonly installed; 40- and 50-year shingles last longer and carry better wind ratings.

Is there someone to call outside business hours?

Yes. Our office hours are 7am to 5pm, Monday–Friday, and our 24-hour support line is (801) 949-7845.
