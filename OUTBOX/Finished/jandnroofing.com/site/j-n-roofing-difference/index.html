About J & N

J & N Roofing Difference

Free Estimate (801) 969-5759

The Salt Lake City Metro area has always had a need for a good roofing services company. And while there are many candidates out there, few can compete with J & N Roofing Maintenance, LLC.

For over 27 years, our experts have served the good people of Salt Lake City with high-quality roofing work. We’ll get the job done in no time flat, at a budget befitting your needs. On top of that, we cover more than just roofing jobs. If you have issues with gutters, or any other roof-related element, J & N Roofing will get you covered. So, what makes us stand a cut above the rest?

Services That J & N Roofing Offers

Here at J & N Roofing, we cover a vast array of roof-related services. First off, we focus on both installing new roofs and breathing life into old ones. After all, re-roofing a house is a costly job and we want to work within your budget in case you can’t afford a total replacement. Naturally, we cover many different types of roofs, including:

  • Bartile Roof Repair
  • Wood Shake Roof Repair
  • Metal Roofs
  • Asphalt Roofs

However, we will also cover other items such as the gutters, siding, and windows. But you might be thinking that these are just the basic services of any Salt Lake City roofer, so what makes us any different? Well, let’s explore that together.

Reasons to Choose J & N Roofing

Growth & Expansion

J & N Roofing might appear “young” with 27 years of roofing experience. However, let’s make one thing clear: even 5 years of non-stop growth are better than half a century of doing exactly the same type of work.

That’s right, over the past two decades, J & N has grown and changed in significant ways. We employ the latest techniques in the business, using cutting-edge technology. In addition, we tend to keep up with the latest trends in the industry. Thanks to our commitment to quality, each of our technicians go through regular training and education to learn the best new ways of handling a roof. On top of that, we keep in touch with various manufacturers and provide our customers with the best warranties. And speaking of customers, our 24-hour customer support is accurate, friendly, and to-the-point. Thanks to their hard work, you will get all the information you’ll need on your project, including the potential cost.

Staff Training?

Few roofing companies can claim that their staff adapts to top-notch, 2024 roofing standards. But our technicians, managers, and foremen all go through regular yearly training. They attend various conferences, connect with different workshops in Utah, and work hard to attain the latest certification documents in the roofing business. Here at J & N Roofing, we believe that any practice can be improved upon, which is why we focus so hard on doing it. In fact, some of the leading roof material manufacturers regularly keep in touch with us thanks to our dedication to getting the job done well.

Other Reasons to Pick J & N Roofing Services

Staff training and continuous growth are just a small part of what makes us stand out in the industry. Here’s a short list of other reasons why J & N will be a perfect fit for your roof:

  • We will offer you a free estimate for each individual project.
  • Our special offer section will notify you of any discounts and giveaways.
  • Thanks to their up-to-date training, our testers and technicians will calculate the best estimated price for your roof job.
  • In addition, our inspectors are certified and can work closely with your insurance company to make everything go smoothly.
  • We take good care of our staff; our senior employees will provide a written safety program for our workers, as well as schedule random periodic testing for substance abuse.
  • Speaking of, we encourage a drug-free, sober working environment to achieve the optimal result, as well as to keep our workers healthy and safe.
