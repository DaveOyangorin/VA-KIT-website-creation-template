About M&M Gutters & Exteriors

Mission Statement

At M&M Gutters and Exteriors, we have built an organization to act as advocates for homeowners. By vertically integrating all exterior trades, our customers can trust their job to be solid from the foundation to the roof.

Our History

M&M Gutters has been in the valley for more than 30 years. We got our start in 1990. The ownership has changed one time. In 2005, Nate Nuttall came on board when his cousin worked here. He started off as an installer. A few months later he became a sales rep which ultimately led to him getting promoted into management.

Then in 2010, the previous owner decided he wanted to retire and Nate purchased the business! He is proud to say he has done everything at the company and he continues to make it part of his regular routine going out to job sites and meeting with customers. And while family brought him to the business, he continues to employ family members. M&M Gutters and Exteriors is a true to their word family business.

Our Core Values

Communication

Communication is the lifeline of our company. It is essential for seamless collaboration and successful project outcomes. We prioritize clear, timely, and transparent communication at every level of our projects. This allows us to become partners in helping to make projects, dreams and hopes a reality for those we come in contact with.

Integrity

Integrity is the cornerstone of our company culture, guiding every decision and action we take. We uphold the highest ethical standards – demonstrating honesty, transparency, and accountability in all our interactions. Our commitment to integrity means that we do what we say we will do, always prioritize the well-being of our clients and employees, and strive to build lasting relationships based on trust and respect. We believe that integrity is non-negotiable and essential for the long-term success and sustainability of our business.

Responsibility

Responsibility stands as the cornerstone of our construction company’s ethos, ingrained deeply within our operations and culture. We believe in shouldering accountability at every stage of our projects, from planning to execution and beyond. Our commitment to responsibility manifests in various forms: ensuring the safety of our workers, doing things the right way and delivering high-quality results that meet or exceed expectations.

Community

Empowering communities through exceptional craftsmanship and sustainable solutions, our company is dedicated to delivering innovative, high-quality projects that enhance lives, foster growth, and build a better future for generations to come. Our commitment to be a positive force in our communities drives us in all the decisions we make.

Adaptability

We embrace innovation and flexibility – responding quickly and effectively to shifting priorities, challenges, and opportunities. Our team is adept at navigating complexities and finding creative solutions to overcome obstacles, ensuring the success of every project. We commit to continually learning, training and innovating the industry. By fostering a culture of adaptability, we stay ahead of industry trends, embrace new technologies, and continuously improve our processes to deliver the highest quality projects.
