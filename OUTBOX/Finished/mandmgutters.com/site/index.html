The path of the water

Rain hits the roof first. We follow it all the way down.

Water runs off the roof, past the heat cable, into the gutters, behind the fascia and down the siding. Every one of those is an M&M service, so one company can get the whole path right.

Utah winters

Snow melts on a warm roof and freezes again at the cold edge.

That ridge of ice at the eaves is an ice dam, and the water backed up behind it finds its way under shingles. Heat cable and ice-melting systems keep a channel open so it can drain.

Questions

Gutter questions we hear most

Or call 801.280.2222.

Do you install seamless gutters?

Yes. We fabricate seamless gutters on site, cut to fit your roofline, in copper, aluminum and steel.

What size gutters do you install?

K-style gutters in two sizes: 5-inch, which is standard on most homes, and 6-inch for bigger roofs and heavier runoff. Our guide explains how to choose: 5-Inch vs. 6-Inch Gutters: Which Size Does Your Home Need?

How long does a gutter installation take?

Most small homes take 2–6 hours. A two-story home usually takes a full day.

Can I finance my project?

Yes, financing is available on all our services, on approved credit. See financing.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We handle residential and commercial projects, including custom box gutters for commercial properties.
