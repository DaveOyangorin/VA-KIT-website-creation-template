Utah is said to have “the greatest snow on earth,” thanks in part to our intense winters. While the snow is beautiful and fun to play in, it can cause extensive problems for Utah homeowners when it builds up on roofs.

So, how can you combat these issues during our long winter season? Heating roof cables! Read on to learn about heat tape installation and why you’ll want to add this product to your roof before the next snowfall.

Say Goodbye to Snow and Ice Problems

Snow can be weighty, and the constant cycle of melting and refreezing can lead to ice dams, roof damage, and water infiltration in your home. Opting for roof heat tape installation means you won’t have to worry about these problems any longer. When the heat cables turn on, they quickly melt built-up snow and ice, removing them from your roof with little effort.

Efficient Snow and Ice Melting

So, how does heat tape installation help melt snow and ice? These heating cables are placed on your roof’s eaves, valleys, and gutters, where they warm up as needed to prevent ice or snow from accumulating. Typically, heat cables are controlled by a self-sensing thermostat that automatically adjusts the heat to the right temperature to prevent snow or ice buildup.

Protect Your Roof and Home

Heat cable installation in Salt Lake City keeps your roof free from snow and ice while protecting the rest of your home. When you stop snow accumulation and ice dams, you’re preventing potential water damage issues in your attic, walls, and ceilings and protecting the structural integrity of your home. Additionally, melting snow and ice before they build up can save your roof and gutters from excessive weight that causes damage and detachment.

Energy-Efficient Solution

Since a roof ice-melt system uses heat, many people wonder how energy-efficient it is. Roof heat cables only activate when they reach a specific temperature and cycle to keep the temps around them above freezing, meaning they are not constantly running.

The cables turn on and off as needed and require very little energy to warm up or regulate temperatures, so you won’t have to worry about using large quantities of electricity to operate the system.

Installation and Maintenance

Heat cable installation is relatively simple, and our team can typically complete the job in less than a day. The cables are securely attached to the roof in spots prone to snow and ice accumulation, like valleys, eaves, and gutters, and need minimal interaction once installed.

Roof heat cables are generally low-maintenance, but it’s still important to inspect them once a year before winter starts to ensure they’ll function properly when needed.

How to Determine What You Need for a Roof and Gutter Heat Cable System

Before scheduling roof heat cable installation, take time to inspect your roof’s size and slope and determine the average winter snowfall in your area to decide whether these are the right choices for your home. If you have questions or need assistance, the M&M Gutters & Exteriors team is here to help.

We can easily and quickly inspect your roof and make professional recommendations regarding the best heat tape installation for your home. With our robust industry experience, you can trust that we’ll give you the reliable information you need to protect your home during Utah’s harsh winters.
