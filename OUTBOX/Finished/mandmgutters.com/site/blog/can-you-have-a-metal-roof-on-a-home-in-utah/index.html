Are you thinking about adding a metal roof to your Utah home? You’ve come to the right place! Metal roofs are becoming more popular in our state because they are highly durable and well-suited to Utah’s weather conditions, from snowy winters in Logan to hot summers in Moab and St. George.

Read on to discover the pros and cons of metal roofing and find out if it’s a good fit for your house.

What Is Metal Roofing?

A metal roof is made of metal materials, like aluminum, copper, tin, or zinc, and functions like any other type of roof to provide protection. You can get a metal roof in several styles, colors, and finishes to create a unique look on your home.

Is a Metal Roof Cheaper Than Shingles?

Shingles are the most popular type of roofing because they are cost-effective and reliable. While metal roofing can be more expensive, it’s more durable and long-lasting and can withstand extreme weather better than other roofing materials.

How Long Does a Metal Roof Last in Utah?

If you’re wondering how long metal roofs last, we have good news! Proper maintenance means a metal roof can last between 40 and 70 years. So, while these roofs may have more upfront costs, they are still cost-effective since you won’t have to replace them often.

How Weather-Proof Are Metal Roofs?

Metal roofs are an excellent choice for Utah homes, as they can withstand our varied weather conditions. These roofs are sturdier, more water-resistant, and longer-lasting than other options, which helps ensure they’ll provide excellent protection for your Utah home, especially if you live in warmer climates like Moab or St. George.

What Is the Average Cost of a New Metal Roof?

The cost of metal home roofs will vary based on the size of your house, the material used, and the company you work with. An aluminum roof can cost between $11,000 and $35,000, while a copper roof ranges between $34,000 and $68,000.

What Are the Pros & Cons of a Metal Roof?

Like with any roofing material, there are always pros and cons to consider. Here are the pros and cons of a metal roof to understand when selecting a roof material for your home

Advantages of Metal Roofs

  • Durability: Metal roofs resist cracking, shrinking, and eroding, which means they can last longer.
  • Sustainability: Metal roofs are environmentally friendly and sustainable since metal is recyclable.
  • Life span: Metal roofs can last over 50 years, making them a more long-lasting solution.
  • Aesthetics: With multiple material options, metal roofs create a beautiful look that enhances curb appeal.
  • Energy efficiency: Metal roofs reflect heat instead of absorbing it, so they won’t make your home hotter hotter and are highly energy-efficient.
  • Fire- and storm-resistant: A metal roof is noncombustible and offers more protection from fire and severe weather.
  • Maintenance and care: Metal roofs need little maintenance, saving time, money, and effort.

Disadvantages of Metal Roofs

  • Noise: Many people are concerned that metal roofs are noisy during rain or hail storms, but insulation technology helps prevent this.
  • Upfront cost and affordability: Metal roofs offer long-term cost savings, though they can be more expensive upfront than other options.
  • Dents: Extreme weather events may cause dents on a metal roof. However, using high-quality materials can minimize this risk.
  • Limited by HOA: Some Homeowners’ Associations won’t allow metal roofs.
  • Installation: Metal roofs require more specialized installation skills than traditional roof options.
  • Contraction and expansion: Metal can expand or contract as temperatures fluctuate, but proper installation can reduce this problem.
  • Color fading: Metal roofs can fade over time, so they need to be sealed with a high-quality finish to prevent this.

Should I Get a Metal Roof?

Metal roofs are an excellent choice for Utah homes, but you should consider budget, aesthetic preferences, and home location before making your final decision. It’s a good idea to speak with roofing professionals, like those at M&M Gutters, to help you determine if a metal roof is right for you.

What Are the Environmental Benefits of Metal Roofs?

Metal roofs offer several environmental benefits to protect your home and reduce your carbon footprint. They are:

  • Recyclable: Metal roofs are primarily made from recycled materials and are 100% recyclable at the end of their lifespan.
  • Energy-efficient: Metal roofs reflect solar heat, resulting in lower cooling costs.
  • Long lifespan: Metal roofs can last up to 70 years with minimal maintenance.
  • Perfect for solar panels: If you plan to install solar panels, a metal roof is a great complement.

How Do I Maintain a Metal Roof?

Metal roofs are generally low-maintenance and require minimal upkeep to keep your roof in good condition. To maintain your metal roof, be sure to:

  • Check your roof after heavy winds, rain, and snowstorms.
  • Trim any overhanging tree branches.
  • Tighten loose screws from time to time.
  • Inspect your roof for signs of rust, scratches, and other damage to coatings.
  • Get a professional roof inspection every few years.

Reviewing this maintenance checklist can help keep your home protected.
