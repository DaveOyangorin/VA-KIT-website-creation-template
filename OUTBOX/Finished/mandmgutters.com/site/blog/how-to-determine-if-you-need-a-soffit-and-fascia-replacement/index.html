What Are Soffits and Fascia?

As you consider soffit and fascia replacement, it’s helpful to clearly understand how
these roof components work. If you stand near your house and look up at the roof, the
soffits are the unexposed underside of the roof’s eaves. Soffits are used to seal the gap
between your home’s siding and the roofline. Fascia boards run along the edges of the
roof, where they help support the outer line of shingles and connect the house’s walls to
the roof.
Both soffits and fascia are essential for protecting your home’s exterior and preventing
water damage.

Common Soffit Issues

Here are some common issues that could indicate it’s time for soffit replacement.

Improper Installation

If the soffits aren’t installed correctly, they become vulnerable to weather conditions and
can experience wind or moisture damage. Additionally, if the soffits are placed too close
together, they won’t provide adequate ventilation for your home. If you notice any
damage, sagging, detached sections, or improper spacing, it’s time to get the soffits
inspected.

Animal and Insect Intrusions

When soffits are damaged or gapping, it creates entry points for small animals or
insects to get into your roof. Once these pests get inside, they cause further damage,

meaning you could face a more extensive and intensive repair instead of just replacing
the soffits.

Moisture Damage

When soffits are gapping or already damaged, water can get into the space and cause
even more issues. The soffits can become warped, start rotting, and as the water
spreads, it can compromise the structural integrity of your roof.

Dry Rot

If you have wood soffits, they can experience dry rot, which is fungal decay that thrives
in wood fibers. When this happens, the soffits become brittle and crumble, meaning
they are no longer effective in protecting your home.

Common Fascia Board Issues

If you notice any of these issues with your fascia, it may be time to consider fascia
board replacement.

Decaying Wood

Like wood soffits, wood fascia boards can experience the same moisture damage or dry
rot issues. If you notice any warping or crumbling, you should have a professional
roofing company inspect all parts of your roof.

Improper Fascia Board Replacement

If you recently had a fascia and soffit replacement done and are still noticing issues,
your fascia boards may have been replaced improperly. Since fascia boards are
necessary for maintaining the structural integrity of your roof and preventing water from
getting in, you’ll need to replace them again to protect your home.

Why You Should Replace or Repair Soffit and Fascia

Putting off replacing soffits and fascia may seem insignificant, but it could cause more
significant problems in the long run. There are several reasons why you should
schedule fascia and soffit repair or replacement.
● Protect your home — Soffits and fascia help keep water and pests out, so they
need to be in good condition to protect your home.
● Enhance curb appeal — Damaged, warped, and rotting fascia and soffits can
be unsightly. Replacing or repairing them helps your exterior look better.
● Preserve energy efficiency — Well-maintained soffits and fascia help regulate
your home’s interior temperature.

● Prevent costly repairs — Fascia and soffit replacement can help prevent more
expensive repairs, so it’s best to address them as soon as possible.

How Much Does It Cost to Replace Soffit and Fascia?

Fascia and soffit replacement costs will vary depending on different factors, including
home size, the extent of damage, and which materials you choose for the replacement.
To better understand what it might cost, contact M&M Gutters & Exteriors for an
accurate estimate for your home.

How Much Does It Cost to Paint Soffit and Fascia?

If your soffits and fascia are still in good condition and you just want to paint them, the
cost will be lower than a replacement. The size of your home and the ease of the job will
influence the overall cost.

decide which option to go with.

Factors Affecting Fascia and Soffit Replacement Cost

Several factors can influence the cost of replacing soffits and fascia, so it’s a good idea
to understand them before moving forward for the sake of your budget.

Accessibility

Are all of your soffits and fascia easily accessible? If there are areas a roofing company
won’t have easy access to, or your house is multiple stories, the extra labor and
equipment needed could increase the overall cost.

Assessing Damage

Soffits and fascia that have extensive damage and need to be replaced entirely will cost
more than minor repairs or replacing small sections. You can expect to pay more if you
need to replace large sections or even all of the soffits and fascia.

Painting

If you’re considering replacing soffits and fascia for looks instead of due to damage,
think about painting them instead. Painting is more cost-effective than replacement,
though you should work with your roofing company to ensure this is a viable option for
your home.

Removal

Whether you need to replace a small section or all of the soffits and fascia on your roof,
you’ll need to factor in the cost of removing the existing materials as well. Additionally,
the complexity of the removal and how much material needs to be hauled away from
your property can also influence the overall cost.
