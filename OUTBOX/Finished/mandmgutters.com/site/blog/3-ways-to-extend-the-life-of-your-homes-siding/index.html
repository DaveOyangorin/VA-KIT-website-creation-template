3 Ways To Extend the Life of Your Home’s Siding

Free quote

By Nathan Nuttall · 2022-02-16 · 2 min read

Extend the life of your home’s siding by implementing a few tips from our team of knowledgeable professionals.

The exterior of your home holds many responsibilities, and keeping it in good condition allows it to do its job. Siding is designed to last for a long time, but it cannot work how it is meant to without proper care and maintenance.

Constant exposure to extreme elements can significantly impact its lifespan and function effectively. By implementing a few pro tips, you can keep your siding in tip-top shape for decades to come.

Keep reading to learn more.

3 Ways You Can Extend the Life of Your Home’s Siding

These simple yet effective tips will help improve your home’s siding look and function. Here’s what to do:

  1. Examine It Regularly — Taking a walk around the perimeter of your house to inspect your siding is a great way to ensure it is still in good shape. Keep your eyes open for damaged areas, such as dents, chips, or holes. Intense winds can cause your siding to loosen, raise, or otherwise become damaged.
    There isn’t a right answer as to how often you should examine your exteriors, but once every season and after intense storms should be sufficient. When you come across any damage, have it repaired as soon as possible to avoid further damage.
  2. Clean It Often — It’s no secret that the siding wrapping your home takes regular beatings; between the elements, critters, and people, the exteriors of your home can begin to look rough. Keep your siding looking great by eliminating grime, mold, and dirt with regular washes. Use your hose and a mixture of water and white vinegar to scrub away any imperfections.
    Keep in mind that using a power washer can damage the siding if not used properly. In some cases, using a power washer can also void the manufacturer’s warranty.
  3. Treat It Carefully — Siding was built to last, but that doesn’t make it indestructible. If you want to increase the life of your siding, it is crucial to treat it carefully. This means avoiding using barbecue grills, fire pits, and compact radiators nearby.
    It also means abstaining from hitting it with anything, including your lawnmower, edger, or a ball. By making conscious consideration and effort to treat your siding carefully, you can add years to the life of your exteriors.
