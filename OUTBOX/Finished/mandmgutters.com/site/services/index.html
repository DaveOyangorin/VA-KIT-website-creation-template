Exterior Project Services

Premier Home Services in Utah

For more than 30 years, M&M Gutters has been a household name in premier exterior project services. Whether you’re looking for existing home remodeling in Utah or building from scratch, our pros are exceptional at roofing, siding, rain gutters, fascia repair, and everything in between.

When you need professional craftsmanship and durable solutions tailored to your home’s unique needs, you can count on M&M Gutters to get it done right.

Full-Exterior Remodel (More than just Gutters!)

M&M Gutters is so much more than just gutters! We provide full exterior home services, and life can be a lot more pleasant when you have multiple projects and one contractor who can take care of them all for you! See below for details on all of the different home exterior services that we provide.

Rain Gutters

We offer custom leaf guard and rain gutter installations throughout Salt Lake City, designed to efficiently channel rainwater away from your property. Using a variety of materials, including aluminum, copper, and steel, our gutters not only protect your home but also enhance its curb appeal. Whether you need a new installation, timely repairs, or routine gutter cleaning, trust M&M to keep your gutters working flawlessly.

Soffit & Fascia

Proper soffit and fascia installation protects your roof’s structure by preventing moisture buildup and ensuring efficient ventilation. These elements also give your home’s exterior a clean, finished look that adds lasting value. Our luxury home renovations experts help you choose the best materials to match your house and protect against Utah weather. And if you need soffit and fascia repair, our team will be there.

Full-Home Window Replacement

Upgrade your home with energy-efficient, noise-reducing windows installed by our experts. We help you select beautiful, durable windows that fit your style and improve comfort, security, and energy savings. Our full-home window replacement services ensure your entire house benefits from modern performance and aesthetic appeal.

Heating Cable

Avoid roof damage and safety hazards caused by ice dams with our professional heating cable installation. Our exterior project services experts recommend and install the correct type of heat cable for your roof — whether self-regulating or constant wattage — to keep your gutters and roofline clear all winter long.

Stucco

Stucco is a popular choice for home exteriors, offering distinct advantages and aesthetic appeal. Stucco provides a smooth, textured surface that can be customized in various colors and styles from smooth, dash, sand, and skip trowel to name a few. These finishes offer a range of textures, from smooth and modern to rough and patterned. Ask your M&M expert about options today

Stone

Stone siding, whether natural or manufactured, offers a classic, elegant look with exceptional durability and weather resistance. And when pairing stone up with a variety of other building materials, we can create a visually striking and unique exterior design. Ask your M&M Exterior expert for a free design consultation today.

Siding

Siding is your home's first line of defense against Utah’s harsh weather. Choose from a wide range of durable siding options that improve your home’s energy efficiency and curb appeal. Our skilled team provides expert siding installation and repairs using quality materials like vinyl, fiber cement, cedar, and more, to fit your budget and home style. From home remodeling in Utah to luxury home renovations, M&M helps you transform your exterior from every angle.

Roofing

A solid roof means a safe, comfortable home. Whether you need minor repairs or a full roof replacement, our team performs thorough inspections and quality installation using shingles, metal, or other high-quality roofing materials. We work diligently to protect your home from leaks, drafts, and weather damage, giving your house the most protection and the longest lifespan. Roofing is one of our top exterior project services, and we always ensure your roof protects your home from the top down.

Leaf Guards

Say goodbye to frequent gutter cleanings with M&M Gutter’s premium leaf guard installation. Our durable, low-maintenance gutter guards keep your gutters clear of leaves and debris so water flows through freely, protecting your home against costly water damage.
Available in wire mesh and aluminum screens, our leaf guards are designed for lasting performance and easy maintenance.

Ice Melting Systems

Protect your home from winter damage with advanced ice melting systems. Our self-regulating heat tape prevents dangerous icicles and hazardous ice dams by automatically adjusting heat output based on outdoor temperatures.
Built to endure Utah’s heavy snow and frigid temps, our energy-efficient ice melt systems keep your roof and gutters clear, helping you avoid costly repairs and slipping hazards.
