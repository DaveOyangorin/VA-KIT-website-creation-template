Our Process

With so many siding contractors in Salt Lake City, it can be difficult to know whom to trust. At M&M Gutters & Exteriors, we take every exterior siding project very seriously, from start to finish. Here’s what you can expect when you hire us to handle your Salt Lake City exterior siding project.

1. Assess and Remove Existing Siding

The very first step is to conduct a thorough evaluation of your home’s current state. Our experienced contractors in Salt Lake City will carefully remove the existing siding, taking precautions to protect windows, doors, and landscaping. This allows us to uncover any hidden issues and prepare a clean slate for your new siding.

2. Evaluate the Sheathing

Once the old siding is removed, we conduct a comprehensive inspection of your home’s sheathing and framework. This step identifies any moisture damage, insect infestation, or structural issues that may have been concealed. Addressing these problems early enables us to prevent any future complications with your new siding, which could be costly.

3. Preparing the Exterior

Before installing new siding, we focus on creating an ideal surface. This may involve repairs or replacing damaged sheathing, upgrading insulation, and installing a weather-resistant barrier, depending on the status of your home.

4. Creating Proper Ventilation and Moisture Control

Proper airflow and moisture management help keep your siding safe and sturdy for a long time. We implement advanced techniques to confirm there’s adequate ventilation and install appropriate moisture barriers. This step helps prevent issues like mold growth and premature deterioration of your new siding.

5. Installation of New Siding

With a solid foundation in place, our skilled exterior siding technicians begin installing your chosen siding. Whether it’s vinyl, fiber cement, or another material, we adhere to manufacturer specifications and industry best practices.

6. Clean Up

As the installation process nears completion, we focus on the fine details that make a big difference. We’ll apply caulk where necessary, painting or touching up exposed nail heads and confirming that all trim pieces are perfectly aligned.

Different Grades of Siding

There are many kinds of siding, each with its own pros and cons. Here’s a quick breakdown of what you can expect from some of the top types of siding in Salt Lake City and whom they might be good for.

Prodigy Siding

This siding grade stands out for its innovative design featuring a built-in insulation system. Prodigy siding provides superior impact resistance, making it an excellent choice for areas prone to severe weather, like Salt Lake City. Homeowners looking for a blend of comfort and durability tend to go for this type of siding.

Charter Oak Siding

For those who love the classic look of wood without the maintenance hassles, Charter Oak siding is the answer. Its authentic wood-grain appearance is nearly indistinguishable from real wood. Choosing the Right Exterior Siding

When you build or renovate a home, your choice of siding will be a major influence on your home’s look and energy efficiency. To choose the type of siding best suited to your home, you’ll need to pay attention to a variety of factors, including the aesthetic style of your home, your budget, and, of course, the climate of your area.

When it comes to style, consider that different materials will also change your home’s overall look and function. Wood and stucco are often used for a more rustic, traditional look, while materials like vinyl or fiber cement are preferred for modern styles.
