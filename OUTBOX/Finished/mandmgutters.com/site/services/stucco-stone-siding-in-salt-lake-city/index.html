Our Stucco & Stone Siding Process

Whether you need a repair or a complete exterior siding installation, our stucco and stone siding contractors in Salt Lake City are here for you every step of the way.

Assess and Remove Existing Siding

We begin by carefully evaluating your current siding to identify any damage, weak spots, or areas of concern, and then remove it safely while protecting your doors, windows, and landscaping from debris.

Evaluate the Sheathing

With the siding removed, we examine the sheathing and framework underneath. If we find moisture damage or deterioration, we make the necessary repairs and add a weather-resistant barrier to give your new siding a strong, dry base.

Prepare the Exterior

Before installation, we repair structural imperfections, upgrade insulation if needed, and apply protective layers. This ensures a smooth, even surface that helps your new siding perform its best.

Create Proper Ventilation and Moisture Control

Moisture is a home’s worst enemy. We install drainage systems, flashing, and ventilation solutions that allow air to circulate while keeping water out. This step is critical for preventing mold, mildew, and premature wear.

Install New Siding

Once the groundwork is laid, our team installs your chosen stucco, stone, or blended siding. We follow a meticulous step-by-step process to ensure every panel and section is secure, level, and aligned, resulting in a durable exterior with a seamless finish.

Clean Up

Once our work is done, we’ll take care of the final touch-ups so you’re home looks polished and ready to enjoy.
