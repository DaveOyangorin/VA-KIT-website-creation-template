Rain Gutter Installation Process

The Value of Professional Rain Gutter Installation

While installing rain gutters yourself can give you a great sense of accomplishment, a professional can identify issues you might miss. Rain gutters must be sloped precisely, anchored to the rafter tails (not just the fascia), and sealed properly to prevent water from seeping into your home.

Rain gutters are an essential component of your roofing system, but finding the time (and patience) to install them can be a hassle. Hiring a licensed, insured gutter installation company like M&M Gutters gives you peace of mind knowing that, if anything goes wrong, your installation is backed by a warranty.

How Are Gutters Installed?

Before you bring in the professionals, you need to know exactly what you’re getting into with a rain gutter installation for your wallet’s sake. A rain gutter installation involves more than one trip up a ladder. Here’s a step-by-step walkthrough of what you can expect with a professional rain gutter installation near you.

Pre-Installation Assessment

Before any gutters go up, our crew will assess your fascia, measure your roofline, and determine the best drainage layout for your home.

Slope Layout and Chalk Line

Rain gutters need to be angled precisely to direct water toward the downspouts. Once the correct slope is determined, we’ll mark a chalk line to ensure proper installation.

Cut and Fabricate the Gutter

After measurements are confirmed, we’ll cut the gutters to fit your roofline. We fabricate seamless gutters on-site and trim sectional gutters to the right length.

Install Brackets and Hang the Gutter

We secure brackets to the fascia or rafter tails and hang the gutters along the chalk line to ensure a proper fit and slope.

Cut Downspout Holes and Connect Downspouts

Our team drills outlet holes in the gutter and installs downspouts to direct water away from your foundation.

Final Inspection and Water Test

Once everything is in place, our installers will run a water test to check for leaks, confirm proper drainage, and make any final adjustments.

How Long Do Rain Gutters Take to Install?

The time it takes to install your rain gutters really depends on your home’s size and the complexity of your roofline. We wrap up most small homes in 2–6 hours, but if you have a two-story home, expect it to take a full day. Below are a few factors that affect the timeline:

Linear Footage/Home Size

The larger your roofline, the longer your rain gutter installation will take. Small homes may need 100-150 feet of guttering, while larger homes require 200 feet or more.

Number of Stories

Single-story homes are easier to work on, while two-story homes require moving ladders more frequently, adding time to the job.

Gutter Material

Vinyl and aluminum gutters are lightweight and quick to install, while steel and copper gutters are heavier, harder to cut, and require more precision.

Number of Downspouts

If your home has more corners or longer runs, you’ll need more downspouts, which will extend the installation time.

Damage to Previous Gutters

Heavily corroded or improperly fastened gutters take longer to remove and delay the start of the installation.

Fascia Repair Needs

If fascia boards are rotted or damaged, they’ll need to be repaired before we can secure new gutters.
