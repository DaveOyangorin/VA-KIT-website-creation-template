Ice and Snow Can Damage Your Roof and Home

When snow piles up on your roof, it doesn’t just sit there — it melts, refreezes, and causes serious problems. Ice dams can form along your roofline, blocking proper drainage and leading to water seeping into your home. This results in leaks, damaged roofing materials, and even mold growth. Without a solution like roof ice-melt systems, you could face expensive repairs every winter.

Cutting-Edge Technology That Keeps You Prepared

At M&M Gutters, our advanced ice-melt systems use self-regulating heat tape to prevent ice dams and icicles from forming. This system automatically adjusts its power output based on outdoor temperatures, ensuring just the right amount of heat to keep your roof safe and ice-free. Our roof ice-melt systems deliver energy-efficient, reliable performance. As the temperature drops, the tape increases conductivity to generate heat, and as it rises, the system reduces heat production — keeping your roof protected without wasting energy. Built to endure Utah’s harsh winters, our solutions are both economical and durable.

Heat Cables and Heat Panels

When it comes to ice-melting roof systems installation in Salt Lake City, choosing between heat cables and heat panels depends on your specific needs. Here’s a breakdown of each option:

HEAT CABLES

Heat cables are flexible, self-regulating wires installed along roof edges and gutters to prevent ice formation by melting snow and ice as it accumulates. They’re a cost-effective solution for preventing ice dams.

Flexible Installation

Heat cables can be easily installed along roof edges, gutters, and downspouts, making them ideal for custom applications.

Cost-effective

Generally, heat cables are more budget-friendly compared to heat panels, providing a good balance between cost and performance.

Self-regulating

Many heat cables feature self-regulating technology that adjusts the heat output based on temperature changes, ensuring efficient energy use.

HEAT PANELS

Heat panels are durable systems installed on larger roof surfaces, providing even heating to prevent ice dams across wide areas. They offer long-lasting protection with minimal maintenance.

Efficient coverage

Heat panels are often installed in larger, flat areas of the roof and provide uniform heating across extensive surfaces, reducing the risk of ice dams.

Durable

Made from robust materials, heat panels are built to withstand harsh weather conditions and have a longer lifespan.

Low maintenance

Once installed, heat panels require minimal upkeep and deliver consistent performance throughout the winter season.
