Soffit and Fascia Installation in Salt Lake City

Soffit and fascia are necessary for protecting your roof from water and moisture damage. That’s why you need knowledgeable and experienced fascia and soffit repair contractors to ensure your home is protected. At M&M Gutters, we’ve been performing soffit roof repair and installing fascia for nearly 30 years, so you can trust that we’ll get the job done right every time.

What Are Soffit and Fascia?

Soffit and fascia are essential to your home’s exterior as they protect against moisture damage and other environmental factors. The soffit goes on the underside of your roof’s overhang to help ventilate your attic and prevent moisture buildup.

The fascia is the board that runs along the edge of your roof, connecting the roof to the walls of your home. It is designed to protect this edging area from weather damage while supporting your gutters.

It is crucial to have proper soffits and fascia on your home to prevent moisture problems like mold, rot, or even structural damage. And since soffits help with ventilation, not having them causes your heating and cooling systems to work harder to maintain comfortable temperatures, which could increase energy costs.

Choosing the Right Soffit and Fascia Materials

When it comes to fascia and soffit installation in Salt Lake City, you want to ensure you’re choosing the right materials for your project. Your options are:

Steel

  • Resistant to mold, mildew, and rot
  • Low maintenance and easy to clean
  • Energy efficient
  • Durable and long-lasting
  • Doesn’t warp or split like wood
  • Modern look and design

Wood

  • Natural and aesthetic appeal
  • Can be stained or painted to match your home’s design
  • Available in various wood species
  • Durable and long-lasting if properly maintained
  • Provides good insulation and energy efficiency

PVC

  • Resistant to mold, mildew, and rot
  • Resistant to peeling, fading, or chipping
  • Does not need to be painted
  • Easy to install
  • Available in various colors and styles

Fiber cement

  • Provides excellent protection and ventilation
  • Resistant to warping or splitting like wood
  • Low maintenance
  • Does not need to be painted
  • Resistant to moisture and water damage

Aluminum

  • Lightweight
  • Non-combustible and fire-resistant
  • Resistant to peeling, fading, or chipping
  • Resistant to moisture and water damage
  • Durable and long-lasting

Different Grades of Soffit and Fascia

In addition to the different types of soffit and fascia, they also come in several grades.

    • Economy — This is the thinnest and least expensive option and is typically used in low-cost housing or temporary structures.
    • Standard — This is a mid-range option that balances cost and durability. It is typically used for residential structures and is designed to withstand moderate weather conditions.
    • Heavy-duty — This is the thickest and most durable option and is ideal for areas with harsh weather conditions.
    • Premium — This is the most expensive option but is highly durable and long-lasting. It is usually used for commercial buildings or high-end residential projects.
