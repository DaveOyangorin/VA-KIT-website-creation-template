Choose Your Dream Windows

When replacing the windows in your Salt Lake City home, you have several different types to choose from. While we can install almost any window you’re looking for, here are some of our most popular installations.

Single-Hung
Single-hung windows are one of the most popular options we install. These windows feature two pieces, one on top, which is stationary, and one on the bottom, which moves up and down. Typically, single-hung windows are more cost-effective and energy-efficient. Double-Hung
Double-hung windows function the same as their single counterparts. The only difference is that the window’s top and bottom move instead of just the bottom. These windows are easier to clean, have more design options, and allow for more ventilation throughout your home. Casement
Casement windows, often called crank windows, have hinges on one side and open to the left or right. These windows offer a unique design detail, have excellent airflow, and generally receive high security ratings. Sliding
Sliding windows are the most common option for any new build or replacement window installation in Salt Lake City. These windows move along a top and bottom track to slide open or closed. These windows are easy to clean, offer lots of natural light, and have a streamlined, simple design. Bay Windows
Bay windows are generally a three-sectioned piece made up of one larger window in the middle and two smaller ones on the sides, all of which are typically single-hung windows. These windows have a beautiful, functional design inside and out, can improve resale value based on their look, and let in lots of natural light thanks to multiple windows.

What’s the difference between full-frame and insert replacement?

A full-frame replacement is placed without your existing frame, whereas a window insert replacement fits a window into your existing frame. If your window frame is in good condition, you’ll most likely only need a window insert replacement.

How long does window installation take?

The timeframe for a window installation depends on the type and number of windows you’re looking to replace in your home. Our professional installers can give you a more accurate timeframe for either a full or insert window replacement.

Maintenance & Benefits of Full-Home Window Replacement

What are the signs I need new windows?

You may need new windows if:

    • You notice faded furniture
    • Your window frame is cracked or rotted
    • You have trouble opening and closing windows
    • You receive higher energy bills
    • You see condensation between the glass panes
    • You feel a draft coming from the windows
How much can I save on energy bills with new windows?

You can save up to 7-15% each year on heating and cooling costs by replacing your old windows with new ones. With M&M Gutters, you have a variety of options to choose from, ensuring your home remains properly insulated and comfortable throughout the year.

What warranty coverage is included?

The warranty coverage for a full window replacement includes protection against window seal failure and defects. With M&M Gutters, we’ll review the details of what’s covered and how long your coverage lasts to ensure your investment is protected.

Seasonal Maintenance and Cleaning Tips

You should care for your windows each season by:

    • Checking for signs of moisture around the frame
    • Using only soap and water to wash your windows
    • Removing dirt and debris from any weep holes in the exterior
    • Lubricating moving parts to avoid squeaky windows
