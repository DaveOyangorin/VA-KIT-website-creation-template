About Cache County

Cache County is one of Utah’s northernmost counties. Settlers – trappers and explorers – begin populating the area in the late 18th century and early 19th century. Utah legislature officially created Cache County in 1856.

Logan, UT, the county seat, is home to Utah State University – and some of the most scenic driving in the country. The Uinta-Wasatch-Cache National Forest is one of the state’s most beautiful nature reserves.

Does Concrete Lifting Last?

How Long Does It Take to Lift Concrete?

How Long Should Concrete Cure Before Sealing?

What Is the Best Foundation Repair Method?

Push piers or Helical piers are the best method for lifting and repairing settled foundations

How Much Do Push Piers Cost?

The cost varies depending on the structure size, an average size home can cost between $10,000 and $20,000

Can I Install Helical Piers Yourself?
