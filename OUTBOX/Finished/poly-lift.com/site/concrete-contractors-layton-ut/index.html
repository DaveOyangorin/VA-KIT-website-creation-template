About Layton

Layton, UT, nestled between the majestic Great Salt Lake and the Wasatch Mountain Range, is a thriving community celebrated for its outdoor recreational opportunities, cultural vibrancy, and a climate that is conducive to a variety of activities all year round. It is home to several landmarks, including the renowned Adams Canyon Trail and the well-appointed Layton Commons Park, both of which provide residents with ample opportunities for outdoor recreation. The city's culture is a blend of urban sophistication and small-town charm that appeals to both young professionals and families alike.

Recognizing the challenges posed by uneven concrete, particularly in areas susceptible to snowfall and significant weather changes, Poly Lift is committed to ensuring the safety and longevity of concrete structures in the community. We are proud to offer concrete lifting and leveling solutions that not only enhance property aesthetics but also contribute to preserving the structural integrity of homes and commercial spaces.
