## Design (premium)

- **Research (screenshotted 20 Oct 2026):** Hilti, Magnum Piering, Ram Jack. HMI Foam (their foam maker) and Alchatek were behind security checks.
- **Chosen three:**
  - **Hilti** (hilti.com): industrial, product-led tiles with one hard accent colour.
  - **Magnum Piering** (magnumpiering.com): their own pier supplier, with engineering blue and tinted product photos.
  - **Ram Jack** (ramjack.com): a dark, bold foundation-repair brand.
- **Concept, "Back to level":**
  - **Hero:** a full-bleed crew-at-work hero (their trailer behind), with the headline "Concrete lifting in Logan, with foam, not a jackhammer" and a fact card ("Less than a day", "15–25× lighter").
  - **The signature, the interactive lift:** a cross-section of a sunken slab over a void. Press "Inject foam" or drag the slider: foam fills the void, the slab rises, and a spirit-level bubble slides to centre. It's a diagram of the client's described method, not a measurement.
  - **Specialty tiles** with real photos.
  - **The Utah State University piering case strip.**
  - **The lifted-garage sequence** as staggered numbered prints.
  - **A stadium parallax band** with the client's own "save landfills" line.
  - **The rest:** an FAQ plus both locations and the estimate form, and blog cards.
- **Brand guide:**
  - deep green-black #14211C
  - logo green #2F7D4F
  - spirit-level yellow #F2C230
  - foam white #FBF7E9
  - concrete greys
- **Fonts:** Big Shoulders Display (condensed caps), Manrope (text), Red Hat Mono (labels).
- **Reduced motion:** respected. The lift jumps straight to level.
