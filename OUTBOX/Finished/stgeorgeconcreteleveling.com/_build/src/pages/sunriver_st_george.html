Frequently Asked Questions

Concrete Lifting & Leveling in St. George - How does it work?

We drill small ports in a grid pattern, inject high‑density closed‑cell polyurethane foam, and monitor lift with a laser for precise results. The foam expands to fill voids, stabilize soils, and raise the slab to grade. Most areas are walkable within 15-30 minutes and, in many cases, drivable the same day. We finish by patching ports and sealing key cracks and joints to keep water out and protect the repair.

Polyjacking vs. mudjacking - which is best for my SunRiver home?

Polyjacking uses lightweight foam with 5/8” holes, cures fast, and won’t add heavy weight to weak soils. Mudjacking uses a cement‑sand slurry with 1-1.5” holes and adds weight, which can be a concern in soft subgrades. We’ll assess your slab, drainage, and budget to recommend the right option. In Southern Utah, foam is preferred for long‑term performance and minimal disruption.

How long does polyurethane concrete lifting last in Southern Utah?

Properly installed polyurethane systems commonly last decades. The closed‑cell foam resists water and won’t break down like soil. Longevity depends on site drainage, irrigation, and soil conditions. We often add deep foam injection for subgrade densification where soils are weak. With good water management and sealed joints, most homeowners enjoy long‑term stability and a multi‑year warranty.

Is polyurethane foam safe and eco‑friendly?

We use professional, high‑density closed‑cell foams tested for compressive strength and water resistance. Once cured, the foam is inert and won’t leach. We also offer low‑GWP foam options upon request and follow PPE and SDS handling practices. Dust and silica are controlled with HEPA filtration while drilling. Ask us for product data sheets if your HOA requires documentation.
