Concrete Leveling & Lifting Services Built for Southern Utah Conditions

We focus on long‑lasting concrete lifting that restores safety and curb appeal fast. Our technicians use high‑density polyurethane foam, laser controls, and proven lift plans tailored to Ivins’ soils.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

Sunken concrete creates trips, pooled water, and liability. We drill 5/8 in. ports with an SDS rotary hammer and inject high‑density closed‑cell polyurethane (HMI/Prime Resins) in controlled stages. The foam expands to fill voids, densify base soils, and lift slabs within millimeters of target height - often ready for use in 15-30 minutes. Smaller holes and lighter weight mean less disruption than mudjacking. We finish with color‑matched patching and clean edges for a near‑invisible repair. Learn more about our Polyurethane Foam Injection services.

Driveway Leveling

Settlement at garage entries or mid‑slab cracks can scrape car bumpers and channel water toward foundations. We map injection ports, underseal voids, and perform staged lifts while monitoring with laser levels and dial indicators. Hydrophobic foam options handle moisture; joint resealing and a silane/siloxane surface sealer help keep water out. You’ll regain a smooth transition you can drive on the same day. Learn more about our Driveway Leveling services.

Sidewalk Leveling

Trip hazards on city walks and HOA paths increase risk. We correct vertical offsets to align with ADA thresholds (≤1/4 in. flush; 1/4-1/2 in. beveled) and coordinate right‑of‑way needs as applicable. Our crew follows MUTCD‑style traffic control when near streets and marks utilities via Blue Stakes 811 before drilling. Where heave prevents lifting, we can blend with light finish grinding. Learn more about our Sidewalk Leveling services.

Pool Deck Leveling

Voids around pool plumbing and washout from irrigation lead to sinking slabs and toe‑stubbing joints. We use low‑pressure polyurethane injection to stabilize soils and raise panels evenly around coping, safeguarding lines and skimmers. Finishing includes crack chase and seal, with UV‑resistant joint sealants for clean, long‑term performance in Ivins’ sun. Learn more about our Pool Deck Leveling services.

Concrete Void Filling

Desert soils and drainage shifts often create hidden cavities. We perform targeted undersealing and Deep Foamjection (deep injection) to densify weak zones beneath slabs, driveways, and porches. GPR scanning is available when unknowns exist. After stabilization, we seal control joints with backer rod and polyurethane to lock out water and help prevent re‑settlement. Learn more about our Concrete Void Filling services.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my concrete needs lifting?
Look for pooling water, trip edges over 1/2 inch, gaps under slabs, doors rubbing, or sunken garage entries. In Ivins, erosion and irrigation can wash out base soils, and desert shrink-swell cycles open joints. We’ll measure with a laser, check voids, and recommend lifting, grinding, or replacement if movement is excessive.
How long does polyurethane lifting take and when can I use the slab?
Most residential projects finish in a few hours, and the foam cures fast. Typical walk‑on time is immediate, with drive‑on often within 15-60 minutes depending on temperature and load. We’ll stage lifts, verify elevations with digital levels, and reopen areas the same day in most Ivins jobs.
Is polyurethane foam safe and environmentally responsible?
We use high‑density closed‑cell polyurethane designed for concrete lifting. Once cured, it’s inert, water‑resistant, and does not leach. Hydrophobic blends are available for wet soils. We also offer renewable‑content options upon request. The lightweight foam reduces added soil load compared to cement grout.
How much does concrete leveling cost near Ivins, UT?
Pricing depends on slab size, void volume, access, and whether deep injection is needed. Small sidewalk panels may be a few hundred dollars, while multi‑panel driveways can range higher. We provide a written, line‑item estimate on site so you know exactly what’s included, including joint sealing and cleanup.
Is polyjacking better than mudjacking?
Polyjacking uses smaller holes, lighter material, and faster cure times than mudjacking, which can be beneficial in Ivins’ red soils. That said, each method has a place. We’ll compare both, explain pros and cons for your slab and budget, and recommend the approach that best meets your goals.
Do you handle permits for sidewalk leveling and ADA trip hazards in Ivins?
Yes. For right‑of‑way work, we coordinate with Ivins City for required permits and traffic control. We also mark utilities via Blue Stakes 811 (3 business‑day notice). Our goal is to remove trip hazards and align with ADA thresholds while keeping your neighborhood safe during work.
Can you level post‑tension slabs or advise when it isn’t recommended?
We evaluate post‑tension layouts and use safe port placement to avoid tendons. In some cases, selective lifting or stabilization is appropriate; in others, we’ll recommend alternatives to protect structural integrity. If needed, we can perform GPR scanning to verify tendon paths before any drilling.
Will you seal joints, repair cracks, and improve drainage after lifting?
Yes. We include crack chase and seal, install backer rod with polyurethane joint sealant, and can apply silane/siloxane sealer. If water is contributing to settlement, we’ll propose drainage adjustments. These steps help protect against future washout and extend the life of your lift.
How long does foam jacking last in Southern Utah?
With proper installation and drainage, polyurethane lifting can last for decades. The foam is closed‑cell and resistant to moisture. Long‑term performance depends on soil conditions and water management, so we stabilize voids, seal joints, and provide maintenance tips to preserve your investment.
Do you work in Kayenta and the Tuacahn/Snow Canyon area, including colored or stamped slabs?
Absolutely. We routinely service Kayenta and the Snow Canyon/Tuacahn corridor. Our crew protects decorative finishes with careful port layout, color‑matched patching, and finish grinding when needed. We’ll provide a mock‑up location and blend patches to keep your architectural concrete looking consistent.
