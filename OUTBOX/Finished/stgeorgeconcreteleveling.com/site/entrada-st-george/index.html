Polyurethane Foam Injection

Sunken slabs create trip hazards and drainage issues around patios, walkways, and pool decks. We lift concrete with high‑density, closed‑cell polyurethane foam (polyjacking) injected through 1/2-5/8 in. drill holes using a proportioner, heated hoses, and an injection gun. Foam expands to fill voids, stabilize soils, and precisely raise panels with a laser level. It’s lighter than mudjacking slurry, resists water, and is walkable within 15-30 minutes. Clean holes are patched to blend. Learn more about our Polyurethane Foam Injection services.

Driveway Leveling

Uneven driveway slabs can tilt toward the garage, cause puddles, and crack tires at the joints. We lift and re‑slope panels to correct drainage at the garage lip using staged injection and lift sequencing. After raising, we can seal expansion joints with polyurea and backer rod to keep water out. Expect a neat, fast process with HEPA dust control and a driveable surface typically within 45-60 minutes. Learn more about our Driveway Leveling services.

Sidewalk Leveling

Trip hazards near Snow Canyon State Park paths or HOA walkways are a liability. We lift sidewalks to meet ADA guidance for changes in level and reduce bevels where needed. Our crews coordinate Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before drilling and use compact tools to protect landscaping. The result is a safer, smoother path that looks great and helps with compliance. Learn more about our Sidewalk Leveling services.

Pool Deck Leveling

Settled pool decks create dangerous lips at coping lines and collect water. We use hydrophobic polyurethane foam to resist moisture around pools and hot tubs, filling voids and lifting slabs without removing tile or coping. Laser‑guided raising, tight injection spacing, and careful cure monitoring help protect finishes. Perfect for Entrada community pools and backyard decks. Learn more about our Pool Deck Leveling services.

Garage Floor Leveling

Garage slabs often settle at control joints or along exterior walls, creating tire dips and door binding. We perform interior lifting with low‑odor foam, HEPA dust extraction, and moisture/cure monitoring. After leveling, we can route and fill cracks with epoxy and reseal joints to prevent water intrusion. Our method corrects slope and restores a smooth, functional surface fast. Common questions

01How much does concrete leveling cost in St. George, Utah?

Pricing depends on slab size, lift height, access, and whether soils need stabilization. Most residential work in the 84770 area ranges from $6-$15 per square foot, or $300-$900 per panel. Polyurethane foam is typically more per square foot than mudjacking but lasts longer and is lighter on soils. We provide a written, line‑item quote after measuring, checking voids, and reviewing drainage. On‑site estimates are free in St. George and nearby Entrada.

02Is foam jacking better than mudjacking?

For many projects, yes. Polyurethane foam jacking uses high‑density, closed‑cell foam that expands to fill voids, is water‑resistant, and adds minimal weight to weak soils. Holes are smaller (about 1/2-5/8 in.), and surfaces are often usable within an hour. Mudjacking with cementitious grout can be effective for large, deep voids but adds more weight and requires larger holes. We’ll recommend the best method after inspection.

03How long does polyurethane concrete leveling last?

Longevity depends on drainage, irrigation, and soil conditions. Where soils are collapsible or eroded, we may add deep foam injection to stabilize subgrade for a longer‑term repair. We also seal joints to limit water infiltration that can undermine slabs in St. George’s desert climate.

04How long does polyurethane lifting take and when can I use the surface?

Most small projects in Entrada finish in a few hours. Foam cures quickly - walkways are usually walkable within 15-30 minutes and driveways drivable in 45-60 minutes. We monitor cure and temperature with an IR thermometer and confirm elevations with a laser level before cleanup. We’ll give you exact return‑to‑service times at completion.

05How big are the drill holes for foam jacking?

We typically drill 1/2-5/8 in. holes with a hammer or core drill and dust‑extraction shrouds connected to a HEPA vacuum for silica control. Injection ports (“pucks”) are set for a tight seal. After lifting, we patch holes with a color‑matched mortar and finish to blend with your slab’s texture as closely as possible.

06Will concrete leveling fix drainage toward my garage?

In many cases, yes. We can re‑establish positive slope (often 1-2%) away from the garage by lifting settled panels and adjusting the driveway‑to‑garage lip. If needed, we add joint resealing to keep water from migrating under the slab. During your estimate, we’ll laser‑check elevations and propose a lift plan that addresses drainage and trip hazards together.

07Do you handle Blue Stakes of Utah 811 utility locates and marking before drilling in Entrada?

Yes. We coordinate Blue Stakes 811 at least 3 business days before work. Markings are generally valid up to 21 days. This helps avoid damage to irrigation lines, landscape lighting, and shallow utilities often present along driveways and sidewalks in Entrada. We also ask you to flag private irrigation or low‑voltage lines not covered by 811.

08Can you level pool decks and patios in HOA communities like Entrada?

Absolutely. We regularly level pool decks and patios for Entrada HOAs and homeowners using hydrophobic polyurethane foam that performs well around water. We provide ARC‑friendly documentation, product data sheets, and proof of insurance. Work areas are protected, foam is injected through small holes, and most decks are back in service the same day.

09What ADA trip‑hazard limits apply to sidewalks in our neighborhood and how does leveling help meet them?

The 2010 ADA Standards Section 303 state that changes in level up to 1/4 in. are permitted; 1/4-1/2 in. must be beveled at 1:2 slope; greater than 1/2 in. requires a ramp. Our sidewalk leveling reduces vertical displacement and, when needed, we add minor beveling to help meet ADA guidance - improving safety and reducing liability for HOAs in St. George.

10Can deep foam injection stabilize collapsible or eroded soils common in Washington County?

Yes. Using soil injection rods, we place expanding foam 3-10 ft below the slab to densify loose soils and bridge voids. This can stop settlement where irrigation or stormwater has undermined support. Deep foam injection is often paired with slab lifting and joint sealing to manage water and keep panels stable long‑term.

11Do you offer warranties on leveling work, and are they transferable?

Warranties are transferable to the next homeowner - great for resale value. Exclusions may apply for new washouts, uncontrolled drainage, or structural movement. Your estimator will review exact terms in writing.

12Will you seal joints and cracks after lifting?

Yes, upon request. After leveling, we can route and fill cracks with epoxy and reseal joints with polyurea over backer rod. This helps block water, reduce freeze‑thaw damage, and extend the life of your lift. It’s a smart add‑on for driveways, sidewalks, and pool decks throughout St. George.
