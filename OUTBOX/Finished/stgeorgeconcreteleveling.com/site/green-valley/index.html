Polyurethane Foam Injection

Sunken concrete doesn’t just look bad - it’s unsafe and can channel water toward your home. Our polyurethane foam injection uses 5/8-inch holes and a two-part high-density foam to fill voids and lift slabs in a controlled, staged process. We monitor lift with a rotary laser to within fractions of an inch. Foam is lightweight, moisture-resistant, and cures in minutes, minimizing downtime. We’re trained on HMI/Alchemy-Spetec systems and follow OSHA silica controls for safe, clean work.

Sidewalk Leveling

Trip lips along sidewalks can violate ADA thresholds and create liability. Our sidewalk leveling Green Valley 84770 service targets settled panels and hazardous edges, lifting low sections and stabilizing subgrade. We use grid-pattern injections and low-pressure lifts near edges and utilities, then seal cracks and joints to prevent washout. When leveling isn’t feasible, we can lightly grind minor lips to meet ADA 2010 §303. Count on a clean, cordoned jobsite and same-day readiness for foot traffic.

Driveway Leveling

Common questions

01Why choose polyurethane concrete lifting over replacement?

Polyurethane lifting is faster, cleaner, and typically 40-70% less than tear-out and repour. We drill 5/8-inch holes, inject high-density foam to fill voids and lift, then patch and seal. Most areas reopen in under an hour. It also adds minimal weight, so soils are less likely to resettle. For residents searching for Concrete Leveling Green Valley UT, foam jacking offers a durable fix without disrupting landscaping or utilities.

02How does the drill-pump-patch process work?

We map the slab, mark utilities (Blue Stakes 811), and identify voids. Using a laser level, we drill 5/8-inch ports, inject foam in a grid pattern to fill voids first, then stage the lift from low to high. Pressure is monitored throughout. Once level, we plug ports, patch with color-matched material, and seal joints or cracks. The area is washed down and barricades removed after cure.

03What types of slabs can you level?

We level sidewalks, driveways, patios, porches, steps, garage and interior slabs, pool decks, approaches, and commercial/municipal walks. We also service HOA common areas and warehouse floors. For delicate areas near foundations, we use low-pressure injections and soil densification to protect footings. If a minor lip remains, we can grind to meet ADA §303 limits and document compliance for your records.

04How long before I can walk or drive on the slab?

Polyurethane foam cures quickly. Most sidewalks and patios are ready for foot traffic in 15-30 minutes. Driveways and garage slabs typically support vehicles in 30-60 minutes, depending on temperature and load. We confirm cure and provide guidance before reopening. Commercial or heavy loads may require a longer wait, which we’ll outline during the estimate.

05How much does concrete leveling cost in St. George?

Costs vary by size, access, and how far a slab must lift. Typical residential projects in Washington County range from $450-$1,400 per slab or $6-$18 per sq ft. Foam’s speed and minimal demolition help keep total project costs down versus replacement. We provide a free on-site estimate with laser measurements, photos, and a clear scope so you can compare options confidently.

06Mudjacking vs. polyurethane: which lasts longer?

Mudjacking uses a heavy cement-sand grout that can add weight and contribute to future settlement. Polyurethane is lightweight, water-resistant, and designed to resist washout. In our Green Valley climate, foam generally offers longer performance, especially near irrigation or the Santa Clara River corridor. We’ll explain when grout-based slab jacking still makes sense and when foam is the better investment.

07Do you offer same-day driveway leveling near Dixie Drive and Valley View Dr?

Yes. For many driveways, we can schedule quickly and complete lift and sealing the same day, with vehicle traffic allowed in about 30-60 minutes after cure. We confirm utility marks, plan port locations to protect expansion joints, and correct drainage back toward the street. Ask about morning appointments for afternoon parking availability.

08Will you coordinate Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before drilling?

Absolutely. We submit an 811 locate request at least 3 business days before drilling and verify marks are current (valid up to 21 days). We also use low-pressure injections near marked utilities and adjust port locations as needed. Your estimate will note any private lines not covered by 811 and options for private locating if required.

09Can you level pool decks and steps without damaging landscaping?

Yes. We use compact hoses, protective mats, and hydrophobic foam near wet soils. Ports are small and discreet, and we place them to avoid coping, tile, and irrigation lines. After lifting, we seal joints to keep chlorinated water from migrating under slabs. Most pool decks are ready for foot traffic within 20-30 minutes.

10Do you work with HOAs and property managers in Green Valley?

We do. We maintain trip-hazard remediation programs for HOA communities, including Sports Village/The Vue. Services include ADA sidewalk compliance audits, phased repairs, resident notifications, and after-action documentation with photos and measurements. We provide W-9/COI, adhere to community rules, and schedule to minimize disruption to residents and guests.
