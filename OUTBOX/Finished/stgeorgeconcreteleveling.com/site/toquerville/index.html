Polyurethane Foam Injection

Foam jacking solves sinking slabs caused by washout and poor compaction. We drill small 3/8"-5/8" holes with an SDS rotary hammer, then inject high-density polyurethane using a proportioner/reactor and injection gun. Staged lifts and a grid pattern ensure a controlled, crack-free raise. Benefits include minimal mess, rapid cure (often within 15 minutes), and strong undersealing. We also use hydrophobic foam for wet soils common near Ash Creek and SR‑17. Learn more about our Polyurethane Foam Injection services.

Driveway Leveling

Sunken or settled driveways create puddles, cracks, and tire-rubbing lips. We restore proper slope by lifting the slab and stabilizing weak soils with deep foam injection when needed. Tools like a ZipLevel elevation gauge and laser level guide precise lifts. Post-lift, we seal joints with polyurea or backer rod and self-leveling sealant to reduce water infiltration. Most driveways in Toquerville are ready for light use the same day. Learn more about our Driveway Leveling services.

Sidewalk Leveling

Sidewalk lips over 1/2" can violate ADA changes-in-level guidelines and pose real liability. We remove trip hazards by lifting panels and, if needed, surface grinding for minor lips. Our crew sets up safe work zones with cones and signage and adheres to OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1153 dust control using HEPA extraction. It’s a quick, affordable fix for neighborhoods near Hurricane and La Verkin. Learn more about our Sidewalk Leveling services.

Patio Leveling

Patios often settle at the house, sending water toward foundations. We re-slope slabs away from structures using controlled foam injection and undersealing to fill voids. For vacation rentals near the Zion gateway, we can level access paths with minimal downtime and a clean finish. Crack routing and sealing and non-shrink patching mortar complete the repair. Learn more about our Patio Leveling services.

Trip Hazard Removal

High spots, settled panels, and fractured corners create daily risks. We assess with a ZipLevel, lift where feasible, and grind minor transitions for a smooth, compliant surface. For busy storefronts and HOA walkways, we schedule fast-turn projects and offer maintenance plans for joint sealing to help prevent recurrence after heavy rains. Common questions

01How does polyurethane foam injection compare to mudjacking?

Polyurethane foam uses smaller holes, cures in minutes, and is lighter, reducing the chance of future settlement. Mudjacking (cementitious slurry) works but adds heavy material and larger ports. In Toquerville, we often recommend foam for precise lifts, deep soil densification, and minimal downtime. For certain large voids or non-structural fills, mudjacking can still be effective. We’ll explain pros and cons on-site and recommend the best method for your slab and budget.

02What types of concrete can you lift?

We level driveways, sidewalks, patios, porches/steps, garage floors, pool decks, RV pads, and many interior slabs. Commercial projects include storefront walks, ADA ramps, warehouse floors, loading docks, and curb and gutter. We also correct approach slabs at parking lots and stabilize edges. If a slab is severely broken or heaved by roots/frost, we’ll assess whether partial replacement plus leveling is the better path.

03Why does concrete sink in Southern Utah?

Common causes include subgrade washout from storms or irrigation, poor initial compaction, and occasional freeze-thaw. In parts of Washington County, water channels under slabs create voids that lead to settlement and tilting. Our solution combines void filling, staged lifting, and joint sealing to keep water out. Where soils are wet, we use hydrophobic foams and, if needed, deep foam injection to densify and stabilize the base.

04How long does concrete lifting take and when can I use the slab?

Most residential projects in Toquerville finish in 2-4 hours. With foam jacking, light foot traffic is typically allowed within minutes, and vehicles can often return the same day after cure checks. We confirm elevations with a ZipLevel and complete joint/crack sealing before cleanup. Larger commercial areas may require phased work to maintain access while we lift and stabilize.

05Is polyurethane foam safe and eco‑friendly?

The high‑density foam we use is an inert, closed-cell material once cured. It will not leach, absorbs minimal water, and is compatible with most soils and landscaping. Compared to replacement, foam lifting reduces demolition waste and trucking. We monitor temperatures with an infrared thermometer for proper reaction and follow spill-control protocols to protect your property and nearby waterways.

06How much does concrete leveling cost in Utah?

Pricing depends on slab size, lift height, access, and whether void filling or deep injection is needed. Many Toquerville driveways or sidewalks cost far less than replacement and finish the same day. We provide a written, line‑item estimate on site and can bundle joint sealing or crack repair. Ask about our free estimate for 84774 and surrounding zip codes.

07Do you level RV pads and re‑slope them for drainage?

Yes. We regularly lift and re‑slope RV pads to move water away from the home or outbuildings. Using staged foam injections, we correct pitch and stabilize edges that often settle under heavy loads. We also seal joints to reduce future washout. It’s a fast, clean solution that minimizes downtime for frequent travelers and short‑term rental hosts.

08Do I need to call Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before a leveling project?

We handle utility locates for our drilling as part of scheduling. Blue Stakes of Utah 811 is the state’s utility notification service and helps prevent damage to buried lines. If you’re planning other excavation on your property, call 811 at least 48 hours in advance. For our foam injection work, we’ll coordinate locates and mark-outs as needed.

09Do you offer same‑week service in Toquerville and nearby areas?

In most cases, yes. We keep crews working across Washington County, including Hurricane, La Verkin, Virgin, and Leeds. If you’re near the Zion gateway or along SR‑17, we can often assess and complete smaller projects within the same week - sometimes sooner. Request a free estimate and we’ll confirm availability based on scope and weather.

10How big are the drill holes and will they be noticeable?

Foam injection uses small 3/8"-5/8" holes placed in a grid to lift evenly. After lifting, we patch them with color‑matched, non‑shrink repair mortar. Most homeowners find the holes hard to spot after a short weathering period. We prioritize neat layouts along joints where possible to blend repairs and maintain a clean finish.
