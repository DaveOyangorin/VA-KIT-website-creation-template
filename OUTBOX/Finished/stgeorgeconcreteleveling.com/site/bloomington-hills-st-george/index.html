Polyurethane Foam Injection

Void pockets and weak soils allow slabs to sink and crack. We inject high‑density, closed‑cell polyurethane (approx. 2-4 pcf) through 3/8-5/8” ports using a heated poly injection rig and precision lift sequencing. Foam expands to fill voids, densify soils, and raise the slab within millimeters of target grade. You can usually drive or walk on the surface in 15-30 minutes. It’s clean, lightweight, and ideal for driveways, sidewalks, patios, and interior floors. Learn more about our Polyurethane Foam Injection services.

Driveway Leveling

Uneven driveway panels create trip hazards, tire ruts, and pooling that worsens cracks and spalling. We use laser/digital levels, small injection ports, and edge‑lifting techniques to bring slabs back to proper slope and elevation - often the same day. Hydrophobic foam is available for wet subgrades or irrigation leaks. Results: safer entries, better drainage, and smoother transitions at the garage apron. It’s faster and more affordable than replacement, with less mess. Learn more about our Driveway Leveling services.

Sidewalk Leveling

Sunken sidewalk panels can violate ADA 2010 Standards and put HOAs and businesses at risk. We mitigate trip hazards by lifting slabs, undersealing, and sealing joints to slow future movement. Our crew follows OSHA silica controls when drilling/grinding and sets precise bevels where required. We help communities document repairs for ADA compliance. Perfect for residential routes, HOA paths, and commercial entries. Learn more about our Sidewalk Leveling services.

Pool Deck Leveling

Settled pool decks create toe‑stubs and coping gaps that collect water and damage finishes. We perform low‑impact foam jacking around pools with tight grout lines, using injection packers, temperature‑controlled hoses, and staged lifts to protect tile and coping. We can add crack chasing and polyurea joint sealing to keep chloride‑rich splash water out. It’s a clean, quick fix that helps restore safe, even walking surfaces. Learn more about our Pool Deck Leveling services.

Garage Floor Leveling

Garage slabs often settle near the entry, causing door binding, puddling, and a harsh bump when parking. We stabilize soils, fill voids, and correct slope to move water toward the driveway - not into the garage. Using Deep Foam injection (as needed) for densification and laser‑guided lifts, we deliver tight tolerances in active spaces. Most jobs are completed in hours with minimal dust and cleanup. Learn more about our Garage Floor Leveling services.

Common questions

01How do I know if my concrete needs lifting?

Look for uneven panels, puddling near joints, garage door rub, step-ups over 1/2 inch, or gaps under the slab. Cracks that widen on one side and settled edges along landscaping are also clues. In Bloomington Hills, irrigation leaks and monsoon washouts often create voids. We use laser measurements, percussion sounding, and probe holes to confirm subsurface loss and recommend lifting, undersealing, or spot replacement if needed.

02How long does polyurethane lifting take and when can I use the surface?

Most projects take a few hours, and the area is ready within 15-30 minutes after injection. The foam cures rapidly, so you can typically walk immediately and drive on a driveway the same day. We’ll stage the lift, verify elevation with a digital level, seal the ports, and clean up so your space is back in service fast - even for busy garages and commercial entries in St. George.

03Is polyurethane foam safe and environmentally inert?

Yes. Once cured, our high‑density, closed‑cell foam is chemically inert and does not leach. It’s lightweight, which reduces added soil stress, and it resists water absorption. For wet subgrades, we can use hydrophobic formulations that react cleanly in the presence of moisture. We dispose of drillings properly and follow OSHA/industry best practices for on‑site handling and ventilation during installation.

04What’s the difference between foam jacking and mudjacking?

Foam jacking uses lightweight polyurethane injected through 3/8-5/8” holes; it expands to fill voids and lift with precision. Mudjacking pumps a cement/limestone slurry through larger ports. Foam is lighter, cleaner, and cures faster - ideal for driveways, sidewalks, and interiors. Mudjacking can be cost‑effective on heavier, thick slabs. We’ll recommend the best method after inspecting soil, load, access, and budget in Bloomington Hills.

05How much does it cost to level a concrete driveway in St. George?

Costs vary by size, drop, access, and void volume. In St. George, many driveway lifts fall between a project minimum and per‑square‑foot pricing. Typical ranges run roughly $8-$18 per sq ft with $650-$1,200 minimums, but complex voids or apron transitions can differ. We provide a written estimate after measurement, including any crack sealing or joint resealing to finish the job right.

06Will concrete leveling fix drainage and water pooling?

Often, yes. Restoring slope and panel alignment moves water away from the house, garage, and joints. We check finished elevations with a laser and target positive drainage to reduce pooling. Where grades or landscaping limit flow, we may suggest joint sealing, downspout extensions, or spot drains. Correcting drainage helps prevent freeze‑thaw damage and further settlement in Bloomington Hills.

07Can you level concrete around my pool without removing coping or decking?

Yes. We use low‑impact foam injections with small ports, staged lifts, and careful monitoring to protect tile lines and coping. Around pools in 84790, we often add crack chasing and polyurea joint sealing to keep chlorinated water out and preserve the lift. Most decks reopen the same day, with minimal dust and no demolition.

08Will foam injection stain or damage stamped/colored concrete in Bloomington Hills?

No. Ports are small and drilled in discrete locations; we match color for patching where possible. The foam remains under the slab and won’t stain stamped or integrally colored surfaces. We use clean containment, HEPA vacs, and careful rinsing to protect decorative finishes common in Bloomington Hills patios and pool decks.

09Do Bloomington Hills HOAs allow sidewalk drilling for trip hazard repairs, and how do permits/utility locates work?

Most HOAs welcome ADA trip‑hazard mitigation when coordinated in advance. We help with documentation and set repairs to align with ADA 2010 Standards (e.g., bevels for 1/4-1/2 inch changes). Before drilling or injecting, we contact Blue Stakes of Utah 811 to mark utilities - typically three business days prior. If city permits are required for sidewalk sections, we’ll assist with submittals.

10Can you level concrete in winter in Southern Utah?

Yes - many lifts are feasible in winter. Polyurethane cures quickly, and we use heated hoses and material controls to maintain proper reaction in cooler temps. We avoid saturated or frozen subgrades and schedule around storms.
