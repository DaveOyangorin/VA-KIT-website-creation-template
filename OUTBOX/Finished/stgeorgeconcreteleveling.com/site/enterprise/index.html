Concrete Lifting & Leveling Services We Specialize In

We focus on proven slab jacking methods that correct settlement, eliminate trip hazards, and improve drainage. Our technicians use high-density polyurethane and precision tools to deliver accurate, durable lifts for homes and businesses.

Polyurethane Foam Injection (Polyjacking)

Sunken concrete creates safety risks and invites water toward your foundation. We inject high-density polyurethane foam using a heated poly rig and high-pressure pumps through 5/8"-5/8+ ports. Foam expands to lift, fill voids, and stabilize weak subgrades. We monitor with a laser level to 1/16" and lift in small increments to prevent cracking. Benefits include minimal holes, fast cure (often traffic-ready in 30-60 minutes), and long service life. OEM-trained techs (Foamjection/HMI) ensure consistent results. Learn more about our Polyurethane Foam Injection services.

Concrete Slab Lifting

Slabs settle from poor compaction, erosion, or freeze-thaw cycles. Our slab jacking blends precise drilling, grid-pattern injection, and void mapping to restore elevations and slope. We correct ponding, align joints, and reduce structural stresses. Materials selected per site conditions - from standard polyjacking to deep foam injection for subgrade reinforcement. Post-lift, we patch flush and color blend. This service extends the life of patios, shop floors, and interior slabs at a fraction of replacement cost. Learn more about our Concrete Slab Lifting services.

Driveway Leveling

Tilting driveway panels can damage vehicles and funnel water toward garages. We lift settled panels, stabilize edges, and restore proper pitch using targeted edge lifting and foam stabilization around utility penetrations. Our process limits dust with OSHA-compliant vacuums and silica controls. Most Enterprise driveways are ready for light use the same day. We can also seal joints with polyurea for added longevity and water control. Learn more about our Driveway Leveling services.

Sidewalk Leveling & Trip Hazard Repair

Sidewalk lips ≥1/4" create liability. We correct panels to meet ADA Section 303 with lifting, or use concrete grinding for minor bevels when lifting isn’t warranted. We provide optional before/after measurements and photos for HOA or municipal documentation. Work zones are barricaded for public safety. Our team services residential walks, parks, and campuses across the SR-18 corridor. Learn more about our Sidewalk Leveling and Trip Hazard Removal services.

Garage Floor Leveling

Low garage slabs cause puddling and door clearance issues. We use laser verification, deep foam where soils are soft, and post-lift joint sealing to control future moisture. The result is a flatter, stronger surface that’s ready for vehicles fast. We can also recommend silane/siloxane sealers to reduce freeze-thaw damage in Enterprise’s colder months. Learn more about our Garage Floor Leveling services.

Common questions

01What is concrete lifting and how does it work?

We drill small 5/8"-1" holes, install injection ports, and pump expanding material beneath the slab to raise it and fill voids. Most Enterprise projects use high-density polyurethane foam for precise lifts and fast cure. We monitor with a laser level to 1/16", lift in small increments to protect the slab, then patch holes flush and seal joints where needed.

02Polyjacking vs. mudjacking: which is better for Southern Utah?

Polyjacking uses lightweight, water-resistant foam and smaller holes, cures in 30-60 minutes, and adds minimal load to weak soils - great for Enterprise’s freeze‑thaw cycles. Mudjacking uses a cement-sand slurry, larger holes, and longer cure times. We offer both, explain pros/cons, and recommend the best option based on soil conditions, access, budget, and long-term performance.

03How much does concrete leveling cost in Washington County?

Typical residential leveling ranges from $8-$18 per sq. ft., with small trip-hazard panels starting around $350-$650. Factors include void size, slab thickness, access, and whether deep foam injection is required. We provide written, line-item estimates and can bundle crack routing, polyurea joint sealing, and water-repellent sealing to protect your investment.

04How long do polyurethane leveling repairs last?

Longevity depends on drainage and subgrade stability. We reduce future movement by stabilizing weak soils, correcting pitch, and sealing joints to keep water out.

05How soon can I use my driveway after lifting?

With polyurethane, most driveways and garage slabs are ready for foot traffic in minutes and vehicle traffic in about 30-60 minutes, depending on slab thickness and temperature. Mudjacking requires longer cure times (often 24-48 hours). We’ll advise exact re‑use timelines on site.

06Do you provide ADA measurement reports before and after sidewalk leveling?

Yes. On request, we document sidewalk lips and bevels to ADA 2010 Section 303 (≤1/4" vertical; 1/4"-1/2" beveled 1:2; >1/2" ramp). We record pre/post measurements, photos, and panel locations - ideal for HOAs, property managers, and municipal projects seeking audit-friendly records.

07What are Blue Stakes of Utah 811 requirements for concrete leveling in Enterprise?

When drilling or injecting, we contact Blue Stakes of Utah 811 at least 3 business days in advance to locate utilities. We also visually verify irrigation lines and private utilities where possible. Work proceeds only after clearances and safe work zones are established with barricades and signage.

08What is Deep Foam Injection and when is it needed?

Deep Foam Injection drives structural resin 3-15 feet to densify weak soils and bridge larger voids. We use it when panels repeatedly settle, soils are poorly compacted, or there’s evidence of washout. It’s common along the SR‑18 corridor where subgrades vary. The process reduces future movement before we fine‑tune the slab elevation.

09Will concrete leveling fix drainage toward my home?

Usually, yes. We lift strategically to re‑establish slope away from foundations, then verify with a laser and water tests. If grade outside the slab causes ponding, we’ll recommend complementary fixes like downspout extensions, minor regrading, or joint sealing to prevent infiltration and freeze‑thaw damage in Enterprise.

10Is crack and joint sealing included after leveling?

We can bundle crack routing and sealing with epoxy or polyurea, plus expansion joint re‑caulking with backer rod as needed. Sealing is highly recommended in Washington County to keep water out of the subgrade. Your estimate will itemize these protective options so you can choose what’s best for your slab.
