Garage Floor Leveling

Common questions

01How much does concrete leveling cost in Washington County?

Pricing depends on slab size, lift height, access, and soil conditions. In Southern Utah, many projects range from $8-$18 per square foot, or $600-$2,500 for small sections. Deep Foamjection or complex void fill can cost more. We provide a written quote after inspection that details injection points, estimated lift, and any add‑ons like crack routing or joint sealing.

02What’s better in Southern Utah - polyjacking or mudjacking?

Polyjacking uses lightweight, high‑density polyurethane that expands and stabilizes with smaller holes and faster cure. Mudjacking uses a cementitious slurry that adds weight and requires larger holes. In Rockville’s variable soils and flood‑prone zones, foam often performs better - especially hydrophobic formulations near moisture. We still offer slurry/pressure grouting when appropriate. After evaluation, we’ll recommend the method with the best long‑term performance for your slab.

03How long does foam jacking last in hot desert climates?

Properly installed geotechnical polyurethane is inert, closed‑cell, and not affected by heat the way soils are. In our climate, lifts routinely last many years - provided drainage and joints are managed. We often add polyurea joint sealant to block water infiltration, the leading cause of repeat settlement.

04Can you level a sunken driveway or garage floor in one day?

Yes. Most driveways and many garage slabs are completed in a single visit. Our foam cures in minutes, allowing light use shortly after completion and normal use typically the same day. Complex lifts, extensive crack repair, or deep voids may add time. We’ll confirm timelines during your on‑site assessment and keep your access needs in mind.

05Will leveling fix cracks, or do I still need crack repair?

Leveling addresses the underlying settlement and restores alignment, but existing cracks usually remain visible. We can route and fill cracks with polyurethane or epoxy resins and reseal joints to prevent water from re‑entering the base. This combination - leveling plus crack/joint repair - delivers the best durability and appearance for Rockville slabs.

06How soon can I drive on a leveled driveway?

With polyurethane injection, driveways are typically ready for vehicle traffic the same day - often within a few hours of completion. We’ll advise based on temperature, slab thickness, and lift magnitude. For projects using cementitious slurry, cure times are longer. We’ll mark safe re‑entry times before leaving the site.

07Is concrete leveling safe near utilities - do I need to call 811?

It’s safe when done correctly. We notify Blue Stakes of Utah 811 at least 48-72 business hours before drilling. Utilities are marked and verified on site, and injection locations are planned accordingly. Near SR‑9 or older neighborhoods, we take extra care with depth and hole placement. You don’t need to call - our team handles the 811 process.

08Do I need a permit to level public‑sidewalk panels along SR‑9 in Rockville?

Public‑right‑of‑way work may require coordination with Washington County or the municipality. We help with permit requirements, traffic‑safe staging, and ADA compliance (e.g., correcting >1/4″ vertical changes, managing bevels and slopes). For private property panels set back from the ROW, permits are typically not required. We’ll confirm during your site review.

09Can you stabilize slabs undermined by flooding or irrigation leaks in Rockville?

Yes. We use void filling, soil densification, and hydrophobic foams for wet soils along the Virgin River corridor. For severe washout, Deep Foamjection can consolidate weak subgrades at depth. We’ll also recommend drainage and joint‑sealing measures to prevent repeat undermining after storms or line repairs.
