Concrete Lifting & Leveling Services You Can Trust

We specialize in concrete lifting and leveling that lasts, using high-density foam and proven slab jacking techniques. Our crew focuses on safety, precision, and clean finishes that blend with your existing surfaces.

Driveway Leveling

Ruts, settled approaches, and uneven panels can damage tires and cause pooling. We lift sunken driveways with high-density closed-cell polyurethane foam (2-6 lb/ft³) using a proportioner like a Graco Reactor, laser levels, and staged injections for controlled elevation. Typical holes are 3/8-5/8 in. and are patched with non-shrink mortar to blend. You get a smoother approach, improved drainage, and same-day return to light vehicle traffic in most cases. Learn more about our Driveway Leveling services.

Sidewalk Leveling

Raised lips and dropped sections create trip hazards - especially along ADA routes to entries and mailboxes. We correct sidewalk panels to within ADA thresholds (≤1/4 in. change without bevel) by lifting settled sections or micro-grinding minor edges. Using rotary hammer drilling, injection ports, and laser control, we achieve precise lifts with minimal disruption in Bloomington Hills and throughout 84790. Joint sealing helps keep water out and prevents re-settlement. Learn more about our Sidewalk Leveling services.

Pool Deck Leveling

Sunken pool deck slabs and open joints are a common issue in St. George’s heat and irrigation cycles. We lift and underseal pool deck panels, then finish with polyurethane or silicone joint sealant and backer rod for watertight seams. Hydrophobic foams are available for wet soils. Expect fast cure, clean work areas, and UV-stable sealing options to extend deck life and reduce trip hazards - perfect for homes near SunRiver and the St. George golf corridor. Learn more about our Pool Deck Leveling services.

Garage Floor Leveling

Settled garage slabs lead to door gaps, puddles, and uneven tool benches. We perform interior slab lifting with precision dial indicators and string lines to restore pitch and alignment. Deep foam injection can densify weak subgrades before final lift. After leveling, we can route and fill cracks with polyurea/epoxy and recommend sealing for durability. Most garages are walkable in 15-30 minutes and drivable within 1-2 hours, temperature dependent. Learn more about our Garage Floor Leveling services.

We inject lightweight, high-strength foam to fill cavities and underseal concrete, stabilizing soil and preventing further settlement. Common questions

01How do I know if my concrete at Bloomington Country Club needs leveling?

Look for trip edges over 1/4 inch, pooling water after irrigation or rain, gaps under slabs, garage doors that won’t seal, or rocking sidewalk panels. Cart paths that thump under golf carts or pool decks with separated joints are also signs. We’ll assess with laser levels and soil probes, then recommend foam jacking, mudjacking, or void filling to correct settlement in 84790.

02How long does polyurethane foam concrete lifting take?

Most residential panels (driveway, sidewalk, porch) are lifted in 1-3 hours. Larger projects - like pool decks or multiple driveway bays - can take half a day. Polyurethane foam cures fast; you can usually walk on it in 15-30 minutes and drive in 1-2 hours, depending on slab thickness and temperature.

03How soon can I walk or drive on a lifted slab?

Foot traffic is typically allowed within 15-30 minutes. For vehicles, plan on 1-2 hours for standard lifts after foam jacking. Heavier loads (RVs, delivery trucks) may require additional time. We’ll confirm readiness before we leave and provide simple aftercare tips to protect the lift.

04What’s the difference between mudjacking and foam jacking?

Mudjacking uses a cement-sand slurry; it’s effective but requires larger holes and adds more weight. Foam jacking uses lightweight, high-density polyurethane injected through 3/8-5/8 in. holes, reducing added load on weak soils. Foam expands to fill voids, resists water, and cures quickly. We recommend the best option after an on-site evaluation in St. George.

05Do I need HOA approval for concrete leveling?

Most HOAs appreciate leveling because it removes trip hazards without replacing concrete. Some require notification for visible work. We provide a clear scope, insurance documentation, and before/after photos for the Architectural Committee. We also coordinate scheduling to minimize disruption in Bloomington Country Club.

06Can you level pool decks and steps around the clubhouse?

Yes. We routinely lift pool deck panels, steps, and adjoining walks using hydrophobic foam where moisture is present. After lifting, we seal joints with polyurethane or silicone and backer rod to prevent washouts. Work areas are contained, clean, and typically reopened the same day - ideal for amenity spaces.

07Will leveling fix drainage and water pooling near my slab?

Raising settled panels often restores the original pitch so water drains away from your home or walkway. We can fine-tune elevations with laser control and recommend sealing or minor grading to prevent future pooling. For severe runoff issues, we may suggest complementary drainage improvements.

08How much does concrete leveling cost in St. George?

Pricing depends on slab size, lift height, access, and void conditions. Sidewalk panels may start in the low hundreds; multi-panel driveways or pool decks can range higher. After a free on-site evaluation in 84790, we provide a fixed quote and timeline - often more cost-effective than replacement.

09Will the drill holes be noticeable after patching?

We drill small holes (typically 3/8-5/8 in.), inject foam, then patch with non-shrink mortar color-matched as closely as practical. On broom-finished or textured surfaces, patches blend well over time. We keep hole placement to edges or joint lines where possible for a cleaner look.

Yes. We schedule early-morning or phased work, lifting panels in sections so access can resume quickly. Foam cures fast, allowing cart or foot traffic typically within 1-2 hours. We set cones and signage, coordinate with staff, and provide photos for records when work is complete.
