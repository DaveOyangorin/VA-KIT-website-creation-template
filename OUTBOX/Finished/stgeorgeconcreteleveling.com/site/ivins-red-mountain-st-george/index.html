Core Concrete Leveling & Repair Services

We specialize in lifting, stabilizing, and sealing settled concrete for homes, HOAs, and businesses.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

When soils sink or wash out, slabs drop and crack. We drill 5/8" ports and inject high‑density, closed‑cell structural polyurethane foam using a heated proportioner and injection gun. Quarter‑point lift sequencing and laser/zip‑level checks prevent over‑lift and new cracks. This lightweight foam won’t add soil weight like cement grout and cures fast - often traffic‑ready in 15-30 minutes. Great for residential driveways, porches, and interior floors in Ivins and St. George.

Driveway Leveling

Uneven approaches and settled slabs create puddles and tire‑catching lips. We lift driveway panels back to grade, restore slope to shed water away from garages, and seal joints to block future washout. Using polyjacking, we correct 0.5"-3" settlement with minimal holes and a clean finish. Expect reduced trip hazards, improved drainage, and better curb appeal - often completed in a single visit. Ideal for long RV pads and approaches common in 84738 neighborhoods near Kayenta and Red Mountain.

Sidewalk Leveling

Sidewalk lips over 1/4" can violate ADA thresholds and cause injuries. We lift individual panels, blend transitions, and, when needed, perform light grinding to meet change‑in‑level guidance. Our silica‑safe drilling with HEPA dust collection keeps your property clean and neighbors happy. We also provide documentation for HOAs and property managers upon request. Great for Red Mountain Resort walkways, school/church campuses, and city‑frontage paths.

Pool Deck Leveling

Pool decks in the Snow Canyon heat can settle and separate from coping. We lift sections to close gaps, reduce trip hazards, and re‑establish proper drainage away from the pool. Our low‑exotherm foam formulations suit hot Southern Utah summers, and UV‑stable joint sealants keep edges protected. We finish with expansion joint caulking to help prevent water intrusion and voids near the shell.

Concrete Void Filling

Irrigation leaks, rodents, or storm runoff can create hidden voids that lead to sudden slab drops. We inject lightweight fill foam or cementitious grout (as appropriate) to underseal and stabilize the slab. Using thermal checks and probing, we confirm coverage and uniform support. Common questions

01Polyjacking vs. Mudjacking - Which Is Best for Ivins?

Polyjacking uses lightweight, closed‑cell foam that won’t add heavy mass to weak or collapsible soils common around Red Mountain. It cures fast, requires smaller holes, and resists water. Mudjacking uses a cement‑sand slurry; it can be effective but adds weight and needs larger ports (about 1‑1/2"). For most Ivins and St. George projects, polyurethane delivers superior long‑term performance and minimal disruption. We’ll recommend the right method after inspection.

02How Long Until I Can Use the Slab After Lifting?

With polyurethane foam injection, foot traffic is typically allowed in 15-30 minutes and vehicle traffic in about 1-2 hours, depending on slab thickness, temperature, and scope. For driveways in St. George/Ivins summer heat, cure times can be even faster using low‑exotherm formulations. We’ll confirm site‑specific reopen times before leaving, and provide care tips for the first 24 hours.
