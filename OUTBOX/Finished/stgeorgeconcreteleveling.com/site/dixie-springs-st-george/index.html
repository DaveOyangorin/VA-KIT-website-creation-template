Concrete Leveling & Slab Lifting Services Near Dixie Springs

We specialize in residential and commercial concrete lifting that restores safety, drainage, and curb appeal.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

Foam jacking solves settling slabs without demolition. We drill 3/8-5/8 in. injection ports, then use a heated-hose poly rig and proportioner to place high‑density polyurethane foam (2-6 lb) beneath your concrete. The expanding foam fills voids, densifies weak soils, and lifts the slab in small, controlled increments. It’s ideal for driveways, garages, and pool decks where quick return to service matters. We stock hydrophobic and hydrophilic formulations, plus cold‑weather foams for winter work. Learn more about our Polyurethane Foam Injection services.

Driveway Leveling

Sunken driveways create tire dips, standing water, and curb appeal issues - especially in the sandy, collapsible soils near Copper Rock and Sand Hollow. We map voids, plan lift points, and use laser levels for accuracy. Foam injection or mudjacking restores approach slabs and panels to the correct elevation, often in a single visit. We also recommend joint sealing to keep water out and reduce future settlement. See details on our Driveway Leveling service.

Sidewalk Leveling

Trip hazards on HOA walks, park pathways, and neighborhood sidewalks are a liability. We correct 1/2 in. to 2 in.+ height differences to meet ADA guidelines, using staged lifts that protect adjacent panels. Our crews set up jobsite traffic control, then lift, grind edges if needed, and finish with caulking to keep water from eroding the base. Need fast fixes near Dixie Springs Park or Copper Rock community paths? Explore our Sidewalk Leveling services.

Pool Deck Leveling

Pool decks near Sand Hollow area homes often settle due to washout and irrigation. We stabilize soils with deep foam injections where needed, then lift slabs flush to coping and drains to remove toe‑stubbing edges. Our small injection holes blend neatly, and most decks are ready for foot traffic the same day. We can also seal joints with polyurea for added protection. Learn more about Pool Deck Leveling near Sand Hollow.

Concrete Void Filling

Hollow sounds, slab rocking, and erosion at downspouts indicate voids. We use targeted foam or cement‑sand slurry to fill gaps under driveways, porches, and entry steps. Where soils are loose, we add deep injections for soil densification. This prevents future settlement and supports heavy loads at garage entries and loading points. Finish the repair with crack routing and sealing to keep water out. Read about our Concrete Void Filling options.
