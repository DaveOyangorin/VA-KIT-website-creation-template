Concrete Leveling & Lifting Services for Homes and Businesses

We specialize in concrete leveling and lifting that saves time and money compared to full replacement. Our team uses industry-proven materials, precision equipment, and safe work practices to stabilize soils, eliminate trip hazards, and relevel slabs for a smooth, lasting finish.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

When concrete settles or voids form under a slab, our high‑density polyurethane foam (approx. 2-4 lb/ft³) expands to lift and support it from below. We use a heated two‑component proportioner and slab lifting guns (e.g., MixMaster Pro/PX‑7) for precise, controlled lifts. Small 5/8 in injection holes, rapid cure, and minimal mess mean driveways and walks are often traffic‑ready within hours - even in colder months with heated lines. It’s ideal for polyjacking applications where speed and accuracy matter.

Driveway Leveling

Sunken or sloped driveways create puddling, cracking, and tire-rubbing transitions at the garage. We laser-level the slab, perform targeted polyurethane injection, and monitor lift with dial indicators to restore proper pitch and drainage. Where soils are weak, we add deep injection for soil densification and void filling. Results: smoother entries, reduced pooling, and longer pavement life - without tearing out concrete or blocking your driveway for days.

Sidewalk Leveling

Raised panels and gaps can breach ADA change‑in‑level limits (1/4 in vertical; 1/2 in beveled) and create liability. We relevel panels with foam jacking to remove trip hazards and dial in slopes to align with ADA guidance (max 2% cross slope; 1:20 running slope). For stubborn lips, we combine lifting with light bevel grinding. Our crews use OSHA-compliant silica controls with HEPA vacuums and shrouds for a cleaner, safer site.

Garage Floor Leveling

When interior slabs drop, you see binding doors, slab cracking, and tripping at thresholds. We correct settlement with precise polyurethane injections and optional self‑leveling underlayment for tight interior tolerances. The process is low‑dust, quick to cure, and designed to stabilize subgrade voids that often form from moisture or hydrocompaction in Iron County soils. Many projects return to foot traffic the same day.

Trip Hazard Removal

Municipal walks, HOAs, and commercial paths must stay safe and accessible. We remove trip hazards by lifting misaligned panels and, when needed, bevel grinding to meet ADA thresholds. Our crew coordinates Blue Stakes of Utah 811 utility locates prior to drilling and follows OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1153 for silica control. The result is a clean, compliant walkway without the cost and downtime of full replacement.

Common questions

01What is polyurethane foam jacking and where can it be used?

Polyurethane foam jacking (often called polyjacking or foam jacking) injects expanding foam beneath a slab to lift and support it. We use small 5/8 inch holes to access voids, then control lift with laser levels. It’s ideal for sidewalks, stoops, driveways, patios, pool decks, garages, and interior floors. In Cedar City and Iron County, it’s a great option where quick cure, minimal downtime, and precise elevation control are important for homes, HOAs, and commercial sites.

02How does slabjacking work?

Slabjacking lifts concrete by injecting material beneath it to fill voids and raise the panel. The process is: drill access holes, inject foam or slurry, monitor with levels and dial indicators, then patch holes and seal joints. We offer polyurethane foam injection for smaller holes and faster cures, and we can discuss mudjacking (cementitious slurry) where appropriate. The goal is a stable, even slab with restored pitch and reduced trip hazards - without full replacement.

03How much does concrete leveling cost in Cedar City?

Costs vary by slab thickness, access, and how much lift or stabilization is needed. As a guideline, Utah projects often range around $4-$7 per square foot for foam jacking; mudjacking can be somewhat lower but uses larger holes and may require longer cure times. After a site visit, we’ll provide a firm, written estimate and options. We also explain long‑term value, including joint sealing and drainage improvements to protect your investment.

04Is polyjacking better than mudjacking?

Each method has pros and cons. Polyjacking uses smaller holes (about 5/8 in), cures quickly - often ready the same day - and is lighter, which helps reduce added soil load. Mudjacking typically costs less up front but uses 1-2 inch holes and can add more weight beneath the slab. In Cedar City’s variable soils and freeze-thaw climate, we often recommend polyurethane for precision and speed, but we’ll advise based on your slab and budget.

05How long before I can drive on leveled concrete with foam?

Most foam-lifted slabs are ready for foot traffic within minutes and light vehicle traffic the same day, often within 30-60 minutes after final injection, depending on temperature and slab thickness. In colder Cedar City conditions, our heated equipment helps maintain proper reaction and cure. We’ll confirm traffic-ready times on site so you can plan around garage access and driveway use.

06How big are the drill holes for foam vs. mudjacking?

Foam jacking typically uses small, discreet holes - about 5/8 inch in diameter. Mudjacking often requires larger ports, roughly 1-2 inches. Smaller holes mean a cleaner finish and less visible patching after leveling, which is why many homeowners and businesses in Cedar City prefer polyurethane for highly visible areas like front walks, porches, and decorative driveways.

07Does leveling fix ADA trip hazards on sidewalks?

Yes. We target trip lips and panel mismatches to meet ADA change‑in‑level limits (1/4 inch vertical; 1/2 inch if beveled). We also pay attention to slope and cross‑slope (2% max cross slope; 1:20 running slope target). For Cedar City projects, we can reference city ramp/sidewalk resources and help restore safe, accessible paths without full replacement, minimizing downtime for pedestrians and customers.

08Do I need to contact Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before concrete leveling at my home?

Yes. Before any drilling, utilities must be located. We coordinate with Blue Stakes of Utah 811 - the statewide one‑call center - to mark buried lines, typically within three business days. This step protects your property and our crew and is standard practice for Cedar City and throughout Southern Utah. We’ll handle the request and schedule the work once clear.

09How do Cedar City’s hydrocompaction or subsidence areas affect leveling?

Hydrocompaction-prone or subsidence zones can leave weak or collapsible soils beneath slabs. We address this by adding soil densification (deep injections), increasing support across the footprint, and sealing joints to limit future water intrusion. We review Cedar City planning maps and site conditions to recommend the right method and foam density, then provide a plan to stabilize the slab - not just lift it.

10Will polyurethane lifting foam perform in Cedar City’s freeze-thaw climate and higher elevation?

Modern high‑density foams are closed‑cell and moisture-resistant, making them well suited for freeze-thaw conditions. We use heated proportioners and hoses to ensure proper reaction and cure at elevation and in colder weather. Once cured, the foam won’t wash out, and it resists water intrusion. We’ll match product and injection strategy to your slab thickness, temperature, and soil conditions for consistent, durable performance.
