How Our Commercial Leveling Process Works - Fast, Clean, and Business-Friendly

We designed our process to be predictable and low-disruption. Clear communication, precise lifting, and clean turnover are standard.

  1. Site Walk & Testing - We inspect the slab, perform DCP probing as needed, and identify voids, joints, and water sources.
  2. Custom Proposal - You receive options (polyjacking or slabjacking), scope, FF/FL or ADA targets, phasing, and after-hours scheduling.
  3. Injection & Lift - We drill 3/8-5/8-inch ports, inject foam with a heated proportioner and poly gun, and monitor lift by laser for accurate elevation.
  4. Seal & Clean - We fill ports, seal cracks and joints with backer rod and polyurethane sealant, then sweep/pressure wash the work area.
  5. Verification & Turnover - We verify slopes and tolerances, document measurements, and reopen the area - often within the same shift.

Common questions

01How do I know if my concrete needs lifting?

Look for trip edges over 1/4 inch, doors that drag, ponding near entries, rocking slabs, or forklifts that bump at joints. In St. George, settlement often follows irrigation leaks, poor compaction, or washouts. We can assess sidewalks, docks, and interiors, check for voids, and recommend polyurethane foam injection, slabjacking, or stabilization based on your building’s use and soil conditions.

02How long does polyurethane concrete leveling take?

Most projects are completed in hours, not days. The foam cures in about 15-30 minutes, allowing foot traffic quickly and forklifts or vehicles shortly after. We often schedule overnight so you reopen on time. Multi-zone warehouses or campuses may be phased over several nights to minimize operational impact while keeping areas safe and accessible.

03Is polyurethane foam safe and environmentally friendly?

We use high-density, closed-cell foam that is hydrophobic and inert once cured. It won’t wash out, and it underseals slabs to limit water movement. Because we drill small 3/8-5/8 inch ports and avoid full tear-out, there’s far less material waste. We follow OSHA silica controls and maintain clean sites for businesses across Washington County.

04What causes concrete settlement in Southern Utah?

Common drivers include inadequate subgrade compaction, irrigation or plumbing leaks, erosion during monsoon storms, and collapsible soils. Heavy repetitive loads at docks or forklift aisles accelerate movement. We address the root cause with void filling under slabs, soil densification, and joint sealing to reduce water intrusion that can undermine support over time.

05How much does commercial concrete leveling cost vs replacement?

Leveling typically runs 50-70% less than demolition and repour, with far less downtime. Pricing depends on slab thickness, access, lift height, and soil stabilization needs. We provide a free estimate with a clear scope, including options like deep foam injection if soils are weak. Most clients in 84770-84791 recover value through avoided closures and faster turnaround.

06How soon can employees or customers use the area after leveling?

In most cases, areas reopen the same shift. Entries and sidewalks accept foot traffic within minutes of cure. Forklifts and light vehicles can return shortly after we verify elevations and seal joints. For parking lots and docks, we coordinate barricades and signage so traffic resumes safely without confusion.

07Do you provide warranties and documentation for commercial projects?
