Concrete Leveling & Repair Services Built for Southern Utah Soils

We focus on precise, long-lasting slab lifting with polyurethane foam injection and targeted repairs. Our crew combines laser leveling, dial indicators, and proven methods to deliver clean results that last.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

Sinking concrete creates trip hazards, standing water, and foundation stress. We solve this with high-density, two-part polyurethane foam injected through small 5/8-3/4 inch ports. The foam expands to lift slabs, fill voids, and densify weak soils - even around the Virgin River. We use hydrophobic formulations for wet conditions and monitor lift with a laser level for millimeter accuracy. Most areas are ready for use in 30-60 minutes, minimizing disruption.

Driveway Leveling

Sunken driveways cause tire ruts, curb snags, and drainage problems. We lift panels with foam jacking to restore smooth entries and correct water flow away from garages. Our crew uses heated-hose proportioners for consistent foam output and edge support techniques to prevent future settlement along joints. We finish with non-shrink grout at port holes and can seal joints with polyurea for added protection.

Sidewalk Leveling

Offset sidewalk panels are a liability and a safety risk. We lift slabs to remove trip hazards and can provide ADA slope corrections for HOAs and municipalities along the Hurricane-La Verkin corridor. Our team handles right‑of‑way work with MUTCD‑compliant traffic control and Blue Stakes of Utah 811 utility locates before drilling. Optional crack chasing and sealing help keep water out and maintain long-term stability.

Garage Floor Leveling

Interior slabs sink from subgrade voids, plumbing backfill settlement, or expansive soils. We use grid-pattern foam injection to lift low areas, reduce slab rocking, and fix door binding. Precision lift monitoring with dial gauges prevents overcorrection. We can work around embedded utilities and radiant heat, and we finish with epoxy crack filler where needed to restore function and appearance - usually in a single visit.

Trip Hazard Removal

Public paths, curb ramps, and community walkways must stay safe and accessible. We remove trip hazards by lifting slabs, then optionally grinding micro-lips for ADA compliance. Our team documents before/after elevations and can provide letters of correction for HOAs or city files. It’s a fast, clean process that avoids costly tear-out and extended closures.

Common questions

01How do I know if my concrete needs lifting?

Look for settled panels, pooling water, rocking slabs, or doors that rub. Sidewalk offsets over 1/2 inch are trip hazards and may violate ADA guidelines. Driveways that dip at the garage threshold can channel water inside. We assess the cause - voids, compaction, or washout - and recommend foam jacking or soil stabilization to restore elevation and drainage without tear-out.

02Is it cheaper to level concrete or replace it?

In most La Verkin and Washington County projects, polyurethane concrete lifting costs 50-70% less than replacement and takes hours, not days. You avoid demo, disposal, and re-pour curing time. Leveling is ideal when the slab is structurally sound but has settled or lost support. We’ll provide a clear estimate and explain when replacement might be smarter.

03How much does concrete leveling cost in Utah?

Pricing depends on slab size, settlement depth, access, and void conditions. Typical residential projects in Southern Utah range from a few hundred dollars for a small sidewalk panel to a few thousand for multi-panel driveways or interior floors.

04Which is better: mudjacking or polyjacking?

Polyjacking uses lightweight, expanding foam that lifts precisely through small 5/8-3/4 inch ports and resists washout. Mudjacking uses a heavier cement-sand slurry and larger holes. For most La Verkin applications - especially where we want minimal added weight and fast return to service - polyurethane foam is preferred. We’ll discuss both and recommend the best fit for your slab.

05How long does foam jacking last?

High-density polyurethane is inert, closed-cell, and designed to resist water and decay. When injected properly over a stable subgrade, results are long-lasting.

06Can you level concrete in winter in Southern Utah?

Yes. We run heated hoses and proportioners to maintain correct foam temperatures and follow cold-weather protocols. As long as the slab is dry and above manufacturer limits, we can lift safely. Cure is rapid, and most sidewalks are walkable within 30-60 minutes, even in cooler months around La Verkin.

07How big are the drill holes for polyjacking?

We typically drill 5/8-3/4 inch ports in a controlled grid that targets voids and lift points. After injection, we patch with non-shrink grout and blend the finish. Compared to replacement, the process is minimally invasive and keeps the original slab intact.

08Do you handle utility locates through Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before drilling?

Absolutely. For sidewalks, driveways near utilities, and right-of-way work, we submit locate requests to Blue Stakes of Utah 811 and use safe drilling practices. On specialized sites, we can add GPR scanning to identify unknown features before injection.

09Can you provide ADA compliance letters for sidewalk trip hazard corrections?

Yes. After sidewalk leveling, we can document before/after elevations and slopes and issue a letter of correction for HOAs or municipal records. We also offer ongoing trip hazard programs for communities to maintain safe, accessible walkways across Washington County.
