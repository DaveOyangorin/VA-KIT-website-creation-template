Concrete Leveling & Lifting Services for Homes and Businesses

We level sunken concrete with durable materials, precise techniques, and the right equipment for Kanab’s soils and weather. Every project is measured with a laser level and delivered with a clean, warrantied finish.

Driveway Leveling

Rutting tires, uneven joints, and pooling water signal settlement beneath your driveway. We lift panels using a strategic hole pattern and incremental injections, verified with a laser level for a smooth approach and proper pitch away from the garage. We use high‑density foam for quick return-to-traffic or a cementitious slurry when heavy bearing is needed. After lifting, we seal joints with UV‑stable polyurethane to reduce future washout and freeze‑thaw intrusion. The result: safer entries, better drainage, and longer pavement life - with no tear‑out mess.

Sidewalk Leveling

Trip edges over 1/2 inch put you at risk for injuries and ADA complaints. We correct sunken sidewalk panels by filling voids, lifting in small, controlled stages, and dialing in smooth transitions. Where lifting isn’t feasible, we can perform ADA bevel/grinding to eliminate trip hazards. Expect minimal dust thanks to HEPA shrouds and vacuum collection, neat 3/8-5/8 in. foam ports (or ~1‑1/2 in. for slurry), and clean patching that blends with surrounding concrete. Ideal for homes, storefronts, HOAs, and municipal routes in downtown Kanab.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

Closed‑cell foam expands under the slab to lift and support while resisting water and erosion - perfect for Kanab’s flash‑flood-prone areas. Our proportioner rig with heated hoses allows precise temperature and flow control, and deep foam injection densifies weak soils. Small, clean ports and rapid cure (often 15-30 minutes) minimize downtime for driveways, patios, interior floors, and pool decks. We’ll compare foam versus slurry based on slab thickness, load, and soil conditions so you get the best long-term result.

Garage Floor Leveling

Sunken garage floors cause door binding, standing water, and uneven tool benches. We map slab highs and lows with a precision level, lift in measured increments, and seal joints/cracks to block future water intrusion. Interior work is performed with dust control (HEPA vacs, wet methods) and low‑odor materials, so your space stays livable. Most floors are walkable the same day, with vehicles returning once the lift is confirmed and joints are sealed.

Concrete Void Filling

Washouts from irrigation, broken downspouts, or monsoon events leave hidden voids that lead to sudden settlement. We stabilize subgrades using foam or flowable fills, then lift, lock, and seal the slab. For larger erosional cavities near culverts or drive aprons, we may use a road‑base slurry with aggregate to rebuild support. Our approach prevents re‑settlement, protects edges, and restores bearing capacity - critical along the US‑89 corridor and flood‑impacted areas.

Common questions

01How do we lift your concrete?

We start with measurements and a laser level to map settlement. Next, we core small ports - typically 3/8-5/8 in. for foam or about 1‑1/2 in. for slurry. We inject in controlled stages, monitoring lift and filling voids first to avoid cracking. After final elevations are confirmed, we patch ports with color‑blended material, seal critical joints/cracks, and clean the site. Most residential projects finish in a few hours with minimal disruption.

02What material is used to lift and support concrete?

We select high‑density, closed‑cell polyurethane for fast curing, water resistance, and deep soil densification, or a Portland cement‑sand slurry when heavier bearing or broad contact is needed. For large erosion cavities, we may specify road‑base slurry or flowable fill. We’ll compare foam, slurry (slab jacking), and hybrid options during your estimate to match your slab thickness, loading, and soil conditions in Kanab.

03How long does leveling take and when can I use the slab?

Most residential lifts in Kanab take 2-4 hours. Polyurethane typically cures in 15-30 minutes; walkways are usable almost immediately, and many driveways can handle light vehicle traffic the same day. Slurry lifts can require longer set times - often 24-48 hours - depending on mix and temperature. We’ll advise exact timelines before we begin so you can plan access.

04Is foam jacking better than slurry lifting for my situation?

It depends on goals and conditions. Foam is ideal for fast return‑to‑service, smaller drill holes, water resistance, and soil densification - great for driveways, sidewalks, and interior floors. Slurry (cement‑sand) can be preferred under heavy, thick slabs or where a broader bearing surface is desired. We’ll recommend the best method after evaluating soil, slab thickness, load, and drainage.

05Do I need to contact Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before concrete leveling?

Yes - if there’s any chance utilities are present, Blue Stakes 811 must be notified at least three business days before drilling. We handle coordination for our projects in Kanab and use utility locating and safe drilling practices. In sensitive areas, we add scanning or vacuum potholing to confirm depths. Safety and utility protection come first.

06What permits or approvals are needed to level city sidewalks along US‑89?

Sidewalks within city or UDOT right‑of‑way may require a right‑of‑way/encroachment permit and traffic control plan. We’ll help determine jurisdiction (City of Kanab vs. UDOT), submit documentation if needed, and stage cones/signage to maintain pedestrian access. For ADA transitions, we follow local standards to remove trip hazards and restore compliant slopes.

07How do flash floods and erosion affect leveled slabs - and how can we prevent re‑settlement?

Floods can wash out supporting soils, creating hidden voids that lead to future drops. Our process fills voids before lifting, densifies weak soils with deep foam injection when needed, and finishes with sealed joints to block water intrusion. We also recommend drainage fixes - grading adjustments, downspout extensions, or trenching - to protect your slab after leveling.

08Can you re‑level cabins in Duck Creek Village where freeze‑thaw is severe?

Yes. Elevated sites like Duck Creek Village (84762) see frost heave and seasonal movement. We stabilize subgrades, fill voids, and lift in small increments to avoid cracking. We’ll also seal joints/cracks to keep meltwater out and may suggest drainage corrections to reduce freeze‑thaw cycles. Winterized installs are available with heated hoses, tents, and additives when conditions require.

09How much does leveling cost in Kanab?

Costs vary by size, lift height, access, and material. As a general guide, many projects fall in the range typical for Southern Utah: slurry lifts from roughly $6-$12 per sq. ft., and polyurethane from about $8-$18 per sq. ft. Small trip edges may be less; large washout repairs can be more. We provide free, written estimates with exact pricing.

10What maintenance should I do after leveling to maximize lifespan?

Keep water away from slab edges: extend downspouts, correct negative grading, and avoid irrigation overspray at joints. For commercial sidewalks, schedule periodic ADA checks to maintain compliant transitions. These simple steps greatly reduce erosion and freeze‑thaw damage.
