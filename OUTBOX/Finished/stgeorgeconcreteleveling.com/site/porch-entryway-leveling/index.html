Common questions

01How does polyurethane porch and entryway leveling work?

We drill small 5/8-3/4 inch ports in strategic locations, then inject high‑density polyurethane foam under your porch, steps, or entry slab. The foam expands to fill voids and lifts the concrete in controlled stages while we monitor with a rotary laser. Ports are patched and color‑matched, joints/cracks are sealed, and your entry is typically walkable within 30-45 minutes. It’s precise, fast, and cleaner than replacement in St. George.

02Can you lift concrete steps and stoops without cracking?

Yes. We use step/stoop targeted lift sequencing, applying low, steady pressure and checking every lift increment with a laser. For heavy masonry steps, we use higher‑density foams (e.g., Precision Lift 4.75#) and can dowel steps with rebar or add helical pier underpinning when needed. This controls stresses, protects stucco or veneers, and helps prevent cracking or spalling during the lift.

03How much does porch leveling cost in St. George, UT?

Costs vary by size, settlement, access, and soil conditions. Typical porch or entry leveling in Washington County ranges from about $650 to $1,800, with complex stoops or deep soil densification up to $2,500+. We provide a firm, written estimate after a laser assessment, and we’ll compare polyjacking with mudjacking so you can choose the best value for your situation.

04Mudjacking vs polyurethane for entry slabs - which is better?

Polyurethane uses smaller holes, is lighter (reducing future settlement risk), cures in about 15-45 minutes, and resists water. Mudjacking can be economical for large, thick slabs but uses bigger holes and adds weight. For most porches, steps, and entry walkways in St. George, polyurethane offers better precision and faster return to service. We’ll explain both and recommend the right fit.

05How soon can I use my porch or steps after leveling?

Most porches and stoops are walkable within 30-45 minutes after foam injection. Doors and thresholds are usable the same day. If we complete crack routing and joint sealing, we’ll advise specific cure times (often 1-4 hours) for sealants. We’ll give you clear guidance before we leave so you know exactly when to resume normal use.

06Will leveling fix existing cracks at my entry?

We lift first to close gaps and restore slope, then route and fill cracks with epoxy crack filler and seal control/joints with backer rod and polyurethane or silicone sealant. While hairline cracks may remain visible, they’ll be sealed against water intrusion. This combination helps protect your porch from future washout and improves appearance and durability.

07Do I need a permit to level a sidewalk in front of my home?

If the sidewalk lies in the public right‑of‑way, St. George City may require a permit under City Code 7‑1‑12. We help you determine jurisdiction and handle ROW permit guidance. We also coordinate Blue Stakes of Utah 811 utility locates before drilling, so the process is safe and compliant.

08Will St. George’s heat affect foam accuracy or cure times?

High temperatures can impact expansion if not managed. We calibrate our proportioner, monitor material temps with an infrared thermometer, stage lifts in smaller increments, and shade work areas when possible. These hot‑weather practices keep lifts accurate and allow normal walk use in about 30-45 minutes - even in summer.

09Can an attached porch be lifted without damaging stucco or thresholds?

Yes. We protect finishes, release binding where needed, and lift in fine increments while monitoring the door alignment. We can also stabilize porch footings adjacent to the foundation and seal ledger or threshold gaps after the lift. Our approach is designed to safeguard stucco, veneers, and door hardware.
