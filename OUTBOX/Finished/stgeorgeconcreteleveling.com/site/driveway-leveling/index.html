How Our Driveway Leveling Process Works

We make the process clear, safe, and efficient from the first call to the final walkthrough. Here’s what to expect.

  1. Assessment & Measure - We inspect the driveway, measure settlement with a laser level, check drainage, and identify causes like washout or poor base.
  2. Safety & Prep - We coordinate Blue Stakes of Utah 811, control dust with HEPA vacs, and set small drill points. GPR scanning is available when utilities are uncertain.
  3. Precision Injection - We drill 5/8-inch holes, inject structural foam in controlled lifts, and monitor elevations in real time for an even rise.
  4. Void Fill & Stabilize - We fill voids and can add deep foam injection for weak subgrade. This helps prevent future sinking.

Common questions

01How long does polyurethane lifting take and when can I drive on it?

Most driveway foam injection projects in St. George take 2-4 hours onsite. The structural polyurethane cures fast - light vehicle traffic is usually okay within 15-30 minutes after the final lift. Larger or multi-panel drives may need staged injections, but you still avoid days of downtime. We’ll confirm drive-on timing during the final walkthrough so you know exactly when it’s safe to use.

02Is polyurethane foam safe for my property and the environment?

Yes. We use closed-cell, structural polyurethane designed for concrete lifting. Once cured, the material is inert, won’t wash out, and resists water. We also offer hydrophobic formulations for damp soils and NSF-61 listed foam near potable-water features. Our crews follow OSHA safety practices for handling uncured materials, ventilation, and PPE to protect your home and landscaping.

03How do I know if my driveway needs leveling vs replacement?

Choose leveling when slabs are structurally sound but settled or rocking, when you see gaps under the slab, or when pooling sends water toward the garage. Replacement makes sense if concrete is severely cracked into many pieces, crumbling, or too thin. We’ll inspect, measure elevations, and provide side-by-side options so you can compare cost, timeline, and long-term performance.

04What causes concrete to settle in St. George?

Common causes include loose red-sand subgrades, irrigation or downspout runoff eroding soil, poor original compaction, and water cutting along drive edges during monsoon storms. Over time, voids form and slabs sink toward the weakest spots. Our solution targets the cause - filling voids, stabilizing soils, sealing joints, and adjusting drainage so the problem doesn’t come back.

05Do I need to contact Blue Stakes of Utah 811 before driveway leveling or slab injection at my home?

We handle Blue Stakes 811 utility marking for you. Marks help avoid underground lines during drilling and injection. In Utah, requests should be made a few business days before work, and markings are typically valid for about three weeks. If private lines exist (sprinklers, lighting), we’ll review their locations with you and adjust port layout as needed.

06What is the difference between mudjacking and foam jacking?

Mudjacking pumps a cement-sand slurry under the slab through larger holes; it’s effective for some big voids but adds weight and needs more cleanup. Foam jacking (polyjacking) uses small 5/8-inch holes and a lightweight, expanding structural foam for precise, fast lifts - often traffic-ready in 15-30 minutes. We’ll recommend the method that best fits your slab, soils, and budget.

07How much does concrete driveway leveling cost in Utah?

Costs vary by size, settlement depth, access, and how much void fill or deep injection is needed. In Southern Utah, many single-panel lifts fall between $900 and $1,800, while larger multi-panel drives can range higher. It’s typically 50-70% less than replacement. We provide a written, line-item estimate so you can see exactly what’s included. Financing options are available on request.

08Will leveling fix driveway drainage and water pooling?

Yes - proper lifts re-establish the slope so water flows away from your garage and toward the street or drains. We verify elevations with a laser level and adjust lift points to fine-tune runoff. After lifting, we reseal joints and may suggest simple drainage tweaks (downspout extensions, grading) to keep water from undermining the slab again.

09Do you offer residential and commercial concrete lifting?

We do. Our crews level residential driveways, sidewalks, patios, and pool decks, plus commercial approaches, shop bays, and warehouse floors. We serve St. George and nearby cities like Washington, Santa Clara, Ivins, and Hurricane. Same-day or next-day appointments are often available in area code 435. We’re equipped for small fixes and large, multi-location programs.

We also document before/after elevations and outline care steps - like sealing joints and improving drainage - to protect your coverage.
