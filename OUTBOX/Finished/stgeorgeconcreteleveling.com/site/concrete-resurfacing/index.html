Our Proven Process & Methods

We clean with pressure washers/surface cleaners, then grind or shot blast to ICRI CSP 3-5. Cracks are chased and filled; spalls are patched with polymer‑modified mortars. Primers and slurry scratch coats promote bond and flatten minor waviness.

Overlays include microtopping, stamped concrete overlay for patterns, or self‑leveling toppings for smooth interiors. Garage systems use epoxy primers, broadcast to refusal with flake or quartz, and polyurea/polyaspartic topcoats for chemical and UV resistance.

Complex Situations We’ve Mastered

High Heat Installs: We follow ACI 305R hot‑weather practices - early starts, shaded staging, adjusted mix ratios, and controlled curing to reduce map cracking and rapid evaporation.

Moisture & Vapor: If in‑situ RH exceeds thresholds, we add moisture‑vapor barrier epoxies before coatings. For stamped overlay over cracked concrete, we reinforce with proper repair techniques and joint recutting to control movement.

Common questions

01Want to See Projects Like Yours?

Ask us for nearby addresses and finish samples. We’ll share real‑world results and connect you with references on request.

02Is it cheaper to resurface or replace a concrete driveway in St. George?

For most driveways in St. George, resurfacing costs less than full replacement. Basic overlays often range $5-$9 per sq ft, while replacement can run $10-$18+ per sq ft with demo, disposal, and forming. Resurfacing is faster, avoids major disruption, and adds texture, color, and sealing. If the base is severely heaved or structurally failed, we’ll advise on repairs or replacement and explain why - so you choose the right path.

03Can you resurface old, cracked, or spalled concrete?

Yes - if the slab is structurally sound. We chase cracks, fill and stabilize them, patch spalls, and recut or fill joints as needed. Proper surface profiling (ICRI CSP 3-5) and bond coats help overlays grab tight. For wider movement cracks, we use flexible joint strategies to manage stress. We’ll inspect on site and recommend the right system for your driveway, patio, or walkway.

04How long does a concrete overlay last in Southern Utah’s heat?

We plan installs around ACI 305R hot‑weather guidance and use UV‑stable sealers to combat fade. Exterior areas typically need resealing every 24-36 months. Following the care plan - gentle cleaners, no harsh de‑icers - extends life and keeps color and texture looking great.

05What is the best non‑slip coating for pool decks in hot climates?

We recommend a cool‑touch knockdown texture with anti‑slip media and a UV‑stable sealer. This combination balances barefoot comfort, traction, and heat reflection. Our pool finishes target wet DCOF ≥ 0.52 and can include IR‑reflective pigments for cooler surfaces. We’ll bring samples and explain texture options so you can feel the difference.

06How much does concrete resurfacing cost per square foot near 84770/84790?

In the Greater St. George area, typical ranges are $5-$9 per sq ft for microtopping or broom/knockdown textures, and $9-$16 per sq ft for stamped or highly decorative overlays. Costs vary with prep (grinding, shot blasting), repairs, colors, and sealer type. We provide a free, written quote with options so you can compare finishes and budgets.

07Do I need permits to resurface concrete in St. George?

Permits usually aren’t required for overlays or coatings because the slab stays in place. HOAs may require approval for color or texture changes. For commercial spaces in Washington County, we coordinate access and safety plans. We’ll flag any jurisdictional requirements and help you navigate HOA guidelines before work begins.

08What prep is required before concrete resurfacing?

Successful overlays require clean, sound, profiled concrete. We pressure wash, then mechanically prep to the specified ICRI CSP, repair cracks/spalls, apply primers or scratch coats, and manage joints. For coatings, we test moisture via ASTM F2170 and, when needed, install a moisture‑vapor barrier epoxy. Proper prep is why professional resurfacing outlasts quick fixes.

09What’s the difference between epoxy, polyurea, and polyaspartic for garage floors?

Epoxy offers strong adhesion and is excellent as a primer/body coat. Polyurea is flexible and fast‑curing. Polyaspartic topcoats cure quickly, resist UV yellowing, and stand up to hot‑tire pickup - great for one‑day systems in St. George. We often use epoxy primer + flake broadcast + polyaspartic topcoat for durability, gloss, and quick return to service.

10How soon can I walk or drive on a resurfaced surface?

Foot traffic is often allowed after ~24 hours for many overlays, depending on temperature and system. For vehicle traffic: 48-72 hours on most driveways, and 24-48 hours for premium polyaspartic garage systems. We’ll confirm timelines in writing for your exact finish and weather conditions.

11Do you perform moisture testing and offer mitigation before coatings?

Yes. We conduct in‑situ relative humidity testing per ASTM F2170. If RH is high, we can install a moisture‑vapor barrier epoxy before the coating system to prevent blistering, bubbles, or delamination. This step is critical for long‑term adhesion, especially in garages and interior slabs.
