How Our Step Leveling Process Works

We keep your project simple, safe, and predictable with a proven, low-disruption workflow.

1) Inspection & Free Estimate

We assess the steps, measure elevation changes with a laser level, locate voids, and explain your options (foam vs. slurry). You receive a clear written estimate.

2) Site Prep & Safety Controls

We establish pedestrian traffic control, set up HEPA silica dust collection, and mask nearby finishes. Utilities and handrails are checked before drilling.

3) Precision Injection & Lift

Using the drill-pump-patch method, we install injection ports and lift in small, controlled stages. Foam expands to densify soils and raise the concrete smoothly.

4) Finish, Seal & Cleanup

We patch holes with color-matched material, seal joints with polyurea where needed, and clean the site thoroughly. Most stairs are ready for foot traffic within minutes.

Common questions

01Want to see projects like yours?

Ask us for local examples and references in your neighborhood. We’ll share similar step repairs and results you can expect.

02Can concrete steps be lifted without replacing them?

Yes. We lift most concrete steps and stoops using polyurethane foam injection (polyjacking) or, in some cases, cement-based slurry (mudjacking). Small 5/8 in. holes allow access under the slab to fill voids and raise the steps evenly. This preserves your existing concrete, landscaping, and finishes - usually at a fraction of replacement cost and with far less downtime in St. George and surrounding communities.

03Is it better to level or replace concrete steps?

Leveling is typically 50-70% less than replacement and is completed in hours, not days. You avoid tear-out, forming, curing time, and mismatched new concrete. Replacement makes sense if the steps are structurally broken into multiple pieces. We’ll evaluate cracks, soil conditions, and finishes to recommend the most cost-effective option for your St. George property.

04How much does it cost to level concrete steps in St. George, Utah?

Most step or stoop lifts range from $450-$1,800 in our area, with larger attached porch slabs or complex void filling running $1,800-$2,500+. Pricing depends on lift height, access, number of injection points, and soil stabilization needs. We provide a free on-site estimate with a firm quote for homes and businesses in ZIPs 84770, 84790, and nearby.

05How long does polyurethane foam step leveling last?

We focus on void filling and, when needed, deep injection for soil densification to reduce future settlement. Proper downspout extensions and sealed joints help maximize service life in Southern Utah’s climate.

06What is the difference between mudjacking and polyjacking for steps?

Mudjacking uses a heavier cement slurry and larger holes (1-2 in.); polyjacking uses smaller holes (about 5/8 in.) and a lighter, expanding foam. Foam reaches tight voids, adds minimal weight, and cures in minutes - ideal for entries that need quick reopening. We’ll recommend the best method for your steps based on soil, lift height, and finish sensitivity.

07How soon can I use my stairs after foam jacking?

Typically within 15-30 minutes after injection. We’ll confirm during your final walkthrough. Color-matched patches and joint sealants are ready for normal foot traffic soon after installation, so most St. George homeowners can resume regular use the same day.

08Do you re-anchor loose handrails and seal gaps after lifting?

Yes. We can re-set handrail posts using corrosion-resistant anchors and structural epoxy, then seal slab-to-wall or stucco gaps to keep water and pests out. If the lift exposes small transitions, we can add joint or edge sealants (polyurea/backer rod) for a finished look.

09Will leveling fix trip hazards to meet ADA guidelines?

We target flush transitions and remove changes in level that create trip hazards. ADA notes 1/4-1/2 in. thresholds as concern points; by restoring even treads and landings, we help entries meet practical accessibility expectations. We also provide documentation for HOAs or facility files upon request.

10Does concrete leveling require a permit in St. George?

Most step leveling projects are considered repair and do not require a building permit. We maintain St. George business licensing, Utah DOPL credentials, and carry insurance. Our crew sets up OSHA-compliant silica controls and pedestrian traffic control for safe, professional execution at your entry.

11Do you offer neighborhood-specific timelines for Little Valley, SunRiver, and Washington Fields?

Yes. We regularly serve Little Valley, SunRiver, Washington Fields, Bloomington Hills, and Ivins. Most projects are scheduled within 2-5 business days, with on-site work completed in 2-4 hours. Request a free estimate for step-specific pricing and a timeline tailored to your address and soil conditions.

12Can you level steps attached to a porch slab without cracking tile or stone?

In many cases, yes. We use staged lifts, monitor bond lines, and adjust injection patterns to protect overlays. We’ll evaluate the tile/stone type, setting bed, and movement joints during your inspection and recommend the best approach to lift safely without damaging finishes.
