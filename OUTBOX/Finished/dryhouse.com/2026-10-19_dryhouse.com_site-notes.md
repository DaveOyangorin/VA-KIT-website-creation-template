## What was done

- **Pages keeping the client's own text:** all, cleaned of print links, ratings, RSS lines and "Click here" sentences.
- **Report applied:**
  - The home page targets "basement waterproofing west jordan" in the title, H1 and first sentence.
  - Every service page names West Jordan in its title, H1 and opening paragraph.
  - Every page has its own description; before, all of them read "Rocky Mountain Waterproofing".
- **Speed and mobile.**
  - The live site scored 56/100 on mobile and scrolls sideways on phones (395 px on a 390 px screen).
  - The draft is plain HTML with WebP photos at 152 KB or less and lazy-loading, and has no sideways scrolling.
  - The phone header and menu passed the mobile check.
- **QA:** 0 issues across 49 pages: one H1 each, canonicals, valid JSON-LD, no broken links, image sizes and alt text. 9 titles run a few characters over 60.
- **Photos.** All are the client's own:
  - The House That SHE Built job series (2020) and the honeycomb photo.
  - The tie close-up, cropped from their flyer to remove its text.
  - Staff headshots.
  - Manufacturer product renders (EcoDrain, strip drain detail) are not used. The badges and job flyers aren't used as images either.

## Design (premium, v3 redesign, 20 Oct 2026)

- **Research (screenshotted and reviewed):** 12 industry sites (Big-D, Layton, Jacobsen, Mortenson, Ram Jack, Copper X, Baker Roofing, Strike Group, Power Design, Tremco Roofing; Groundworks blocked by a bot check; Okland timed out). Chosen three:
  - **Tremco Roofing** (the brand of membrane they install): technical, system-led presentation.
  - **Layton Construction** (Utah): editorial serif inside the headline, a stat row.
  - **Big-D** (Utah): dark full-bleed photography, heavy condensed uppercase type.
- **Concept, "Submittal", v3:** a full-bleed hero on their own black-waterproofed foundation under a transparent header, with an animated **water line** across the foot of the hero; a product marquee (Tuff-N-Dri, Watchdog H3, TremProof, Warm-N-Dri, Paraseal...); an editorial intro and counted stats; the **sticky wall-section story** as the signature (the drawing stays pinned while each of the six layers lights up as its step scrolls past); a dark product-data band (mil gauge, hydrostatic head); a bento of the four divisions; a parallax band with their motto; tilted site-log prints of The House That SHE Built; a split full-height photo for the family history.
- **Inner pages:** full-bleed job-photo headers; pages without a photo get the dark ground with the wall-section drawing.
- **Brand guide:** amber #F0A91E (the logo's sun) on near-black #121315 and drafting paper #F2F0E9; the logo's sky blue for water. Fonts: Archivo (condensed uppercase display), Instrument Serif italic for emphasis, Instrument Sans (text), IBM Plex Mono (section codes), all Google Fonts.
- **Motion:** reveals, hero and band parallax, marquee, counters, the waves, the story highlights; all off with reduced motion.
