03 01 00 · Form ties

Foundation Tie Leak Repair in West Jordan

The form ties left in every poured wall.

Request a Bid 801-282-2100

Rocky Mountain Waterproofing seals foundation tie leaks in West Jordan. When concrete is formed, ties hold the forms in place, and it is critical to have them removed and sealed before backfill.

In order to form concrete, form ties are placed to hold wood or metal forms while concrete is poured into the channels and cured. The forms are then removed but the ties remain since they are poured into the concrete wall. Typically these ties are broken and patched on the exterior of the structure, but they can still be a point of water entry if not treated properly.

On new foundations our crews break and patch the ties, clean the footings and then waterproof.
