Responsibilities: (We will train you)

  • Prepare foundation to be Waterproofed ( Sweeping, occasional digging, breaking foundation metal ties, waterproofing foundation ties, and other labor as needed )
  • Assist in applying waterproofing to foundations. (basements, decks, etc.)
  • Assist in installation of foundation drainage systems (footing and wall drains)
  • Assist in maintaining a safe work environment.
  • Follow verbal and written instructions.
  • Work as a team to complete projects.

Requirements:

  • Must be 18+ Years Old to be eligible for this position.
  • Attendance: Crew members must be able to be to work consistently. (Mon-Fri Work Week - We may be able to accommodate some schedules - Please let us know your availability. )
  • Location: Crew members must be able to come to our location in West Jordan each workday.
  • Time: Crew members must be able to report for work by 6:30 AM each workday.
  • May be required to work long hours on occasion. (Overtime Pay - 1.5x pay rate)
  • Outdoors: Crew members work outdoors in many different types of weather.
  • Able to lift 50+ lbs.
  • Able to walk around construction sites safely.
  • Able to read and write english (Preferred)
  • Maintain a positive attitude and work well with other team members.
  • Drug/Alcohol free workplace - Due to the nature of construction, being under the influence of drugs/alcohol is not permitted. We do random drug screenings throughout the year.
  • Must Pass Mandatory Drug Screening prior to being hired. (If You Can't Pass We Won't Hire You)

Helpful Certifications/Training: (Not Required but Valuable)

  • OSHA 10 / OSHA 30
  • CPR / First Aid
  • Eagle Scout
  • Military Experience
  • Service Experience (Humanitarian, Mission, Community)
  • Confined Space / Fall Protection / Forklift / Boom Lift
  • Utah State Pesticide Applicator License (Wood Destroying Organisms)
  • Others - Please let us know any certification/training you have.
