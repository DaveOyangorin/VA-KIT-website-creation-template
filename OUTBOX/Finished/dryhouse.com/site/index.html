Questions

Good to know.

01What is the difference between damp proofing and waterproofing?

Damp proofing is a thin asphalt coat (10–20 mils) that slows vapor through the wall and cracks with the foundation. Waterproofing is an asphalt emulsion blended with rubber, at least 40 mils and up to 80+ mils thick, so it stretches over the cracks that form.

02Is damp proofing enough for a basement?

It is the code minimum for most new homes. If you plan to finish the basement, waterproofing is the better match: damp proofing cracks with the concrete when settling occurs.

03Can you fix a leak in a finished basement?

Often, yes. We fix active leaks from inside or out: cracks under windows, form ties, honeycomb, cold joints and pipe penetrations. We don’t fix plumbing, but we routinely fix where pipes come through the foundation.

04Do you work on commercial projects?

Yes, from simple additions to complex commercial and government projects. Most work is on Net 30 terms.
