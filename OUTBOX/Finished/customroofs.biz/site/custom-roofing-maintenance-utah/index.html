Custom Roofing maintains and repairs copper roofs in Lehi and throughout Utah: regular inspections, debris removal, resealing, panel and seam repairs, and patina care. We recommend inspections once or twice a year.

Your home is one of your biggest investments. Copper roofing is known for its beauty and durability, but like any roof, it performs best with regular care.

Why copper roofs still need maintenance

A longer life. Copper is one of the most durable roofing materials there is, and with proper care it can last for generations. Regular inspections catch minor issues before they become major ones.

Less damage from debris. Leaves and branches collect on a roof. Copper resists corrosion, but debris holds moisture against the surface and can cause localized wear. Keeping the roof clear prevents it.

An even patina. Copper weathers naturally, and that patina helps protect it. Good maintenance helps the patina form evenly, without streaks or stains from dirt and runoff.

Problems found early. Severe weather or an unexpected event can still damage a strong roof. Inspections find loose panels, cracked seams and pooling water before they cause leaks or structural problems.

What our maintenance and repair covers

Regular inspections

We recommend an inspection once or twice a year. We check every part of the roof: seams, joints, flashing, and how the patina is developing.

Preventive maintenance

Cleaning debris off the roof, making sure it drains properly, resealing vulnerable areas, and fixing minor issues like loose panels or weather-worn sections.

Copper roof repair

Storms, falling branches and accidents happen. We repair damaged panels, reseal seams and address water damage to the materials underneath, restoring the roof without spoiling its looks.

Patina management

If you want to control how your roof weathers, or restore areas that have weathered unevenly, we offer specialized cleaning and treatments that help the roof age evenly while keeping its protective layer. Curious how patina forms? Read why copper turns green.
