Your roof does more than cover the house. It protects your family and adds value and character to the property. Copper does both jobs well, and it’s the material we know best.

Copper roof installation and replacement

Copper is striking to look at and built to stand up to Utah’s weather. Whether you’re building a new home or replacing the roof you have, we bring the same care to both: precise panels, tight seams and clean details at every edge.

Why homeowners choose copper

Three things bring people to copper, and they usually want all three:

  • The look. Copper gives any home a distinctive, premium finish that people notice from the street.
  • Durability. Copper roofs are known for long life, resisting corrosion and handling Utah’s varied climate.
  • Less upkeep. Copper naturally resists mildew, mold and insect damage, so it needs fewer repairs than many materials.

Complete copper roof overhauls

When a roof needs more than a patch, a full copper renovation is the long-term answer. We start with a detailed inspection of the existing roof to see the damage and decide on the best approach. Then our team rebuilds the roof in high-quality copper.

Copper accents for an existing roof

Not ready for a full copper roof? Copper valleys, dormers and chimney caps add the same character to the roof you already have. Copper gutters finish the look and handle drainage.

Copper roofing designed around your home

Every project is different. From small copper accents to a full replacement, we work with you to design a roof that suits your home’s architecture and your own taste.

Keeping a copper roof in shape

Copper lasts a long time, and a little care helps it last longer. Our preventive maintenance includes inspections and debris removal, and our copper roof maintenance and repair page covers the rest.

Copper roofing questions

Does a copper roof need maintenance?

Less than most roofs, but some. Copper resists mildew, mold and insect damage. Inspections and debris removal still help it last, and we offer both.

Can you add copper to the roof I already have?

Yes. Copper valleys, dormers and chimney caps can go on an existing roof, and copper gutters pair well with them.

Will my copper roof turn green?

Over time copper weathers from bright metal through browns toward a blue-green patina. In a dry climate like Utah’s, that last stage can take a very long time. Read why copper turns green.
