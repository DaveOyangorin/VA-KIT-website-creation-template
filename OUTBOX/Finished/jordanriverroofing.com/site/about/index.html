About Jordan River Roofing

Jordan River Roofing is a roofing company in Taylorsville, Utah, founded in 2019 by Phil, a Utah native who spent over a decade in the roofing industry first.

Free Inspection 801-857-1417

Our story

Phil spent over a decade in the roofing industry before deciding to build something different: a company that treats every roof like it’s on his own home. He founded Jordan River Roofing in 2019, as a two-person operation in Taylorsville.

It has since grown into a team serving the whole Salt Lake Valley. Phil is still on site, still personally involved in every project, and still answers the phone when you call.

“To protect Utah homes and businesses with roofing work that outlasts the elements, delivered with honesty and craftsmanship.”

Phil, founder
