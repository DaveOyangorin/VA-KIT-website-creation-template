Questions

Before you call

Or just call: 801-857-1417.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes. Inspections are free, with a photo-documented report on your roof’s condition and no obligation.

Which cities do you serve?

Thirteen cities in the Salt Lake Valley, from Draper and Riverton in the south to North Salt Lake in the north. See the service areas.

Who will I be dealing with?

Phil founded Jordan River Roofing in 2019 and is still personally involved in every project. Nearly every review mentions him by name.

What warranty do you offer?

Qualifying TAMKO roofs can carry the TAMKO DiamondShield warranty, with up to 25 years of workmanship coverage. See how it works.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. We document storm damage with photos, write an itemized estimate and meet your adjuster on site.
