Storm Damage & Insurance Claims in Taylorsville

Jordan River Roofing helps Taylorsville and Salt Lake County homeowners after wind, hail and storm damage: emergency tarping to stop active leaks, a damage assessment with photo documentation, and help with your insurance claim, including meeting your adjuster on site.

Free Inspection 801-857-1417

First, stop the water

After a storm, the first job is protecting the inside of your home. Heavy-duty tarps over the damaged area keep water out of the ceilings, walls and floors until the permanent repair can be done.

Then, document everything

An insurance claim depends on clear evidence of the damage and its cause. We inspect the roof and document the damage with detailed photos, then write an itemized estimate for the repair.

Working with your adjuster

We meet your insurance company’s adjuster on site to go over the damage and the scope of work, and make sure the proposed repairs meet current building code. You stay in control of your claim; we handle the roofing side of it.

What we can’t promise

Whether a repair is covered depends on your policy and on what caused the damage, and only your insurance company can decide that. What we can do is make sure the damage is properly documented and explained.

What’s included

  • Emergency tarping
  • Hail and wind damage assessment
  • Photo documentation for your insurance claim
  • Meeting your adjuster on site
  • Itemized estimates
  • Permanent repairs or replacement

Questions

Emergency & Insurance Claims: common questions

Will homeowner’s insurance pay for my roof repair?

It depends on your policy and on the cause of the damage; storm damage is often covered and normal wear usually isn’t. Your insurer decides. We document the damage and meet the adjuster so the claim is based on a clear picture.

Should I call you or my insurance company first?

If water is coming in, call us to get the roof protected, and let your insurer know about the damage. A documented inspection helps either way.

What do you do at the adjuster’s inspection?

We meet the adjuster on site, show the damage we documented, and go over the scope of the repair and any code requirements.
