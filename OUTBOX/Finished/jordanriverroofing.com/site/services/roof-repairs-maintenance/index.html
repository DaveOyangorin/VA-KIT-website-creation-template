Small problems, fixed before they grow

A single missing shingle or a lifted piece of flashing is a small repair. Left alone through a Utah winter, it can let water into the deck, the insulation and the ceiling below. Repairs are about finding where the water actually gets in, which isn’t always right above the stain, and fixing it properly.

Signs your roof needs attention

  • Missing, broken or curling shingles
  • Water stains or damp spots on ceilings and walls
  • Sagging along the ridge or roof deck
  • Daylight showing through the attic boards
  • Lots of shingle granules in the gutters
  • Lifted, rusted or missing flashing around chimneys and vents
  • Moss or algae on shaded slopes

Roof Repairs & Maintenance: common questions

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. Inspections are free and come with a photo-documented report of the roof’s condition, with no obligation.

Do you offer roof maintenance plans?

Yes, annual maintenance plans are part of our repair and maintenance service. Ask about one when we inspect your roof.

Can you match my existing shingles?

We replace missing and damaged shingles as part of a repair. Whether an exact match is still made depends on the shingle; we’ll tell you what’s available after looking.
