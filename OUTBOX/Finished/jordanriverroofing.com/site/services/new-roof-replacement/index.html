Repair or replace? An honest answer first

Not every old roof needs replacing. Knowing when to repair and when to replace protects your home and your budget, so the first step is a free inspection and a straight answer. If targeted repairs will keep a sound roof going, we’ll say so. Reviewers mention it too: “Phil listens. He listened to what we wanted, then advised us whether what we were asking for was possible.”

What a full replacement includes

A replacement starts with a complete tear-off, so the crew can inspect the roof deck itself. Then comes premium underlayment and ice and water shield, the architectural shingles, and new flashing and ventilation. Ventilation matters more than most people think: one customer, Ket, chose us because other bidders “didn’t even include the new ridge venting system” and wanted to keep the old turtle vents. We explain the difference in Ridge Vent vs. Turtle Vents: Which Is Better?.

How long it takes

Every roof is different, but two customers’ reviews give a feel for it. Judy’s full tear-off, with new underlayment, new vents and two chimneys roofed around, “took only 1.5 days”, with a complete clean-up each day. Pat’s was “completed within two days”.

Warranty

On qualifying TAMKO roofs, we offer the TAMKO DiamondShield warranty: up to 25 years of workmanship coverage and a 50-year Full Start period. It needs TAMKO Titan XT or StormFighter FLEX shingles plus four TAMKO accessory products. How the DiamondShield warranty works →

Questions

New Roof & Replacement: common questions

How long does a roof replacement take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the roof. Customers’ reviews mention a full tear-off and new roof finished in 1.5 days and in two days.

Do you tear off the old roof?

Yes. A complete replacement includes a full tear-off and disposal, so the deck can be inspected before the new roof goes on.

Is the new roof warrantied?

Qualifying TAMKO roofs can carry the TAMKO DiamondShield warranty, with up to 25 years of workmanship coverage. See the warranty page for what qualifies.
