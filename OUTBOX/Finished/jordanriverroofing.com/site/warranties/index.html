Warranty

TAMKO DiamondShield Warranty

On qualifying TAMKO roofs, Jordan River Roofing offers the TAMKO DiamondShield enhanced warranty: up to 25 years of workmanship coverage, a 50-year Full Start period, and one transfer within 20 years.

Free Inspection 801-857-1417

What it covers

Most shingle warranties cover the material only. DiamondShield is TAMKO’s top enhanced limited system warranty, and it adds contractor workmanship coverage for up to 25 years: if there’s an installation error, labor, tear-off and re-installation are covered.

The material side has a 50-year Full Start period, the opening stretch of the warranty during which coverage isn’t reduced by proration.

Selling your home?

The warranty can be transferred once within the first 20 years to the next owner.

What a roof needs to qualify

  • Installed by a TAMKO certified contractor at the qualifying level
  • TAMKO Titan XT or StormFighter FLEX shingles
  • At least four specified TAMKO accessory products
  • A full tear-off or new installation
  • Registered with TAMKO by the contractor after the job

We register the warranty with TAMKO when the project is done.

Terms checked against TAMKO’s own TAMKO Edge contractor program summary (tamko.com/edge), 17 October 2026. The full terms are in the warranty document itself.

Questions

Warranty FAQ

What is the TAMKO DiamondShield warranty?

TAMKO’s top enhanced limited system warranty, available only through TAMKO certified contractors at the qualifying level. It adds contractor workmanship coverage to the material warranty.

How long is workmanship covered?

Up to 25 years under DiamondShield, according to TAMKO’s contractor program.

What is the Full Start period?

The opening period of the warranty during which coverage isn’t prorated. TAMKO lists a 50-year Full Start period for DiamondShield.

Can I transfer it if I sell my home?

Yes, once within the first 20 years.

What does a roof need to qualify?

TAMKO Titan XT or StormFighter FLEX shingles, at least four TAMKO accessory products, a full tear-off or new installation, and registration by the contractor.
