Hello, From Salt City Roofers

As you likely have dozens of questions for any contractor about your roofing and exterior remodeling needs, we've tried to summarize some of them so you can get to know us a bit. Find out what makes Salt City Roofers stand apart from other contractors, and why we pride ourselves as a roofing contracting company in a class of our own.

Roofing Repair and Installation Expand

We make roofing easy so you can sit back and enjoy your home and community. Depending on the needs of your home, we will provide tear-off services, re-sheeting with new plywood, and re-roofing with professional quality materials. We wrap every roof project up with both labor and material warranty's so you can continue to enjoy your home for years to come.

Roof Conversions Expand

There are dozens of reasons a home may benefit from a roof conversion, and we can help you achieve the perfect result. Whether for energy savings, aesthetics, or more living space, Salt City Roofers can bring your ideas and needs from mere thoughts into reality.

Cold Roof Systems Expand

Across the state of Utah, we range in zones, snow load, temperatures, elevations, and recreational opportunities, and we love Utah for this variety. At Salt City Roofers, we are aware of the distinct needs of the highest snow-load cities and the need for residents to have specialized roofing systems to meet their needs. Called a cool-roof system, we will install a combination of unique to your climate roofing materials and techniques to ensure your home has the right roof for your zone.

Metal Roofing Expand

Known for its durability and longevity, metal roofing systems have become a great choice for many residences. Over the years, metal choices have increased to include a wide variety of styles and designs, so it is definitely worth looking into this as an option for your home if you've considered it in the past.

All the Accessories Expand

During the process of re-roofing a home, we come across a variety of needs including gutters, skylights, and chimney fixes. While we provide many of these services as part of our normal service offerings, we may run into a scenario where we may bring in a contractor to assist on the project. We source other local contractors who share the same philosophies as we do: Quality and Customer first. Each of our contractors are vetted to make it so you have the most seamless roof installation process on the market.
