About Us

Brock A. Horton, CEO, Asenath Horton, Owner, WVC Past Mayor Mike Winder, ChamberWest President Alan Anderson

Salt City Roofers, a Salt City Builders, Inc. Company, is Utah’s premium roofing contracting and general contracting company in Salt Lake City and Surrounding communities. We bring to your home the finest quality materials and workmanship, resulting in the best roof on your home.

In business since 2007, we have completed roofing and remodeling projects across the state of Utah, from the University of Utah’s Guest house to multiple jr. high and high schools, from massive slate roofing projects to beautiful residential properties.

Brock A. Horton, CEO and Project Manager, holds several industry certifications These include: CertainTeed ShingleMaster, AirVent Installer, Contractor’s EDGE Certificate. He regularly participates in the Professional Roofers Advisory Council (PRAC) for the Greater Salt Lake Region.

Salt City Roofers supports the community through regular Chamber of Commerce support, where Asenath Horton, Owner, received the Volunteer of the Year award in 2010 for outstanding efforts.
