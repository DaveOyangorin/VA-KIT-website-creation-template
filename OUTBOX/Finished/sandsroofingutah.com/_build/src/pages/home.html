Roofing Material Types

At S & S Roofing, we’re proud to offer some of the most trusted and durable roofing solutions available today, including asphalt shingles, metal roofing, and TPO membrane systems. We also have the experience to work with other materials that may be more climate specific or traditional, such as tile, wood/shake shingles, EPDM, and PVC. Every roof has unique needs, and our team is here to guide you through the best options for your home or business. If you have questions about a material not listed, please contact us. We’re always happy to help find the right solution for you.  

Asphalt Shingles

Asphalt shingles are one of the most popular roofing choices thanks to their affordability, durability, and wide range of colors and styles. They’re designed to protect your home from the elements while boosting curb appeal, making them a great all around solution for most residential roofs.

Metal Roofing

Metal roofing is known for its long lifespan, energy efficiency, and sleek appearance. It can withstand harsh weather conditions, including heavy snow and high winds, while reflecting heat to keep your home cooler in the summer. A smart investment if you want both durability and modern style.

TPO Membrane

TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin) is a single-ply roofing membrane commonly used for flat or low slope roofs. It’s highly resistant to UV rays, punctures, and chemicals, while also being energy efficient with its reflective surface. TPO is a top choice for both commercial buildings and residential flat roofs.
