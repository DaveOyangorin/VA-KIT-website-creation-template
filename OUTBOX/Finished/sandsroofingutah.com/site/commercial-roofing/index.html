Commercial Roofing Solutions for Your Business

Your commercial building’s roof plays a critical role in protecting your business, employees, and assets. Whether you need a new installation, routine maintenance, or emergency repairs, our expert commercial roofing services provide durable and cost-effective solutions for you and your business.

Our Commercial Roofing Services

We specialize in a variety of commercial roofing systems, to ensure your property remains secure and energy-efficient. Our services include:

  • Roof Installation – Whether constructing a new building or upgrading an existing one, we offer high-quality roofing materials and professional installation to ensure long-lasting protection.
  • Roof Repair – From minor leaks to major damage, our team quickly assesses and repairs roofing issues to prevent costly structural damage.
  • Roof Replacement – When repairs are no longer an option, we provide efficient roof replacement services with minimal disruption to your business operations.
  • Preventative Maintenance – Routine inspections and maintenance help extend the lifespan of your roof, reducing the risk of unexpected problems and costly repairs.
  • Emergency Roofing Services – When storm damage or other emergencies strike in the Salt Lake City and surrounding areas, we offer quick response services to minimize downtime and keep your property safe.

Types of Commercial Roofing We Offer

Our professionals are skilled in working with a variety of commercial roofing systems. Licensed and insured, along with years of combined experience, you can trust us to protect your business. Our commercial roofing includes:

  • Flat Roofing – A popular choice for commercial buildings, we’re specialists in installing and maintaining TPO, PVC, and EPDM roofing systems that provide energy efficiency and durability.
  • Metal Roofing – Known for its longevity and low maintenance, metal roofing is a strong investment for businesses seeking a long-term solution.
  • Modified Bitumen Roofing – A reliable and flexible roofing material that offers better protection against extreme weather conditions.
  • Built-Up Roofing (BUR) – A time-tested solution that provides excellent waterproofing and insulation.
