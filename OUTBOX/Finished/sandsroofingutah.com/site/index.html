03Layer by layer

From tear-off to final inspection, every layer.

Shingles, underlayment, flashing and ventilation all work together like a team. Drag the handle up the roof to put one on, the way our crews do.

Drag up the slope · drawing for illustration, photos from our jobs

Stage 01 of 05

Down to the deck

From tear-off to final inspection, every layer is installed with precision. It starts at the deck, which plays a key structural role by distributing weight evenly across the home.

Stage 02 of 05

Flashing and underlayment

Leaks can occur due to damaged shingles, broken flashing, or clogged gutters. Shingles, underlayment, flashing and ventilation all work together like a team.

Stage 03 of 05

Course by course

Our experienced team will install your new roof with precision and care, ensuring your home is fully protected.

Stage 04 of 05

Ridge and ventilation

Ventilation is part of the team. In the drawing the ridge is capped and vented: the last layer before the final check.

Stage 05 of 05

Final inspection

After installation, we perform a detailed inspection to ensure the roof is properly installed and meets our high-quality standards.

06In the community

A new roof for the Utah Ukrainian refugee hub.

With materials from Roofers Supply and labor donated by our installers, we replaced the roof on the Utah Ukrainian Refugee Organization’s community hub in Salt Lake City: a gathering place, food pantry and temporary shelter for families displaced by the war.

  1. 01
    The roof

    Leaks, age, visible wear. The home stores food and sometimes shelters families, so leaks, mold or structural damage were a real threat.

  2. 02
    Tear-off

    The entire home and garage roof was stripped down to the bare wood.

  3. 03
    The crew

    Our installers donated their labor, and Roofers Supply donated the materials.

  4. 04
    Every layer

    Every layer was replaced for a secure, leak-free system.

  5. 05
    Finished

    The hub keeps doing its work: a gathering place, a food pantry and a temporary shelter, under a new roof.

09Questions

Good to know.

01What kinds of roofs do you work on?

All roof types: asphalt shingles, metal, flat and low-slope roofs (TPO, EPDM, PVC), tile and slate, and modified bitumen and built-up roofing, on homes, multi-family buildings and commercial properties.

02Do you give free quotes?

Yes. Tell us the address, the roof type (shingle, flat or metal) and whether you need a repair, a full replacement or a recover, and we’ll get back to you with a quote.

03Do you work with HOAs and property managers?

Yes. We help condominium associations, HOA boards and apartment owners with documentation, timelines, budget planning and keeping disruption to residents low.

04Do you offer financing?

Financing is available. Ask about it when you request your quote.

05Do you serve Park City?

Yes. Our Park City line is +1 (435) 658-4344; for the Salt Lake Valley call +1 (801) 272-7000.
