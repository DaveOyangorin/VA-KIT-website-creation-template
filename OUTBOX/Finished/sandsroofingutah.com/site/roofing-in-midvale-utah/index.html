Full Service Roofing in Midvale

Our roofing services include:

  • Re-Roofing & Roof Repair: From small leaks to major storm damage, we offer fast, affordable repair services to protect your home or building.
  • Roof Replacement: If your roof is beyond repair, we provide complete tear off and replacement with a wide selection of durable, energy efficient materials.
  • New Roof Installation: Building a new home or commercial property? Our team works with contractors and property owners to install high quality roofs on time and on budget.
  • Roof Maintenance & Inspections: Preventative care is key. We offer detailed inspections and routine maintenance to help extend the life of your roof.

Residential, Multi-Roofing, & Commercial Roofing

We specialize in both residential, multi-roofing, and commercial roofing projects. No job is too big or too small, whether it’s a single family home or a large industrial building, we bring the same attention to detail and commitment to quality.

Our roofing systems include:

  • Asphalt shingles
  • Metal roofing
  • Flat/low slope roofing (TPO, EPDM, PVC)
  • Tile and slate
  • Modified bitumen and built up roofing

Our team here at S&S Roofing uses quality roofing materials and proven techniques to ensure long lasting performance, weather resistance, and aesthetic appeal. From repairs and replacements to full installations, we’re committed to delivering results that stand the test of time, with a commitment to clear communication and professional service from start to finish.
