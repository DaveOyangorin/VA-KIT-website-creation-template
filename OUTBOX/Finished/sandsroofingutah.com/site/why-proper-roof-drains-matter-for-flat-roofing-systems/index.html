Why Proper Roof Drains Matter for Flat Roofing Systems

By S&S Roofing, Inc. · January 2, 2026

When it comes to flat and low-slope roofing, drainage is everything. Unlike pitched roofs that naturally shed water, flat roofs rely on a carefully designed drainage system to move water safely off the surface. Without proper drains, standing water (also known as ponding) can lead to leaks, structural damage, and premature roof failure.

At S & S Roofing, we install high-performance drainage solutions designed to protect your building, extend the life of your roof, and prevent costly repairs. One of the most effective options we use for TPO roofing systems is the OlyFlow Hercules Drain.

Why Roof Drains Are So Important

Flat roofs are constantly exposed to rain, snowmelt, and debris. Without efficient drainage, water can:

  • Pool and add unnecessary weight to the roof
  • Break down roofing materials over time
  • Leak into insulation, decking, or interior spaces
  • Cause mold, rot, and structural damage
  • Shorten the overall lifespan of the roof

A properly designed drain system prevents these issues by moving water quickly and safely away from the roof surface.

Why OlyFlow Hercules Drains Are Ideal for TPO Roofs

Seamless Compatibility with TPO Membranes

OlyFlow Hercules drains are designed specifically for single-ply roofing systems. Their TPO-coated flange allows the membrane to be hot-air welded directly to the drain, creating a seamless, watertight connection that becomes part of the roofing system itself.

This method eliminates weak points and reduces the risk of future leaks.

Superior Watertight Protection

These drains feature U-Flow mechanical seal technology, which creates a tight, secure connection to existing PVC or cast-iron leader pipes.

  • Prevents water backup
  • Protects interior spaces
  • Exceeds ANSI/SPRI RD1 performance standards
  • Ideal for retrofit or replacement applications

This means long-term reliability and peace of mind.

Built for Durability & Performance

OlyFlow Hercules drains are made from:

  • One-piece spun aluminum body
  • Heavy-duty cast aluminum strainer and clamping ring
  • Corrosion-resistant materials

They’re designed to last and withstand harsh weather conditions without rusting or deteriorating. The depressed sump design also helps water flow efficiently toward the drain, preventing ponding water that adds unnecessary weight and stress to the roof structure.

Faster Drainage with Vortex Breaker Technology

Hercules-Plus models include an integrated vortex breaker, which can increase drainage capacity by up to 2.5 times compared to standard drains. This feature is especially beneficial during:

  • Heavy rainstorms
  • Rapid snowmelt
  • Commercial or large flat-roof applications

Faster drainage = less standing water = longer roof life.

Simple, Efficient Installation Process

One of the biggest advantages of OlyFlow drains is how efficiently they can be installed.

Step-by-Step Installation Overview:

  1. Remove existing drain components (if retrofitting)
  2. Insert the new drain into the existing leader pipe
  3. Secure using the U-Flow mechanical seal
  4. Hot-air weld the TPO membrane to the flange
  5. Install the strainer dome or vortex breaker

Most installations can be completed in 15–30 minutes, saving time and minimizing disruption to building operations. Because installation is done from the rooftop, there’s no need to access interior plumbing, making it ideal for commercial or occupied buildings.

The Bottom Line

A roof is only as strong as its drainage system. High-quality drains like the OlyFlow Hercules protect your roof from water damage, extend its lifespan, and improve overall performance. Especially on TPO systems. At S&S Roofing, we use proven materials and expert installation techniques to ensure your flat roof drains properly, efficiently, and reliably for years to come.
