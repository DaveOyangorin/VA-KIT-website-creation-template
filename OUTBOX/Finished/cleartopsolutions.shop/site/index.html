The first 48 hours

Water keeps working after the leak stops.

It soaks into drywall, under flooring and into cabinets. The longer it sits, the greater the damage and the harder it is to put right. Here’s how a job runs.

  1. 0hCall us anytime

    Reach our 24/7 hotline. Our team responds quickly and helps you schedule water damage restoration or emergency repair.

  2. first hoursAssess & remove the water

    We inspect the property to identify the source and extent of the damage, then extract standing water with pumps and vacuums.

  3. 24–48hDry & dehumidify

    Drying equipment goes in so every affected area is thoroughly dried, inside the window the EPA gives for preventing mold.

  4. doneFinal inspection

    We check that your property is dry and safe, and confirm everything meets your expectations before we leave.
