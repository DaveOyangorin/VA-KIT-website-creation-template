Chimney caps & shrouds

The detail people notice from the street

Black metal boxes over stone and cedar, louvered shrouds, copper bells that weather to brown. A chimney cap keeps out rain and animals; a shroud also changes how the whole roofline reads.

Before you pick one, read our guide: Chimney Cap vs. Chimney Shroud: what’s the difference? It covers what each one does and the code rule for shrouds on factory-built chimneys.
