Signs & Symptoms

Cracks in your home foundation or concrete pads? You should know when to worry as a homeowner. Checkout these signs and symptoms that could be a result of foundation problems and when to call an expert.

On the interior of your home, pay attention to cracks in drywall, concrete floors and ceilings, sticking or misaligned doors and/or windows, and sloping or sagging floors. On the exterior of your home, look for gaps windows, doors or archways, cracks in your foundation, water intrusion, leaning chimneys, soil separation around your foundation, and water pooling or draining around your home. If you have a basement, look for bowed walls, water leaking, cracked floors or walls, and water stains on your floors and walls.

Common questions

01How much does foundation repair cost?

The cost of foundation repair varies depending on the severity of the problem and the type of repair needed. However, it is typically a significant investment. We offer free inspections and estimates so you can get a clear understanding of the costs involved

02How long does foundation repair take?

The time it takes to repair a foundation depends on the extent of the damage and the type of repair method used. Most repairs can be completed in a few days.

03What are the different types of foundation repair methods?

There are a variety of foundation repair methods available, including:

* Piering: This involves installing steel piers into the ground to support the foundation.

* Wall anchors: These are used to stabilize bowing or leaning foundation walls.

* Waterproofing: This helps to prevent water from entering the basement or crawlspace and causing further damage.

04Do you offer a warranty on your foundation repair services?

Yes, we offer a  warranty on our foundation repair services.

05How do I choose a foundation repair contractor?

It's important to choose a qualified and experienced foundation repair contractor. Be sure to get multiple quotes and ask for references. You can also check online reviews.

06What are the consequences of not repairing a foundation problem?

If foundation problems are not addressed, they can lead to:

* Structural damage to your home

* Decreased property value

* Safety hazards
