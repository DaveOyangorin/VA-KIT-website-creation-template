Case 01 · Artesian Road, Orem

A house the city planned to condemn.

Case 01 · 1 / 6

The house

A 1995 split-level on Artesian Road in Orem: 5 bedrooms, 3 baths, 2,100 square feet. Doors out of square, walls cracked.

Case 01 · 2 / 6

11 inches

Years of shifting had sunk one corner 11 inches. The city planned to condemn the building.

Case 01 · 3 / 6

A swamp underneath

When we dug, the soil smelled like a swamp: lake-bed sediment, a natural spring under the front of the property, a high water table across the street.

Case 01 · 4 / 6

23 helical piers

Driven 33 to 40 feet, some to 43, to reach load-bearing soil. Brackets on all four walls let the house rise evenly.

Case 01 · 5 / 6

Gas lines by hand

Pipes move when a house moves, so the gas lines were dug out by hand first to give them room.

Case 01 · 6 / 6

Passed

Void fills under the lifted sections, then an independent structural engineer’s inspection. The home passed, and the condemnation plans were canceled.

Questions

Good to know.

01Is the inspection really free?

Yes. We inspect the foundation, explain what we find, and give you a detailed, no-pressure estimate if a repair is needed. Sometimes the honest answer is that it isn’t.

02What signs should I look for?

Inside: cracks in drywall, floors and ceilings, sticking or misaligned doors and windows, sloping floors. Outside: gaps around windows and doors, foundation cracks, leaning chimneys, soil separating from the foundation, water pooling. In a basement: bowed walls, leaks, cracked floors or walls, water stains.

03Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We install helical pile systems for everything from residential decks to multi-story commercial properties.

04Where do you work?

We have offices in Salt Lake City and St. George and work across Utah, including Ogden, Park City, Provo, Herriman, Price, Cedar City, Kanab and Duck Creek Village.

05Can you work in winter?

Ask us about your project. Utah weather affects some exterior work, but interior repairs and many pier installations can go ahead year-round.
