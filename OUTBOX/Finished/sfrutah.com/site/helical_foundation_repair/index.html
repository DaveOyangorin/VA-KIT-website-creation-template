Signs & Symptoms

Addressing foundation damage early can prevent more expensive repairs down the line. Common signs of structural issues include foundation settlement.

  • Uneven or sloping floors
  • Cracks in walls or ceilings
  • Gaps around windows and doors
  • Doors that stick or won’t latch
  • Visible movement or separation in the foundation

These warning signs may indicate that your foundation system is no longer properly supported. Helical pier foundation repair is one of the most effective ways to stabilize shifting or settling structures—especially in areas like Utah, where soil conditions can be unpredictable. Helical piers are also highly effective for foundation leveling, correcting uneven or sinking foundations without the need for costly rebuilding.

Schedule a free foundation inspection. Contact us today to see if your foundation system needs attention. Helical piles or another solution is right for your property.

Why Helical Piers?

Helical piers—also known as screw piles—are steel shafts with helical (spiral-shaped) plates that are advanced deep into the ground until they reach stable soil. This allows them to support and lift your foundation back to its original position using push piers.

Benefits of using helical piers:

  • Fast, minimally invasive installation
  • Ideal for both new construction and repair
  • Long-lasting and resistant to corrosion
  • Applicable for residential and commercial properties

Common questions

01What are helical piers, and how do they work?

Helical piers are deep foundation supports made of steel with spiral plates that allow them to be screwed into load-bearing soil. They transfer the weight of your structure to stable layers, stopping settlement and even lifting sinking areas.

02How much does helical pier installation cost?

The cost depends on depth requirements, soil type, and the size of your footing. We offer free inspections and detailed quotes so you can make informed decisions without pressure.

03Do helical piers work for commercial buildings?

Yes, we install helical pile systems for everything from residential decks to multi-story commercial properties. They’re scalable and adaptable to virtually any structure.
