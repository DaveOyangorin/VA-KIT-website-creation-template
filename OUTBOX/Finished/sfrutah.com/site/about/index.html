About Superior Foundation Repair Utah

At Superior Foundation Repair, our mission is simple: to provide exceptional foundation repair in Utah and waterproofing services that combine expert craftsmanship, integrity, and a customer-first approach.

Our company culture is founded on strong organizational values, transparent leadership, and a commitment to open communication with our clients and the community. Founded in 2019 by Matt Baker, we have built a reputation for delivering top-quality repairs that are both reliable and competitively priced

When Matt Baker founded Superior Foundation Repair, he saw a gap in the industry for a service provider that put customer satisfaction and quality at the forefront. The inspiration for starting the company came from Matt’s desire to raise industry standards and help homeowners feel confident in their repairs.

Our origin story is rooted in Matt’s personal motivation to build a trustworthy business after witnessing common industry shortcomings. Since its founding, the company's history has been marked by continuous growth and evolution, adapting to new challenges and expanding our expertise to better serve our community.

On the interior of your home, pay attention to cracks in drywall, concrete floors and ceilings, sticking or misaligned doors and/or windows, and sloping or sagging floors.

On the exterior of your home, look for gaps in windows, doors or archways, cracks in your foundation, water intrusion, leaning chimneys, soil separation around your foundation, and water pooling or draining around your home.

If you have a basement, look for bowed walls, water leaking, cracked floors or walls, and water stains on your floors and walls.

At the heart of Superior Foundation Repair is our close-knit, dedicated team. Our employees embody the company's values of integrity, reliability, and excellence, actively contributing to our mission of delivering top-quality foundation repair services.

We’ve worked together for years and have a deep understanding of all aspects of foundation repair. Our team brings years of experience and expertise, making sure your property gets the attention and care it deserves.

You can trust that when you work with us, you’re not just another job; you’re part of our family. We treat each of our customers with respect and dedication, ensuring the job is done to the highest standard with minimal disruption to your day-to-day life.
