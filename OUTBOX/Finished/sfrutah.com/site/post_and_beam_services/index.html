Signs & Symptoms

A failing foundation can lead to serious structural integrity concerns if not addressed promptly. A post and beam foundation can cause widespread issues in your home. Here are the most common warning signs to look out for:

Soft or Sagging Floors:

If your floors feel spongy or dip in certain areas, your support beams or joists may be weakening. Damaged or uneven flooring is also a common indicator of foundation damage.

Wall and Ceiling Cracks:

Gaps or cracks near windows and doors often indicate shifting in your foundation supports, which can lead to serious foundation problems. Drywall cracks are a frequent sign of foundation damage and should not be ignored.

Post or Pier Movement:

Visibly leaning or sinking piers are a serious sign that your foundation is unstable and may require helical piers for stabilization.

Separation at Walls and Floors:

Noticeable gaps along the baseboards can mean that the structure is pulling away from its supports.

Moisture or Mold Below:

Excess moisture around wood piers or beams can lead to rot and long-term structural damage.

Common questions

01What is a post and beam foundation?

Also known as pier and beam, this type of foundation uses vertical posts to support horizontal beams under the home. It’s common in older homes or those with crawl spaces that experience foundation settlement.

02How do I know if I need post and beam foundation repair?

Signs include uneven floors, cracked walls, leaning piers, visible wood damage beneath your home, cracks in the foundation wall, or issues in the basement such as water intrusion or moisture problems. If you’re unsure, our team can perform a full inspection.

Repair costs of foundation repair beams can vary depending on damage severity and home size, We provide a Free inspection of foundation repair tailored to the specific needs of your home.

04What repair methods are used?

Typical methods include beam replacement, adding new piers, re-shimming, house leveling, foundation wall bracing, and basement repairs. Every repair plan is customized based on your foundation’s condition, and we address issues in basements such as bowing or leaning walls.
