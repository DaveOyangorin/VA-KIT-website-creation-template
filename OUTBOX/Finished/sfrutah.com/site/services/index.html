1. Foundation Repair

Complete structural foundation solutions for settling, cracking, and instability issues. Our foundation repair specialists use proven techniques and American-made materials to restore your home's structural integrity permanently.

2. Concrete Crack Repair

Professional concrete crack injection and structural repairs for foundation walls, basement floors, and exterior concrete structures. We address both cosmetic and structural cracks to prevent water intrusion and further damage.

3. Helical Piles

Deep-driven steel pier systems that anchor below unstable soil layers for permanent foundation support. Our helical piles experts provide the most reliable foundation stabilization solution for Utah's challenging soil conditions.

4. Mobile Home Leveling

Specialized leveling services for manufactured homes using precision jack adjustments and permanent support systems. Our mobile home leveling saves time and money while ensuring long-term stability.

5. Crawl Space Cleaning

Professional debris removal and sanitization services to improve air quality and provide safe utility access. Our crawl space cleaning transforms neglected areas into clean, functional spaces.

6. Crawl Space Encapsulation

Complete moisture barrier installation and climate control systems that turn crawl spaces into dry, usable areas. Our encapsulation services prevent mold, improve energy efficiency, and protect structural components.

7. New Construction Waterproofing

Preventive waterproofing systems for new builds to avoid future structural damage and moisture problems. Protect your investment from day one with our comprehensive new construction waterproofing solutions.

8. Basement Waterproofing

Complete interior and exterior waterproofing systems designed specifically for Utah's mountain snowmelt and seasonal moisture challenges.

9. Post and Beam Repair

Our post and beam repair services address foundation support issues common in older Utah construction.

10. House Leveling

Sophisticated lifting systems using up to 20-ton jacks for precise foundation correction. Our house leveling services restore proper foundation alignment and eliminate structural problems caused by settling.

Yes, we serve both residential homeowners and commercial property owners throughout Utah. Our foundation specialists have experience with everything from single-family homes to large commercial buildings, adapting our techniques to each property's unique needs.

02Do you offer warranties on your foundation repair services?

Yes, we stand behind all our work with comprehensive warranties. Our American-made products and proven installation techniques ensure long-lasting results, and we guarantee the quality of our foundation repair services throughout Utah.

03Can you perform foundation services during Utah winters?

We provide foundation repair services year-round throughout Utah. While some exterior work may be weather-dependent, most foundation repairs including basement waterproofing, crawl space work, and structural repairs can be completed during winter months.
