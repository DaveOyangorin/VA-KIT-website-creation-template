## Design (premium, v3 redesign, 20 Oct 2026)

- **Research (screenshotted and reviewed):** 12 industry sites (Big-D, Layton, Jacobsen, Mortenson, Ram Jack, Copper X, Baker Roofing, Strike Group, Power Design, Tremco Roofing; Groundworks blocked by a bot check; Okland timed out). Chosen three:
  - **Ram Jack** (foundation repair): dark, bold, high-contrast presentation.
  - **Big-D** (Utah): a map of its offices, dark condensed type.
  - **Mortenson**: a full-bleed photo hero with DIN-style condensed caps.
- **Concept, "The honest inspection", v3:** a full-bleed golden-hour hero (their pier rig at a Southern Utah home) under a transparent header, with a Google-review card; an I-15 city marquee; an editorial intro and counted stats; a review wall led by the "what we DIDN'T need" quote; the **sticky Orem case story** as the signature (their own job photos cross-fade step by step from the sunken corner to the engineer's sign-off, ending on the drawn pier section); a bento of all ten services with their gold icons and photos; a split with the drawn Utah map (the I-15 line draws itself); a parallax band with the homeowner quote from KUTV; an owner split with Matt's photo and the KUTV still.
- **Inner pages:** full-bleed job-photo headers; pages without a photo get the navy ground with their gold service icon.
- **Brand guide:** logo navy #0C0F3A and grey #B9B8BD, icon gold #E9C12C (buttons, gold on navy passes contrast), red rock #B4532A, sandstone #F6F3EC. Fonts: Barlow Condensed (uppercase display), Public Sans (text), JetBrains Mono (labels), all Google Fonts.
- **Motion:** reveals, parallax, marquee, counters, case cross-fades, the map line drawing; all off with reduced motion.
