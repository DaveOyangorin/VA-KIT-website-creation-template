Common questions

01How do I know if my deck needs repair or replacement?

Minor issues like loose boards or small cracks can usually be repaired. However, a full replacement may be necessary if your deck has severe structural damage or extensive rotting wood.

02Can I repair my deck myself?

DIY repairs are possible for small issues, but for safety and long-lasting results, we recommend hiring a professional. Our experts can detect underlying problems that may not be visible to the untrained eye, ensuring your deck is restored properly.

03Do you offer emergency deck repair services?

Yes! If you have an urgent fix need, we’re ready to assist with fast, reliable service across Utah and the surrounding areas. We understand how important it is to address issues promptly.

04What types of decks do you repair?

While we specialize in wood deck repair, we also have extensive experience working with composite and other materials. No matter the type of deck, our team is equipped to provide effective repairs

05How long will my deck repair take?
