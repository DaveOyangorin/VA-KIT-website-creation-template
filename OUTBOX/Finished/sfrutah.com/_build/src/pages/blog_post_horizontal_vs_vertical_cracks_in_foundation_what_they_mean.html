How much does it cost to have a plumber snake your pipes?

Costs depend on the location of the clog and how severe it is. A simple sink or shower line is usually on the lower end; main line blockages or heavy roots take more time and specialty equipment. We provide up‑front pricing after a quick assessment—and we’ll only recommend the work you actually need.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service in American Fork, Lehi, and Salt Lake City?

Yes. Our emergency plumber team is on call around the clock for burst pipes, sewer backups, no‑hot‑water situations, overflowing fixtures, and other urgent issues.
