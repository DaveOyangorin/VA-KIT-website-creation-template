Symptom finder

What are you seeing on your roof?

Pick a symptom. The manual shows the likely causes on Fig. 1 and the page with the fix.

Diagnosis 01

Water stain on a ceiling

Water usually travels before it shows. The most common sources are flashing at a chimney, skylight or wall, a cracked pipe boot, or a valley.

Check on Fig. 1: 1 Chimney flashing, 3 Pipe boot, 4 Skylight, 2 Valley

Go to the fix

Diagnosis 02

Missing or lifted shingles

Wind, hail and age loosen shingles, most often along the ridge, the rakes and the eaves. Exposed felt lets water in quickly.

Check on Fig. 1: 6 Ridge and vents, 5 Eaves / ice dam

Go to the fix

Diagnosis 03

Icicles and ice at the eaves

Ice dams form when roof snow melts and refreezes at the cold eaves, backing water up under the shingles.

Check on Fig. 1: 5 Eaves / ice dam

Go to the fix

Diagnosis 04

Water pooling on a flat roof

Ponding points to sagging, blocked drains or worn membrane. Standing water finds seams and punctures.

Flat roofs: see the membrane and drains.

Go to the fix

Diagnosis 05

Damage after a hail or wind storm

Bruised or cracked shingles, dented metal and torn membrane are common after storms. Document it before you call your insurer.

Check on Fig. 1: 6 Ridge and vents, 2 Valley

Go to the fix

Diagnosis 06

An old roof that keeps leaking

When leaks keep moving and repairs keep adding up, the roof may be at the end of its life.

Check on Fig. 1: 1 Chimney flashing, 2 Valley, 3 Pipe boot, 4 Skylight, 5 Eaves / ice dam, 6 Ridge and vents

Go to the fix
