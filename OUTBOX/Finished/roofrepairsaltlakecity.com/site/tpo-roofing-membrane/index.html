TPO Roofing Membrane in Salt Lake City

(385) 355-4849Free Estimate
Material: tpo

Roof Repair Salt Lake City installs and repairs TPO roofing membrane in Salt Lake City on homes and commercial buildings with flat or low-slope roofs.

TPO which is short for Thermoplastic Polyolefin, it is the mixture of polypropylene and ethylene-propylene (rubber), these elements are combined using state-of-the-art polymer manufacturing technology. This creates the perfect residential roofing system that is very sturdy, affordable and energy efficient. It is also very easy to install and is environmentally friendly. Systems can be installed fully adhered to or mechanically fastened. TPO is the number one solution for new construction, re-roofing projects and overlays.

Built to Withstand

A roof that is black can reach very high temperatures in the summertime, sometimes in excess of 170*F. However, the highly reflective white surface of EverGuard TPO can be as low as 110*F in the same weather. This is due to the high reflective and emittance qualities of the white membrane; much less solar radiation is penetrated through the roof and into your home. In today’s economy energy prices have sky-rocketed. The white EverGuard thermoplastic roofing system is definitely the way to go when it comes to quality and affordability.
