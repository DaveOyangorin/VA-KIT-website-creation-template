Driveway sealing

Armour Crete provides driveway sealing in St. George and across Washington County, Utah. DECO 20 Clear, penetrating, no film. Back on it in 24 hours.

Your driveway takes more punishment than any other concrete on the property:

full sun, vehicle weight, and every winter night that dips below freezing.

We clean it, seal it with a penetrating Deco product, and hand it back to you in twenty-four hours.

Before you drive on it again

Films, streaks or plastic sheen, because it soaks in

The hardest-working
slab you own.

A west-facing driveway in Washington County swings through more than a hundred degrees between a January night and a July afternoon, and concrete moves with every degree of it. That movement opens hairline cracks; the slab is porous, so water follows the cracks down and carries dissolved salt with it: off the road, out of the softener discharge, up from the soil itself. When that water freezes, or the salt crystallises as it dries, the surface gets pushed apart from the inside. Scaling, spalling and the white bloom of efflorescence are all the same story.

Sealing does not stop the movement. Nothing does. It keeps the pore network from staying saturated, which starves freeze–thaw and salt crystallisation of the one thing they both need.

Cracks and failed joints get handled first. Sealer over a broken surface is money down the drain, so filling, patching and expansion joint work come before anything else goes down.

Four steps. No shortcuts
in any of them.

  1. Clean

    The slab gets cleaned end to end. Nothing goes down over dirt.

    Dry

    We wait for it to dry completely. Sealer over moisture is how finishes fail.

    Seal

    Deco product, matched to the surface and the exposure it lives in.

    Cure

    Twenty-four hours before you walk or drive on it. Then it is yours again.

    DECO 20 Clear,
    as a rule.

    • A reactive penetrating sealer that soaks in rather than sitting on top
    • Dries 100% transparent: no film, no sheen, no sign it is there
    • Sharply slows how fast mould and mildew come back after a wash
    • Non-hazardous, water-based, no solvents

    It goes down early in the morning, before the slab has taken on the day's heat. On a hot surface it flash-dries and leaves whiting and streaks.

    In southern Utah that is not a small detail: it is most of the job. If you want the colour deepened instead of left alone, that is the wet-look conversation.

    Common questions

    01How long am I without the driveway?

    Twenty-four hours after the sealer goes down. Plan for one day of street parking and you will not be caught out. We would rather tell you up front than have you find out on the way to work.

    02Will it change how the driveway looks?

    Not with the clear penetrating sealer. It dries fully transparent.

    Wet-look and decorative finishes deliberately deepen the colour, and that is a choice, not a side effect. We show you the difference on your own concrete before anything is committed to.

    03My driveway is brand new. Can it be sealed now?

    Not immediately. Fresh concrete has to cure and give up its moisture first, and sealing over that is how finishes fail early. If you are mid build, talk to us at the pour and we will schedule it properly.

    04How often does it need doing again?

    It depends on the product and the exposure: a shaded slab and a west-facing one do not wear at anything like the same rate. Our maintenance program runs on a two-year cycle: we come back, clean it, and put a fresh coat down before the surface ever gets far enough gone to need restoring.
