The Deco Sealers We Use

Six Deco products, matched to the surface and the exposure it lives in.

Free Assessment 435-994-5235

Materials

Deco Products has been manufacturing in Denver since 1991, water-based and solvent-free from the start. We are a certified Deco applicator, which is what lets us put down the restricted products. Shine-A-Cast and the Deco 20

Seal foundation membrane are approved-applicator only. Which sealer goes on your property is decided by the surface, the exposure and the finish you want, not by what happened to be on the truck.

1991

Coverage

1,200–1,500 sq ft / 5 gal

  • Driveways
  • Walkways
  • Patios
  • Garage floors
  • Roof tile

The original, and still the default.

A non-hazardous reactive sealer that soaks in rather than sitting on top, and dries 100% transparent: no film, no sheen, no sign it is there. It is the one we reach for on ordinary flatwork, and it is the product Deco built the company on back in 1991.

  • 100% transparent, so it changes nothing about how the surface looks
  • Sharply slows how fast mould and mildew come back after a wash
  • The only clear sealer ICC-approved for dampproofing basement foundations
  • Non-hazardous, water-based, no solvents

It goes down early in the morning, before the slab has taken on the day’s heat. On a hot surface it flash-dries and leaves whiting and streaks. In southern Utah that is not a small detail: it is most of the job.

Coverage

1,000–1,500 sq ft / 5 gal

  • Pool decks
  • Salt pool surrounds
  • Softener discharge
  • Driveways
  • Sidewalks

For pool decks, and anywhere else salt lands.

A clear water-based silane/siloxane emulsion that penetrates deep into the slab to block water and the chlorides riding in it. Salt is the specific problem: it dissolves in water, soaks into the pores, then crystallises as that water evaporates, and crystals growing inside a confined pore push hard enough to break the surface apart from underneath.

  • Does not change the skid or slip resistance of the concrete
  • Dries clear, with no film and no residue
  • Reduces spalling, freeze–thaw damage, dusting and mildew
  • Non-hazardous, non-toxic, non-flammable

This is the answer to the pool-deck question. It is a penetrating sealer, not a coating, so it does not make a wet deck slicker than it already was.

Coverage

700–1,000 sq ft / 5 gal

  • Paver driveways
  • Stone patios
  • Roof tile
  • Retaining walls

Wet look, without the film.

Beauty Enhancing Sealer, a non-hazardous polymer emulsion for porous units: concrete pavers, roof and barrel tile, natural stone. It penetrates and deepens the material’s own colour from below rather than laying a shiny film on top, and it carries two forms of UV protection.

  • Never needs stripping before it can be re-coated
  • Enhances colour from within the substrate, not from a surface film
  • Two types of UV protection
  • Lowest coverage of the range, because porous units simply drink more

Worth knowing the difference: a topical acrylic sits on the surface as a film. Films can yellow under UV, they can peel, and taking one off means harsh chemicals. This does not build a film, so none of that applies.

Coverage

750–1,250 sq ft / 5 gal

  • Wet-look driveways
  • Decorative patios
  • Paver work
  • Feature entries

When you want it obvious.

A hybrid: it penetrates to enhance the colour and also leaves a protective layer on the surface, which is what produces the high shine and what locks paver joint sand in place. It will bring a sun-faded surface back, and pigment can be added to tune the colour.

  • Wet-look, high-shine finish
  • Locks joint sand on paver work
  • UV protected and scratch resistant
  • Restricted to approved applicators

This one leaves a surface layer, so for a pool deck or anywhere that gets walked on wet it is a conversation rather than a default. Salt Shield is usually the better call there.

Coverage

750–1,250 sq ft / 5 gal

  • Travertine
  • Spanish tile
  • Flagstone
  • Stone coping

For stone that will not absorb anything.

A single-component acrylic built for non-porous materials (travertine, Spanish tile, natural stone) where a penetrating sealer has nothing to penetrate into. It bonds to the surface instead, and comes UV protected.

  • Adheres to non-porous stone and tile that penetrating sealers cannot treat
  • Strong chemical resistance, and holds up against water blushing
  • Guards against most staining and helps prevent discolouration
  • Self-levelling, though back-rolling while wet helps

Travertine is everywhere around southern Utah pools, and it stains in ways that do not come back out. This is a topical acrylic, so it will need redoing more often than the penetrating products above.

Varies with asphalt condition

  • Asphalt driveways
  • Blacktop parking
  • Private lanes

Asphalt, without the tar.

Almost every blacktop sealer on the market is an asphalt or tar-based product: a by-product of the oil industry, painted back onto your driveway. This is not that. It is a blend of natural oils and co-polymers that conditions dried-out asphalt as well as sealing it, so an old grey driveway gets its colour and its flexibility back rather than just getting painted over.

  • Contains no asphalt and no tar
  • Conditions old, dried-out asphalt rather than coating it
  • Chemical and UV resistant
  • Dries fast, and cleans up with soap and water

It gets sprayed and back-rolled, never squeegeed. A squeegee pulls too much material to the surface and you end up with a driveway that looks painted.

Coverage varies with surface condition and porosity. A rough, older or thirstier slab drinks more, so treat these as planning figures rather than promises. Every job gets a test section before the rest of it goes down.

Deco 20 Seal, on the
foundation side.

The waterproofing membrane we spray on new foundation walls is a hybrid acrylic resin for poured concrete, pre-cast, masonry and ICF forms. It goes on as one continuous coating with no seams to fail, it can be applied to damp walls, and it removes the need for 6 mil poly.

  • Approved by the International Code Council

  • Independently tested to

    ASTM C 1306 for hydrostatic pressure over cracks,

    C 836 for low-temperature flexibility, crack bridging and adhesion strength,

    D 2939 for water resistance, and

    E 96 for water vapour permeance.
