What Is The Best Time Of Year To Install Windows?

By EZ RoofingPublished 2025-10-15
Free Inspection (888) 575-3025

I recently had EZ Roofing in Cabot, Arkansas, handle my window installation, and the process was smooth and professional. They replaced my old, drafty windows with energy-efficient models just before the cold season. The team managed the full window replacement, from measuring and removing old windows to installing and sealing the new ones. I highly recommend EZ Roofing for homeowners seeking reliable service.

Why Did I Decide To Replace My Windows In Cabot, AR?

As a homeowner in Cabot, I started noticing that my energy bills were increasing every month, especially as fall approached. My home felt colder near the windows, and during strong Arkansas winds, I could feel drafts sneaking in. My old wooden window frames had cracked paint, worn seals, and some of the glass had scratches and small foggy spots. The windows were over 20 years old, and it was clear they were affecting my home’s comfort and energy efficiency.

I live in a brick ranch-style home with multiple large windows in the living and dining rooms. Each window is different in size, which made me hesitant to attempt any DIY replacement.

On top of that, fall weather in Arkansas can be unpredictable, with cooler nights and occasional rain, which made me realize the timing of the installation was important.

I began searching for a local roofing company that also offered professional window services. That’s when I found EZ Roofing, known for both roofing and window replacement in Cabot.

What Problems Did My Old Windows Cause?

Old or damaged windows affect more than just energy bills. In my case, I noticed several issues:

  • Energy Inefficiency: My home was losing heat in the winter and cool air in the summer. This made my HVAC work harder, raising utility costs.
  • Drafts and Moisture: Leaky seals allowed cold air to enter, and in some rooms, I noticed condensation between panes.
  • Security Concerns: The old locking mechanisms were outdated and less secure than modern windows.
  • Noise Pollution: Street noise felt louder inside because the older windows didn’t have effective sound insulation.
  • Aesthetics: The worn frames and scratched glass made the house look dated, impacting curb appeal.

I realized that window replacement was the best way to fix these issues and improve both comfort and home value.

How EZ Roofing Managed My Window Installation

EZ Roofing handled the window installation professionally from start to finish. Their team scheduled a detailed consultation to measure all windows and discuss energy-efficient options. I appreciated that they explained the differences in materials, like vinyl, fiberglass, and wood-clad options, and helped me choose ones suitable for Cabot’s climate.

During installation, the crew removed each old window carefully to avoid damaging walls or siding. They inspected the frames for any hidden rot or damage and made small repairs as needed. They used professional-grade tools and techniques to ensure a perfect fit. Each new window was sealed with high-quality caulking to prevent leaks and improve insulation.

I was impressed with how efficiently the team worked. They coordinated the removal and installation of multiple windows in a logical order, so the process was smooth and organized. They even wore shoe covers and cleaned up each day, leaving my home tidy. The entire project, for a mid-sized ranch-style home with 10 windows, was completed in two full days.

Materials and Energy Efficiency

EZ Roofing recommended energy-efficient windows with double-pane Low-E glass, which reduces heat transfer while letting in natural light. The frames were insulated and durable, perfect for Arkansas weather. Choosing windows with proper R-values helped me understand how much energy I could save each month.

The installation team explained how the windows’ locking mechanisms and tilt-in features would make maintenance easier and improve security. I also learned that the type of window I selected could help reduce noise from nearby traffic, making my home quieter.

Seasonal Factors And Timing For Window Replacement In Cabot

Fall proved to be the ideal time for my window installation. Early fall temperatures were moderate, which helped ensure the caulking and sealants were set correctly. If I had waited until winter, cold nights and occasional frost could have affected the sealing process and made installation more difficult. Summer would have been hotter, making the work less comfortable and potentially slowing curing times for sealants.

Scheduling installation before the cold season also allowed me to maximize energy efficiency, keeping my home warmer without immediately relying on the heater. Planning ahead gave me peace of mind knowing my windows were ready for winter weather.

How Much Should I Expect To Pay For Window Installation In Cabot, AR?

For my project, the cost of window replacement varied depending on the size and material of the windows. Typical pricing for Cabot ranges:

  • Standard vinyl windows: $350–$500 each
  • Premium wood-clad or fiberglass windows: $600–$700 each

The total project cost depended on the number of windows, any repairs to existing frames, and the energy-efficiency options selected. EZ Roofing provided a clear, written estimate up front, which made budgeting simple.

The job took two full days, which is typical for a home of this size. Larger homes or custom-sized windows may require extra time.

Frequently Asked Questions About Window Replacement

How Do I Know If My Windows Need Replacing?

Check for drafts, foggy panes, damaged frames, or difficulty opening and closing. Rising energy bills can also indicate aging windows.

Can I Replace Windows Myself?

DIY replacement is possible, but mistakes can cause air leaks, water intrusion, or frame damage. Professional installation ensures proper fit and sealing.

Will New Windows Increase My Home’s Value?

Yes. Modern, energy-efficient windows improve curb appeal and are attractive to buyers.

How Often Should Windows Be Replaced In Arkansas?

Quality windows last 15–25 years. Homes exposed to humidity or temperature swings may need earlier replacement.

Does Window Installation Affect The Roof Or Siding?

Not directly. However, companies like EZ Roofing can inspect nearby roofing or siding to prevent leaks or hidden damage during installation.

What Types Of Windows Work Best In Cabot, AR?

Energy-efficient double-pane or Low-E glass windows are ideal. Vinyl and fiberglass frames handle seasonal changes well and require minimal maintenance.
