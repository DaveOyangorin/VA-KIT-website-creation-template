Brockington Rd. Commercial Plaza

This project was contracted with EZ Roofing after successfully replacing the landlord of this plaza’s personal roof. We sent our Roof Inspectors Storm, Cameron, as well as the owner Joseph to the plaza to create a detailed damage report. Our inspection revealed that there was hail damaged shingles and hail damaged vents.

We helped the homeowner file a claim and met with their adjuster who we have a good and trusting relationship with, which made the approval of this roof a quick and smooth process, it was approved immediately. About a week later, the adjuster report came back and within 10 days we were completing the roof replacement.

We upgraded the property from a 3-tab shingle to Architectural with a synthetic felt underlayment. We installed drip edge around the perimeter, starter strip, and ice & water in the valleys, sidewalls and all penetrations to prevent water intrusion.

A full roof replacement and the above upgrades only cost the landlord their $2,000 deductible. What was once a leaking and dilapidated roof is now a brand new, leak and damage free, attractive roof which has increased the property value, all for the low cost of $2,000. With our six nail shingle installation, workmanship warranty, and lifetime material warranty, this landlord has nothing to worry about and we will be here in the future if he needs us.

Upgrades to this Roof

– Architectual shingle

– Ice & water in all valleys, sidewalls, and sealing all roof penetrations.

– Synthetic Felt

– Drip Edge

– Starter Strip on the eaves

– 6 Nail per shingle “High Wind Nailing Pattern”

OUR FREE INSPECTION

The best way to assess your roof’s condition is to take advantage of our free inspection.

We do a physical evaluation of all aspects of your roof including shingle and slope wear-and-tear and overall roof condition. We also assess the condition of other exterior home features, including soffit, fascia, gutters, windows, and siding. That’s why we commonly refer to our roof inspections as property evaluations.

Our team’s property evaluation accurately determines your property’s condition and acts as a guide for keeping your home in top shape. In fact, we recommend a free inspection each year for that reason.
