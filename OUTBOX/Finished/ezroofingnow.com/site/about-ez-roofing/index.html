ABOUT EZ ROOFING

About us

EZ Roofing, LLC was formed from the ground-up in Salt Lake City, Utah and Cabot, Arkansas in 2017.

Before EZ Roofing was even founded, our founder Brian Stephens knew he wanted to build more than just a construction company — he wanted to redefine what homeowners could expect from a contractor. Built on a foundation of honesty, reliability, and integrity, these weren’t just business goals; they were the values Brian was raised with. This vision led to the Welcome Home Guarantee — a commitment that our core values show up at every step of your project, whether you’re working with Brian directly or one of our Project Managers. By treating our team like family, we’ve created a culture of care that extends directly to you.

Customer Service
We believe that customer service is not only important but should be our primary focus and that integrity and honesty is important on every job. It is how we can deliver an outstanding experience and product.

Pride of Workmanship
Our professional team will work with you to deliver the best results for even the smallest project. Every installation follows our proprietary EZ Shield Roofing System — a standard that includes synthetic felt, ice & water barrier, full perimeter drip edge, real starter strip, 6-nail high-wind nailing pattern rated for 130 MPH, hail-resistant ridge cap, and comprehensive flashing at all walls and penetrations. We don’t compromise on standards.

The Catch-All System
Before a single shingle is touched on your property, we deploy our proprietary Catch-All System — heavy-duty tarps and full perimeter protection that shield your landscaping, siding, windows, and everything around your home. We treat your property like it’s our own. That’s our Welcome Home Guarantee.

Live Digital Timeline
During every full replacement, your dedicated Project Manager is on-site from start to finish. You’ll receive a Live Digital Timeline — real-time photos and status updates sent directly to you as they happen. No matter where you are, you’ll always know exactly what’s going on with your roof.

New Services
At EZ Roofing, we’re always looking for new ways to better serve our community. In addition to our trusted roofing solutions, we’re proud to now offer professional Siding Services, Window Services, and Door Installation. Whether you’re looking to refresh your home’s exterior, improve energy efficiency, or enhance curb appeal, our skilled team is ready to deliver high-quality craftsmanship you can count on.

A Job Well Done
When your home or business needs attention, our seasoned professionals are trained to work with you to assess your needs for the roofing project that will improve your property within your budget.

Our Service Area
We have two main offices located in Utah and Arkansas. We serve all of Salt Lake City, UT and the surrounding areas; as well as Little Rock, AR and the surrounding areas.

And we are licensed, bonded and insured.
We carry over $2,000,000 in general liability insurance — more than $1,000,000 above what the state requires. The moment a member of our team walks onto your property, all parties are fully protected. We are also a licensed Residential & Commercial Builder, which requires more knowledge and expertise to obtain than a standard roofing license.

Manufacturer Certified, Preferred Contractor

As an Owens Corning Preferred Contractor, we are approved and certified by the largest shingle brand. This manufacturer warranties our installation directly — we met their standard for providing the highest-quality products and customer service, and we passed their background check requirements for being licensed and insured.

We’re also Google Guaranteed — meaning we passed a rigorous screening process by Google, including checks for business licenses, insurance, and other credentials. Google provides a money-back guarantee if a customer is not satisfied with the quality of work provided by our business.

We’re screened for financial stability and customer service. As a Preferred Contractor, we can also offer extended manufacturer warranties of up to 50 years.
