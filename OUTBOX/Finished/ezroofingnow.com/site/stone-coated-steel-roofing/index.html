IS IT TIME TO UPGRADE YOUR ROOF?

Let us help you determine if stone coated steel roofing is the right fit for your home. Our team will walk you through costs, long-term savings, and what to expect from installation. Take advantage of our free inspection for a clear, honest recommendation.

A 50-Year Roofing Investment That Pays for Itself

When comparing roofing options over a 50-year period, homeowners typically replace asphalt shingle roofs two to three times. With stone coated steel, you install once—and you’re done.

Over time, this translates into significant financial benefits:

  • Energy savings on cooling: $15,000 – $30,000
  • Insurance premium savings: $12,000 – $30,000
  • Deductible savings: $2,000 – $8,000
  • Maintenance savings: $3,000 – $5,000

👉 Total estimated savings: $30,000 – $73,000
👉 Average savings: around $50,000 over the roof’s lifespan

This makes stone coated steel roofing not just a premium upgrade—but a smart long-term investment that can essentially pay for itself.

🔹 Built for Durability and Backed by a 50-Year Warranty

Stone coated steel roofing systems are engineered to handle extreme weather conditions common in Arkansas and Utah, including high winds, hail, and heavy storms.

These roofs come with 50-year warranties, giving homeowners peace of mind that their investment is protected for decades. Unlike traditional shingles that degrade over time, stone coated steel maintains its structural integrity and performance year after year.

🔹 Lock In Your Roofing Costs Before Prices Rise

Roofing costs continue to increase due to inflation, rising material costs, and labor shortages. Asphalt shingles, in particular, have historically increased in price faster than general inflation.

By installing a permanent roofing system today, you effectively lock in your roofing cost, avoiding the need to pay double or even triple for future replacements 20–40 years down the line.

🔹 Lower Energy Bills with Reflective Technology

Stone coated steel roofing reflects up to 50% of solar radiation, significantly reducing heat absorption into your home.

This results in:

  • Lower indoor temperatures
  • Reduced HVAC workload
  • Long-term energy savings

Over a 50-year period, these energy savings can add up enough to cover up to half of the original roof cost.

🔹 Long-Term Insurance Savings You Can Count On

Unlike asphalt shingles that lose granules and impact resistance over time, stone coated steel roofing maintains its UL 2218 Class 4 impact resistance rating throughout its entire lifespan.

This means:

  • You can qualify for 20%–35% insurance premium discounts
  • Those savings remain consistent for decades
  • No loss of protection as the roof ages

🔹 Increase Your Home’s Resale Value

A stone coated steel roof isn’t just a benefit while you live in your home—it also adds value if you decide to sell.

Homes with these roofing systems can see a 2%–6% increase in resale value, as buyers recognize the advantage of a “never replace your roof again” solution.

In contrast, older shingle roofs are often seen as a future expense and can reduce buyer interest or negotiating power.

🔹 Is Stone Coated Steel Roofing Right for You?

If you’re planning to stay in your home long-term or want a roofing system that delivers maximum value, durability, and savings, stone coated steel is an excellent choice.

Our team at EZ Roofing will walk you through your options, explain the long-term benefits, and provide expert installation you can trust.
