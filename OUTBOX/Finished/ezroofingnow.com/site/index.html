Our free inspection

We call it a property evaluation, not a roof inspection

We do a physical evaluation of every part of your roof: shingle and slope wear-and-tear and overall condition. Then we check the soffit, fascia, gutters, windows and siding too.

Some roofing contractors charge $75–$200 for an inspection, depending on location, roof size and materials. At EZ Roofing, all roof inspections are free, and we recommend one every year. If your roof is healthy, we save the data so next year’s check can track how it’s aging.

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. All of our roof inspections are free, including a free annual inspection to track wear from year to year, and free inspections after storm damage or another urgent concern.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. If storm damage is found, we can help you decide whether to file a claim, file it with you, and meet the insurance adjuster on the roof during their inspection.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is provided by Foundation Finance Company. Ask us which options are available for your project, or see our financing page.

What warranty comes with a new roof?

We provide a 5-year workmanship warranty and extended 25-year warranties, plus a 24/7 warranty hotline: your call is answered and a workman scheduled the same day.

Where are your offices?

EZ Roofing Utah is at 357 S 200 E, #205, Salt Lake City, UT 84111, and EZ Roofing Arkansas is at 13 Nalley Road, #C, Cabot, AR 72023. Both offices are staffed locally.
