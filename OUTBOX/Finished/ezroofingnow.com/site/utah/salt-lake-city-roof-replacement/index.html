How to Know It’s Time for a Roof Replacement

Delaying a roof replacement can lead to bigger, more expensive problems like structural damage or mold growth. Here are some clear signs that it’s time to call our experts:

  • Multiple Leaks: Water intrusion is a sign that your roof’s protective barrier is compromised.
  • Shingle Wear:Curling edges, missing granules, or bald spots weaken your roof’s durability.
  • Structural Sagging:A sagging roof deck can indicate serious underlying damage.
  • Storm Damage: Hail, wind, or debris impact can shorten your roof’s lifespan significantly.
  • Aging Roof: Most asphalt shingle roofs last 20–25 years; if yours is older, replacement is likely the safest choice.
