Repair vs. Replace — We’ll Tell You the Truth

Many contractors push for a full replacement when a repair will do. We won’t. Here’s how we decide:

Repair Makes Sense When…

  • Damage is isolated to a small area
  • The roof is less than 15 years old
  • Decking underneath is still solid
  • Insurance doesn’t cover full replacement

Replacement Makes Sense When…

  • Storm damage is widespread across the roof
  • The roof is 20+ years old
  • Multiple areas are failing at once
  • Insurance approves a full claim

Either Way, We’ve Got You

EZ Roofing handles both repairs and full replacements. We’ll give you an honest recommendation and a clear quote — same day. No obligation.
