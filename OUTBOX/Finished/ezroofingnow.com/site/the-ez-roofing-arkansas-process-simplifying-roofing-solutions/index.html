The EZ Roofing Arkansas Process: Simplifying Roofing Solutions

By EZ RoofingPublished 2023-11-07
Free Inspection (888) 575-3025

When it comes to roof repair or replacement, the process can often be overwhelming and stressful. At EZ Roofing Arkansas, we believe that the journey to a secure and sturdy roof should be smooth and hassle-free. That’s why we’ve developed the EZ Process, a straightforward and efficient method designed to ensure your roofing needs are met effectively and with ease.

The EZ Process:

  1. Schedule a Free Inspection: The first step in the EZ Process is effortless – simply reach out to EZ Roofing for a free inspection. Give us a call to schedule an appointment, and our expert team will take it from there.
  2. Inspection + Develop Game Plan: During the inspection, an EZ Roofing representative will meticulously assess your roof and exterior using our comprehensive 26-point inspection process. We identify any signs of deterioration, potential leak areas, and storm damage. Following the inspection, we collaborate with you to create a personalized game plan, outlining the necessary repairs, replacements, or maintenance options.
  3. Commencement of Work: Once the game plan is in place, our skilled team swings into action. We handle everything, ensuring that your project is executed efficiently and with precision.
  4. Project Completion: Your roofing project is overseen by our dedicated Production Manager, who guarantees that the work is completed up to code, on-time, and within budget. Attention to detail is our priority, and we conclude every project with a final 31-point inspection and Certificate of Completion.
  5. Providing Warranty and Peace of Mind: After the project is successfully completed, our office takes care of invoicing, payment collection, and sends you your warranty. Your satisfaction and peace of mind are our top priorities.

Service Options and Additional Steps:

We understand that every roofing project is unique. That’s why we help you determine the best way EZ Roofing can assist you, whether it involves filing an insurance claim, exploring financing options, or paying out of pocket.

For projects involving storm damage, our process includes extra steps:

  • Filing an Insurance Claim: We guide you through the process of filing a claim with your insurance company.
  • Attending the Adjuster Meeting: EZ Roofing accompanies you to the adjuster meeting, ensuring that all damages are thoroughly reviewed.
  • Approval Process: We work diligently to have your insurance claim approved, using our expertise and industry-standard documentation to maximize your entitlements and save you money.

At EZ Roofing Arkansas, we believe that quality roofing services should come with ease and peace of mind. Our EZ Process ensures that your roofing needs are met promptly, efficiently, and with the highest level of professionalism. Don’t let roofing issues stress you out – contact EZ Roofing today and experience the simplicity and effectiveness of our proven process. Your roof deserves the best, and with EZ Roofing Arkansas, you’re in safe hands.
