How Fast Can You Get a Roof Quote in Arkansas? (Real Timeline)

By EZ RoofingPublished 2026-04-20
Free Inspection (888) 575-3025

If you’ve got roof damage in Arkansas — whether it’s from a hailstorm, high winds, or something you just noticed during a routine check — one of the first things running through your mind is probably: how long is this going to take?

The good news: it doesn’t have to take long at all. Here’s a real, honest breakdown of the timeline when you work with EZ Roofing.

Step 1: You Call or Book Online — Response Within Minutes

When you reach out to EZ Roofing, you’re not leaving a voicemail and waiting two days. Our team responds quickly — typically within minutes during business hours — to confirm your appointment and get you scheduled.

We offer same-day inspections across our Arkansas service area. That means if you call us Monday morning after a weekend storm, someone can be at your property Monday afternoon.

Step 2: The Inspection — Same Day, Usually 30–60 Minutes

Our inspector performs a full 26-point property evaluation. This isn’t a guy looking at your roof from the driveway — we get up there, check every section, and assess your shingles, decking, flashing, gutters, soffit, and fascia.

Most residential inspections take 30–60 minutes depending on the size of the home and complexity of the roof. You don’t need to take time off work — we can work around your schedule.

Step 3: Your Quote — Within 2–6 Hours of the Inspection

After the inspection, we walk you through what we found and present your options clearly. Your written quote is typically delivered within 2–6 hours of the inspection — same day in most cases.

We’ll tell you whether you need a repair, a full replacement, or whether we recommend filing an insurance claim. No pressure, no vague estimates — a real number with a clear scope of work.

If You’re Going Through Insurance — Here’s That Timeline

For insurance claims, the timeline extends a bit — but we manage every step so you don’t have to figure it out alone:

  • Day 1: We inspect your roof and help you file the claim
  • Days 2–5: Insurance assigns an adjuster
  • Days 5–10: Adjuster meeting — we attend with you and provide documentation
  • Days 7–14: Most insurance claims are approved within this window
  • After approval: We schedule your installation — most roofs completed in 1–2 days

From first call to completed roof, most insurance jobs in Arkansas wrap up within 3–6 weeks. Cash or financing jobs can move faster — sometimes within a week of your initial call.

Why Waiting Costs You Money

Here’s something most homeowners don’t realize: the recoverable depreciation portion of your insurance claim is only released after work is completed and invoiced. Most insurance companies set a deadline — sometimes as short as 6–12 months from the claim date — to collect that money.

On top of that, deductibles are rising. They’re now often 1–2% of your home’s total value. On a $300,000 home, that’s $3,000–$6,000 out of pocket on your next claim. A proactive repair or replacement today can protect you from those costs tomorrow.
