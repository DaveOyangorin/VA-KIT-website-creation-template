B2B Opportunities With EZ Roofing

Free Inspection (888) 575-3025
Work With EZ Roofing

With EZ Roofing, There’s Options.

EZ Roofing, LLC was formed from the ground-up in Salt Lake City, Utah and Heber Springs, Arkansas in 2017.

Authorized Dealer
You can become an EZ Roofing Authorized Dealer. What this means is that you will become an extension of our main office in Cabot, except the main focus is lead generation, which means a second stream of income for your business without any extra employee training or management required.

How the Process Works:

-You will sign an agreement with us which guarantees payment to your business for each job built, immediately upon project completion.

-Your business will be provided a small EZ Roofing Authorized Dealer decal to placed somewhere on the exterior of your business, we will also have a google business listing stating the same.

-A unique phone number will be attached to your Dealer location, so that we can track any jobs sourced from your business. Additionally, our office staff will track any business sent to us by any in-person leads you may receive via our CRM. Also, our staff is trained to request where potential customers heard about our company, in case your Dealer location spurred their interest and they reached out to us via a non-trackable method.

-You will be provided marketing material to present to inquiring customers.

Benefits:

-Opportunity to partner with a trusted, quality and growing business.

-Receive a second stream of income, only by continuing business as usual.

-Can be as hands-off or as hands-on as you’d like, it’s up to you. We have found that Authorized Dealers who are more hands-on make the most income. They are actively presenting our company materials to their customers. But, even Dealers that simply continue business as usual, and only have the Google listing, Dealer sticker, and marketing material in plain view are still producing a very attractive stream of income.

This is the most limited B2B opportunity available as spots are limited. We only need an Authorized Dealer every 50 miles, contact us now if you’re interested in learning more details.

Ad Placement
We will work out a deal that benefits both your business and ours. From ads above urinals, standing banner ads, pamphlets, to small outdoor billboards and more. There are many options for marketing ad placement, and we love to get creative with new ideas!

One of the highest benefit and producing arrangements we’ve experimented with is when we place an ad at a business and offer a voucher or coupon to your customers, paid by us, when they allow us the opportunity to earn their business, no obligation. This means money, that can only be spent at your business, going into your customers pockets, which means a fun way to earn repeat business and an increase in revenue.

Contact us now if you’re interested in learning more details.

Referral Program
If becoming an Authorized Dealer or Ad Placement isn’t for you, we can arrange a unique deal to earn your referrals. Each and every customer who walks through your door could be a potential referral, someone who is in need of help with roofing, siding, or other trades. Now imagine referring them to a company who will give them an outstanding experience and getting paid for it.

Contact us now if you’re interested in learning more details.

Our Service Area
We have two main offices located in Cabot, Arkansas and Salt Lake City, Utah. We serve all of Salt Lake City, UT and the surrounding areas; as well as Little Rock, AR and the surrounding areas. See the footer of our website to see our full service area, if you are outside of our service area, do not hesitate to reach out; we are expanding our service areas.
