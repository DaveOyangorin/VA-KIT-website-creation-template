TO PATCH OR NOT TO PATCH

Let us help you determine if your roof can be patched or what options you have to fix your roof. Take advantage of our free inspection to provide you with a highly accurate assessment for the cost of repairs.

Making the best decision for your roof

Deciding between a shingle “patch job”, a slope replacement, or a full roof replacement can sometimes be a tough decision.

There are many factors that come into play regarding reparability and price. Here at EZ Roofing we offer shingle patches, slope repairs, and full​ roof replacements. We can help you decide on the best plan of action for your situation.

Reparability – Shingle Repair

Most roofs can be “patched”, but there are some variables in the mix that determine whether a roof is truly repairable or not. In our area, many roofs lack proper ventilation and this causes the roofing system to age rapidly, making the shingles brittle. Some roofs are too brittle to be properly repaired, and if that is the case, we will not be able to repair your roof in good faith.

Slope Replacement

If your roof is not repairable, we can explain the other options that you may have. A slope replacement may be one of those options. This is when we replace an entire section of your roof in order to avoid the issue of brittleness.

Slope replacements are not always a viable solution depending on how many valleys your roof has and the amount of damaged shingles. In this case, sometimes roof replacement is your best option.

OUR FREE INSPECTION

The best way to assess your roof’s condition is to take advantage of our free inspection.

We do a physical evaluation of all aspects of your roof including shingle and slope wear-and-tear and overall roof condition. We also assess the condition of other exterior home features, including soffit, fascia, gutters, windows, and siding. That’s why we commonly refer to our roof inspections as property evaluations.

Our team’s property evaluation accurately determines your property’s condition and acts as a guide for keeping your home in top shape. In fact, we recommend a free inspection each year for that reason.
