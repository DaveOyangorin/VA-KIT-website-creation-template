The old door also had scratches, peeling paint, and rusted hardware, which made my home look older than it really was. I wanted a solution that would improve security, energy efficiency, and curb appeal.

Since my house is a traditional Cabot-style home with a brick façade, I also wanted a door that matched the exterior aesthetic. After some research, I realized professional exterior door installation was the safest way to ensure proper fitting, sealing, and long-term performance.

If you’re planning exterior door installation in Cabot, AR, here are a few practical tips:

FAQs About Exterior Door Installation in Cabot, AR

How Often Should I Replace My Exterior Door?

Most doors last 15–20 years, but signs of drafts, difficulty opening, or water damage mean it’s time to replace them sooner. Arkansas weather can accelerate wear, especially for older wooden doors.

Can I Install an Exterior Door Myself?

DIY installation is possible but risky. Professional installation ensures proper insulation, alignment, and security. Incorrect installation can lead to drafts, leaks, or a door that sticks or warps.

What Type of Door Works Best in Cabot, AR?

Steel and fiberglass doors are ideal for Arkansas homes. They resist warping from humidity, provide excellent security, and require less maintenance than wood doors.

Should I Consider a Storm Door?

Adding a storm door improves energy efficiency and protects the main door from rain and seasonal storms. EZ Roofing offers storm door installation as a secondary service for homeowners looking to enhance protection.

How Can I Maintain My Exterior Door?

Regular cleaning, inspecting weather stripping, and lubricating hardware are simple steps to keep your door functioning well. EZ Roofing also checks these details during installation.
