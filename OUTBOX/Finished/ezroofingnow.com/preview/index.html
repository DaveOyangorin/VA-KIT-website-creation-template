Summary

Notes on the list

  • Pre cast concrete: this is not roofing. People searching it are likely looking for concrete suppliers, not a roofer. Ask Angelo whether the page is a real service (for example concrete roof tiles) or should be left alone and not optimized.
  • Commercial roofing + commercial roof: same topic, two pages. They compete with each other. Suggest one page targeting commercial roofing salt lake city, with the other redirected to it. Angelo decides.
  • Roofing reviews: keep it as a reviews page with real customer reviews only. Never write or change reviews.
  • Roof installation vs. roof replacement: both have pages; keep them different. Suggest: installation = new builds and additions; replacement = tearing off and replacing an old roof.
  • Flat roof repair links naturally to commercial roofing.

Takeaways

  • Aim for about 1,600–1,900 words on the main service pages, with an FAQ on each. Compare the current pages against this.
  • No top repair or replacement page shows prices. Real prices (if the client gives them) would set this site apart.
  • Topics competitors cover: insurance claims and storm damage, a simple step-by-step process, Wasatch Front weather, roof lifespan in Utah, how roof size and material change the cost, metal roof pros and cons, recent projects, why choose us.

Use competitor headings for topic ideas only. Do not copy them. One top result for roof repair is a list of competitors ("Whitaker Roofing", "The Roof Doctor"…), and others name their owner or run promotions. Owner bios, promotions and project photos go in only with real facts from the client.
