About us

About Aluminum Lock Roofing

A Salt Lake City company focused on one thing: interlocking aluminum shingle roofs.

(801) 484-3321Contact Us
Aluminum Lock Roofing is a Salt Lake City roofing company that specializes in interlocking aluminum shingle roofing. We manufacture and supply aluminum roofing systems, install aluminum shingle roofs, and sell roofing materials to roofers.

A Focus on Aluminum Roofing

Our roofing systems combine the appearance of traditional shingles with aluminum construction. Four-sided interlocks connect each shingle to the surrounding shingles. Underlayment and correctly fitted flashings work with the shingles to manage water at roof edges, valleys, walls and penetrations.

The complete roof assembly matters. Roof slope, the condition of the existing roof and decking, and project requirements all help determine the installation approach.

Roof Installation

For homeowners planning a new roof, we can discuss the property, existing roofing and the result you want. Contact us with your project location to confirm installation availability. Ask about preparation, any necessary removal, materials and finishing details when reviewing an estimate.

Materials for Roofing Professionals

We also supply aluminum roofing materials to roofers. Tell us where your project is located and the products and approximate quantities you need so we can discuss options, required components and ordering arrangements.
