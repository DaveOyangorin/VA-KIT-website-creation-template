How the interlocking aluminum shingle system works

Four-sided interlocks connect each shingle to the surrounding shingles. The shingles are one part of the system: underlayment and correctly fitted flashings at eaves, rakes, valleys, walls and penetrations work together to manage water.

  • Interlocking edges help form a connected roofing surface
  • Concealed attachment details reduce exposed fasteners across the shingle field
  • Installation details accommodate expansion and contraction
  • Perimeter flashings help direct water at roof edges and transitions
