FAQ

Aluminum Shingle Roofing FAQ

Common questions about our roofs, installation and buying materials.

(801) 484-3321Contact Us

Find answers to common questions about Aluminum Lock Roofing, installation and buying materials. For advice about a particular roof, contact us with your project details.

What does Aluminum Lock Roofing offer?

We manufacture and supply interlocking aluminum roofing systems, install aluminum shingle roofs, and sell roofing materials to roofers. See our services page for more about installation and material inquiries.

How does the interlocking roofing system work?

Four-sided interlocks connect each shingle to the surrounding shingles. The shingles work together with underlayment and properly fitted flashings at edges, valleys, walls and roof penetrations. Roof slope and project details determine the installation approach.

Can aluminum shingles be installed over my existing roof?

A roof-over may be possible on some homes. The condition of the existing roof, the number of roof layers, decking, local requirements and installation instructions determine whether removal is needed. The existing roof needs to be assessed before deciding.

Do you sell roofing materials to contractors?

Yes. We sell aluminum roofing materials to roofers. Contact us with your project location, the products you are considering and approximate quantities to discuss components and ordering arrangements.

Do you install roofs in my area?

Contact us with your project location to confirm installation availability. If you are looking for materials rather than installation, let us know so we can discuss your needs separately.

What information should I provide when requesting an estimate?

Start with your project location, current roof type and what you want to accomplish. Tell us whether you need roof installation or materials. For material inquiries, include the products and approximate quantities you need. Ask what the estimate includes, such as preparation, removal, materials and finishing details.

What should I ask about warranties?

Ask for the applicable written warranty before choosing a roofing system. Review its coverage, exclusions and maintenance requirements, and confirm which terms apply to your materials and installation.

How do I get started?

Call (801) 484-3321 or email service@aluminumlockroofing.com. You can also visit our contact page for the details to include with your inquiry.
