What can cause roof damage?

Visible roof damage may come from weather, age, installation issues or other conditions. An inspection helps clarify what is actually happening before you decide what to do next.

What Utah’s Insurance Department tells homeowners

The Utah Insurance Department’s guidance on home claims (insurance.utah.gov) includes these steps:

  • Call your insurance company or agent with your policy number as soon as possible, and ask what documents they need.
  • Take photos or video of the damage.
  • Make temporary repairs to prevent further damage, such as covering a leaking roof, but don’t have permanent repairs made until your insurer has inspected the property and you’ve agreed on repair costs.
  • Keep all receipts, including for temporary repairs.
  • If you have a dispute over the claim, you can contact the state insurance department for help.
