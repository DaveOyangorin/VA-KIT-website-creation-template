Small problems turn into bigger ones

Leaks and damaged roofing materials can turn into bigger problems. If you see damage or notice water inside, schedule an inspection before the problem spreads. RoofWise will inspect the issue and explain the practical repair options.

What we inspect and repair

  • Leaks, including water stains on ceilings and walls
  • Damaged shingles: missing, cracked or lifted
  • Storm damage from hail and heavy weather
  • Wind damage
  • Flashing issues around chimneys, vents and walls
  • Other roofing problems you’re not sure about

Repair or replace?

Sometimes a repair is all a roof needs; sometimes the damage or the age of the roof means replacement makes more sense. The inspection is how you find out, and you’ll get a straight explanation either way. Roof replacement →

Questions

Common questions

I have a leak. What should I do first?

Contact RoofWise and, if you can do it safely, protect the area inside from water. An inspection will find where the water is getting in.

Can you tell if damage is from a storm?

RoofWise inspects the roof for visible signs of damage and explains what we find. Whether damage is covered by insurance is decided by your insurance carrier.
