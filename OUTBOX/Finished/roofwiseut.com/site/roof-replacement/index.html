When repair isn’t the right answer

A roof replacement is a big decision, so RoofWise starts by helping you understand the condition of the roof, the replacement options and the project scope before any work begins. If a repair will do, the inspection will show it.

Quality materials, experienced labor, straightforward pricing

RoofWise uses high quality roofing materials selected for long-term performance. The work is done by an in-house crew with more than 20 years of roofing experience.

You get an itemized proposal, so the cost of each part of the project is clear before you agree to anything.

A 10-year workmanship warranty

Roof replacement projects include a 10-year workmanship warranty. Material warranties come from the shingle manufacturer and depend on the product you choose; ask about them when you review the proposal.

Storm damage and insurance

If the roof was damaged by hail or wind, a replacement may be part of an insurance claim. RoofWise can document the damage and explain the roofing scope. See insurance claims help.
