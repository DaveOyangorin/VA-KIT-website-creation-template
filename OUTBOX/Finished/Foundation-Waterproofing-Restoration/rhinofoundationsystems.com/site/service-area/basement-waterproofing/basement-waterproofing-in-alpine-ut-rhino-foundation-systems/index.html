Protecting Homes from the Ground Up

If you’re a homeowner in Alpine, Utah, and you’ve noticed water seeping into your basement, you’re not alone—and you’re in the right place. Due to Alpine’s unique climate and mountainous geography, moisture intrusion is a common concern, particularly following heavy rains or spring snowmelt.

Rhino Foundation Systems, we specialize in comprehensive basement waterproofing solutions tailored to the challenges of our local environment. Our goal is simple: to help you protect your home, preserve its value, and restore your peace of mind.

With decades of experience serving Alpine and surrounding communities, our team understands the nuances of foundation waterproofing in this region. We combine technical expertise with industry-leading solutions to ensure your basement remains dry, safe, and structurally sound—regardless of the season.

Basement water problems rarely have a single source. In Alpine, a variety of contributing factors often work together to create the perfect storm for water intrusion. Melting snow, sudden rainfall, and fluctuating groundwater levels can build up hydrostatic pressure around your foundation. This pressure forces water through any existing vulnerabilities—such as cracks in foundation walls or floors.

Poorly functioning gutters, downspouts that discharge too close to the home, and improper grading can further aggravate the problem. Left unresolved, these issues can lead to more than just damp floors: they can promote mold growth, damage stored belongings, weaken structural components, and negatively impact indoor air quality.

At Rhino Foundation Systems, we address not just the symptoms, but the root causes of basement moisture—delivering results that last.

Every home is different, and so is every waterproofing challenge. Our process begins with a thorough, on-site evaluation of your home’s foundation, drainage systems, and landscaping. This allows us to identify the precise source of water infiltration and tailor a solution specifically for your needs.

Whether you’re dealing with persistent dampness or a sudden flood, we design our waterproofing systems to provide long-term protection and enhance the structural resilience of your home. Our team brings decades of hands-on experience and a reputation for quality workmanship across Alpine and the greater Utah County area.

Comprehensive Interior and Exterior Waterproofing

A successful basement waterproofing strategy considers both interior and exterior defenses. At Rhino Foundation Systems, we implement layered solutions that address moisture at every possible entry point.

Interior Basement Waterproofing:

When water does find its way inside, interior systems serve as a critical second line of defense. We may recommend:

French drains and perimeter drainage systems to intercept water at the cove joint (where the floor meets the wall) and channel it to a sump basin

By integrating these components into a cohesive system, we create a robust, multi-layered defense against basement moisture.

Exterior Basement Waterproofing:

Exterior waterproofing focuses on preventing water from entering your foundation in the first place. This may include:

Applying weather-resistant waterproof membranes to foundation walls

These proactive measures significantly reduce the pressure exerted on your foundation and minimize the likelihood of leaks.

Early detection is key. If you notice any of the following signs in your basement, it may be time to consult a waterproofing specialist:

Warped wood, rusting appliances, or signs of mold

These indicators often point to ongoing moisture problems that will only worsen with time. Prompt intervention helps you avoid more extensive (and expensive) repairs later on.

Foundation Crack Repair

Foundation cracks are more than just cosmetic—they are often the entry point for groundwater. Our technicians are trained to identify even the smallest fractures and apply advanced sealants that bond chemically with the concrete. This process not only blocks current leaks but also prevents future water intrusion, reinforcing your foundation’s integrity and protecting your home from structural damage.
