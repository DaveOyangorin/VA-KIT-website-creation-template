Our St. George Concrete Repair Services

At Rhino, we restore more than just concrete—we restore peace of mind. Our trained specialists diagnose hidden issues and deliver long-term repair systems, not quick fixes.

Concrete Crack Repair

Cracks may indicate soil movement, settling, or damage. We use industrial-strength sealants, injection methods, and grinding to prevent further deterioration and resurface a smooth finish.

Concrete Tear-Out & Replacement

When necessary, we perform full tear-outs and re-pour broken or failing concrete—cleanly, efficiently, and with durable results that stand the test of time.

Surface Resurfacing & Leveling

Bumpy, peeling, or uneven concrete is repaired using polymer-modified overlays and leveling systems that return a safe, attractive, and uniform finish.

Decorative Overlays

Add texture, stain, or stone-like finishes to breathe new life into old surfaces. From rustic to modern, we offer custom looks that enhance curb appeal and property value.

Pool Deck Repair & Resurfacing

In St. George, outdoor living means sun, pools, and time spent outside—but these conditions wear down your pool deck. We offer resurfacing built for the desert climate and daily use.

Benefits of our pool deck systems:

Personalized styles to match your outdoor space

Vuba Stone Overlays: Luxury that Lasts

Vuba transforms cracked, plain concrete into a showpiece—ideal for patios, pool decks, walkways, and entryways. It’s beautiful, durable, and built to handle the St. George climate.
