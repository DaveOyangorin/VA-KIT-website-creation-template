Why Concrete Settles in American Fork

Concrete doesn’t just sink by chance—it moves because the ground beneath it shifts. In American Fork, several natural and environmental factors combine to make concrete settlement a common issue:

Expansive clay soils in Utah County absorb water and expand, then shrink when dry. This cycle stresses concrete slabs and causes them to shift or sink.

Freeze-thaw cycles in the spring and winter cause moisture in the soil to expand and contract. Over time, this process creates air pockets and voids under your concrete.

Erosion and poor compaction are also culprits. If the soil wasn’t compacted properly during construction, or if water runoff from sprinklers or rain washes it away, your slabs lose support.

Poor drainage is often to blame. Improper grading, gutter discharge, or pooling water can wash away the soil supporting your walkways, patios, or driveway.

These conditions are especially tough on concrete surfaces like:

  • Driveways and garage floors
  • Front walkways and steps
  • Backyard patios and porches
  • Pool decks and garden paths

Luckily, most sunken concrete doesn’t need to be demolished. In many cases, it can be raised, lifted, and saved in just a few hours.

Mudjacking

Also called slab jacking or pressure grouting, mudjacking involves pumping a cement-based slurry beneath the slab. The mixture fills empty spaces and hydraulically lifts the concrete.

Polyurethane Foam Injection

Some companies use polyurethane foam, which expands to lift the slab. Rhino Foundation Systems currently specializes in traditional mudjacking, a time-tested and highly effective solution.

Our Concrete Lifting Process

At Rhino Foundation Systems, we’ve perfected a streamlined approach to concrete lifting in American Fork that gets results fast—without cutting corners.

Free On-Site Inspection

We assess your property, measure settlement, and identify the cause—whether it’s erosion, poor drainage, or weak soil.

Strategic Drilling

We drill small holes (about the size of a quarter) in the affected slab.

Mudjacking Injection

A dense slurry is pumped beneath the slab, filling any voids and gradually lifting the concrete to its original grade.

Patching and Clean-Up

Once the lift is complete, we patch the holes and clean up the worksite. Your concrete is ready for light use almost immediately.
