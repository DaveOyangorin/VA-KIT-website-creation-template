Alpine

Uneven, cracked, or sunken concrete is more than just an eyesore—it can be a hazard. If you’re a homeowner in Alpine, Utah, you’ve likely noticed how our unique mountain climate and shifting soil conditions can wreak havoc on driveways, sidewalks, patios, and garage floors.

The freeze-thaw cycles, clay-heavy soils, and elevation changes throughout Alpine increase the risk of concrete settling or becoming unstable. Left unaddressed, these issues can lead to safety hazards, water drainage problems, or even foundational damage.

At Rhino Foundation Systems, we specialize in lifting and leveling concrete the right way.

Instead of replacing the slab entirely, we drill small holes and inject specialized materials beneath the concrete. These materials expand to fill voids, gently lifting the slab back to a safe and level position. The result? A smoother, safer surface at a fraction of the time and cost of replacement.

Signs You May Need Concrete Lifting

Think your concrete might be settling? Watch for these common red flags:

Sinking Slabs

One portion of a driveway, sidewalk, or patio is noticeably lower than the rest

Large Cracks

Spreading cracks or wide gaps that suggest movement below the surface

Gaps Between Slab and Soil

Edges of the slab appear to be “floating” or separated from surrounding ground

Pooling Water

Rain or snowmelt collects in specific areas, indicating poor slope and drainage

Misaligned Garage or Entry Doors

Settling slabs can throw off alignment, making doors harder to open or close

If you’re noticing any of these symptoms at your Alpine property, it’s time to call in the pros

Our Concrete Lifting Process

At Rhino Foundation Systems, our process is engineered for speed, precision, and long-lasting results. Here’s what you can expect:

Free On-Site Inspection

Our Alpine-based specialists perform a thorough inspection of your concrete surfaces. We identify the cause of settlement, assess damage, and provide a clear, upfront estimate.

Site Prep

We carefully prepare the area by clearing debris and protecting surrounding landscaping or structures. Minimal disruption—maximum respect for your home.

Strategic Drilling

We drill small, precise holes in the slab to allow for injection of the lifting material. These access points are carefully chosen for optimal lift and minimal visual impact.

Injection & Lifting

We use industry-leading polyurethane foam or cementitious slurry to lift your concrete. The material expands beneath the slab, filling voids and gently raising the surface until level. Foam cures in minutes—making it fast, clean, and effective.

Final Touches

Once the slab is level, we seal the injection holes with durable patching compound and thoroughly clean the site. Most surfaces are ready for use the same day.

Final Inspection & Recommendations

We walk you through the finished work, answer any questions, and provide maintenance tips or additional recommendations—especially if drainage or foundational issues contributed to the problem.
