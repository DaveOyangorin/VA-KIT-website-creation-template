Vuba Stone

Rhino Foundation Systems, we are committed to providing innovative, long-lasting solutions for concrete repair and resurfacing. One of the most exciting advancements in the industry is

Vuba Stone

—a durable, visually stunning, and environmentally friendly surfacing system designed to restore, enhance, and protect concrete surfaces.

If you have cracked, worn-out, or unsightly concrete on your driveway, patio, pool deck, or walkway, Vuba Stone offers a seamless, high-performance alternative to expensive concrete replacement.

How Does Vuba Stone Work?

The system consists of two primary components:

Natural Aggregates

These are high-quality decorative stones available in various colors and sizes to create a unique, customized appearance.

UV-Stable Resin

This advanced resin binds the aggregates together, ensuring the surface remains permeable, flexible, and resistant to discoloration over time.

Once applied, Vuba Stone adheres seamlessly to existing concrete, creating a smooth yet slip-resistant finish that enhances both function and curb appeal.

Long-Lasting Durability

Vuba Stone is engineered to withstand harsh weather conditions, heavy foot traffic, and vehicle loads. Its UV-stable resin prevents fading and discoloration, ensuring that your surface maintains its beautiful appearance for years to come.

Crack-Resistant Flexibility

Unlike traditional concrete, which is prone to cracking due to ground movement, Vuba Stone is designed with 70% elongation to break, meaning it can adapt to shifts in the underlying surface without breaking or deteriorating.

100% Permeable & Environmentally Friendly

Vuba Stone allows water to drain naturally through the surface, preventing standing water, erosion, and flooding. Its permeability makes it an excellent choice for homeowners concerned about drainage issues or eco-friendly construction solutions.

Low Maintenance & Easy to Clean

With Vuba Stone, you won’t have to worry about weeds, moss, or algae growth. The sealed surface repels stains, and routine maintenance requires just occasional washing with water and mild detergent—no need for sealants or resurfacing!

Slip-Resistant & Safe for All Environments

Vuba Stone’s textured finish enhances traction, making it a safer option for pool decks, patios, and walkways—especially in wet conditions.

Cost-Effective Alternative to Concrete Replacement

Instead of tearing out and replacing old concrete, Vuba Stone resurfaces existing concrete, providing a stronger, more visually appealing finish at a fraction of the cost of a full replacement.

Driveways & Walkways

Vuba Stone provides a strong, flexible surface that withstands vehicle traffic while significantly improving curb appeal.

Pool Decks & Patios

Its slip-resistant, water-draining properties make it an excellent choice for wet areas, reducing the risk of slips and falls.

Garage Floors & Interior Spaces

For homeowners who want a durable, easy-to-clean surface in garages or basements, Vuba Stone offers a sleek, polished look without the maintenance hassle.

Customizable Color & Design Options

Vuba Stone offers a wide selection of natural stone blends, allowing you to choose a color and style that complements your home’s aesthetic. Some popular options include:

Lake Tahoe

A sophisticated blend perfect for modern driveways and walkways.

With endless customization options, you can achieve a unique, high-end finish tailored to your specific design preferences.
