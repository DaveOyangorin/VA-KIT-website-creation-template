Recognizing the Warning Signs of Foundation Issues

Early detection of foundation problems is key to preventing costly repairs. Watch for these common warning signs:

Cracks in Walls or Floors

Horizontal, vertical, or stair-step cracks are often signs of shifting or settling.

Uneven or Sloping Floors

Floors that sag or slope may indicate foundational instability.

Gaps Around Windows

Gaps around windows can indicate potential foundation settlement.

Bowing or Leaning Walls

Walls under pressure from unstable soil may buckle or tilt inward.

Water Intrusion and Moisture Problems

Persistent dampness can weaken your foundation and promote mold growth.

Crack or Gaps in Brick

Call us right away if you see a crack or gap in your exterior brick.

If you notice any of these issues, don’t wait to act. Contact our team for a thorough inspection and tailored recommendations.

Structural Cracks

Large, widening cracks or those running horizontally often indicate significant stress on your foundation.

Sinking or Settling

Uneven settling caused by unstable soils can lead to serious structural issues.

Expanding Clay Soils

In Utah, clay-rich soils are a common culprit behind foundation problems. These soils expand and contract with moisture changes, creating pressure that can crack or shift your foundation.

Approximately 60% of homes in the U.S. are built on clay-containing soils, making soil stabilization a critical component of foundation repair. At Rhino Foundation Systems, we specialize in diagnosing these issues and implementing solutions to address the underlying causes effectively.

Cost and Duration of Foundation Repair

Foundation repair costs vary based on several factors:

Type and Extent of Damage

Minor cracks may cost a few thousand dollars to repair, while severe structural damage can range into tens of thousands.

Size and Accessibility of the Property

Larger homes or areas with restricted access may require more extensive repairs.

Chosen Repair Method

Different techniques, such as helical piers, push piers, or soil stabilization, impact the overall cost.

Repairs typically take anywhere from a few days to several weeks, depending on the complexity of the project. Our team works efficiently to minimize disruption to your home while ensuring the highest quality results.

Expert Solutions for Every Foundation Issue

At Rhino Foundation Systems, we employ a range of proven techniques to restore your foundation’s stability:

Push Piers and Helical Piers

These systems provide robust support by anchoring your foundation to stable soil or bedrock. Push piers are ideal for addressing sinking or settling, while helical piers are suited for projects requiring deep stabilization. Both methods are quick to install and provide long-term durability.

Carbon Fiber Wall Reinforcement

For bowing or leaning walls, carbon fiber strips offer a non-invasive solution. These ultra-strong materials reinforce walls against lateral pressure, preventing further movement.

Soil Stabilization

Unstable soils, such as clay or loose sand, often require specialized treatment. Our team uses advanced techniques, including chemical grouting and micropile installation, to stabilize soil and support your foundation.

Waterproofing and Drainage Systems

Poor drainage can exacerbate foundation problems by increasing soil moisture. Our exterior and interior drainage systems protect your foundation from water intrusion, reducing the risk of cracking or shifting.
