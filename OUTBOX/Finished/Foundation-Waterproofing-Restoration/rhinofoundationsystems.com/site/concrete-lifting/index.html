Concrete lifting is the process of restoring sunken or uneven concrete surfaces to their original level and function. Over time, concrete slabs can shift due to changes in soil conditions, moisture levels, and natural settling. This can lead to cracks, uneven surfaces, and dangerous tripping hazards. Concrete leveling addresses these issues by lifting and stabilizing the concrete, improving both its appearance and its structural integrity.

At Rhino Foundation Systems, we utilize a cutting-edge polyurethane foam injection method that is fast, efficient, and highly effective in lifting and stabilizing sunken concrete slabs. Our expert team ensures that your property’s concrete surfaces are restored to their original level, eliminating gaps, cracks, and dangerous slopes.

Assessment and Preparation

We begin with a comprehensive inspection to assess the extent of the settling and identify the underlying causes. Our team will mark the areas where the foam injections will be made, ensuring that we target the problem areas for effective lifting.

Drilling Small Injection Holes

Small, dime or quarter-sized holes are drilled into the sunken concrete slabs. These holes provide the entry point for our polyurethane foam.

Injection of Polyurethane Foam

Our proprietary polyurethane foam is injected beneath the concrete. As the foam expands, it fills the voids beneath the slab, lifting it back to its original position. This foam is lightweight yet highly effective at stabilizing the soil underneath, providing a long-term solution.

Precise Lifting

As the foam expands, our technicians carefully monitor the lifting process to ensure the concrete is raised evenly and aligned with the surrounding surfaces. We ensure that all uneven areas are corrected to restore the surface to its proper level.

Completion and Clean-Up

Once the concrete is leveled, we remove any remaining injection ports, clean the area, and ensure the surface is smooth and ready for use.

Our Concrete Lifting Process: A Proven Approach

At Rhino Foundation Systems, we follow a streamlined and effective three-step process to ensure that your concrete surfaces are leveled accurately and efficiently:

Drilling

We drill small, discreet holes in the concrete slabs to access the space beneath the surface. These holes are placed strategically to maximize the effectiveness of the foam injection.

Pumping

Our specialized polyurethane foam is injected under the concrete, where it begins to expand and fill voids beneath the surface. The foam gently lifts the concrete back to its original position without causing damage to the existing slab.

Lifting

As the foam continues to expand, our team monitors the process to ensure the concrete is lifted precisely and evenly. We adjust the injection process to ensure a smooth and level surface that conforms to the surrounding area.

The entire process is fast, minimally disruptive, and leaves your concrete surfaces looking as good as new.
