What to do

  • Compress and resize images, lazy-load images below the fold, and remove scripts or plugins the page does not need. At this score, speed comes first.
  • Keep a unique meta description on every page.
  • Give every image a short alt text that says what is in the photo, naming Salt Lake City where it fits.
  • Name Salt Lake City in the title, H1 and first paragraph of every service page.
  • Keep the page addresses (URLs). With 51 pages live, changing URLs can lose rankings. If a URL must change or two pages merge, Angelo sets up a redirect.
