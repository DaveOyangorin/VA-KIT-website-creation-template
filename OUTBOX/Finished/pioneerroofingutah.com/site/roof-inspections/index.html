Roof Inspections in Salt Lake City

Book an Inspection (801) 943-7663
Pioneer Roofing inspects roofs in Salt Lake City and across Utah: after storms, when insurance companies need a roofing professional’s assessment, for new home buyers, and to find out how much life an aging roof has left.

Schedule your roof inspection

Roofing inspections are crucial for the long-term health and safety of a building. A roof that is damaged or in disrepair can lead to serious issues such as leaks, mold growth, and even structural damage. Regular inspections can identify potential problems early on, allowing for repairs to be made before they become more extensive and costly. Performing a roof inspection will allow property owners to protect their investment, prevent leaks, and know the remaining lifespan of their roof.

Roof Inspections for Insurance Claims

Major storms can cause severe damage to your roof. After these types of events, insurance companies will often require an inspection of your roof by a roofing professional to determine the extent of damage. Pioneer Roofing is a fully licensed and insured company and can help you in the insurance claims process by assessing your home after any major damage.

Inspections for New Home Buyers

If you are buying a home, you’ll want to know what the condition of the roof is. General home inspectors will look at the roof, but many roofing issues require more specialized expertise and knowledge. As a new home buyer we can give you the assurance you want and the information you need to make the best decision possible. Following roof inspections we can also provide any repairs needed to keep your home leak-free and extend the lifetime of your roof.

Common Issues on Roofs

Some of the most common issues we see with roofs include missing shingles from wind damage, cracks around pipe flashings, holes in roof membranes, or gaps in flashings around chimney or roof ducts. Our comprehensive roof inspections will identify any issues on your roof and give you a full picture of your roof’s condition.
