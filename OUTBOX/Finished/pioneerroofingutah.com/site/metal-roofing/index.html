Metal Roofing FAQs

What are the benefits of choosing a metal roof?

Metal roofs are known for their durability, longevity, and energy efficiency. They can last 40-70 years with proper maintenance, are resistant to extreme weather conditions, and reflect heat, which can help reduce cooling costs in the summer. Additionally, they are often recyclable, making them an environmentally friendly option.

Are metal roofs noisy during rain or hail?

While it’s a common misconception that metal roofs are excessively noisy, the sound can be mitigated with proper insulation and underlayment. Most homeowners report that the noise is not significantly different from traditional roofing materials, especially when installed correctly.

How much do metal roofs cost compared to other roofing materials?

Metal roofs typically have a higher upfront cost than materials like asphalt shingles. However, they can be more cost-effective in the long run due to their longevity and reduced maintenance needs. The total cost can vary based on the type of metal used, roof design, and installation complexities. Call us for a free quote!

Will a metal roof make my home hotter?

Metal roofs will actually help keep your home cooler. They reflect sunlight rather than absorbing it, which can reduce heat buildup in your attic and lower your cooling costs. Adding insulation can further enhance energy efficiency.

Can metal roofs be installed over existing shingles?

Yes, metal roofs can often be installed over existing shingles, which can save time and money on removal and disposal. However, it’s essential to consult a professional to assess the structure and ensure that it can support the additional weight and to check for any local building codes.
