Roof Replacement Cost in Salt Lake City

Book an Inspection (801) 943-7663
Roof replacement cost in Salt Lake City, from our own jobs: real price ranges for a typical 2,000 sq. ft. home over the last 12 months, what drives the price up or down, and the costs homeowners don’t see coming.

Most roofing projects don’t have a fixed price because cost depends on size, material, condition, access, and what’s found during inspection. But that doesn’t mean you should be left guessing. Below you’ll find realistic ranges, what drives cost up or down, and how to compare quotes confidently.

Typical Roof Price Ranges

What New Roofs Actually Cost

These are our real price ranges for a typical 2,000 sq. ft. home in Salt Lake City and the Wasatch Front over the last 12 months:

  • Asphalt roof replacement — straightforward $9,100-$12,600

    Simple ranch, one layer, standard architectural shingles, easy access

  • Asphalt roof replacement — complex or premium $13,400-$16,600

    Steep pitch, dormers, valleys, skylights, multiple layers, premium warranty

  • TPO / Flat Membrane Roofs $13,100-$16,300

    Steep pitch, dormers, valleys, skylights, multiple layers, premium warranty

  • Roof Repair - Basic $250-$1,000

    Minor leaks, missing or lifted shingles, exposed nails, flashing repairs, minor storm damage, evaporative cooler removal

  • Roof Repair - Advanced $1,100-$2,000

    Steep pitch, extensive leak tracing, flashing replacement, decking repairs, valleys, skylights, structural damage

What Changes Final Price?

What Drives Price Up

  • Steep pitch or difficult access
  • Multiple existing tear-off layers
  • Rotted decking or hidden damage
  • Chimneys, dormers, valleys, skylights
  • Premium materials or warranties
  • Ventilation or code upgrades required
  • Existing solar panels on your roof

What Drives Price Down

  • Simple roof line with easy access
  • Only one existing shingle layer
  • No decking damage found
  • Standard materials selected
  • Repair vs. full replacement
