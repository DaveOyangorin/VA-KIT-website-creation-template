Roof Repair services

As your roof ages, you may begin to see leaks appearing in your home or business. Roof leaks can cause damage to your drywall or ceiling tiles, cause bubbles and cracks in paint, and rot away at your roof deck. The longer the leak goes on, the more potential damage it can cause. Leaks should be fixed quickly to avoid extra cost and damage in the future.

We offer reliable and efficient roofing repair services to address any issues with your roof and restore its integrity and functionality.

types of repair we offer

1. Leak Detection and Repair: Our team will locate the source of the leak and repair it quickly.
2. Shingle Replacement: Strong winds can damage or blow off shingles and compromise your roof. We replace any damaged shingles and ensure that they are properly secured.
3. Flashing Repair: We repair and replace vents, chimney flashings, and other pipe flashings.
4. Cooler Removal and Cancellation: We remove evaporative roof coolers and install new roof decking and materials to keep your roof water tight.
5. Roof and Gutter Cleaning: We help maintain roofs by clearing debris from roof surfaces and gutters.
