Roofing Built for Utah Weather and Property Types

Roofing needs can change significantly from one Utah community to another. Foothill neighborhoods can see stronger wind exposure, mountain communities must account for snow and freeze-thaw cycles, and valley homes experience intense sun and seasonal temperature swings. The Wasatch Front also includes a wide range of housing – from older established neighborhoods to rapidly growing communities with newer construction. Those differences are why a roof should be evaluated based on its actual condition, materials, design, age, and exposure rather than by a one-size-fits-all recommendation.

  • Wind and storm exposure around foothill and open-valley properties
  • Snow, ice, freeze-thaw and drainage details in mountain markets
  • UV exposure and large seasonal temperature changes
  • Older flashing, penetrations, chimneys and aging shingles in established neighborhoods
  • Low-slope TPO and other commercial systems in retail, office and industrial areas
