What is TPO Roofing?

Thermoplastic Polyolefin (TPO) is a single-ply membrane made from rubber and polypropylene. It’s known for its strength, flexibility, and ability to reflect sunlight — reducing energy costs for commercial properties. Lightweight and resistant to UV, chemicals, and punctures, it’s a go-to roofing system for flat or low-slope roofs in Utah’s extreme climate.

Benefits of TPO Membrane Roofing

Energy Efficiency

White TPO surfaces reflect sunlight, helping reduce heat buildup and AC costs.

Durability

Resistant to tears, punctures, and long-term weather exposure.

Low Maintenance

Minimal upkeep is required, saving time and money over the life of the roof.

Weather Protection

Performs well in extreme temperatures, snow, and rain.

Cost-Effective

Offers an excellent return on investment for large-scale commercial properties.

Versatile Installation

Adapts easily to different roof shapes and sizes.
