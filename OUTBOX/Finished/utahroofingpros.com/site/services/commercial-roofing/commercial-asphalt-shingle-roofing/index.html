A Durable, Cost-Effective Solution for Your Business

Why Choose Asphalt Shingles for Commercial Roofing?

Asphalt shingles offer a strong balance of performance, appearance, and affordability — making them a top option for sloped commercial roofs.

Long-Lasting Durability

Built to withstand wind, snow, rain, and hail — ideal for Utah’s four-season environment.

Cost-Effective Protection

Lower material and labor costs make asphalt an economical option with long-term value.

Design Flexibility

Choose from a wide range of colors and styles to match your building’s architecture and brand.

Fire & UV Resistance

Many asphalt shingles are fire-rated and reflect heat, boosting energy efficiency.

Fast Installation

Asphalt shingles are quick to install and easy to repair or replace when needed.

Asphalt Shingle Types

3-Tab Shingles

Economical and practical, these are perfect for businesses seeking a simple, budget-friendly solution.

Architectural Shingles

Thicker and dimensional, these mimic wood or slate for a high-end look with enhanced durability.

Fast, Professional Roof Repairs

Noticed missing shingles or a leak? Our repair team acts fast to fix damage, restore protection, and prevent bigger issues. Whether it’s small fixes or major restoration, we’ve got you covered.

How to Maintain Your Shingle Roof

  • Inspect regularly for cracks or missing shingles
  • Clean gutters to prevent water backup
  • Trim trees to avoid abrasion and debris
  • Schedule annual inspections with our team
