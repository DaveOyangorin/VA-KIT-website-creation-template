Types of Metal Roofing

Steel

The most common metal roofing material is steel. It provides a great balance of strength, durability, and affordability that many homeowners are looking for. Its look and color can be adapted with the use of alloys and coatings.

Copper

Copper is perhaps the most striking metal roofing component. It is manufactured into tiles, shingles, panels, and sheets to add a beautiful accent to your home. It is also an excellent choice for maintaining the appeal of historic homes.

Copper roofing can last up to 100 years with a gradual color change as patina develops, enhancing its depth and beauty.

Zinc

Zinc roofing is a very durable option that is attractive, non-corrosive, fire-resistant, and inhibits mold growth. Zinc roofing works well for low-sloped, steep-sloped, flat, and mansard roofs, and your metal roof investment may last up to 100 years.

Many homeowners enjoy its very distinctive look as patina forms over time.

Innovative Metal Roofing Designs

Metal roofing has come a long way in fashion and design to give your roofing a more natural look. You can choose from a wide variety of colors and styles. No matter your home’s aesthetic, there is a metal roofing option that will really shine.

Standing Seam

The standing seam metal roof is the most popular and recognizable option for many homes and businesses. These interlocking panels join with long seams that run vertically from peak to eaves, providing exceptional protection from the elements.

A standing seam roof can be constructed from steel, copper, or zinc in a variety of colors. This roofing is a great option for regions with heavy snowfall.

Corrugated

Corrugated metal roofing is very distinctive in its look and texture. Made from hot-dipped galvanized steel, this is an affordable option with easy installation for an old-fashioned rustic look. It is cost-effective, lightweight, and rust-resistant.

Metal Shingles

If you’re looking for a more classic look that many associate with regular asphalt shingles, metal shingles are the way to go. Just like traditional shingles, metal shingles have a lot of versatility.

Metal shingles can mimic different shingle styles and patterns—including the look of wood shingles. These interlocking shingles come in a variety of colors and design options to match your home’s architecture perfectly.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How Long Do Metal Roofs Last?

High-quality metal roofing is designed to last at least 40 years. Some roofs can even last up to 70 years. Compared to the 20-year lifespan of an asphalt shingle roof, metal roofs can be a great investment!

Is There Any Upkeep on a Metal Roof?

Yes, a metal roof will still need some maintenance and upkeep, but it is generally much less intensive than traditional shingles. This usually means keeping the roof clean of debris, snow, and ice, and performing routine inspections for needed repairs.

Can You Put Solar Panels on a Metal Roof?

Yes! Metal roofs are a great choice for solar panels. They offer more durability and longevity, which also maintains the integrity of the solar panels.

Do Metal Roofs Make a House Hotter?

No. A metal roof can actually offer extra help in keeping your house cool by reflecting the sun’s heat rather than absorbing it as a standard asphalt shingle would.

How Long will Insurance Cover a Metal Roof?

There isn’t one easy answer to this. Every insurance company has its own rules for the coverage you might receive. Your insurance company will likely make its decision based on factors such as the age and condition of the roof, the quality of the materials and installation, typical weather patterns for your area, and any current warranties in effect.
