FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What Credit Score Do You Need for a New Roof?

Contrary to common misconceptions, a high credit score isn’t always necessary for roofer financing. While a higher credit score can qualify you for lower interest rates, American Roofing Company is dedicated to working with you to find a flexible financing option that best suits your situation.

How Often Does a Roof Need to Be Replaced?

Roofs have varying lifespans depending on the materials used, how well they’re maintained, and the quality of the installation. On average, most roofs require replacement every 20 to 25 years. However, factors like severe weather, poor-quality materials, or incorrect installation can shorten this timeline.

How Much Does a New Roof Cost?

The average cost of a roof replacement in the US is around $10,000. However, replacement costs can vary widely based on factors like the size of your home, the roofing material used, and any additional services performed during the process. Fortunately, if you’re searching for new roof financing near you, American Roofing Company offers flexible financing solutions and quality service.

What Is the Cheapest Roof to Replace?

Asphalt shingles tend to be the most affordable roofing material to replace. Since an asphalt roof typically lasts 15 to 30 years and is more cost-effective than other materials, it’s a popular choice among residential and commercial property owners.

How Much Equity Does a New Roof Add to a House?

A new roof can significantly boost your home’s curb appeal and overall value. However, like all home improvement projects, you’re not likely to see a 100% return on investment (ROI).
Additionally, the amount of equity a roof replacement adds depends on the type of materials used. For example, according to Zillow, asphalt shingles have an average ROI of 68.2%, while a metal roof returns an average of 60.9% of its cost.
