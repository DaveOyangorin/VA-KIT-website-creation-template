Common Causes for Roof Leak Repair in Utah

Utah residents and businesses experience heavy snow, melting ice in the winter, and the full range of harsh elements for the remainder of the year. Our weather results in the following causes for roof leak repair.

Damaged or Missing Shingles

When shingles crack, curl, or loosen, they can expose the underlayment to moisture, snow, and rain. In some cases, roof patching can resolve minor damage before it spreads.

Clogged Gutters

Clogged gutters mean that rainwater and melting snow often back up under shingles and seep onto the roof deck.

Worn Flashing

Flashing is a thin material that seals roof valleys, chimneys, and vents on your roof. When flashing deteriorates, you can experience a damaging leak quickly. Worn flashing is one common reason homeowners call us for residential roof leak repair in Utah.

Ice Dams

Utah residents and businesses often have ice dams where warm and cold air meet at the edge of their roofs, causing poor draining that leads to leaks.

Improper Installation

Unprofessional installations are a common cause for unexpected leaks. Patching a roof leak is best left to professionals.

Signs of a Leaky Roof

If you notice a new puddle on your floor after a recent rain or see a new drip from the ceiling, and think “I need to fix my roof before this gets worse,” contact a licensed roofing professional. But what are some less overt signs you have a leaky roof that needs repair? Signs include:

  • Water stains on your ceiling or walls
  • Sagging or buckling drywall
  • Warping ceiling tiles
  • Mold or mildew growth
  • Exposed insulation
  • Missing shingles
  • Cracked flashing
  • Unusual dripping or trickling sounds after a rain
  • Musty odors
  • Soft or rotting wood
  • Pooling water around your home or business’s foundation
  • Damaged soffits
  • Gaps around the vent flashing

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How much does roof leak repair cost in Utah?

Roof repair costs vary greatly depending on the materials, the surface area that must be repaired or replaced, and the extent of roof damage. Extensive localized issues like water damage, rot, or structural damage can be more expensive than replacing a few shingles.

What should I do if I discover a leak in my roof?

It’s vital to take immediate action. A small leak can turn into more expensive damage if left unresolved. Contact a professional to identify the leak’s source and mitigate the damage before it gets more extensive.

How do you fix a leaking shingle roof?

Fixing a leaking shingle roof is best left to professionals. Since it requires working at a great height, you must consider the proper safety gear, weather conditions, and using the correct materials. You must find the leak, assess the damage, potentially replace the underlayment, and place and secure new shingles.

How serious is a roof leak?

A roof leak can be a minor drip that’s only begun to cause minimal damage, but more severe roof leaks can mean thousands of dollars.

Even a tiny drip can indicate a more significant, undiagnosed problem. Any sign of a leak is always a good warning sign to have a trustworthy professional assess potential damage.

How do I calculate the cost of a new roof?

Estimating the cost of repairing or replacing a roof is complicated. You must consider the following:

  • Your roof’s square footage
  • Tear-off expenses
  • The extent of any leak damage
  • The cost of repairing leak damage
  • The materials needed
  • The roof’s pitch
  • Added labor costs

The best way to accurately assess the cost of a new roof is to request an estimate from multiple professional roofers. With that being said, cost alone should not be a primary determination of a roofing estimate’s quality. For this reason, when receiving estimates from roofing contractors, you should more or less be vetting the contractor more so than you are their estimate. Some Utah companies, like American Roofing, provide a free roof estimate.

What time of year is best to replace a roof?

The best time of year to consider replacing your roof is late spring, summer, or early fall. This timeframe experiences warmer, drier months and is safer for avoiding risks like leaks and other weather-related damage during installation. The actual replacement date ultimately depends on specific local weather conditions.

How long does a 30-year roof really last?

Ideally, a 30-year roof can last 30 years. However, weather damage, water leaks, and poor maintenance can lead to a shorter lifespan. If you maintain a roof correctly and extreme weather events don’t hasten wear and tear, it can last that long. Typically, a roof will last 20 to 25 years.

How long does a shingle roof last in Utah?

Typically, a shingle roof lasts 15 to 30 years in Utah. However, specific weather conditions, material quality, and maintenance determine how long a shingle roof will last.
