Quality Shingle Choices

Not every shingle is created equal. We can help you find the best choice for your home.

Asphalt

Residential asphalt shingles are a popular choice for homeowners in Utah. They are a smart choice for their function, style, and affordability. You’ll find a wide selection of colors, patterns, and textures available. In general, these shingles are easy to install and maintain for an overall 20-year lifespan.

Architectural

Architectural shingles can also be referred to as laminate or dimensional shingles. These shingles are designed to be robust and fashionable. Along with a variety of styles and colors, architectural shingles create a stylish, textured look that adds dimension to your home’s aesthetic.

Impact-Resistant

If you live in one of Utah’s hot spots for severe weather conditions, impact-resistant shingles offer better protection for your home. They are designed to resist cracking, splitting, or other damage from hail, high winds, and flying debris. Impact-resistant shingles may be made from reinforced asphalt, metal, or synthetic composites to achieve durability and a longer lifespan, even in harsh conditions.

Luxury

An upgrade to luxury asphalt shingles means premium-quality construction, style, and protection for your home, all wrapped up in a beautiful package. Luxury shingles carry the sophistication associated with styles like cedar shakes or slate. These shingles are a smart choice for high-end homes in regions with harsh weather conditions.

Energy-Efficient Cooling

Advances in science and engineering have introduced a new line of energy-saving asphalts with higher solar reflective and thermal properties than the average asphalt shingle. These shingles can help reduce household energy consumption and protect the environment without compromising aesthetics or comfort.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long do shingles last?

The most common type of asphalt shingle lasts typically about 20 years. Our professional shingle contractors can help you decide when it’s time to replace or repair your roof.

How do I know when it's time to replace my roof?

There are several ways to tell when your roof is at the end of its life. Look for warping of the roof line, missing or damaged shingles, mold, or discolored areas. Call in an expert when you see anything that concerns you to avoid larger problems.

Can you install shingles year-round in Utah?

Yes, it is possible to install shingles year-round in Utah, but winter introduces some distinct challenges in safety and getting the best installation possible. These jobs often require more flexibility and communication to ensure a quality project result.
