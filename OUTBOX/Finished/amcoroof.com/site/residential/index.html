Signs Your Utah Roof Needs Repair or Replacement

Do not wait for a leak to become a bigger problem. Look for:

  • Broken, curling, or missing shingles
  • Damaged or failing flashing
  • Granules collecting in your gutters
  • Moss or algae growth
  • Water stains on interior ceilings or walls
  • Shingle pieces in your yard after a storm

If you are seeing any of these, an inspection is the right first step.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How often should an asphalt roof be replaced?

Generally speaking, asphalt roofs will need to be replaced every 15-30 years, often sooner in warm, humid climates. Severe winters and heavy rainfall can also shorten the material’s lifespan, so be sure to review your roof’s condition regularly.

When is it better to repair or replace a roof?

Some roof damage can be mended with a repair rather than a full roof replacement. Repair is ideal when:

  • Your roof is relatively young.
  • You’re not planning on staying in the same house for a lengthy period of time.
  • Damage is specific to a small area.

On the other hand, if severe damage has occurred or you have major flooding inside your home due to an old and malfunctioning roof, a replacement is likely the better choice. A roof can only be repaired so many times before repairs will no longer get the job done.

Will insurance cover roof damage or replacement?

It depends on the cause of the damage. Storm damage from hail, wind, or severe weather is typically covered under homeowner’s insurance policies. Damage from age or lack of maintenance usually is not. We recommend starting with an inspection so you have documentation before filing a claim.

What does a roof replacement include?

Roof replacement means all the roofing material down to the deck is torn off and replaced with new materials. That includes the shingles, underlayment, and flashings.

Roof replacement differs from a shingle recover, where shingle recover is done, a new layer of shingles is laid on top of the current roofing. This can make sense in some cases, but a roof replacement is generally the more thorough and longer lasting option for your home.

Plus, American Roofing offers a 5-year warranty on new roof installations, keeping your family and home sheltered for years to come.

What time of year is best to replace a roof?

It can depend on where you live and the climate, but you can have your roof replaced at any time of year. The warmer and drier months of the year are more conducive to roof replacement, but the off season months can also offer savings on materials. It is best to plan around precipitation and other weather interfering with working outdoors, especially snow and ice.

How do I choose a residential roofing contractor in Salt Lake City?

Look for a contractor with verifiable local experience, manufacturer certifications, clear warranty coverage, and a track record of honest assessments. American Roofing holds CertainTeed Gold Star and SELECT ShingleMaster certifications and has been serving Utah homeowners since 1954.
