What Is a Downspout? - American Roofing Company

What is a downspout, and how does it work with your gutters to keep your home dry and safe? Learn about appearance and upkeep for proper water drainage.

An effective drainage system working with your high-quality roof will keep your home well-protected from the elements. One vital component of the drainage system is the downspout.

So, what exactly is a downspout? Most homes require a rain gutter system that runs horizontally along the roofline. Your downspout is the outlet off the gutter that directs water down from your roofline and safely to the ground or drainage system. It keeps your gutters from overflowing and protects your roof, home structure, and foundation from water damage.

What Happens If You Don’t Have a Downspout?

If your home does not have gutters or downspouts, water from rain and snow will simply seep into your roof and exterior structures. This water will eventually result in costly damage to your walls, foundation, and yard.

The water also encourages the growth of moss, mold, and mildew and invites unwanted pests.

How Long Can a House Go Without Gutters?

It goes without saying that if your home is in a wet climate, it will be important to install new gutters and downspouts as quickly as possible. If your roof is steep or the climate is dry you might be able to go up to a year without gutters. However, it is always advisable to consult a professional about your gutter and downspout needs to make sure your home is properly protected.

What Does a Downspout Look Like on a House?

A downspout is most often about three to four inches in diameter and runs vertically down the side of your home to the ground. Downspouts are generally made of the same material as your gutters. Materials like aluminum and copper are often used.

How Are Downspouts Attached to the House?

Downspouts are attached to your home with metal straps and screws to keep them from dislodging or becoming damaged in adverse weather.

How Many Downspouts Does a House Have?

Several factors will go into determining how many downspouts your home needs to create an effective drainage system. Rainy climates will need more gutters and downspouts than dryer climates. Large homes will also require more gutters and downspouts.

You can estimate the number of downspouts your home may need by measuring the length of each horizontal roof section. Every section will need at least one downspout. For longer lengths, over 40 feet, two or more drainage points will be needed.

What Is Another Name for a Downspout?

A downspout may also be called a waterspout, downpipe, drain spout, drainpipe, roof drain, rone, or leader.

What Is a Yankee Gutter?

A Yankee gutter is attached to your home’s roof rather than along the edge of the roof line. This design is seen often in older, historic homes.

Where Should a Downspout Drain To?

Your downspout should direct water as far away from your home as possible. It can end about one foot above the ground to direct water at least four feet away from your foundation, landscaping, and sidewalks. Downspout extensions and splash blocks can also be used to further direct the water where you’d like it to go.

Should You Bury Your Downspouts?

Done correctly, burying your downspout can be an effective way to direct water where you want it to go. This needs to be planned out carefully, using a downspout extension and an appropriate trench for the water to flow away from your home.

How Do You Maintain Your Gutters and Downspouts?

Proper maintenance is needed to keep your gutters and downspouts in good condition. A general rule of thumb is to clean them of dirt and debris twice a year.

Should I Take Downspouts Off in Winter?

No. Do not remove your downspouts unless they need repairs or replacement. Taking them down and putting them back up can inadvertently cause damage or structural strain.

How Long Do Downspouts Last?

Careful maintenance and repair of damaged sections can help your gutters and downspouts last as long as 20 years. In general, consider whether you may need new gutters and downspouts when it is time to replace your roof.

Do Roofers Replace Downspouts?

Yes, most roofing companies also offer gutter and downspout installation. American Roofing provides this professional and affordable service for residents of Utah.

How Much Should New Gutters and Downspouts Cost?

Depending on the size of your home and the materials chosen, your new gutters and downspouts may cost anywhere from $15 to $40 per foot. It is always best to talk with a professional to make sure your home’s needs are met within your budget.
