Luxury Asphalt Shingles - American Roofing Company

If you plan to install a new roof, consider luxury asphalt shingles. See how they compare to standard and architectural asphalt shingles in this glossary.

Luxury asphalt shingles are a premium version of standard shingles, designed to mimic the look of high-end roofing materials like cedar shakes or slate. They come in various colors and styles, giving your roof a sophisticated, textured look that can improve its aesthetic appeal.

However, luxury asphalt shingles offer more than just good looks. Their thicker, heavier profile makes them more durable than standard or architectural shingles. These shingles offer better protection against harsh weather, making them a smart investment for storm- and high-wind-prone regions.

Are Luxury Shingles Worth It?

When shopping for luxury shingles, you’ll notice they cost more than standard asphalt shingles. Given the extra cost, you might wrestle with whether to buy them or stick to the standard. The answer often boils down to your budget and what you value in a roof. However, many homeowners eagerly opt for luxury asphalt shingles for the following reasons:

  1. Due to the fact that luxury shingles are often thicker and heavier than traditional asphalt shingles, they tend to wear more slowly and as a result often last longer, which can mean fewer replacements over time.
  2. Remember, luxury asphalt shingles are generally more visually appealing than the standard variety. As a result, they can increase your home’s resale value.
  3. While they cost more than standard shingles, luxury asphalt shingles offer the upscale look of slate and cedar at a fraction of the price.

What Is the Difference Between Luxury and Architectural Shingles?

It’s easy to confuse luxury asphalt shingles with architectural shingles since they both offer more than standard 3-tab shingles. However, there are some key differences.

  • Thickness: Luxury asphalt shingles are 1/2 to 3/4 inches thick, compared to the 3/10 to 1/2 inch thickness of lighter architectural shingles. The extra thickness and weight provide better protection and a more pronounced, dimensional look for your roof.
  • Aesthetics: While architectural shingles have a more refined look compared to standard shingles, luxury shingles take it a step further. They are designed to imitate slate, wood, or other products to provide a similar feel of elegance.
  • Cost: Luxury shingles are generally more expensive than architectural shingles due to their enhanced features and superior materials. However, the investment can pay off in the long run with increased durability and curb appeal.

What Maintenance Is Required for Luxury Asphalt Shingles?

Maintaining luxury asphalt shingles is fairly straightforward, which is one of the reasons they’re so popular. Unlike other roofing materials that might need frequent repairs or special treatments, luxury shingles require minimal upkeep.

Using a soft bristle brush or a leaf blower to clear away debris like leaves and branches helps prevent moisture buildup, which can lead to mold or algae growth on your luxury shingles. Avoid using a power washer, as the strong water stream can damage the shingles. It’s also a good idea to inspect your roof annually, especially after severe weather, to catch any minor issues before they escalate.

Overall, luxury asphalt shingles are built to last. As long as you keep an eye on them and address any concerns promptly, they should serve you well for decades.

Can Luxury Asphalt Shingles Be Installed on Any Roof?

Luxury asphalt shingles are versatile, but they aren’t the perfect fit for every roof. They work best on roofs with a moderate to steep slope, where their thicker profile and dimensional look can stand out.

If your roof has a lot of intricate angles or a complex design, luxury shingles can add that extra touch of elegance. However, if you have a flat or low-slope roof, other roofing materials might be a better choice, as these shingles are designed to shed water efficiently on steeper pitches. Before deciding, it’s a good idea to consult with a roofing professional to ensure that luxury shingles are the right match for your home’s structure.
