Questions we hear every week

Straight answers about choosing a Salt Lake City roofer.

How do I choose the right Salt Lake City roofer?

Choose a Salt Lake City roofing company with local experience, proper certifications, warranty support, and a track record with the type of roof or property you own. American Roofing Company is an SLC roofer that has served Utah since 1954 and works on residential, commercial, apartment, and condo roofing projects.

Does American Roofing work outside Salt Lake City?

Yes. American Roofing Company is a Utah roofer based in Salt Lake City and serves roofing customers across the Wasatch Front, from Brigham City to Ogden, Salt Lake City, Park City, Tooele, Provo, and other Utah communities.

Can a Utah roofing company help with storm damage documentation?

Yes. American Roofing Company can inspect visible storm damage, document roofing issues, and provide a repair or replacement estimate. Insurance coverage depends on the policy, cause of damage, age, and roof condition.
