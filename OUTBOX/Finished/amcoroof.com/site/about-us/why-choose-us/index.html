About American Roofing

Why Choose American?

From 60+ years of being in the business, to exclusive warranties, American Roofing has several reasons why we are the superior choice for all your Salt Lake City, UT roofing needs.

801-269-1276Free Quote

Why should you choose American Roofing over the others? Here is why!

We Have Been In Business Since 1954

In an industry where many roofers are in business today and out-of-business tomorrow, it is unusual to find a company with our experience and proven record of quality and reliability.

We Stand Behind our Roof Warranty

Some roofing contractors offer guarantees that exceed the length of time that they have been in business. Remember that a guarantee is only as good as the company behind it.

We Do a Final Inspection on Every Job

All of our roofing projects are given a thorough final inspection to insure the quality of each job and your personal satisfaction with our work.

Exclusive 5-Year Roof Warranty on Labor and Workmanship

While a two-year warranty on labor and workmanship is standard in the roofing industry, we offer a five-year warranty on all new roof installations. Warranties are also available for up to 10 or 20 years on flat roofs.

We Give a Written Manufacturer’s Warranty

Upon completion of your roofing project, we provide you with a written manufacturer’s warranty on your shingles or flat roof, for your peace of mind and protection.

Honesty and Integrity

We will never try to sell you a new roof if your roof does not need to be replaced, or if your money is better spent in repairing or maintaining your existing roof.

Customer Satisfaction is our #1 Priority

We want you to be so pleased with your roofing experience with us, that you will gladly recommend us to a friend or relative. We offer a $50 incentive to our previous customers whose referral or recommendation leads to a reroofing project.

We Accept Major Credit Cards

For your convenience we are happy to accept payment with your Visa, MasterCard, or Discover card.

Contact American Roofing Today

If you’re ready to hire the best roofers in the business, contact American Roofing today. Whether you’re looking for a roofing company in Salt Lake County, Utah County, or Davis County, we’re happy to provide a free quote and show you what it means to deliver premium craftsmanship and put customers first.
