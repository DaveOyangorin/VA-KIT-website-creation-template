Midvale, UT

We’re proud to offer our high-quality roofing services to the Midvale, UT area because we are devoted to helping keep Midvale beautiful and robust.

Midvale is an urban city on the outskirts of Salt Lake but with a personality all its own. The city’s name comes from the fact that Midvale is situated quite literally in the middle of the valley at the base of Cottonwood Canyons. A few films have even been filmed in Midvale, most notably The Sandlot.

Midvale is a great place to work and live. There are excellent schools and many jobs available. In Midvale, 44% own their own home, and the main industries in the area are health care and retail.

The weather in Midvale is fairly temperate from Spring to Fall, but Winter can be bitingly cold, with around 42 inches of snow per year. All of that snow and ice can be hard on your roof and cause lasting damage. In addition to roof repair in Midvale, the American Roofing Company offers snow removal to take some of the pressure off of your housetop.

How to Know If You Need a Roof Replacement

Here are some signs to look for:

  • Shingles that are cracked or damaged
  • Shingles that have come out of place or come off entirely
  • Sagging in the roof
  • Signs of trapped moisture or rotting
  • Water leaking through the ceiling
  • Your roof is more than 20 years old
