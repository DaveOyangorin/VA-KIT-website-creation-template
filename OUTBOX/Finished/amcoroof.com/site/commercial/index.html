All Major Commercial Roof Systems

American Roofing installs, repairs, and maintains every major commercial roofing system used in Utah.

TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin)

Lifespan: 20-30 years

UV-reflective, energy-efficient, and leak-resistant thanks to heat-welded seams. A common choice for office buildings, retail centers, and warehouses in Utah.

EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer)

Lifespan: 20-30 years

A rubber-based membrane valued for cold-climate flexibility, cost-effectiveness, and ease of repair. Well-suited for warehouses, office parks, and manufacturing facilities.

PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)

Lifespan: 20-30 years

Superior resistance to chemicals, grease, and fire with heat-welded watertight seams. The top choice for restaurants, hospitals, food processing, and industrial properties.

Modified Bitumen

Lifespan: 15-25 years

A multi-ply asphalt system with enhanced flexibility and puncture resistance. Proven for schools, government facilities, and institutional properties with heavy rooftop traffic.

Built-Up Roofing (BUR)

Lifespan: 20-30+ years

Alternating layers of bitumen and reinforcing fabric create redundant waterproofing. One of the most durable systems available for large industrial and institutional buildings.

Metal Roofing

Lifespan: 40-70+ years

Fire-resistant, energy-efficient, and built for the long term. Available in standing seam, batten seam, and panel styles for commercial and industrial properties.

Roof Coatings and Restoration

Lifespan: 10-20+ years

Manufacturer-approved coating systems can extend roof life and may qualify under existing warranty terms. The right system depends on your roof type and current condition.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long should a commercial roof last?

The longevity of a given commercial roof depends on several factors, which can lead to varying roof lifespans of approximately 10 to 40 years. These factors include:

  • The harshness of local weather conditions. In some regions, the weather breaks down commercial roofs more quickly, and more repairs and maintenance are needed to draw out roof lifespans. Ice dams are common on Utah roofs during the winter, as is snow buildup and condensation that can really damage a roof, if not dealt with properly.
  • The roofing materials used. Options include industrial metal roofing, asphalt, PVC (polyvinyl chloride), TPO (thermoplastic polyolefin), and EPDM (ethylene propylene diene monomer).
  • The roof design. Options include pitched, flat, and low-sloped. There are advantages and disadvantages for each design, and longevity is not the only factor considered when choosing one for a particular building.
What is commercial roof restoration?

Commercial roof repair, or restoration, is a process of adding life to your business’s roofing. It can involve repairing leaks, flashing, or edges on your roofing. It can also include sealing the entire roof to keep it protected and free of leaks.

Commercial roof restoration is a cost-effective option that adds years of life to your roofing. It also reduces waste, and it can save you a lot of money over a complete roof replacement if replacement is not necessary.

How long does a roof restoration last?

Commercial roof restoration is surprisingly durable. While a new commercial roof typically lasts over 20 years, a roof restoration can continue offering protection for 10 to 15 years. If roof replacement is not necessary, a roof restoration is a cost-effective and lasting option.

What’s the difference between a free quote and a paid inspection?

A free quote is appropriate when your scope is already reasonably clear. A paid inspection is for complex or unknown situations where you need a full condition report with photographs, findings, and recommendations for budgeting, stakeholder review, or ownership transition.

Can you maintain manufacturer warranty coverage?

Yes, and this is one of the most important reasons to choose a certified contractor. American Roofing holds CertainTeed’s Gold Star commercial certification, which authorizes no-dollar-limit warranties up to 25 years. We perform repairs and restorations in compliance with manufacturer specifications to keep existing warranties active.

How do property managers choose a commercial roofing contractor?

Prioritize certification level, insurance coverage, experience with insurance work and claims, documented experience with your building type, and long-term warranty support. Licensing is a legal baseline. Certification means the contractor has met specific standards for training, bonding, and quality
