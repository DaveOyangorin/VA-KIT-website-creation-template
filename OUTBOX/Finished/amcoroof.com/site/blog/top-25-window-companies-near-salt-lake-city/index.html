Top 25 Window Companies Near Salt Lake City

By American Roofing CompanyPublished 2023-01-20
801-269-1276Free Quote

Are you looking for a company to repair or replace your windows in or near Salt Lake City? Maybe your windows are old and drafty, or perhaps you’ve had a mishap and have some broken glass that needs replacing.

We’ve compiled a list of the best window companies in Salt Lake City.

Best Window Companies in the Salt Lake City Area

Renewal by Anderson

Who is the best company to buy windows from? One of our top choices is Renewal by Anderson. Anderson offers beautiful replacement windows that are custom crafted and made to order.

Advanced Windows USA

This company specializes in commercial and residential windows, doors, and siding. They supply factory-direct vinyl replacement windows, sliding glass doors, French doors, and pet doors.

Rocky Mountain Windows

Providing residential replacement windows, this company can help give your home an affordable upgrade by replacing your old, damaged windows with new custom wooden windows.

Window World Utah

Window World of Utah installs energy-efficient windows and doors throughout northern Utah. The company helps you increase your curb appeal and improve efficiency.

HomeStar Windows

At HomeStar Windows & Doors, you get the best price guaranteed on windows, entry doors, and patio doors. HomeStar replaces vinyl and wood windows and also offers vinyl patio doors.

Peak Windows

Peak Windows is one of the few Utah companies that manufactures and installs windows. They sell directly to customers, eliminating the middleman markup. Peak Windows has commercial and residential windows and doors.

Wasatch Window

This company provides door and window replacement in Utah. They claim to supply the best value in vinyl replacement doors and windows. Their professional installers will make you feel comfortable in your home while they work.

Complete Glass

Complete Glass uses Milgard vinyl replacement windows to beautify your home. These windows have a lifetime warranty on the whole window, including the glass. If your window breaks, it’s covered!

Jones Paint & Glass

Jones Paint & Glass has been supplying windows, mirrors, doors, and paint products since 1938. They provide high-quality vinyl and wood windows, as well as custom mirrors and shower doors.

Champion Window

At Champion Window, your replacement windows are guaranteed for life. The company also provides sunrooms, siding, and doors. Champion will help you choose the perfect windows for your home.

Valley Glass

This company was started in 1956 and is now a full-service glass company, supplying glass for residential, commercial, and auto glass. Their skilled technicians have years of experience installing vinyl and triple-pane windows.

The Glass Company

This family-owned business repairs and replaces mirrors and windows in the Salt Lake City area. While they can service home residences, most of their work is commercial. They provide window repair for restaurants, stores, and property management companies.

Sawyer Glass

The professional glaziers at Sawyer Glass have the knowledge and experience to provide first-class service for all of your glass needs. They offer shower doors, cabinet glass, custom mirrors, glass furniture tops, and, of course, energy-efficient vinyl windows.

Pella of SLC

Pella Windows & Doors of Salt Lake City supplies fiberglass, wood, and vinyl windows and patio doors. They also sell wood, steel, and fiberglass entry doors. Pella’s experienced professionals have the skills to make any project a success.

Central Glass Company

This company provides automotive glass repair, residential windows, and custom glass. They also offer custom mirrors, insulated glass, RV glass repair, and trailer glass repair.

Solar Window and Door

Solar Window & Door supplies replacement doors and windows, shower doors, mirrors, energy-efficient window tinting, and glass railings. Their trained glaziers and salespeople will help you choose the right products for your home.

Alta Glass & Window

Alta Glass & Window provides single and double-pane window repair, framed and frameless glass shower enclosures and shower doors, tabletop glass, and mirrors for your bathroom, workout room, or anywhere else in your home.

The Brokerage

The Brokerage Windows & Doors supplies windows, doors, shower doors, and mirrors for every budget. You’ll find everything from simple designs to the most beautiful architecture. They do both new construction and replacement windows.

Murray Glass

Murray Glass has all of your glass needs covered, from sliding glass doors to home window repair and replacement. Murray also has glass shower doors, custom glass, commercial glass, glass walls and doors, and glass tables and shelves.

Sugar House Glass

Sugar House Glass has exactly what you need when it comes to residential window repair and replacement. They also offer decorative glass inserts, glass tabletops, glass shower enclosures, and other household glass.

Sanders Glass

A family-owned and operated company, Sanders Glass offers 24-hour emergency service, seven days a week. The company does glass installation and replacement in homes, offices, and vehicles.

Glass Unlimited

Glass Unlimited offers expert auto glass repair, replacement windows, glass doors, mirrors, and more. They do glass installations in storefronts and other commercial properties, as well as vinyl replacement windows for residences.

Clear Choice Windows

This owner-operated business has free estimates and a satisfaction guarantee. Their replacement windows are also guaranteed by the manufacturer. They do window installation, glass repair, and patio door installation.

Residential Glassworks

Residential Glassworks provides vinyl replacement windows with high-performance glass options and low-E energy-efficient glass. They also supply European shower doors and custom mirrors.

Mountain States Windows

Mountain States Windows & Siding offers durable, well-insulated replacement windows and siding. They can assist you in planning, designing, and building your dream home with the perfect siding and windows.

Get Your Windows and Your Roof Repaired

If you’re in the Salt Lake City area and you need replacement windows for your home, any of the companies on this list will do an outstanding job.
