Pleasant Grove, UT

Pleasant Grove was founded by Mormon pioneers in 1850 and got its current name because the settlers erected permanent cabins among a “pleasant grove” of cottonwood trees in the area. The city used to have numerous fruit- and berry-producing farms because of the area’s rich soil; however, urban growth has led to their conversion to modern structures.

Summers in Pleasant Grove are hot but dry, while winters are pleasant with a few storms. Although it snows, like most places in Utah, the mountains shelter the city from snow buildup.

Despite the area’s pleasant climate from being nestled between Utah Lake and Mount Timpanogos, sunlight, rain, and snow can damage your property’s roof. Is It Time to Repair or Replace My Roof?

Ignoring the signs of a failing roof usually results in bigger, more expensive problems down the road.

It’s time to call American Roofing for repairs or new roofing in Pleasant Grove once you observe the following symptoms:

  • Dark, water stains extending across ceilings or running down walls
  • Daylight coming through the roof
  • Mold or mildew on exterior walls
  • Damaged, loose, or worn-out roofing materials
  • Drooping or sagging spots on the roof

In addition, verify your roof’s age and compare it to the manufacturer’s estimated lifespan. For example, asphalt shingles last for 20 to 25 years. If yours is nearing or at the end of its expected lifespan, consider replacing instead of repairing it.

How Does a New Roof Increase Home Value?

Installing new roofing in Pleasant Grove will cost you more than repairs, but it’s a worthy investment in the long run. Here’s how a new roof adds value to your home.

Improves Curb Appeal: The roof is often the home’s most visible exterior feature, and when it is new and in good condition, your home is more appealing. The improved looks can be especially beneficial if you plan to put your property up for sale. With a new roof, you can quickly impress buyers and passersby at first glance.

Increase Energy Efficiency: A new roof may reflect heat from the sun and insulate your home interiors more efficiently than an old roof. As a result, your HVAC system doesn’t have to work as hard to keep indoor temperatures comfortable, saving you money.

Safety: The additional safety and security provided by a roof replacement is perhaps its most significant benefit. The roof protects your home interior from the elements and safeguards your belongings, giving you more peace of mind.
