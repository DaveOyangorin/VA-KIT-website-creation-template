# amcoroof.com redesign: notes for Angelo

Draft, 16 Oct 2026. Nothing is live. Client: **American Roofing Company** (amcoroof.com). This is *not* the roofamc.com client.

- `site/`: the files to put live. Same URLs, titles, meta descriptions and canonicals as today, with tracking kept.
- `preview/`: the private review copy. No tracking, forms off, draft strip on top.
- `_build/`: the source.
  - `extract.py` pulls the content out of the old pages.
  - `imgprep.py` downloads the images and converts them to WebP.
  - `build.py` and `pages.py` build the pages; `p_home.py` and `p_blog.py` are the hand-made pages.
  - `qa.py` checks the output.

- Redesign (v2, 18 Oct 2026): "Since 1954". Alfa Slab One + Instrument Sans + IBM Plex Mono; navy and flag red from the eagle logo. Transparent header over the winter crew photo, an enamel Est. 1954 pin, a year ruler with one tick per year from 1954 to 2026 next to the real stats (72+, 10.2 yr, 81%, 50%), the Repair-First Promise as a stamp on navy, and a project wall that now includes Hunter High and Hillsdale Elementary. The top bar was removed.

## What was done

- **107 pages rebuilt.** The home page and the new blog post were designed by hand. The other 105 existing URLs were moved into the new design with their own text, H1, title, meta description, canonical, FAQs and testimonials kept.
- **Every image is local** in `site/assets/img/`: 243 WebP files, all under 150 KB, with width/height and alt text set. That includes 229 photos from the client's own pages and their project galleries (Hillsdale Elementary, Hunter High, Gregson Square and more).
  - No AI images.
  - The home page and the new blog post use only real project photos, roofs and buildings. Four iStock "team" photos (clear-communication, cost-transparency, quality-workmanship, workmanship-warranty) were removed everywhere.
  - Migrated blog and service pages keep the images the client already uses on those pages. Some are iStock photos of people (for example "workers replacing shingles" and "man calling landlord"). Swap them for real crew photos if the client has any.
- **Unsplash photos used (real, Unsplash License):** icicles-snow-overhang-roof (photo-1770128076146), icicles-log-cabin-eave (1642344497711), snowy-house-evergreens (1553550319), snow-on-roof-edge-leaves (1763046041616, not used yet).

## Design direction

- **References actually reviewed:**
  - Filson (filson.com): heavy heritage display type over photos.
  - Walsh Group (walshgroup.com): a fourth-generation family builder, navy, leading with heritage.
  - Bartlett Cocke (bartlettcocke.com, from the earlier roofamc research): navy and red, "since 1959", big stat numbers.
  - Kiewit and Barton Malow blocked automated visits, so they weren't used.
- **Colors come from the eagle logo:** navy #13284D, royal #22418B, flag red #A32226, on a cool off-white.
- **Fonts:** Bitter (a slab serif with a heritage-sign feel) for headings, and Public Sans (the typeface of the U.S. Web Design System) for body text. Verified loading.
- **Signature details:** a "Since 1954" seal, a navy/red double-rule label, the real stat band (72+ years, 10.2-year foreman tenure, 81% repeat/referral, 50% of projects generate a referral), and the Repair-First Promise badge.
- **Fixed from the old site:**
  - Mulish everywhere, with gray headings.
  - Blank image boxes and a large empty gap in the steps section.
  - Identical white cards in every section.
  - The form crammed into the hero.

## SEO

- **URLs and canonicals are unchanged.** Every page's canonical is the exact URL it has today (`https://www.amcoroof.com/...`, with www and the trailing slash).
- **Titles and meta descriptions are kept exactly as the client has them.** 76 titles fall outside 50–60 characters and 51 metas outside 150–160. The home title is 94 characters: "Best Roofing Company in Utah | Salt Lake City Roofers & Roofing Contractors | American Roofing". There's no keyword file for this client, so I didn't change them. Recommend a title/meta pass once Angelo has keyword data.
- **Schema on every page,** using the business's real NAP, geo, founding date and sameAs links from their current schema:
  - Service pages: Service (+ FAQPage where visible FAQs exist) + Breadcrumb.
  - City pages: Service with areaServed = that city.
  - Blog posts: BlogPosting with their original publish/modified dates.
  - Glossary: DefinedTerm / DefinedTermSet.
  - About pages: AboutPage.
  - Quote page: ContactPage.
  - Home: Organization + RoofingContractor + WebSite + FAQPage.
  - Every JSON-LD block parses.
- **robots.txt:**
  - Allows everything and keeps their existing `Crawl-delay: 10`. Google ignores it, but Bing respects it; consider removing it.
  - Explicitly allows OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot.
  - Points to `https://www.amcoroof.com/sitemap.xml`.
- **sitemap.xml** has 106 live URLs. Their current Yoast sitemap is at `/sitemap_index.xml`, so update Search Console after go-live.
- **The current sitemap lists 9 URLs that are really 301 redirects.** Those weren't rebuilt and were left out of the new sitemap:
  - `/services/` → `/`
  - `/services/low-slope-roof/` → `/` (a service page that now just sends people home; worth restoring)
  - `/blog/do-solar-panels-damage-roofs/` → `/` (a blog post that is gone from the live site)
  - `/commercial-roofing-replacement/` and `/commercial/testimonial/` → `/commercial/`
  - `/residential/portfolio/` and `/residential/testimonials/` → `/residential/`
  - `/apartment-roof-repair/portfolio/` and `/apartment-roof-repair/testimonials/` → `/apartment-roof-repair/`

  Keep these redirects in place when the new site goes live.

## New blog post (1,813 words)

`/blog/how-to-prevent-ice-dams-on-a-utah-roof/`, "How to Prevent Ice Dams on a Utah Roof"

- **Why this topic:** they already have a post called "How long do roofs last in Utah", so repeating that would compete with their own page. None of their 33 posts targets ice dams. Five service pages mention ice dams in passing, but none is about them.
- **Main phrase:** "how to prevent ice dams" + Utah. It's informational and long-tail, and stays on the blog only.
- **Supporting long-tail phrases:** what causes ice dams, are icicles bad for your roof, ice dam removal, heat cables for ice dams, ice and water shield.
- **No keyword data:** volume and difficulty aren't known. Please check.
- **Kept off the post on purpose:** the hard commercial terms owned by service pages (roof leak repair, emergency roofing, rain gutters, roof repair / roofing company Salt Lake City / Utah). The post only *links* to `/services/roof-leak-repair-service/`, `/services/emergency-roofing-utah/` and `/services/rain-gutters/`.
- **No overlap with their existing posts.** It links to them instead: the seasonal maintenance checklist, roof repair vs replacement, how long roofs last in Utah, ridge vents, cleaning gutters, and removing a swamp cooler.
- **Sources (linked in the post):**
  - NDSU Extension (how ice dams form, ~15" of insulation, air sealing, heat tape warning)
  - University of Utah (65" of snow at the airport, ~100" on the benches, lake snowbands)
  - ICC (Utah adopted the 2021 IRC)
  - Fine Homebuilding (the 24-inch ice barrier rule explained)
- **Author:** the byline is "American Roofing Company". [[NEEDS: author]] Can a named team member (from Meet the Team) be the author, with their role?
