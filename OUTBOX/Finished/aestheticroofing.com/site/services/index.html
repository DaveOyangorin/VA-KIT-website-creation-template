Residential Roofing Services for Every Stage of Your Roof

Roofing needs change throughout the life of your home. Our local roofing services cover everything from inspections and targeted repairs to complete replacements, giving you a trusted team to call whenever your roof needs attention.

Roof Replacement

When an aging or extensively damaged roof can no longer provide reliable protection, a full roof replacement can give your home a fresh start. We’ll evaluate your existing system, explain your options, and help you select roofing materials that fit your home’s appearance and long-term needs.

Roof Repair

Not every roofing problem requires replacement. Our roof repair services address leaks, missing or damaged shingles, flashing problems, and other localized issues. We focus on identifying the source of the problem and recommending a practical repair to restore your roof’s integrity.

Roof Inspections

You don’t have to wait for an active leak to find out something is wrong. A professional roof inspection can uncover deterioration, storm damage, and other concerns that may be difficult to see from the ground. We’ll thoroughly assess your roofing system and provide clear information about its condition and any next steps we recommend.

Storm Damage Roofing Services

Hail, wind, falling branches, and flying debris can cause both obvious and hidden roofing damage. Following severe weather, we’ll evaluate your roof to determine whether targeted repairs are sufficient or a storm roof replacement is the better long-term solution.

Gutter Installation

Your gutters work alongside your roof to move rain and melting snow away from your home. We provide professional gutter installation for homeowners who need to replace damaged, aging, or poorly performing gutter systems.

Roofing Options for Your Home

Choosing a roofing material involves balancing appearance, durability, budget, and the specific needs of your home. Aesthetic Roofing installs several roof types, giving Northern Utah homeowners options for different architectural styles and performance goals.

Our roofing options include asphalt shingles, designer asphalt shingles, standing seam metal roofing, and flat roofing systems. Rather than recommending the same material for every property, we’ll help you understand the differences so you can choose a system that makes sense for your home.

Professional Roofing Focused on Long-Term Performance

Quality professional roofing involves more than the materials you see from the street. Every component needs to work together to help your roof perform properly over time.

Ventilation is one area where Aesthetic Roofing takes an especially thorough approach. While some roofing contractors primarily consider exhaust ventilation, we also evaluate intake ventilation. Maintaining an effective balance between intake and exhaust helps support the performance and longevity of the overall roofing system.

We take the same complete-system approach throughout our work, considering how roofing materials, underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and drainage work together rather than treating each component in isolation.
