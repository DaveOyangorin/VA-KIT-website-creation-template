Frequently Asked Questions About Gutter Replacement

Do I need gutter guards with new gutters?

Gutter guards are optional, but they make a meaningful difference for homes surrounded by trees or heavy foliage. They reduce debris buildup, minimize clogging, and lower the frequency of gutter maintenance over time. During the estimate, we can assess whether guards make sense for your property and include them in your gutter replacement quote if you’d like to explore the option.

Should I replace gutters when replacing my roof?

In most cases, yes. If your gutters are aging, damaged, or improperly sloped, combining gutter replacement with our roofing services is often the smartest approach. Our team handles both systems together, aligning the roofline, flashing, and gutters from the start to ensure proper drainage and a streamlined process.

What are the signs my gutters need replacement?

The most telling indicators are frequent leaks at the seams, sections pulling away from the fascia, rust or standing water inside the gutter, and peeling paint or rot appearing on the fascia and siding below. Water pooling around your foundation after rainfall is perhaps the most serious sign. If you’re noticing several of these, a professional assessment is the right next step.

How often should gutters be replaced?

With proper installation and routine gutter maintenance, most systems last up to 20 years depending on material and local weather conditions. If your gutters are consistently leaking, sagging, or have been repaired multiple times, replacement is the more cost-effective and reliable solution.

How do seamless gutters differ from standard systems, and are they better?

Each seamless gutter run is formed on site from a continuous piece of metal, with no joints along its length where water could work its way through. Compared to sectional systems, they require less upkeep and deliver a cleaner finish along the roofline. For most residential properties, seamless gutters are the more durable and low-maintenance solution.

How long does gutter replacement take?

Most residential gutter replacement projects are completed in a single day. We remove the old system, install new seamless gutters and downspouts, verify proper slope and water flow, and clean up before we leave. Homes with more extensive or intricate rooflines may require additional time. Every project comes with a clear timeline provided upfront for you to plan around with confidence.

How much does gutter replacement cost?

Gutter replacement cost varies based on your home’s size, number of stories, material, and roofline configuration. Most full seamless gutter replacements for a single-family home fall in the low thousands. We conduct a full on-site assessment before any work starts and deliver a detailed written estimate, so you have clarity on scope and cost from the beginning.

When should I replace my gutters instead of repairing them?

Repairs work well for isolated issues like a loose section or a minor leak. Gutter replacement makes more sense when you’re seeing multiple leaks, sections pulling away from the fascia, sagging runs that collect standing water, or widespread corrosion. It is also a clear sign when gutters still overflow after cleaning, which usually means they are undersized.
