Residential Roofing Services in Salt Lake County

Every roof has different needs depending on its age, condition, materials, and exposure to the elements. Our Salt Lake County roofers provide residential roofing services ranging from targeted repairs to complete roof replacements.

Roof Repair

Roof damage doesn’t always mean you need a new roof. Our roof repair services address problems such as damaged or missing shingles, leaks, flashing issues, and other localized concerns.

We’ll evaluate the problem, explain what we find, and recommend repairs based on the condition of your roofing system.

Roof Replacement

When repairs are no longer the right solution, a professional roof replacement can give your home reliable protection with a new roofing system.

Our team helps you navigate material selection and other important project decisions before installation begins. We install the entire system with careful attention to underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and the other components that support long-term roof performance.

Roof Inspections

A professional roof inspection can help uncover damage and wear that may not be visible from the ground. Inspections can be useful after severe weather, when your roof begins showing signs of age, or when you simply want a better understanding of its condition.

Our roofing experts examine the system and provide straightforward information about any issues we find.

Storm Damage Roof Repair

Hail, strong winds, falling branches, and other severe weather can damage shingles and other roofing components. Our storm roof repair services help Salt Lake County homeowners identify and address damage after a storm.

Depending on the condition of the roof, the right solution may involve repairing an affected area or replacing a roofing system with more widespread damage.

Gutter Installation

Your gutters help move rainwater and snowmelt away from your roof and home. Aesthetic Roofing provides professional gutter installation for homeowners who need new gutters or want to replace an aging or damaged system.

Properly installed gutters work alongside your roofing system to manage water and help protect your property.

Roofing Materials for Salt Lake County Homes

The right roof should fit your home’s design, your priorities, and the needs of the structure itself. Aesthetic Roofing installs several types of roofing materials, giving Salt Lake County homeowners options for different styles and roof designs.

Asphalt Roofing

Asphalt roofing is a versatile option available in a wide range of styles and colors. A properly installed asphalt shingle system can provide dependable protection while complementing many different home styles.

Designer Asphalt Roofing

For homeowners who want to give their roof a more distinctive appearance, designer asphalt roofing offers additional dimension and visual character. These premium shingles can enhance curb appeal while providing the familiar protection of an asphalt roofing system.

Standing Seam Metal Roofing

Metal roofing offers a clean, distinctive appearance along with durable performance. Standing seam systems are an option for homeowners looking for a long-lasting roofing material with a modern or refined aesthetic.

Flat Roofing

Low-slope and flat roof areas require materials designed for their specific drainage and waterproofing needs. Our flat roofing services include installation, repair, inspection, and replacement for applicable residential roof areas.

Roofing Built for Northern Utah Conditions

Salt Lake County homes experience changing conditions throughout the year, from summer heat to winter snow and freezing temperatures. Wind, hail, and seasonal storms can also put additional stress on a roofing system.

That’s why a quality roof involves more than the material you see from the street. Underlayment, flashing, ice and water protection, drainage, and ventilation all contribute to the performance of the finished system.

Aesthetic Roofing pays particular attention to ventilation, including both intake and exhaust. A properly designed system helps manage airflow through the attic and supports the long-term performance of the roof.

Our top-notch roofing contractors take these details into account when installing your system, rather than treating the shingles or other exterior material as the entire roof.

Frequently Asked Questions About Roofing in Salt Lake County

How Often Should I Have My Roof Inspected in Salt Lake County?

It is a good idea to have your roof inspected periodically and after severe weather. Salt Lake County’s snow, wind, hail, and changing temperatures can cause damage that may not always be visible from the ground.

What Are Signs That I Need Roof Repair in Salt Lake County?

Common signs include missing or damaged shingles, leaks, water stains, damaged flashing, and excessive shingle granules in your gutters. You should also consider an inspection if you notice roof damage after a storm.

What Roofing Materials Work Well for Salt Lake County Homes?

Asphalt shingles, designer asphalt shingles, standing seam metal roofing, and certain flat roofing systems can all be options for Salt Lake County homes. The right material depends on your home’s roof design, appearance, budget, and other priorities.

Can You Repair Roof Damage Caused by Salt Lake County Storms?

Yes. Aesthetic Roofing repairs roof damage caused by hail, wind, falling debris, and other severe weather. We’ll inspect the damage and determine whether a repair or roof replacement is the better solution.

What Areas of Salt Lake County Does Aesthetic Roofing Serve?

Aesthetic Roofing serves homeowners throughout Salt Lake County, including Salt Lake City, Sandy, West Jordan, South Jordan, Draper, Murray, and surrounding communities.
