Complete Roofing Services for Utah County Homes

Roofing needs can range from repairing a small area of damage to replacing an aging system. Aesthetic Roofing provides comprehensive services so Utah County homeowners can turn to one local team throughout the life of their roof.

Roof Repair

Leaks, damaged shingles, flashing problems, and other localized issues can leave your home vulnerable if they aren’t addressed. Our residential roof repair services begin with identifying the source of the problem rather than simply treating the visible symptoms.

When repair is practical, we’ll recommend a solution based on the condition of your roof and the extent of the damage.

Roof Replacement

An aging or extensively damaged roof may eventually reach the point where replacement is a better option than continued repairs. Our roof replacement services give Utah County homeowners a completely new roofing system designed around their property.

Our team can help you select materials, prepare for installation, and understand what to expect throughout the project. We pay attention to the entire system, including underlayment, flashing, ventilation, and other components that contribute to reliable performance.

Professional Roof Inspections

Not every roofing problem is easy to spot from your yard. A professional roof inspection can provide a clearer picture of your roof’s condition and identify concerns that deserve attention.

An inspection can be particularly useful after a storm, when you’re noticing signs of a possible leak, or when an older roof hasn’t been evaluated recently.

Storm Damage Roofing Services

Utah County storms can bring wind, hail, and debris that damage shingles and other parts of a roofing system. Aesthetic Roofing provides storm damage roof repair to help homeowners understand the extent of the damage and determine what needs to happen next.

Some roofs may only need targeted repairs, while widespread damage can make replacement the more appropriate solution. We’ll evaluate the roof before recommending either approach.

Gutter Installation

Roof drainage doesn’t stop at the edge of your shingles. Gutters help direct rainwater and melting snow away from the roof and home, making them an important part of exterior water management.

We provide new gutter installation for Utah County homeowners who are replacing worn or damaged gutters or adding a new system as part of a larger roofing project.

Find the Right Roofing Material for Your Home

Roofing materials can change both the appearance and performance of your home. Aesthetic Roofing offers several residential roofing materials so you can compare options based on your home’s architecture, roof design, budget, and preferences.

Asphalt Shingle Roofing

Asphalt shingle roofing is a versatile choice that works with many Utah County home styles. Homeowners can choose from different colors and profiles while getting the familiar performance of an asphalt roofing system.

Designer Asphalt Shingles

For homeowners looking for more dimension and architectural character, designer shingles provide a premium take on asphalt roofing. Their distinctive appearance can make the roof a more prominent part of your home’s exterior design.

Standing Seam Metal Roofing

Standing seam metal roofing combines durable construction with clean, defined lines. It can be a good option for homeowners interested in a long-lasting roofing material with a distinctive appearance.

Flat Roofing Systems

Some homes include flat or low-slope roof areas that require a different approach than traditional pitched roofs. Our flat roofing systems are designed to address the drainage and waterproofing needs of these roof sections.

Roofing Systems Designed for Northern Utah Homes

A roof in Utah County has to perform through hot summers, cold winters, snow, wind, and changing temperatures. Those conditions make the quality of the complete roofing system just as important as the material visible from the street.

Aesthetic Roofing considers the components working underneath and around your chosen roofing material. Underlayment, flashing, ice and water protection, drainage, and ventilation all contribute to how well a finished roof performs.

Ventilation deserves particular attention. Our roofing contractors consider both intake and exhaust to help create balanced attic airflow rather than focusing on only one part of the system. These details can make a meaningful difference in the performance and longevity of your roof.

Frequently Asked Questions About Roofing in Utah County

How Do I Know if My Utah County Home Needs a New Roof?

Widespread shingle damage, recurring leaks, significant wear, and an aging roofing system can all indicate that replacement should be considered. A professional inspection can help determine whether repairs are still practical or a new roof makes more sense.

Does Utah County Weather Affect the Type of Roof I Should Choose?

Yes. Snow, freezing temperatures, summer heat, wind, and storms are all worth considering when selecting a roofing system. Our team can help you compare materials based on your home’s design, your budget, and your long-term priorities.

Do You Provide Storm Damage Roof Repair in Utah County?

Yes. Aesthetic Roofing repairs roofing damage caused by hail, wind, falling debris, and other severe weather throughout Utah County. We’ll assess the damage and recommend repair or replacement based on the condition of the roof.

What Roofing Materials Do You Install on Utah County Homes?

We install asphalt shingles, designer asphalt shingles, standing seam metal roofing, and flat roofing systems. The best option depends on factors such as your roof design, preferred appearance, budget, and long-term goals.

What Areas of Utah County Does Aesthetic Roofing Serve?

Aesthetic Roofing serves homeowners throughout Utah County, including Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Spanish Fork, Springville, and surrounding communities.
