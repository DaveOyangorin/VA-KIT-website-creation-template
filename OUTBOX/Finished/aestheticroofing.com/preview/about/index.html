About Aesthetic Roofing

Learn about Aesthetic Roofing, a 4.8★ rated roofing company in South Jordan, UT with 350+ reviews, 495+ completed projects, and GAF Master Elite Contractor status.

(801) 810-2276Book Online

Aesthetic Roofing is a local Northern Utah roofing company built on honest recommendations, clear communication, and doing the work the right way.

We want homeowners to understand what their roof actually needs, feel comfortable asking questions, and know they are working with a real local team that will still be here after the project is finished.

Roofing Should Start With an Honest Conversation

We believe homeowners deserve straightforward answers.

If your roof can be repaired, we’ll tell you. If replacement makes more sense, we’ll explain why. Our goal is to help you make the right decision for your home without unnecessary pressure.

A Local Team That Takes Ownership

Aesthetic Roofing is made up of people who live and work in the communities we serve.

Our team stays involved throughout the project, from scheduling and product selection to installation and the final walkthrough. We keep communication clear and work to make the process feel organized from beginning to end.

We Look at the Entire Roofing System

A roof is more than the material you see from the street.

We pay attention to the full system, including underlayment, flashing, ventilation, ice and water protection, and other components that help the roof perform properly. Those details matter just as much as the finished appearance.

Clear Communication at Every Step

You shouldn’t have to wonder who to call or what happens next during a roofing project. Aesthetic Roofing assigns a dedicated representative at each stage, so there is always someone responsible for keeping things moving.

Our customer service team helps get you scheduled, your sales consultant walks you through your options, the install coordinator manages materials and crews, and your project manager is on-site to make sure the work goes as promised.

That structure helps keep communication clear from the first conversation through the final walkthrough, with fewer handoff gaps and fewer surprises along the way.

Quality Work Backed by Professional Standards

Aesthetic Roofing is a GAF Master Elite® contractor, and we pair professional roofing standards with a strong focus on workmanship and customer care.

That means using quality materials, following proper installation practices, and treating every home with the attention it deserves.

A Faster, More Organized Roofing Project

We know homeowners don’t want to live in a construction zone for weeks at a time. Aesthetic Roofing guarantees a project start within two weeks of commitment, and about 80% of our roofs are completed in a single day.

That means less disruption, a more efficient installation, and a quicker return to normal.

We’re Here for the Long Term

We are not a storm-chasing company that comes into town and disappears after the work is done.

Aesthetic Roofing has a real local presence in Northern Utah, and we want homeowners to know they can reach us again when they need help, have questions, or are ready for another project.

What You Can Expect From Aesthetic Roofing

When you work with our team, you can expect:

  • Honest recommendations: We explain what we find and what your options are.
  • Clear communication: We keep you informed throughout the project.
  • Attention to detail: We look beyond the surface and consider the entire roofing system.
  • Respect for your property: We work to keep the job organized and clean.
  • A real local team: We are here to serve Northern Utah for the long term.

Work With a Roofing Company That Cares

Your roof is a major part of your home, and you should feel confident in the people working on it.

Aesthetic Roofing combines professional roofing expertise with a local team that cares about doing the work correctly and treating homeowners well along the way.
