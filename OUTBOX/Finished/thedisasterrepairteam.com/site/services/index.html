Cleanup / Restoration

When a leak, flood, burst pipe, or storm loss hits, the next step is fast cleanup and drying before the damage spreads. That is what this service is built to handle.

Disaster Repair Team assesses the damage, finds hidden moisture, extracts standing water, dries affected materials, and coordinates the repair path so the project moves forward without extra handoffs.

Local water-damage pages: Salt Lake City, Ogden, West Valley City, Provo, West Jordan, Orem, Sandy, Layton, South Jordan, and St. George.

Contaminated Water / Mitigation

Sewage backups and contaminated-water events need stricter handling than a clean-water leak. The goal is to control exposure, remove impacted materials, document the loss, and coordinate repairs so the space can be made safe again.

If you are dealing with a toilet overflow, drain backup, or “black water” event, the next step is usually a direct call so the team can help stabilize the situation.

Cleanup / Restoration

Fire damage recovery requires more than demolition and cleanup. Property owners need a team that can document the loss, handle smoke and water-related complications, and move the project toward a full restoration plan.

Disaster Repair Team works with customers and insurance carriers to coordinate cleanup, repair, and reconstruction after a fire, keeping communication clear as the project moves forward.

Local fire pages: Salt Lake City, Ogden, West Valley City, Provo, West Jordan, Orem, Sandy, Layton, South Jordan, and St. George.

Cleanup / Restoration

Smoke and soot can spread well beyond the original fire area. Cleanup requires the right tools, odor-control process, and restoration approach to make damaged spaces safe and usable again.

The team uses specialized equipment and cleaning methods to remove smoke residue, manage odor, and protect surfaces while the property moves through the larger restoration process.

Local smoke pages: Salt Lake City, Ogden, West Valley City, Provo, West Jordan, Orem, Sandy, Layton, South Jordan, and St. George.

Remediation / Restoration

Mold can develop quickly after unresolved moisture problems, creating health concerns and structural issues in homes and commercial properties. Proper remediation starts with identifying the moisture source and containing the spread.

Disaster Repair Team uses containment procedures, filtration, antimicrobial treatments, and restoration planning to remediate mold while helping property owners understand the cause and next steps.

Local mold pages: Salt Lake City, Ogden, West Valley City, Provo, West Jordan, Orem, Sandy, Layton, South Jordan, and St. George.

Repair, Specialty, and Commercial Services

Foundation, insulation, and commercial work for larger projects and repair coordination.

Cracks / Leaks / Water Intrusion

Foundation problems do not all call for the same repair path. This service is focused on the issues property owners most often need help with: foundation wall cracks, leak points, seepage, and water intrusion that can affect basements, finishes, and nearby materials.

Disaster Repair Team can help with repair-focused solutions such as crack sealing, epoxy-related crack repair, leak repair, and waterproofing-related corrective work where appropriate. When a condition appears to need a specialty structural evaluation, the next step is honest guidance instead of an overbroad repair promise.

Related foundation issues: foundation crack repair, foundation leak repair, basement wall crack repair, seepage repair, water intrusion repair, and foundation wall leak repair.

Removal / Replacement / Reinstall

Insulation is handled in-house for attic insulation removal, attic insulation replacement, blown-in insulation, wall insulation replacement, spray insulation, and reinstall work after remediation or reconstruction.

This matters most after mold, fire, smoke, or water damage because the same team can remove damaged insulation, bag it out properly, coordinate cleanup and rebuild work, and reinstall new material without waiting on an outside trade handoff. That improves speed, coordination, and quality control across the project.

Related insulation services: attic insulation removal, blown-in insulation, wall insulation replacement, insulation removal after mold, insulation replacement after water damage, and insulation reinstallation after remediation.

Commercial Restoration

Emergency Response / Large Loss / Reconstruction

Commercial losses require more than basic cleanup. Property managers, facility teams, landlords, and business owners need a contractor that can respond quickly, document clearly, work in occupied spaces, and coordinate the project with less operational disruption.

The commercial service line is positioned around 24/7 emergency response, water, fire, smoke, mold, drying, reconstruction, leak-related repair, insulation support, and organized communication for Northern Utah commercial properties. W9 and COI readiness, larger-loss capability, and preparedness offers make it easier to build long-term commercial relationships instead of waiting until the next emergency.

Related commercial services: commercial restoration, commercial water damage restoration, multifamily restoration, occupied-space drying, large-loss response, and commercial reconstruction.
