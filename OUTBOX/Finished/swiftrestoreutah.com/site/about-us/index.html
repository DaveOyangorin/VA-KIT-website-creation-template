Swift Restoration and Remodeling, we're dedicated to providing quality restoration and construction services to the people of Ogden. We're a locally owned and operated business, and we've been serving the community since 2009. We have over 13 years of experience in restoration, and over 30 years of experience in construction. We're always available to help locals with any restoration needs they may have, and we're ready for anything. Our experienced team can do it all, including restoring your home or business back to its original condition after a disaster, or remodeling it to match your exact specifications. So if you're in need of a restoration or construction company in Ogden, don't hesitate to call us. We'll be happy to help. Our experienced team can do it all, including:

Our team is dedicated to restoring your home back to normal as quickly as possible. We want to make sure that the job is done correctly, and we're committed to providing you with a 3 – 5 year satisfaction guarantee for our restoration services. We want you to be happy with the work that we do!


Our team understands how difficult and frustrating dealing with an insurance company can be. We want to make it as easy as possible for you. We will work directly with your insurance companies, including direct billing, so you will not have to worry about payments. This helps to alleviate the stress and anxiety that often accompany property cleanup and remediation projects.
