    Disinfecting Services FAQs

    Disinfection is the process of decontaminating and disinfecting a property that has been contaminated with hazardous materials. This can include blood, bodily fluids, or other potentially infectious materials.

    Common questions

    01Why are disinfection services important?

    Disinfection is important because it helps to prevent the spread of disease and illness. Contaminated materials can harbor harmful bacteria or viruses that can cause serious illness or even death.

    02How are disinfection services performed?

    Disinfection services are typically performed by trained professionals using specialized equipment and products. The process usually involves a thorough cleaning of the affected area, followed by the application of a powerful disinfectant.

    03What are some common misconceptions about disinfection services?

    One common misconception about disinfection services is that it is the same as cleaning. However, disinfection is a much more detailed and thorough process that is designed to eliminate harmful bacteria and viruses.
