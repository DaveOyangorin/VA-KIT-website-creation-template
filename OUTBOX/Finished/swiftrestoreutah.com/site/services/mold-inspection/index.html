Mold Inspection Services FAQs

Mold inspection is the process of identifying and locating mold in a building. It involves both visual assessments and specialized testing to detect mold growth.

Common questions

01Why is mold inspection important?

Mold inspection is crucial because mold can cause health issues like allergies, respiratory problems, and other serious conditions. It can also cause significant structural damage to your property.

02How often should mold inspection be performed?

Mold inspection should be performed at least once a year, especially if you live in a humid environment or have experienced water damage in the past.

03What are the signs of mold growth?

Common signs of mold growth include a musty smells, visible discoloration on walls or ceilings, water stains, and increased allergy symptoms in occupants.
