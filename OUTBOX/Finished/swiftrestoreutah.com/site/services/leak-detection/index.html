Slab Leak Detection & Repair

Slab leaks occurring beneath your foundation can be especially dangerous as they’re not noticed until water damage has already devastated your home.

These leaks form when aging pipes crack beneath the slab or when shifts in the foundation stress the plumbing system. You may notice warm spots on floors, high water bills, or even cracked tiles.
