Mold Awareness: Tips for Prevention and Safety

As part of our commitment to community health, we offer valuable mold awareness tips for Ogden residents. From identifying common signs of mold growth to applying preventive measures, we empower homeowners to take control of their indoor environment and protect their loved ones from potential health risks associated with mold. Here are some quick tips for preventing mold issues you can use:

Maintain Proper Ventilation

Use exhaust fans in bathrooms and kitchens, and open windows when possible to ensure good airflow throughout your home.

Monitor Humidity

Control indoor humidity levels by keeping them between 30-50% and use a dehumidifier in moist areas like basements and crawl spaces.

Tackle Water Damage Quickly

Fix leaks and dry out wet materials within 24-48 hours.

Use Mold-Resistant Products

During renovations, choose mold-resistant paints and drywall to reduce the chances of mold growth.

Inspect Regularly

Make sure to inspect your home routinely, paying attention to areas prone to moisture, such as bathrooms, kitchens, and basements.
