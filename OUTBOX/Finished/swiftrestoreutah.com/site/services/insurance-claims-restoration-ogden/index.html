Common questions

01What is the process for filing a claim?

The first step is to contact your insurance agent or company to report the loss. They will then send someone out to assess the damage and give you an estimate of the repairs that need to be made. Once you've submitted your claim, the insurance company will investigate it and determine if you're entitled to coverage. If so, they will send you a check for the amount of the covered damages.

02What if my claim is denied?

If your claim is denied, you have the right to appeal the decision. The insurance company will review your case and may reverse the decision if they find that they made a mistake. If your appeal is unsuccessful, you can file a complaint with the state insurance department.

03What is replacement cost coverage?

Replacement cost coverage is a type of insurance that pays to repair or replace your belongings if they're damaged or destroyed. This coverage typically covers the cost of repairing or replacing your belongings without taking depreciation into account.
