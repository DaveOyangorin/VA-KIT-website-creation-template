An Attic Conversion

Case study

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

At Swift Restoration and Remodeling, we take pride in turning our clients' visions into reality. Recently, a long-time client contacted us with a request for a major remodel. They wanted us to add an entirely new level above their garage and complete an attic conversion that would transform the unused space into a beautiful living area. But we didn’t stop there - this project also involved improving the exterior of their entire home.

A Call for a Complete Attic Conversion

The attic above the garage wasn’t just being converted into a livable space; the project required building a whole new level. Our team was tasked with turning this unused area into a cozy living room, bedroom, and bathroom while ensuring the exterior transformation of the house matched the updated space. Given that we had previously completed several successful projects for this client, we were eager to exceed their expectations once again.

Steps We Took

We followed a detailed, step-by-step process to complete this ambitious project:

Before breaking ground, we worked closely with the client to finalize the design and secure all necessary permits.

  • Framing the New Level:

    With permits in place, we began framing the new level above the garage. This included setting up the structure for all the rooms, as well as providing proper support for the rest of the house.

  • Exterior Remodeling:

    While we were working on the new level, we also focused on updating the home’s exterior. The client wanted a modern look, so we replaced the traditional brick siding with a fresh design. On the upper level, we installed board-and-batten siding, while the lower level received a clean, modern white-painted brick finish. We also upgraded the garage doors to match the contemporary feel of the new exterior.

  • Insulation and Drywall:

    After framing, we installed insulation to keep the new space energy-efficient and comfortable. Once insulated, we applied drywall to the interior for a clean, professional finish.

  • Electrical and Plumbing Work:

    The new level required complete electrical wiring and plumbing. Our team installed everything from light fixtures to plumbing for the bathroom.

  • Flooring and Paint:

    For the living areas, we installed durable wood laminate flooring, which provides both style and ease of maintenance. The interior walls were painted with neutral colors to create a bright, open feel throughout the new space.

  • Bathroom Finishes:

    In the bathroom, we used high-quality tiles, installed a modern vanity, and selected fixtures that balanced both style and practicality.

    Materials and Equipment

    Here's a closer look at what we used to bring this attic transformation to life.

    Materials Used

    We used a variety of materials, including high-grade wood for framing the new level, durable drywall, wood laminate for flooring, and stylish tiles for the bathroom. These materials were chosen for their long-lasting quality and cost-effectiveness.

    Equipment We Used

    Our team relied on various equipment to complete the remodel. We utilized framing tools and power saws to construct the new level, a moisture meter to ensure proper insulation and electrical tools for wiring the new lighting and outlets. For the exterior work, we used specialized siding installation equipment.

    The Final Result

    The end result was nothing short of remarkable. What was once an attic turned into a completely new level, featuring a spacious living room, bedroom, and modern bathroom. The home’s exterior was also transformed, giving it a fresh, updated look. The client was extremely happy with the outcome, praising our team for turning their vision into reality.
