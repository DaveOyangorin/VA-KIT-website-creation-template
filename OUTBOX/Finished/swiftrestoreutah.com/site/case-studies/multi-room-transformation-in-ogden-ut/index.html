Multi-Room Transformation

Case study

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

At Swift Restoration and Remodeling, we recently completed a home renovation project in Ogden, UT that showcases our expertise in handling multi-room transformation. The project included modernizing a family room, upgrading a laundry area, and converting an unfinished basement space into a stunning new bathroom.

The Challenge: Multi-Room Transformation

Our journey began when Ogden homeowners approached us with their vision of updating multiple areas in their home. The family and laundry rooms featured outdated 1990s tile flooring that needed replacement, and the laundry room lacked proper storage and functionality. The most challenging aspect was a basement closet that the homeowners wanted to convert into a full bathroom - a space that had only basic drain pipes and required extensive modifications.

How We Approached the Situation

We started with thorough planning and site evaluation. Our team identified that the basement bathroom conversion would require relocation of the foundation drain and complete electrical wiring installation. This careful initial assessment allowed us to create a detailed renovation strategy that would minimize disruptions and ensure efficient project flow.

Project Execution

Each room presented unique challenges that required specialized attention and expertise. Here's how we transformed each space:

The transformation began with the complete removal of outdated 1990s tile flooring. Following this, we thoroughly prepared and leveled the subfloor to ensure a perfect foundation for installation. We then installed premium luxury vinyl planking throughout the space. The project was completed with new baseboards and careful attention to creating seamless transitions between all connecting rooms.

  • Laundry Room Upgrade:

    To maintain visual flow throughout the home, we installed flooring that matched the family room perfectly. The space was enhanced with custom storage cabinets and a new basin sink featuring modern fixtures. We modernized the room's functionality by updating the plumbing system and installing enhanced lighting for improved visibility.

  • Basement Bathroom Creation:

    This project involved installing a complete electrical system and relocating the foundation drain. We built out the entire plumbing infrastructure and implemented a proper ventilation system. The space was finished with moisture-resistant flooring and modern fixtures throughout. Final touches included refined finishes that elevated the overall design.

    • <

    Project Management

    Throughout the project, our project manager maintained constant communication with the homeowners. We provided regular progress updates and consulted with them at every major decision point to ensure the final result would match their vision. Our experienced crew took pride in checking every detail with the homeowners, making sure each element was installed exactly as envisioned.

    Results

    The end result transformed three distinct spaces into beautiful, functional areas that exceeded our clients' expectations. The family room now features modern, durable flooring that enhances the entire space. The laundry room has been converted from a basic utility space into an efficient, well-organized room with ample storage. Perhaps most impressively, an unutilized basement closet has become a fully functional, beautifully appointed bathroom.
