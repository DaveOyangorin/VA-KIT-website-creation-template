When a Farmington homeowner reached out to our team about a roof that had seen better days and a garage that was basically falling apart, we knew this was going to be a fun one. At Swift Restoration and Remodeling, our general contracting services cover everything from storm damage rebuilds to ground-up construction projects, and this job checked both boxes. The homeowner wanted a complete roof replacement and a brand new garage, and they wanted it done right.

This particular house had solid bones, but the roof was worn out and the existing garage had reached the end of its useful life. Time for a fresh start.

What Brought This Farmington Homeowner to Us

The homeowner had noticed some warning signs over the past couple of years. Missing shingles after winter storms. A few small leaks that kept getting patched but never fully resolved. And the garage? It had structural issues that made it more of a liability than an asset.

Here's the thing about roofs in Northern Utah. Between heavy snowfall, freeze-thaw cycles, and the occasional wind event, roofing materials take a beating. The Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS)

recommends regular roof inspections specifically because weather-related wear can shorten a roof's lifespan significantly. This homeowner had been pushing it, and they knew it was time.

They came to us because they wanted one contractor who could handle both projects. Not one company for the roof and another for the garage. Just one team they could trust to manage the whole thing from start to finish. That's exactly how we like to work.

Tackling the Full Roof Replacement

We started with the roof. Our crew stripped everything down to the decking so we could inspect the underlayment and framing for any hidden damage. Good thing we did. There were a few soft spots in the decking that needed to be replaced before we could move forward. That's something you just can't see from the ground, and it's a big reason why a proper tear-off matters.

Once the decking was solid, we installed new ice and water shield along the eaves, fresh synthetic underlayment across the entire roof, and high-quality architectural shingles rated for Northern Utah's climate. With over 30 years of combined construction experience on our team, our crew knows how to build a roof that handles heavy snow loads and wind-driven rain. We also replaced all the flashing around the chimney and vents, because cutting corners on flashing is how leaks happen five years down the road.

Key Steps in the Roof Installation

  • Full tear-off and decking inspection to catch hidden rot or soft spots
  • Replacement of damaged decking sections before new materials went on
  • Ice and water shield along eaves and valleys for extra leak protection
  • Architectural shingles selected for durability in Utah's climate
  • New flashing around all penetration points, including chimney and vents

Building a Brand New Garage from the Ground Up

With the roof wrapped up, we turned our attention to the garage. The old structure came down, and we started fresh with a new foundation, framing, and everything in between. This wasn't just a patch job. The homeowner wanted a garage that matched the style of the house and would actually last.

Our reconstruction team handled the full build, including foundation work, framing, roofing to match the new shingles on the house, siding, and a new garage door. We made sure everything was up to code and that the finished product looked like it had always been part of the home. That continuity matters. Nobody wants a garage that looks like it was bolted on as an afterthought.

One detail we always pay attention to is drainage. A new garage sitting next to the house needs proper grading and gutter routing so water flows away from the foundation. We've seen too many homes where poor drainage around an addition leads to water damage down the road. A little planning upfront saves a lot of headaches later.
