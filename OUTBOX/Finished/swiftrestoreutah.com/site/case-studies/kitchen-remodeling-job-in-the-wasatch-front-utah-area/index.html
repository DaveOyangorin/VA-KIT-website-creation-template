The Wasatch Front Home Remodeling Project: The Details

Swift Restoration and Remodeling has done our fair share of remodeling Wasatch Front homes. This area is full of split-level homes that all share very similar designs and floor plans. As a result, many homeowners find themselves wanting to make a change or two.


In this case, the task was more than a small change of cabinets or a fresh coat of paint. When we were first approached about the home remodeling project, we were faced with a challenge.


Our client’s goal was to completely transform the kitchen, dining room, and living room areas with an open floor plan. The walls were starting to make the spaces feel small and closed off from the rest of the house. Ultimately, the homeowner wanted to integrate the key living spaces to create a house that felt more like a home.


In order to help the homeowner achieve that feeling, Swift Restoration and Remodeling presented a few ideas for:

  • Demolishing the Walls
  • Building a Kitchen Island
  • Changing the Layout
  • Replacing Cabinets and Countertops
  • Installing New Flooring

How Swift Restoration and Remodeling Helped

As a full-service home remodeling company, Swift Restoration and Remodeling was able to handle every part of this project from start to finish. After meeting with the homeowner and deciding on the direction to take the project, we were able to follow through and provide expert service in every area.

Creating a New Layout

Knowing that the client’s goal was to improve the flow between the kitchen, dining room, and living room, Swift Restoration and Remodeling set out to create a unique layout design. It completely opened up the floor plan to make the rooms all feel like one space, and it helped to make the home feel larger.


Creating an open floor plan in a house isn’t just about changing the layout and adding upgrades to the kitchen. It requires a lot of extra attention to electrical work, wall supports, and more. Swift Restoration and Remodeling ensured that all of the demolition steps were done with care to protect the home from damage. The contractors worked with the homeowner to agree on which walls would be removed and how the change in layout would be executed.

Upgrading the Kitchen

While the project centered around the floor plan, it also required extensive kitchen remodeling so that the kitchen would complement the living room and dining room areas. To help the client achieve their dream kitchen, Swift Restoration and Remodeling went beyond the usual. We installed granite countertops and topped off the look with new, gorgeous oak cabinets in a unique color.


We also added a stunning kitchen island that serves both an aesthetic and functional role. To help make the open floor plan feel more thought-out, our contractors also installed new luxury vinyl flooring that tied the separate living spaces together.

Installing Lighting

Finally, we had our preferred electrician come to the home to finish off the electrical work. Not only did they rewire everything that had previously run through the old walls, but they also installed new ceiling lights to make a brighter and more open space.

Results of the Wasatch Front Area Home Remodeling Project

At the end of the home remodeling project, the homeowner was completely satisfied with not just the results but also the service. He was especially appreciative of the crew's timeliness and respect for his property. Swift Restoration and Remodeling always tries to go above and beyond when doing kitchen remodeling in Wasatch Front Utah area homes. We treat every home as if it were our own.
