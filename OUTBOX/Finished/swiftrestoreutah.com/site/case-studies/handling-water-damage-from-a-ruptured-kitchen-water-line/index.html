A Ruptured Kitchen Water Line

Case study

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

At Swift Restoration and Remodeling, we recently completed a project for a residential homeowner and tackled water damage restoration in Farmington, UT. The cause of the damage was a ruptured kitchen water line that led to significant water intrusion throughout the home.

The Cause of The Issue

The kitchen water line ruptured unexpectedly, causing water to flood not only the kitchen but also the hallway, bathroom, and even down into the basement. The water saturated the kitchen's walnut floors, hallway tile flooring, bathroom tile, and various structural materials. The basement was also affected, with water soaking into the drywall and carpet.

Our Approach to Handling The Water Damage After a Ruptured Kitchen Water Line

When a kitchen water line ruptured in a residential home in Farmington, the damage was extensive and required immediate attention. Here are the steps we took to handle the water damage in this particular case:

Initial Assessment

When we arrived, we did a thorough inspection of the affected areas to understand the extent of the water damage. We used moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to detect hidden moisture in the walls, floors, and structural materials, making sure we had a complete picture of the situation.

Containment and Water Extraction

Before beginning water extraction, we established containment to prevent the spread of moisture to unaffected areas of the home. The first important step was to remove the standing water. We used high-powered pumps to extract the water from the affected areas quickly, focusing first on the kitchen and basement, where the most water had accumulated.

Drying Process

Once the bulk of the water was removed, we moved on to the drying process, which we handled in several crucial steps:

We began by setting up specialty drying equipment throughout the affected areas to ensure thorough drying.

  • Kitchen Floors:

    In the kitchen, we used hardwood floor drying mats specifically designed to draw moisture out of the walnut floors. This step was critical in saving the flooring from further damage.

  • Hallway and Bathroom:

    Next, we strategically placed dehumidifiers and air movers in the hallway and bathroom. This equipment worked together to dry the tile flooring, removing any trapped moisture that could lead to future issues.

  • Basement:

    In the basement, we used a combination of dehumidifiers and air movers to dry out the carpet and drywall. This step was necessary in preventing mold growth and maintaining the integrity of the structural materials.

    • <

    Material Removal

    Unfortunately, not all materials could be saved. The carpet in the basement was beyond repair due to prolonged exposure to water, so it was removed and properly disposed of. We also removed any compromised drywall to prevent mold growth and ensure the structural integrity of the home.

    Final Steps

    After the drying process was complete, we performed a final inspection to confirm that all moisture had been removed and that the home was safe from potential mold growth.

    A Successful Project and a Satisfied Client

    Thanks to our thorough process, we saved key features like the kitchen's walnut floors and prevented future issues like mold growth. The homeowner was delighted with the results. Their home was restored to its pre-damage condition, and they were impressed by our efficiency and professionalism.
