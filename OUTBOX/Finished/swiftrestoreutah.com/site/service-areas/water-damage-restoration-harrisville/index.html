Every moisture reading, photo, and scope note is recorded to support your claim. Our technicians follow EPA mold and moisture guidelines alongside industry-standard protocols. For more on what triggers these events, see our guide on common causes of water damage

Basement Water Damage

Groundwater pressure and surface runoff force moisture through Harrisville basement walls and floor joints during spring thaw. We pump out the water, tear back damaged material, and bring the space to a dry, stable condition.

Wall Water Damage

Slow leaks inside walls go undetected for weeks until discoloration finally surfaces. We pinpoint hidden moisture with infrared cameras and moisture probes, then cut back only the sections too far gone to dry.

Plumbing Failures

Burst pipes, failed fittings, and corroded supply lines trigger sudden flooding. We stop the water at its source, remove what has pooled, and put the affected spaces back together.

Kitchen Water Damage

Dishwasher hoses, refrigerator water lines, and under-sink connections fail without warning. Once water seeps beneath cabinets and into the subfloor, damage escalates fast. We dry what survives and rebuild anything too damaged to keep.

Floor Water Damage

Water trapped beneath hardwood, laminate, or carpet degrades the subfloor and encourages mold.

Ceiling Water Damage

Upstairs plumbing failures and roof leaks send water into the space above your ceiling, soaking insulation and weakening drywall. We create access to the cavity, pull out the moisture, and patch the finished surface.

Bathroom Water Damage

Cracked shower pans, leaking tub drains, and failed toilet connections cause damage behind tile and inside wall cavities. We address the source, eliminate moisture from the structure, and rebuild the finished surfaces.

Appliance Leaks

Water heaters, washing machines, and condensate overflow pans can dump significant water in a short time. We contain the damage quickly and bring every affected area back to pre-loss condition.
