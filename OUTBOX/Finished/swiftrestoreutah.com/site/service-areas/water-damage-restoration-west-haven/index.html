Basement Water Damage

Flat terrain and elevated groundwater force moisture up through West Haven basement floors and foundation joints. We pump out accumulated water, peel back finished materials to expose the wet structure, and run commercial dryers until readings confirm the space is safe to close up.

Wall Water Damage

Hidden leaks behind drywall soak insulation and framing for weeks before telltale stains bleed through. Our moisture detection gear identifies the full scope, and we remove only the compromised sections rather than gutting the entire wall.

Plumbing Failures

Supply line breaks, corroded fittings, and failed water heater connections rank among the most frequent causes of indoor flooding in West Haven. We manage water removal, coordinate plumbing repair, and handle the full restoration.

Kitchen Water Damage

Refrigerator supply lines, dishwasher hose clamps, and garbage disposal connections loosen over time and leak beneath cabinetry. By the time the subfloor softens, the clock is already running. We dry aggressively and reconstruct what the water ruined.

Floor Water Damage

Carpet, hardwood, and laminate all hold moisture against the subfloor differently, and each demands a specific drying strategy. We match equipment and approach to the flooring type so you keep as much original material as possible.

Ceiling Water Damage

Second-floor plumbing failures and roof breaches drive water into the space between joists, saturating insulation and weakening drywall from above. We cut access where needed, pull moisture out of the cavity, and restore the surface.

Bathroom Water Damage

Shower pan failures, slow tub overflows, and cracked toilet supply valves force water into the wall cavity behind tile and beneath vanity cabinets. We chase the source, dry the hidden structure, and rebuild properly.

Appliance Leaks

A failed water heater or washing machine hose can flood a room in minutes. Quick containment keeps the damage from spreading into adjacent rooms, and we restore every affected area.
