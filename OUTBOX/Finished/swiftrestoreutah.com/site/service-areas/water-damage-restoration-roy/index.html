Water is deceptive. By the time you see pooling on your floor, moisture has likely wicked two feet up your drywall. That hidden migration is where mold starts. When you need water damage restoration in Roy, the water your eyes miss is almost always doing more damage than the puddle you can see.

Basement Water Damage

Foundation cracks and poor exterior drainage push water into Roy basements during spring runoff. We extract standing water, strip compromised materials, and bring the space down to safe moisture levels.

Wall Water Damage

Moisture wicks upward inside drywall long before stains appear on the surface. We use thermal imaging and pin-type meters to map hidden saturation and remove only what cannot be saved.

Plumbing Failures

Aging supply lines, corroded drain pipes, and failed shut-off valves account for most indoor water damage in Roy. We coordinate source repair and restore affected areas so the same problem does not repeat.

Kitchen Water Damage

Dishwasher connections, refrigerator supply lines, and under-sink plumbing fail quietly. By the time water reaches the subfloor, cabinetry is often compromised. We salvage what we can and rebuild what we cannot.

Floor Water Damage

Saturated hardwood, laminate, and carpet padding trap moisture against the subfloor. Rapid extraction paired with targeted dehumidification can preserve flooring that would otherwise need full replacement.

Ceiling Water Damage

Roof leaks, burst second-story pipes, and overflowing fixtures send water through ceiling cavities. We cut targeted access points, address trapped moisture in the insulation and framing, and patch everything back to its original condition.

Bathroom Water Damage

Tub surrounds, shower pans, and toilet supply lines are common failure points. Moisture trapped behind tile and inside vanity cavities leads to hidden rot if not addressed quickly.

Appliance Leaks

Washing machines, water heaters, and condensate drain pans can release large volumes with little warning. We respond fast to contain the spread and restore every room the water touched.
