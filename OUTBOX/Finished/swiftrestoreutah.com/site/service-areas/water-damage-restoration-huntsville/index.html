One company handles everything, and our guide on signs of hidden water damage behind walls covers what to watch for between visits.

Basement Water Damage

Snowmelt pressure and elevated water tables force moisture through foundation walls and floor slabs. We remove accumulated water, gut whatever the moisture has ruined, and dry every surface before rebuilding.

Wall Water Damage

Moisture migrates upward inside wall cavities before any visible mark appears on the paint. We scan walls with thermal imaging to map hidden saturation, then strip back only the material that testing confirms is beyond saving.

Plumbing Failures

Frozen pipes, failed connections, and worn supply lines cause sudden water events, especially during January and February cold snaps. We manage the full cleanup and coordinate plumbing repairs.

Kitchen Water Damage

Under-sink leaks, dishwasher failures, and refrigerator line breaks push water beneath cabinetry and into subfloor layers. Left unchecked for even a day, the damage doubles. We intervene early and rebuild what the water claimed.

Floor Water Damage

Hardwood and engineered flooring absorb moisture faster at altitude where humidity swings are sharper. Pulling water out within hours and running precision drying gives floors their best shot at recovery.

Ceiling Water Damage

Ice dams and heavy snow loads let water seep into the space above your ceiling. We access the cavity, pull out saturated insulation, dehumidify the structural members, and restore everything to a finished state.

Bathroom Water Damage

Failed shower pans, leaking drains, and toilet overflows cause hidden rot behind tile and inside enclosed vanity walls. We trace the failure back to the source, dry out all concealed framing, and put the room back together.

Appliance Leaks

Boiler systems, hot water tanks, and laundry hookups can dump substantial water when a line or valve fails. Speed matters, and we mobilize quickly to limit the footprint of the damage.
