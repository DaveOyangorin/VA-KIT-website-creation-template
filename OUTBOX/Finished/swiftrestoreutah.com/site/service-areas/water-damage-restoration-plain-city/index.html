Basement Water Damage

Seasonal irrigation and a high water table drive moisture up through basement slabs and foundation walls across Plain City. We clear out the water, tear back wet finishes, and run drying equipment until every reading hits baseline.

Wall Water Damage

Slow plumbing leaks inside wall cavities degrade insulation and studs long before any stain appears. Our detection tools locate the wet zone, and we cut back only what cannot be recovered.

Plumbing Failures

Corroded galvanized lines, worn fittings, and aging water heater valves are frequent culprits behind indoor flooding in Plain City's older homes. We handle full water removal and rebuilds end to end.

Kitchen Water Damage

Supply lines behind refrigerators, worn dishwasher fittings, and aging disposal seals are common failure points. We trace the leak, dry affected cabinetry and subfloor, and rebuild where damage has gone too far.

Floor Water Damage

Water beneath carpet, hardwood, or laminate weakens the subfloor and sets the stage for mold.

Ceiling Water Damage

Roof leaks, ice dam melt, and second-floor pipe failures send water into the joist bays above your ceiling. We open the cavity where needed, clear soaked insulation, dry the framing, and close everything up cleanly.

Bathroom Water Damage

Shower pan cracks, drain failures, and toilet overflows drive water into sealed wall cavities behind tile and under vanity counters. Rot develops in those enclosed spaces quickly. We find the origin, dry the hidden framing, and rebuild the room.

Appliance Leaks

A ruptured washing machine hose or corroded water heater tank can fill a room before you notice. We get on site fast, limit the spread, and restore every damaged area.
