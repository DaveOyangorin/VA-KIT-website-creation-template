A Comprehensive Guide to Recognize Hidden Dangers of Water Damage

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

Water damage and flooding is a problem in many places, including Ogden, Utah. It can occur from a major weather event or from overflowing sinks, toilet clogs, burst pipes, and faulty water-related appliances like dishwashers and washing machines. Understanding the process of water damage assessment and addressing the hidden dangers of water damage are critical to keeping homes and buildings safe and healthy.

Most people think the extent of flood recovery is water removal. However, expert water damage restoration in Ogden, Utah, is crucial due to the many problems flood damage can cause. Water damage in homes and buildings can lead to several hidden dangers that are not always immediately apparent. Understanding these risks is important for ensuring the safety and well-being of the occupants.

The Far-Reaching Effects of Flood Damage

Your home doesn't have to be under a foot or more of water to sustain considerable damage or produce problems for you and others. Even minor water damage from a persistent plumbing leak could be problematic.

Here are some of the effects a flood or plumbing issue could produce:

Water and electricity don't mix. If water touches wires, outlets, or switches, it could lead to electric shock or electrocution. In some situations, electrical fires could start.

Depending on where the water comes from, different parts of your home could sustain varying levels of structural damage. For example, wood floors could warp and rot, ceilings can sag, the foundation could develop cracks, and the roof could weaken due to missing or damaged shingles.

Though water damage from a sink or water pipe usually doesn't pose health risks, contaminated water from broken appliances, sewer lines, rivers, and streams is full of microbes like bacteria and viruses.

Regarding water contamination, the microbes from various water sources can seep into carpeting, furniture, clothing, and other items, increasing the risk of exposure to germs. Even stagnant water can become a breeding ground for microbes.

Water damage restoration in Ogden, Utah, is necessary if you don't want to open your home or business to pests. Cockroaches, beetles, and other insects will travel indoors for water. Aside from seeing bugs inside your building, the pests could trigger illnesses and leave messes everywhere.

How To Avoid the Consequences of Flood Damage

Water damage restoration in Ogden, Utah, is always an option when disasters happen, but the risk of needing the service decreases significantly when you do the following:

Your roof could leak long before you notice warning signs like watermarks on the ceiling and ruined attic insulation. Schedule annual roof inspections to repair and prevent leaks.

A dripping faucet may be annoying, but it could indicate a plumbing issue. Routinely check your pipes and fixtures, and call a plumber to fix leaks. The better you maintain your plumbing, the less likely you'll experience major water damage from a leaky or burst pipe.

Insurance is important for protecting you financially from unexpected problems within your property. You could file an insurance claim for reimbursement on your water damage repairs. Without the service, you could be responsible for paying hefty repair and replacement costs.

Still, most policies have specific restrictions regarding floods, plumbing leaks, and other sources of water damage.
