Water Damage from Bathroom Sink Issues

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

What are the signs that my bathroom sink is causing water damage? If you’ve noticed water pooling under the sink or strange smells coming from your vanity, your bathroom sink could be the culprit. At Swift Restoration and Remodeling, we often provide water damage restoration services in homes throughout Ogden, UT that started with a simple bathroom sink issue.

While sinks might seem like small fixtures, they can lead to big problems if leaks or drainage issues go unnoticed. The good news is, many of these issues can be spotted early and with the right response, major damage can be avoided.

Common Bathroom Sink Issues That Can Easily Bring Water into Your Home

There are a few common reasons why a bathroom sink might lead to water damage:

Leaky Faucets

Dripping faucets can cause long-term damage. Even though it can take a minor repair to avoid this mess, the slow, constant drip of water can lead to pooling, encourage mold growth, and even weaken nearby materials like wood or drywall.

Broken or Worn Seal

Just like with kitchen sink leaks, the rubber or silicone seals around your faucet, drain, or supply lines can wear out or shift out of place in your bathroom as well. Once the seal is broken, water can seep underneath the sink and into the cabinet, flooring, or walls.

Clogged or Slow Drains

Hair, soap, and grime can collect in the drain over time, leading to clogs. When drains get backed up, they can cause overflow or water to back up into the sink basin. This water can spill out and begin damaging your flooring, cabinetry, and nearby walls.

Signs Your Bathroom Sink Might Be Causing Water Damage

Here are a few signs that could mean you’ve got a bathroom sink problem that needs attention:

Water draining slower than usual could signal a clog.

This could be a sign of mold or mildew growing due to moisture.

Water stains, bubbling paint, or warped wood under the sink or nearby walls.

Even if you don’t see a puddle, a drip could be coming from a hidden leak.

The key to minimal damage and reduced repair costs? Spotting these signs of water damage early!

Maintenance Tips to Keep Your Sink in Good Shape

Follow these five tips to keep your bathroom sink always running smoothly:

Use a safe drain cleaner or hair catcher to avoid buildup.

Replace worn-out seals or cracked caulking around fixtures.

A small drip can waste water and lead to bigger problems if ignored

Store items in waterproof bins and don’t overcrowd under the sink to keep the air moving under it

These five steps can go a long way in preventing water damage and costly repairs.
