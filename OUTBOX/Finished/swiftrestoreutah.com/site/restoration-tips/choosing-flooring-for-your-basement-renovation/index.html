Choosing the Right Flooring for Your Basement Renovation

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

A finished basement is a great project for homeowners who would like to add more living space to their homes. It can also increase a house’s value if they plan to sell it in the future. While a basement renovation has many benefits, it also requires a lot of work and decision-making.


One of the biggest decisions that a homeowner has to make when choosing to finish their basement is the type of flooring that will be installed. This might seem like a minor decision, but basements are prone to water damage and humidity, so it is important that a homeowner choose wisely. Fortunately, this guide can highlight the important factors to consider when comparing the different flooring options.


Things to Consider When Choosing Flooring for a Basement Renovation

In order to choose from all of the different options, homeowners need to know what factors are most important in their decision. The types of flooring available for basement renovations should offer the following qualities and characteristics:

The durability of the floor depends largely on how the homeowner plans to use it. For example, a home gym will need to have tougher floors than a basement used for a home theater.

  • Comfort:

    The intended use for the basement also impacts the level of comfort a homeowner may prefer.

  • Warmth: Since the foundation of a basement is cold, the flooring installed on top will also be cold, but some materials are warmer than others.

    Different Types of Basement Flooring Options

    Once a homeowner has an idea of the qualities they are looking for in flooring, they will be able to compare the different options. While there are many different types of flooring materials, the following are some of the most popular choices for basement renovations. Here’s how they compare to one another:


    Vinyl Flooring

    Not to mention, it comes in a wide variety of beautiful color and style options. There are even several different plank and style sizes. Therefore, it is a great fit no matter how the homeowner plans to use the new space.


    Some people are hesitant to install this flooring material because they feel it is similar in touch and look to linoleum. However, vinyl flooring manufacturing has come a long way, and there are many products that are elegant and excellent at mimicking the look of luxurious wood.


    Carpet

    For years, carpet has been a popular choice of basement flooring despite the fact that it is not waterproof. Homeowners love that it gives the cold hard floors a cozier feeling, and it is perfect for a kid’s playroom or home theater. That’s not all! The carpet can also make the basement quieter.


    The downside to choosing carpet is that it is porous. Therefore, it is susceptible to stains if something is spilled on it. It can also lead to mold growth if the basement is not waterproofed effectively.


    Hardwood & Laminate

    Some homeowners opt for hardwood or laminated flooring because they prefer the high-end look and feel of the materials. It’s also a great choice if the rest of the home has hardwood floors because it will keep everything matching.


    However, wood is extremely susceptible to water damage. Not only can it mold, but it can also rot, warp, twist, and ruin the finish.


    Tile

    Tile is another option that has been popular among homeowners for many decades. Its water resistance makes it an excellent choice, and the many varieties allow homeowners to choose a look that suits their taste. Unfortunately, it is time-consuming and expensive to install, and it often results in cold floors unless heat is installed.


    Epoxy

    Lastly, an epoxy floor is one of the most durable options for a basement renovation. It is also incredibly long-lasting and water-resistant, so there is no concern for mold growth. It’s not always a popular choice, though, because it is cold and uncomfortable compared to other options.
