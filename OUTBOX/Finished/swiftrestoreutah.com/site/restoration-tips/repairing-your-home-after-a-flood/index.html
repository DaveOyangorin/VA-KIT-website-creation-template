Repairing Your Home After A Flood

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

When a flood happens inside your home, there are many different areas of the home that can be affected.

Water damage from floods can destroy your home’s structure, carpet, flooring, and more. Electrical problems often occur after home flooding and there are many steps that need to be taken to start repairing the damage the flood has caused.

Flood damage restoration is going to start with water removal before the drying can begin. Home repairs from water damage can become extensive. If the carpet has suffered extensive water damage and cannot be successfully dried out, it will be removed and the flooring will be dried before new carpet can be put in. Carpet water damage could be minimal and you may not have to replace your carpet. The restoration process will include home repairs focused on the structure of the property and more.

In case of mold.

If flood waters stand for too long, the damage goes beyond water damage and your home is going to require mold cleanup and remediation. Luckily, Swift Restoration offers water and flood damage restoration, as well as mold remediation services so that we can get the job done quickly.

When it comes to flood damage, the water that has flooded your property may not be clean water. If the water has come from an outside source, there is a good chance that the water is contaminated and can be hazardous to your health. You do not want to risk you or your family’s health by trying to remove and clean the water on your own. Swift Restoration knows how to handle different types of water damage and have the tools needed to clean and disinfect contaminated areas so that your property can be safe and livable.

If you experience a flood inside your home and need assistance, Swift Restoration is available for 24/7 emergency help and is happy to answer any flood damage emergency.
