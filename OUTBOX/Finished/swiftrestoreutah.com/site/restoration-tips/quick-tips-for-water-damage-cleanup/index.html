Quick Tips for Water Damage Cleanup

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

If you have water damage in your Ogden home, whether it is a water stain on your ceiling or walls, or a basement filled with water, there are crucial things you should know before you attempt to cleanup the mess. Taking the wrong steps after water damage can result in even more damage, but the right steps can make cleanup quicker and safer.

Do I Need to Hire A Professional?

If you are asking whether you need to hire a water damage cleanup professional , then the answer is likely yes. While homeowners can often effectively clean and dry small areas of their homes, when larger areas are concerned, or if there is hidden water damage or the potential for mold growth, it is always best to call a

Ogden water damage cleanup company

It doesn’t hurt to get an estimate from a water damage cleanup company when deciding whether you can handle cleanup on your own, or if you should hire a professional. Knowing the extent of the damage can help with your decision.

Protect Furniture and Remove Contents

You should lift furniture off wet floors and remove items from the area than can cause staining. Use aluminum foil under furniture legs, wood blocks, or even Tupperware bowls to get furniture off the floor to prevent moisture wicking. Wet items can be moved from the area to dry or to be restored.

What You Should Know About Water Damage Cleanup

Extract and Dry

The most crucial step for effective water damage cleanup is to extract the water and begin the drying process as soon as possible. If you hire a water damage cleanup professional, they will use specialized equipment to extract water from carpeting, detect hidden moisture in walls, and to dry the property quickly and thoroughly.

This step should happen no more than 24 hours after the water damage occurs to prevent mold growth. Indoor humidity levels should also be monitored and managed to prevent mold.
