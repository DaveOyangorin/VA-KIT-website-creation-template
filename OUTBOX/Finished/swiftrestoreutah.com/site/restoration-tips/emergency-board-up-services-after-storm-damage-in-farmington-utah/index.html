Storm Damage in Farmington: What We're Up Against

Living in northern Utah means dealing with some pretty intense weather patterns. Farmington sits right in the path of seasonal storms that can pack serious punch. We're talking about:

Wind Damage: Those notorious Utah winds can easily hit 60+ mph, turning everyday objects into projectiles that shatter windows and punch holes in siding.

Hail Damage: Golf ball-sized hail isn't uncommon here, and it can absolutely destroy roofing materials and create immediate openings in your home's protective barrier.

Heavy Snow and Ice: Winter storms can cause roof collapse or damage that leaves your home exposed to the elements.

Flash Flooding: Spring runoff and sudden downpours can cause flooding that damages doors, windows, and foundation areas.

The thing about storm damage is that it doesn't stop when the weather clears. A broken window or damaged door is like leaving your front door wide open – to more weather, wildlife, and unfortunately, people with bad intentions.

What Emergency Board Up Services Actually Include

When we talk about emergency board up services, we're not just talking about slapping some plywood over a broken window and calling it good. Professional board up services involve:

Immediate Property Assessment

We quickly evaluate all damaged areas to identify every vulnerability – not just the obvious ones. Sometimes the most dangerous openings are the ones you can't see from the ground.

Professional Grade Materials

We use heavy-duty plywood, proper fasteners, and weather-resistant materials that can withstand whatever Mother Nature throws at them next. No cardboard or tarps that'll blow away with the next breeze.

Strategic Installation

Our team knows how to properly secure materials without causing additional damage to your property. We're not just covering holes – we're creating a temporary but effective barrier.

Documentation for Insurance

We photograph and document all emergency board up work for your insurance claim, making sure you have the evidence you need for coverage.

Professional board up services protect against wind, rain, and security threats while permanent repairs are planned.

Why DIY Board Up Isn't Worth the Risk

Look, we get it. When you're staring at a gaping hole in your wall, the urge to grab some plywood and a hammer is strong. But here's why calling professionals makes more sense:

Safety Concerns: Storm damage often involves working at height, around broken glass, and potentially unstable structures. One wrong move can turn a property damage situation into a personal injury crisis.

Insurance Issues: Improperly installed board up materials might not meet insurance requirements, potentially affecting your claim coverage.

Security Gaps: DIY board up often leaves gaps or weak points that don't actually secure your property. Professional installation ensures complete coverage.

Time Factor: While you're figuring out materials and methods, every minute your property stays exposed increases the potential for additional damage.
