Common questions

011. Do I Need to Hire a Mold Remediation Company?

Homeowners who notice signs of mold should hire a mold remediation company immediately. Mold can eat away at a home’s construction, weakening vital sections of its structure and spreading mold spores throughout the house. In addition, those same spores cause various respiratory problems and worsen allergic reactions or asthma symptoms.

022. Is Mold Removal and Mold Remediation the Same Thing?

Many homeowners try to take care of mold themselves using store-bought sprays or bleach. Unfortunately, many of these solutions don’t kill the roots of the problem, only wiping away mold that’s on the surface. Leaving behind traces of mold allows it to grow back, making it necessary to clean constantly without ever getting rid of the mold.

033. How Do I Find the Right Mold Remediation Company?

However, searching for the most reputable mold remediation company requires diligent research.

044. Do Mold Remediation Companies Test or Document Mold?

A reputable mold removal company often skips testing unless the insurance company requires it. Since mold remediation technicians see mold every day, they recognize it on sight and don’t require a test to ensure that’s the problem. While they may not test the substance, they often photograph their progress to document the mold elimination.

055. What Does the Mold Remediation Process Include?

Mold remediation starts with locating all affected areas in a home. Next, the team removes the mold and uses HEPA equipment to eliminate mold spores and vacuum up any excess moisture. Once the home dries out, professional mold remediators spray fungicide on the affected areas and the space around them, effectively killing invisible mold colonies.
