Common Toilet Issues That Cause Water Damage

Just like appliance-related water damage, toilet leaks can happen quietly and create serious issues before you're even aware. Toilets are used every day, which means wear and tear is bound to happen.

Why is there water around the base of my toilet after flushing?

If you’re asking this, chances are you’re dealing with one of the following problems in your bathroom:

Wax Seal is Broken

The wax ring that sits between your toilet and the floor can break down over time. When it fails, water can leak out at the base during every flush, slowly damaging the floor and subfloor underneath.

There’s a Pipe Clog

Clogs don’t just make a mess - they can cause serious backups. If the blockage is deep in the drain line, flushing the toilet might cause water to back up and spill out onto the floor.

Toilet Is Constantly Running

A toilet that runs nonstop wastes water and can cause the tank to overflow if the float valve doesn’t shut it off correctly. This overflow can spill out quickly and cause flooding.

Connections Are Loose

Water supply lines and fittings behind the toilet can loosen or crack. Even a small drip from a worn-out hose can cause long-term damage if it’s hidden from view.
