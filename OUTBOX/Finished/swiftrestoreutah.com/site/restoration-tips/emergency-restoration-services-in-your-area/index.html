How to Find the Best Emergency Restoration Services in Your Area

Get a Quote (385) 412-8633

Darin Jenks

Disasters typically happen when they are least expected, so most homeowners don’t have a plan in place for dealing with the aftermath. Instead, they find themselves scrambling to get their property back to its pre-loss condition. This typically involves hiring a professional to assist, but the average person doesn’t have a restoration company on speed dial.


That doesn’t mean that customers should settle for the first business that pops up in their internet search. Instead, they should take special care to find a reputable contractor who specializes in emergency restoration services. The following steps can help homeowners choose the right team for the job.


Spend Time Doing Research and Know What Questions to Ask

While time is always of the essence in emergency situations, that doesn’t mean that homeowners should rush the process of hiring an emergency restoration contractor. Instead, it’s crucial that they take their time to do plenty of research. This involves checking reviews and testimonials, requesting references, and collecting quotes.


It’s also important that homeowners ask a lot of questions before they hire a contractor. Specifically, they should ask the professionals how long their response time is, what type of equipment they use, and their current availability.


Choose a Company with a Local Office

Next, it’s important that homeowners take the time to hire a local company. When widespread disasters like winter storms, hail, and tornados hit an area, it’s very common for outsider companies to come in and try to get a slice of the “insurance claim” pie. Unfortunately, these people can be unreliable, and they can skip town just as quickly as they showed up, leaving homeowners with an even bigger mess to clean.


To avoid working with any companies like this, customers should look for contractors who are local to the area. They should be able to provide a local address for their office. If their office is located outside of the state, it’s best to choose another option.


Fairly Compare Estimates

One emergency restoration company might create their estimates differently than another. For that reason, it’s important that homeowners take the time to break down each estimate and understand what they are getting for their money.


Does the company offer full-service solutions? Will the homeowner need to hire outside contractors? Can they provide assistance with filing an insurance claim

? The answers to these questions can help point homeowners in the direction of the company that offers the most value.


Make Sure They Offer a Written Guarantee

Homeowners should never work with a contractor who won’t put everything in writing. It’s critical for homeowners to verify that the company will provide a written guarantee for their emergency restoration services. The document should detail the scope of the project, and it should include any type of protection or coverage that is included with the service. Taking this step ensures that the work done won’t be compromised in the future.


Avoid Taking Shortcuts

While it might be tempting to take shortcuts for convenience or cost, it’s best to avoid doing so when dealing with a disaster. Restoration projects are enough of a challenge, so homeowners don’t need to make things worse by hiring a company that isn’t reputable. Instead, they should take every precaution to vet the contractors before agreeing to let them work on the vulnerable property.


Check for Proven Experience

Finally, it’s vital that homeowners choose a company with proven experience in emergency restoration services. Though a new company might offer lower rates, it’s always worth paying more to avoid extra damages down the road.


The first step to finding a contractor with experience is looking at how long they have been in business. Customers can also check for experience by reviewing case studies, asking about certifications, and requesting more information on the company’s processes. An experienced company should be able to answer any questions and walk customers through their protocols.
