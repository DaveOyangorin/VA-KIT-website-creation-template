Common Causes of Water Damage in Ogden

Water damage can happen to anyone, anytime. Common causes of water damage include frozen, burst or broken pipes; household appliances like clothes washers, refrigerators and dishwashers; overflowing sinks, bathtubs or toilets; and extreme weather and flooding.

Tips for Preventing Water Damage

Knowing and understanding the common causes of water damage and your risks of experiencing water damage can help you take steps to prevent water damage in your home. Here are some things you should know:

  • Insulate exposed pipes to prevent freezing, and open cabinet doors under sinks when temperatures fall to extreme levels
  • Turn off water supply lines to appliances when you will be away for an extended period of time
  • Locate and know how to turn off your main water valves
  • Identify a water damage cleanup professional you can call in the event of an emergency

Water Damage Cleanup Can be Dangerous

Water damage can sometimes cause other problems, and Ogden homeowners should be aware of the potential hazards that may be present following water damage and during water damage cleanup. Many of these hazards will not be obvious, and can cause serious injury or illness.

Tips For Water Damage Cleanup

  • Risk of Electrical Shock – Turn off electricity to the affected area, unplug appliances, and do not enter an area that has electricity and is flooded, as the water may carry an electrical current. Do not use outlets in the affected area until an electrician has inspected them.
