The recovery timeline

What happens after you call.

0024/7, any hour

The call

A real person answers (385) 412-8633, day or night, and a crew heads out.

01Day 1

Extract & dry

Standing water out, then air movers, air scrubbers and dehumidifiers set to pull moisture out of the structure.

02Days 1–3

Contain

Plastic containment walls keep dust, spores and moisture out of the rest of the house.

03Days 2–5

Open it up

Wet drywall, insulation and flooring come out so the framing can dry and nothing is hidden behind the wall.

04Week 2 onward

Rebuild

Framing, plumbing and new drywall. We’re a general contractor, so the rebuild stays with the same crew.

05The good part

Finish

Tile, vanities, paint and floors. Often the room comes back better than it was before the water.

That’s the whole job.

Water, fire or mold today. A finished room at the end. One company the whole way.

About us

Questions

Good to know.

01Do you really answer at night?

Yes. Emergency service is 24/7 from our Ogden office at (385) 412-8633. Water keeps spreading while you wait, so call as soon as you find it.

02Can you help with my insurance claim?

Yes. We document the damage and work with your insurance company through the claim, from the first moisture readings to the rebuild.

03Do you do the rebuild too, or just the cleanup?

Both. We’re a licensed general contractor (license 4853926-5501), so the same company handles the drying, the tear-out, the rebuild and the finishes, and can remodel while the walls are open.

04Which areas do you cover?

Ogden and Weber and Davis counties: Layton, Kaysville, Farmington, Bountiful, North Salt Lake, Roy, Harrisville, Huntsville, West Haven, Plain City and up to Brigham City.

05Can I finance the work?

Yes. You can restore now and pay over time through Hearth, and see options before work starts.
