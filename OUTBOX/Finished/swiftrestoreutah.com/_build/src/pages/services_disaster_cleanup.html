FAQs

  • Will my disaster cleanup be covered by insurance?

    In most cases, yes. We work with your insurance company to help you get the maximum benefit from your policy.

  • How long does disaster restoration take?

    It depends on the extent of the damage. Minor cleanups may take 1–3 days, while major rebuilds can take up to several weeks.

  • How much does cleanup and repair cost?

    Costs vary, but we provide a detailed estimate after our initial inspection. We always aim for cost-effective, high-quality service.

  • Do you work with all insurance companies?

    Yes, we collaborate with all major insurers and assist you with paperwork and documentation.

  • What should I do immediately after a flood or fire?

    Make sure everyone is safe and then call our 24/7 emergency response team. Avoid touching damaged materials and keep a record for insurance purposes.

  • Are your technicians certified and insured?

    Absolutely. All our technicians are IICRC-certified, trained, and fully insured.

  • How fast can you respond to an emergency?

    We typically arrive within 60 minutes for emergencies in Ogden and nearby areas.
