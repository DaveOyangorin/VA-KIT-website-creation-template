Essential Fire Safety Tips

In addition to our restoration services, we believe in empowering our community with valuable fire safety tips. Here are some essential fire safety guidelines to keep in mind:


  • Install smoke detectors on every level of your home and test them regularly.
  • Create and practice a fire escape plan with your family.
  • Never leave cooking appliances unsupervised while in use.
  • Keep flammable materials such as curtains, bedding, and furniture away from heat sources (candles and space heaters).
  • Keep a fire extinguisher readily accessible, for example in the kitchen, and familiarize yourself with its operation.
  • Check electrical cords and outlets for signs of wear or damage.
  • Properly store and dispose of flammable liquids such as gasoline, propane, and lighter fluid. 
  • Regularly clean dryer vents to prevent lint accumulation and ensure proper airflow.
  • Inspect heating sources including furnaces, chimneys, and wood stoves annually.
