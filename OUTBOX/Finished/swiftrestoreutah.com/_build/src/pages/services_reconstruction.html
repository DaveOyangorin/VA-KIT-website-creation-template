Reconstruction Services FAQs

  • What is the service called reconstruction?

    Reconstruction is the process of repairing or rebuilding a property that has been damaged by fire, water, smoke, or other natural disasters. This can include repairing structural damage, replacing fixtures and finishes, and restoring utilities.

  • What are the benefits of getting reconstruction services?

    The benefits of reconstruction include restoring a property to its pre-loss condition, improving its value, and making it safer.

  • What are the steps involved in reconstruction?

    The steps involved in reconstruction vary depending on the extent of the damage. However, the process generally includes repairing or rebuilding structural damage, replacing fixtures and finishes, and restoring utilities.

  • Are reconstruction services covered by insurance?

    Reconstruction services are typically covered by insurance if the damage is caused by a covered event, such as a fire or water leak. However, it is important to check with your insurer to see what is covered.
