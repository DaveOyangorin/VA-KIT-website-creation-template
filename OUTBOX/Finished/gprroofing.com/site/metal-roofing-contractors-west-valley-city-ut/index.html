Frequently asked questions

How long does a metal roof last?

A properly installed metal roof can last anywhere from 40 to 70 years, depending on the type of material used and the level of upkeep. Unlike asphalt shingles, which typically need replacing every 15 to 25 years, metal roofing is built for long-term performance. In Utah, where roofs face heavy snow in winter and high heat in summer, a metal roof gives you decades of consistent protection with minimal maintenance.

Is metal roofing more expensive than shingles?

Yes, the initial investment is usually higher than that of asphalt shingles. However, when you look at the lifespan and the cost of multiple shingle replacements over the same period, metal roofing often turns out to be the more affordable choice long term. It also saves you money on energy bills because metal reflects heat, which keeps homes cooler in the summer. Many homeowners also see an increase in resale value since buyers appreciate the durability and low maintenance of a metal roof.

Do you offer warranties?

Absolutely. At Go Pro Roofing, every installation comes with a 5-year workmanship warranty. On top of that, we install materials backed by leading manufacturers like Owens Corning, CertainTeed, and GAF, which carry warranties of up to 50 years. That means your investment is protected both by us and by the manufacturer, giving you peace of mind that your roof is covered for the long haul.

Yes. We provide free roof inspections and detailed estimates with no obligation. What makes metal roofing a good choice for Utah homes?

Utah’s climate puts roofs to the test. Metal roofing handles heavy snow loads, resists high winds, and won’t warp or crack under extreme temperature swings. It’s also fire-resistant, which adds another layer of protection. For homeowners looking for long-term durability and lower maintenance, metal roofing is one of the smartest investments you can make in Utah.
