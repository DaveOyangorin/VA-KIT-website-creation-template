Frequently asked questions

How much does an asphalt shingle roof cost in Utah?

The cost of a new asphalt shingle roof in Utah typically ranges between $8,000 and $15,000, depending on your roof’s size, pitch, and material selection. Factors like tear-off of old shingles, ventilation upgrades, and underlayment can also influence pricing. Our free inspections include a detailed breakdown so you understand exactly what you’re paying for, with no hidden surprises. Homeowners often compare costs among asphalt shingle roofing contractors to make informed decisions.

How long does an asphalt roof last in Salt Lake Valley?

Standard asphalt shingle roofs in Utah last about 20–30 years when properly installed and maintained. Premium dimensional or architectural shingles can extend that lifespan up to 50 years. Extreme weather, such as heavy snow loads and strong winds, can significantly shorten a roof’s lifespan, which is why choosing a certified asphalt roof installer is crucial for maximizing durability.

What warranties do you offer?

Go Pro Roofing offers a 5-year workmanship warranty on every installation, covering our labor and installation practices. Additionally, we offer manufacturer warranties of up to 50 years from trusted brands such as Owens Corning, CertainTeed, and GAF. These cover material defects, giving you peace of mind that both the product and the installation are guaranteed.

Do you serve areas outside West Valley City?

Yes, our service extends across the entire Salt Lake Valley. We regularly work with homeowners and businesses in Salt Lake City, Murray, Sandy, Taylorsville, West Jordan, Draper, and surrounding communities. If you’re in Utah, there’s a good chance we can serve you.

How do I know if I need a new roof?

Signs you may need a new roof include visible leaks, missing or curling shingles, bald spots where granules are worn away, sagging areas, and water stains on ceilings or walls. If your roof is more than 20 years old, it’s often more cost-effective to replace it before problems worsen. Our free inspections provide a professional evaluation, enabling you to make an informed decision and select the right asphalt roofing contractor for your project.
