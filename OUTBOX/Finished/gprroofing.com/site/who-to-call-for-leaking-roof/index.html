What’s Really Causing That Leak?

Most leaks start small and grow into big problems. Common causes include:

  1. Cracked or missing shingles
  2. Damaged flashing around chimneys or vents
  3. Pooling water on flat or low-slope roofs
  4. Clogged gutters are pushing water under the shingles

Utah’s freeze-thaw cycles and surprise storms make roofs especially vulnerable. Fixing the leak without addressing the cause? That’s just putting a bucket under a waterfall.

How to Tell If Your Roof Is Leaking (Before It’s Too Late)

Recognizing the early signs of a roof leak can prevent major damage. Watch for:

  1. Water stains on ceilings or walls
  2. Mold or mildew smell in attic or walls
  3. Shingle granules accumulating in gutters
  4. Sagging ceilings or bubbling paint
  5. Rusted nails or metal flashing in roof areas
The sooner you act, the easier (and cheaper) the fix will be. Don’t wait for the storm to hit before taking action.
