How TPO Roofing Works

TPO, short for Thermoplastic Polyolefin, is a single-ply roofing membrane engineered for strength, flexibility, and long-term energy savings. It is made of three layers: a durable thermoplastic surface, a reinforced scrim, and a flexible base layer. Together, they create a watertight shield that resists punctures, chemicals, and UV damage.

What sets TPO apart is the installation method. Instead of using adhesives, our crews heat-weld the seams, fusing the material into a single continuous surface. This creates stronger joints than glue or tape can achieve. There are no weak points and no leaks. The result is a uniform, reflective roof that holds up through Utah’s scorching summers, heavy snow, and freeze-thaw cycles.

Because of its white, reflective surface, TPO reduces heat absorption, keeping your building cooler and your energy bills lower. It is an excellent choice for both commercial and residential flat roofs where longevity and efficiency matter most.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long does a TPO roof last?

A well-installed TPO roof typically lasts between 20 and 30 years. Longevity depends on the thickness of the membrane, the quality of installation, and regular maintenance. Thicker TPO membranes (60–80 mil) offer longer lifespans and better resistance to punctures. With annual inspections and timely seam resealing, many Utah property owners see their TPO systems last well beyond 30 years.

Is TPO roofing good for Utah’s climate?

Absolutely. Utah’s high UV exposure and freeze-thaw cycles are exactly what TPO was designed for. The material remains flexible in cold weather, resists cracking, and reflects up to 80% of sunlight in the summer. Its heat-welded seams also prevent leaks during snowmelt and heavy rain, making it one of the most durable options for Salt Lake Valley properties.

Can TPO roofing be used on homes?

Yes. TPO roofing is excellent for modern residential homes with flat or low-slope sections. It offers a clean, modern look, great insulation performance, and a much lower maintenance cost than traditional tar-and-gravel or rubber roofs. Homeowners love its white finish, which keeps interiors cooler in summer and reduces AC use.

How much does TPO roofing cost in Salt Lake City?

Costs generally range between $5 and $12 per square foot installed. The exact price depends on roof size, access, insulation type, and membrane thickness. While TPO can cost slightly more upfront than older systems, its energy efficiency and low maintenance requirements make it far more economical over time. Go Pro Roofing offers free estimates so you can understand your exact costs before committing.

Do you provide warranties?

Yes. Every TPO project includes a 5-year workmanship warranty and a manufacturer-backed material warranty of up to 50 years. These warranties cover both product integrity and installation performance. Unlike many contractors, we handle the registration process directly with the manufacturer, so your coverage starts the moment your roof is complete.

For more tips and guides, visit our roofing blog.

What maintenance does a TPO roof need?

TPO roofs are low-maintenance but not maintenance-free. We recommend a professional inspection once a year, ideally before winter. Inspections check seam integrity, flashing, and drainage. Keeping the surface clean of debris, leaves, and standing water helps prevent early wear. With this simple routine, you will get the full lifespan your TPO system is designed for.
