Oakley Roofing is certified by Tesla to install the Tesla Solar Roof in Utah. We’re locally owned in Salt Lake City and handle the complete installation, for homes from 900 to 15,000 square feet.

A roofing company that installs solar

The Tesla Solar Roof is a roof first: solar tiles and non-solar tiles that look the same, laid as a complete new roof. It makes sense to have it installed by a roofing company, and Oakley Roofing has been roofing Salt Lake City since 1993.

What we handle

  • Installation of the complete Tesla Solar Roof
  • Upgrades for homeowners replacing an existing roof
  • Upkeep and repair of your roof over time
  • Powerwall battery storage, to keep power on when the grid goes down
