Keeping Utah covered

Bryce Oakley founded Oakley Roofing in 1993 with a mission to cover Utah. An expert in both residential and commercial roofing, he built the company around quality, affordability and customer service.

Small businesses, developers of every size, government agencies and everyday Utahns have trusted Oakley Roofing to repair, replace and maintain roofs of every kind.

The owner works with you

Most of our business comes from referrals and repeat customers. Customers tell us they recommend Oakley Roofing because the owner is involved in their project: answering questions during the bid, keeping quality control during installation, and doing the final roof inspection.

We believe in explaining your roofing options so you can choose the right one, and we guide you through the whole process.

What we do

New construction roofs, re-roofing, roof installation, repairs and maintenance for residential and commercial customers, FHA roof inspections, and the Tesla Solar Roof, as Tesla Certified Solar Roof Installers.
