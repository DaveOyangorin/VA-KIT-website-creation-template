Oakley Roofing installs commercial roofs in Salt Lake City: TPO, EPDM, PVC, shingle and metal systems on new construction and re-roofs, from small buildings to large complexes.

New construction and re-roofing

Re-roofing commercial buildings is our specialty, and we build roofs on new construction too, from apartments and condominiums to offices, retail and warehouses. Tear-off and installation are done fast and safely, and every job is built to code.

Five roof systems

We install TPO, EPDM and PVC single-ply membranes, shingle roofs and metal roofs. Each membrane is joined differently and suits different buildings; our guide compares them: TPO vs. EPDM vs. PVC: Which Flat Roof Membrane Fits Your Building?

Custom commercial projects

Constructing a custom building or complex? With a custom fabrication shop in Salt Lake City and dozens of trusted suppliers, we can take on the roof you have planned.

Plan for inspections

We recommend having a commercial roof inspected twice a year, in spring and fall, before the extreme seasons. Between inspections, owners or maintenance staff should keep an eye out for problems, and call us early when they spot one.
