Oakley Roofing installs roofs in Salt Lake City on new construction and custom homes, and replaces old roofs with a fast, safe tear-off, in shingle or metal.

New construction and custom homes

Building a custom home? With a custom fabrication shop right in Salt Lake City and dozens of trusted suppliers, we’re ready for the roof you have planned, whether it’s shingle or metal.

Roof replacement

When an old roof has to come off, the crew tears it down and installs the new one quickly and safely, then cleans up and hauls the debris away. Every replacement is built to code.

An experienced crew

Our installers are licensed and trained and certified by the roofing manufacturers whose products we install, and some of our roofers have been with us for more than a decade. We’d rather keep experienced people than train new ones every season.

Small or large projects

Homes from 900 square feet to 15,000 are in range. Call (801) 651-1000 or send us the details for a free quote.
