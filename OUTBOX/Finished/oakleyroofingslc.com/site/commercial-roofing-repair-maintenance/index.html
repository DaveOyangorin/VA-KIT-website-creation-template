Oakley Roofing repairs and maintains commercial roofs in Salt Lake City: water leaks, disaster damage, preventative maintenance and inspections on TPO, EPDM, PVC, shingle and metal roofs.

Maintenance first

A well-maintained roof needs fewer repairs. Sealing leaks and replacing damaged sections as they appear keeps a roof working, and small spot repairs can put off a full replacement.

What we repair

  • Water leaks on low-slope and steep roofs
  • Disaster repair after storms and other damage
  • Preventative maintenance plans to catch problems early
  • Inspections, ideally in spring and fall

Keeping the building running

Your roof keeps employees, customers and inventory dry. We run on time and on budget, and clean up and dispose of debris when the work is done. Whether the issue is a single leak or a roof at the end of its life, we’ll inspect it and give you a straight answer.

What’s included

  • Water leak repair
  • Disaster repair
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Inspections
  • TPO, EPDM, PVC & shingle
  • Metal roofs
  • Cleanup and disposal
