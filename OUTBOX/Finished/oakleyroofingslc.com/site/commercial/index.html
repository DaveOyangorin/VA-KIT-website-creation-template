Oakley Roofing is a commercial roofing contractor in Salt Lake City: TPO, EPDM, PVC, shingle and metal roofs, from 900 square feet to 45,000, with re-roofing as our specialty.

On Salt Lake City’s commercial roofs since 1993

If you own or manage a factory, office building, apartment or condominium complex, or a warehouse, you know how much depends on the roof.
