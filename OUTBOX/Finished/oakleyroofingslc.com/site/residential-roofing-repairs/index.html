Oakley Roofing repairs roofs in Salt Lake City: water leaks, storm and disaster damage, and worn or damaged shingles, plus inspections and preventative maintenance to keep small problems small.

Fix it early, and the roof lasts longer

Keeping a roof well maintained is the best way to limit the repairs it needs. Replacing damaged shingles and sealing leaks as they appear keeps a roof in good shape, and small spot repairs can push back the day you need a full replacement.

What we repair

  • Water leaks, traced to where the water is actually getting in
  • Disaster and storm damage, including emergency work after a tree or a storm
  • Damaged or missing shingles
  • Preventative maintenance to catch problems before they leak
  • Inspections, including FHA roof inspections for home sales

We clean up and haul away the debris when the work is done, and every quote is free.

Repair or replace?

Whatever is going on with your roof, we start with a thorough inspection and tell you what we find. If spot repairs will keep the roof going, we’ll say so. If it’s time for a new roof, see residential roofing installation.

Questions

Common questions

Q01Can you repair a roof after storm or tree damage?

Yes. Disaster repair is part of what we do. One customer’s home was hit by a fallen tree, and we replaced the garage roof and main house roof in two days.

Q02Should I repair or replace my roof?

It depends on the roof’s age and how widespread the damage is. Small spot repairs can extend a roof’s life; we inspect first and explain the options.
