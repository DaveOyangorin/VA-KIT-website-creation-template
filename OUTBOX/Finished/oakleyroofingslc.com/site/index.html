What’s under a flat roof

Most commercial roofs in the valley are low-slope assemblies, built in layers. Pick a membrane to see how it’s joined and what it’s made of.

TPO Thermoplastic polyolefin

Seams
Heat-welded
Usual color
White
Standard
ASTM D6878

A thermoplastic sheet that softens with heat, so the seams are welded together on the roof. No added plasticizers.

EPDM Synthetic rubber

Seams
Adhesive or tape
Usual color
Black or white
Standard
ASTM D4637

A thermoset rubber that doesn’t melt once cured, so seams are bonded with tape or adhesive. Sheets come up to 50 ft wide.

PVC Poly(vinyl chloride)

Seams
Heat-welded
Usual color
White or light
Standard
ASTM D4434

Also heat-welded. Resists oils and grease, which is why it suits restaurant and kitchen exhaust areas.
