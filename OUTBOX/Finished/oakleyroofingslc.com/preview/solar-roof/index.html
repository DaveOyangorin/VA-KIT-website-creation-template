The Tesla Solar Roof replaces your roof with tiles that make power. Solar and non-solar tiles look virtually the same, and Oakley Roofing installs it across Salt Lake City and Utah.

Solar you can’t pick out from the street

Each solar and non-solar tile is virtually indistinguishable in color and trim, so the roof looks the same from every angle. The tiles are tempered glass, engineered for all-weather durability, including active snow and ice melting.

More tiles, more power

A single larger tile in each module means more of the roof can produce energy, so the system can be sized to what your home uses. Tesla designs each roof with drone analysis and sun and shade modeling, and the modular design installs in a matter of days.
