Sky Bridge Roofing installs architectural metal wall panels in South Salt Lake. These exterior cladding systems give commercial and modern residential buildings a clean, durable face, like the dark panels around the entry in the photo on this page, one of our own jobs.

What a wall panel system is

Metal wall panels hang on the outside of the building over a weather barrier, attached to girts or clips. The panels are the visible finish; behind them, the barrier and the flashing at windows, doors and corners keep water out of the wall.

Where they are used

  • Commercial entries and storefronts
  • Offices, shops and warehouses
  • Modern homes and accent walls
  • Additions that need to match a metal roof

How we install them

  • Weather barrier over the sheathing
  • Girts or clips set plumb and level
  • Panels hung with consistent reveals
  • Trim and flashing at openings, corners and the base

Roof and walls together

Because we also install metal roofs, we can roof and clad the same building and detail the joint between them.

Questions about wall panels

Q.01Are wall panels only for commercial buildings?

No. They are common on modern homes and accent walls too.

Q.02Can wall panels match a metal roof?

Yes, panels and roofing can be ordered in matching colors.

Q.03Do you work from architectural drawings?

Yes. Send blueprints and RFPs to cesar@skybridgeroofing.com.
