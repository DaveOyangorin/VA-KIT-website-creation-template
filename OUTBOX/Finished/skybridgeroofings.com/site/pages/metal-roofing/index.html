Sky Bridge Roofing installs and repairs metal roofing in South Salt Lake for homes, shops and commercial buildings. We work with three kinds of metal roof: standing seam panels, corrugated panels and metal shingles, and we can repair seams, fasteners and damaged panels on metal roofs that are already in place.

Three kinds of metal roof

Standing seam

Panels run from ridge to eave and lock together at raised seams, with the fasteners hidden under the seam on clips. The look in the drone photos on this page.

Corrugated metal

Ribbed panels fastened through the face. A simpler, classic profile common on shops, barns and outbuildings.

Metal shingles

Interlocking metal pieces shaped like slate, wood shake or tile, for a traditional look in metal.

Why people choose metal

Metal sheds snow and rain well, does not rot, and in a light or reflective finish it helps keep summer heat out of the attic. It is also lighter than tile. On the other side of the ledger, it costs more up front than asphalt shingles, and it has to be installed with room for the panels to expand and contract.

How we install it

  • Deck and framing checked before the panels go on
  • High-temperature underlayment under the metal
  • Panels anchored with clips or fasteners for the profile
  • Trim, flashing and closures at every edge, wall and penetration

Questions about metal roofing

Q.01Is metal roofing loud in the rain?

Over a solid deck with underlayment, as on a house, rain noise is close to that of other roofs. The loud metal roofs people remember are usually panels over open framing on barns.

Q.02Can you put metal over shingles?

In some cases, but we usually remove the old roof so the deck can be checked and the panels sit flat. We will tell you what your roof allows.

Q.03Do you repair metal roofs?

Yes: loose fasteners, failed seams and sealant, leaking penetrations and damaged panels.

Q.04Standing seam or corrugated?

Standing seam has concealed fasteners and a cleaner look; corrugated is simpler and costs less. We can show you both.
