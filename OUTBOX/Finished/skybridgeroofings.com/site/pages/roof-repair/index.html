Sky Bridge Roofing handles roof repair in South Salt Lake for homes and commercial buildings: leaks, missing or damaged shingles, loose or rusted flashing, and storm damage. We start by finding where the water is really getting in, because the stain on the ceiling is rarely right under the problem, then repair it with materials that match what is already on your roof.

What a repair visit covers

A leak is usually a symptom. Water runs along the deck, the rafters or the top of the insulation before it shows up indoors, so the first job is to trace it back. We look at the places roofs most often fail: flashing at walls, chimneys and skylights; pipe boots and vents; valleys; the eaves; and any shingles or panels that have lifted, cracked or blown off.

  • Leaks traced from the inside stain back to the entry point
  • Damaged or missing shingles replaced and sealed
  • Step and counter flashing reset or replaced at walls and chimneys
  • Pipe boots, vents and penetrations resealed
  • Metal panels and seams re-secured
  • Storm damage made safe first, then repaired

Matching what is already there

A repair should not look like a patch. We source shingles and metal that blend with your existing roof as closely as the product line allows, so the repair protects the house without leaving a different-colored square on the front slope.

Repair or replace?

Not every roof needs replacing. If the damage is local and the rest of the roof is sound, a repair is the right call and we will tell you so. When the problems are spread across the roof, the decking is soft, or the same areas keep failing, putting money into repairs stops making sense. In that case we will walk you through roof replacement and show you why.

If you are not sure which one you need, start with a free roof inspection.

When it can’t wait

If water is coming in now, call or text (435) 659-4915. Emergency response is available, and the first step is always to stop more water getting in and protect the inside of the house.

Questions about roof repair

Q.01What causes roof leaks?

Most leaks start at a transition: flashing at a wall, chimney or skylight, a cracked pipe boot, a valley, or the eaves. Missing or lifted shingles, loose metal fasteners and clogged gutters that back water up under the roof edge are the other common causes.

Q.02Can a handyman fix a roof leak?

A handyman can seal an obvious gap, but leaks rarely show up where they start. Without tracing the water path, a patch often moves the leak instead of stopping it. A roofer also knows how flashing and underlayment are meant to overlap, which is what keeps water out.

Q.03Do you repair metal roofs too?

Yes. We repair both shingle and metal roofs, including resealing seams and penetrations and replacing damaged panels.

Q.04How do I get a repair started?
