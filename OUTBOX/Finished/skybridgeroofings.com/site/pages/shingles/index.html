Sky Bridge Roofing installs asphalt and architectural shingle roofs in South Salt Lake, along with shingle repairs and full replacements. Shingles are the most common roof on homes here: they suit almost any style, come in many colors and cost less than metal.

Three-tab or architectural?

Three-tab asphalt

A single flat layer with a uniform look. The simplest and most budget-friendly shingle.

Architectural (dimensional)

Two or more layers laminated together for a thicker shingle and a shadowed, textured look. The usual choice for replacements today.

The parts you don’t see

A shingle roof is more than shingles. Underneath are the deck, underlayment, ice and water protection along the eaves and valleys, starter strip, drip edge and flashing at every wall and penetration, with ridge vents or other ventilation for the attic. Every piece has to overlap the right way for water to run off instead of in.

How we install it

  • Old roof removed and deck checked
  • Underlayment and edge metal
  • Flashing at walls, chimneys and penetrations
  • Shingles placed and nailed course by course
  • Ridge cap and ventilation finished
  • Site cleaned and nails picked up

Repairs

Wind-lifted, cracked or missing shingles can usually be repaired. See roof repair.

Questions about shingle roofing

Q.01How long does a shingle roof last?

It depends on the shingle, the ventilation and the weather it sees. We can tell you more about the specific shingle you are considering.

Q.02Can you match my existing shingles for a repair?

We match as closely as the product line allows, so the repair blends in.

Q.03Do shingles work on low-slope roofs?

Shingles need enough slope to shed water. Flat and very low-slope areas get a membrane like TPO or EPDM instead.

Q.04Do I need a permit to reshingle?

Yes, South Salt Lake requires a building permit for any reroof.
