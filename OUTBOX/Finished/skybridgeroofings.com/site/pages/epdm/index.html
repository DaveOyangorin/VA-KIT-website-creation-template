Sky Bridge Roofing installs and repairs EPDM roofing in South Salt Lake. EPDM is a synthetic rubber membrane for flat and low-slope roofs on commercial buildings and homes. It comes in large sheets, so a roof has fewer seams, and the seams it does have are bonded with tape or adhesive.

What EPDM is

EPDM (ethylene propylene diene monomer) is a rubber roofing sheet, usually black. Unlike TPO it does not melt, so the seams are joined with seam tape or adhesive rather than heat. It stays flexible in cold weather and is a long-standing choice for low-slope roofs.

How it is installed

  • Deck checked and repaired
  • Insulation boards
  • Membrane fully adhered, mechanically fastened or ballasted
  • Seams bonded with tape or adhesive
  • Flashing at curbs, walls, drains and penetrations

EPDM or TPO?

EPDM is rubber, usually black, with bonded seams; TPO is white with welded seams. We will recommend the one that fits the building.

Repairs and coatings

Older EPDM roofs can often be repaired, or restored with a roof coating instead of being replaced.

Questions about epdm roofing

Q.01What is a ballasted roof?

A ballasted roof holds the membrane down with the weight of stone or pavers instead of fasteners or glue, like the gravel roof in the photo on this page.

Q.02Can EPDM be repaired?

Yes. Splits, punctures and loose seams can be patched with compatible EPDM materials.

Q.03Is EPDM only black?

Black is most common; white EPDM is also made.

Q.04Can you coat an old EPDM roof?

Often, yes, if the membrane is still sound. We check it first.
