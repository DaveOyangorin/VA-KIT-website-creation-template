Sky Bridge Roofing is a roofing company based in South Salt Lake, Utah, working on homes and commercial buildings. We repair and replace roofs, install metal, shingle, TPO and EPDM systems, apply roof coatings, and install gutters and metal wall panels.

Our mission

“At Sky Bridge Roofing, our mission is to deliver superior roofing solutions with unmatched craftsmanship, integrity, and professionalism.”

In practice that means three things we care about: quality materials and careful work, honest advice about whether a roof needs repairing or replacing, and the same standard on a small repair as on a commercial roof.

How we work

A roof is a system: deck, underlayment, flashing, the covering and the details where they meet walls, chimneys and drains. We plan those details before the crew goes up, and on commercial projects we work from the drawings. Send blueprints and RFPs to cesar@skybridgeroofing.com.
