Sky Bridge Roofing installs seamless gutters in South Salt Lake. We form the gutter on site from a continuous aluminum coil, so each run is one piece without leak-prone joints, then hang it, set the pitch and add downspouts that send water away from your foundation.

Gutter options

5-inch seamless

The standard size for most homes.

6-inch seamless

More capacity for large or steep roofs and long runs, with oversized downspouts.

Gutter guards

Stainless steel mesh that keeps leaves and debris out of the gutter.

Why gutters matter

A roof sheds a lot of water. Without working gutters it pours off the edge, splashes the siding, wears away the soil and soaks the ground beside the foundation. Overflowing or clogged gutters can also back water up under the roof edge.

How we install them

  • Measure runs and plan downspout locations
  • Form seamless gutter on site
  • Hang with internal hangers and set the pitch
  • Seal the corners and end caps
  • Downspouts aimed away from the foundation

Gutters with a new roof

If you are replacing your roof, it is a good time to replace old gutters too. See roof replacement.

Questions about gutters

Q.01What does seamless mean?

Each straight run is one continuous piece formed on site, so there are no joints along the length to leak. Only the corners and downspouts are joined.

Q.025-inch or 6-inch gutters?

Five-inch suits most homes; six-inch is for bigger or steeper roofs that send more water to the edge.

Q.03Do gutter guards work?

Mesh guards keep leaves and debris out, which means far less cleaning. They still need an occasional check.

Q.04Do you install gutters on commercial buildings?

Yes. Ask us for an estimate.
