Sky Bridge Roofing applies roof coatings in South Salt Lake, mainly silicone coatings on flat and low-slope commercial roofs. A coating is rolled or sprayed over the existing roof to form one seamless layer that keeps water out and reflects sunlight, often adding years of life to a roof that is worn but still sound.

When a coating makes sense

A coating is a restoration, not a replacement. It works on a roof whose deck and insulation are dry and solid but whose surface is aging: worn membranes, seams starting to open, small leaks. If the roof underneath is wet or failing, a coating will not fix that; the roof needs to be repaired or replaced first.

How we apply it

  • Inspection, including checking for wet insulation
  • Repairs to seams, flashing and damaged areas
  • Cleaning and surface preparation
  • Primer where the surface needs it
  • Silicone coating applied in a continuous layer, thicker at seams and details

Why silicone

Silicone forms a seamless, monolithic surface and handles standing water and UV well. A bright white coating also reflects the sun, which helps keep the building cooler in summer.

What it goes over

Coatings are commonly applied over EPDM, TPO, metal and other low-slope roofs. We will tell you whether yours is a good candidate.

Questions about roof coatings

Q.01Will a coating stop my roof leaking?

If the leaks come from worn seams or surface wear on an otherwise sound roof, yes. Leaks from wet insulation or failed decking need repair first.

Q.02Can you coat a shingle roof?

Coatings are mainly for flat, low-slope and metal roofs. Shingle roofs are usually repaired or replaced instead.

Q.03Does a coating replace a new roof?

No. It extends the life of a roof that is still sound. A roof past that point needs replacing.

Q.04How do I know if my roof qualifies?

Book a free inspection and we will check it.
