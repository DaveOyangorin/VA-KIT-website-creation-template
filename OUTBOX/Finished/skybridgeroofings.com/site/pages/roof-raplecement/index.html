Sky Bridge Roofing replaces roofs in South Salt Lake for homeowners and property owners. A replacement means tearing off the old roof, checking and repairing the deck, and installing a new system from the underlayment up: shingles, standing seam metal or metal shingles, with new flashing and edge metal.

How a replacement goes

  1. Inspection and estimate. We look at the roof, the deck where we can, the attic and the gutters, and give you a written estimate.
  2. Tear-off. The old roofing comes off down to the deck, so nothing is hidden under the new roof.
  3. Deck repair. Soft or damaged decking is replaced before anything goes on top of it.
  4. New layers. Underlayment, flashing at every wall and penetration, edge metal, then the new roof covering.
  5. Clean-up and final walk. Nails and debris picked up, and a final look at the finished roof with you.

Choosing the new roof

The three systems we install most for replacements:

Architectural shingles

Layered asphalt shingles with a dimensional look. The most common choice for homes. Shingle roofing

Standing seam metal

Long panels with raised seams and concealed fasteners. A clean modern line. Metal roofing

Metal shingles

Metal formed to look like slate, shake or tile.

Flat or low-slope sections get a membrane instead: TPO or EPDM.

Replacement or new installation?

A replacement takes off a roof that is already there. New roofs on new builds and additions follow the same layer-by-layer approach without the tear-off; the metal roof in the photos on this page is a new build we roofed.

Permits

South Salt Lake requires a building permit for any reroof, and permitted work is inspected. We cover what that means for you in our guide: Do You Need a Permit to Replace a Roof in South Salt Lake?

Questions about roof replacement

Q.01How do I know I need a new roof and not a repair?

Damage spread across the roof, soft decking, granule loss across many shingles, or repeated leaks in different places all point to replacement. One damaged area on an otherwise sound roof usually means a repair. A free inspection will tell you which.

Q.02Can a new roof go over the old one?

Sometimes a second layer is allowed, but not over two existing layers, and not over a roof that is wet or deteriorated. We usually recommend a full tear-off, because it is the only way to see and fix the deck.

Q.03Do I need a permit?

Yes. South Salt Lake requires a building permit for any reroof, and all permitted work is inspected.

Q.04What does a new roof cost?

It depends on the roof size, pitch, the number of layers coming off, deck repairs and the system you choose. We give a written estimate after a free inspection.
