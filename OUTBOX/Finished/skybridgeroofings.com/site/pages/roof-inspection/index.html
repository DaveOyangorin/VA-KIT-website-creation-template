Sky Bridge Roofing offers free roof inspections in South Salt Lake. We check the roof covering, flashing, penetrations, gutters and the signs of trouble inside, so homeowners and property managers can fix small problems early instead of paying for bigger repairs later. Drone-assisted inspections are available for roofs that are steep, large or hard to walk.

What we look at

  • Shingles: lifting, cracking, missing pieces and granule loss
  • Metal: loose fasteners, seams, sealant and rust
  • Membranes: seams, punctures and ponding water
  • Flashing at walls, chimneys, skylights and valleys
  • Pipe boots, vents and other penetrations
  • Gutters, downspouts and the roof edge
  • Inside: stains, damp insulation or daylight in the attic

Drone-assisted inspection

On steep, tall or fragile roofs, a drone gets a close look without anyone walking on the surface, and gives you photos of what we found. The roof photos on this site come from our own drone footage.

When to get one

After a hail or wind storm, before buying or selling a home, when you notice a stain or a drip, when a roof is getting old, or simply once a year as a check-up. Catching a lifted shingle or a cracked boot early is far cheaper than drying out a ceiling.

What you get

A plain answer: what we found, photos, and whether it needs a repair, a replacement, or nothing at all yet.

Questions about roof inspection

Q.01Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. Our roof inspections and estimates are free.

Q.02How long does an inspection take?

It depends on the size and access of the roof. We will give you a time window when we schedule it.

Q.03Do you inspect commercial roofs?

Yes, including flat and low-slope roofs with TPO or EPDM membranes.

Q.04What happens if you find a problem?

We show you what we found, explain your options and give you a written estimate. There is no obligation.
