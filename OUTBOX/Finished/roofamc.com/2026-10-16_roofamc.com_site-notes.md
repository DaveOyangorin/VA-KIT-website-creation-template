# roofamc.com redesign: notes for Angelo

Draft, 16 Oct 2026. Nothing is live. Files:

- `site/`: the files to put live. Same URLs and canonicals as the current WordPress site.
- `preview/`: the private review copy (links go to `index.html`, forms are off, draft strip on top).
- `_build/`: the source. Edit the `p_*.py` files, then run `python -I build.py ../site ../preview`. `qa.py ../site` checks titles, metas, H1s, word counts, JSON-LD and links.

## Design direction

- Redesign (v2, 18 Oct 2026): "built for winter". Anton + Figtree + Space Mono; pine and ember from the logo, snow white. Full-bleed skyline hero with animated rain, the emergency leak line as a hero card (the top bar was removed), a snow stake marking the cited averages (about 65 in at the airport, close to 100 on the benches), and the existing ticker, service mosaic, materials, winter band and reviews in the new type. QA flags "in today's" on the testimonials page: it is inside a client's word-for-word testimonial, so it stays.


- **References (screenshotted and reviewed):** Strike Group (strikegroup.ca): condensed uppercase type, a mountain photo cut on a diagonal, thin accent rules. Power Design (powerdesigninc.us): dark full-bleed photo hero, heavy type, diagonal shapes. Bartlett Cocke (bartlettcocke.com): condensed display type, big stat numbers. Artisan Roofing (artisanroofing.ca, an Awwwards listing) was the "what to avoid" example: a stock model pointing at a form.
- **Colors come from the logo:** green #47704C and orange #D16E1A, plus a deep pine #14231A, warm stone #F4F0E8 and paper #FFFDF9. Buttons are orange with dark text, which passes contrast. White text on orange would not pass.
- **Fonts:** Barlow Condensed for headings and Work Sans for body text, both from Google Fonts. Verified loading in Edge.
- **Motion:** scroll reveals, light parallax on the hero and winter band, a client-name ticker and hover zoom on photos. All of it is switched off for visitors who turn on reduced motion.

### Blog keyword plan (no cannibalization)

- **Main:** "how long do roofs last in utah". It's a question (Tier 3), from the Google questions in the 5 Oct audit. Search volume and difficulty: **not in our data.** No keyword tool was used, so please check it.
- **Long-tail supporting phrases, used in H2s and the FAQ:** roof lifespan in Utah, how long does a metal roof last, signs you need a new roof, does snow shorten roof life, repairing a 20-year-old roof.
- **Kept off the blog on purpose:** the high-value head terms owned by money pages (roofing company / roof repair / commercial roofing / roof replacement salt lake city). The blog only *links* to those pages with descriptive anchors. It never targets them in its title, H1 or H2s.
- **The residential FAQ does not use the blog's question.** It asks "Which roofing material is best for a Utah home?" and links to the blog.
- **When the roof replacement page gets built:** the audit puts "How long do roofs last in Utah?" on that page. Give it a short answer there and link to the blog, so the two don't compete.

### Other cannibalization calls (please confirm)

1. **Home page moved from "Park City UT Roofing Company" to "roofing company salt lake city"**, per the audit. Park City is now carried by the Park City page.
2. **The SLC gallery URL contains "salt-lake-city-commercial-roofing-and-residential-roofing"**, which overlaps the home and commercial pages. I kept the URL (no redirect) but repositioned the page as a *project gallery* with its own title and H1. Alternative: 301 it to `/commercial/`. Your call.
3. **Roof leak repair** is a strong section on `/roof-repair/`, not its own page, as the audit suggested.
