The Best Time of Year to Pour Concrete in Southern Utah

If you’ve ever wondered when’s the best time to pour concrete in St. George, you’re not alone. Southern Utah’s unique desert climate makes timing crucial for long-lasting driveways, patios, and foundations. At

M3 Industries, we’ve spent more than 16 years perfecting our pour schedules to make sure your concrete cures strong, smooth, and crack-free.

☀️

Why Timing Matters

Concrete strength depends on how it cures. Extreme heat or cold can cause cracking, uneven surfaces, or weak spots. In Southern Utah, where summer temps soar above 100 °F, it’s all about striking the right balance between warm days and cool nights.

🗓️

The Ideal Pouring Seasons

Temperatures are mild and humidity levels are steady—perfect for large pours like driveways and patios.

  • Fall (September – November):

    Cooler weather allows the concrete to set evenly without drying too fast. This is often our busiest and most recommended season for residential projects.

  • Winter & Summer:

    Still possible, but require special care. In summer, we pour early mornings and use curing compounds to prevent moisture loss. In winter, we monitor ground temps and use warming blankets when needed.
