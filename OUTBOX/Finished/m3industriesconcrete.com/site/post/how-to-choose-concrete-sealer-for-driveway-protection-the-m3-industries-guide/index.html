You’ve invested a lot into your home’s curb appeal, but out here in Southern Utah, your concrete is under constant attack. Between the scorching 100-degree sun, our famous red dirt, and the inevitable oil drips from the truck, an unsealed driveway is a ticking time bomb for stains and cracks.

If you’re wondering how to choose concrete sealer for driveway protection, you’ve probably seen a dozen options at the big-box stores. But in St. George, a "cheap" sealer is often worse than no sealer at all.

Why Most Sealers Fail in the Desert

Most DIY sealers are "topical"—they sit on the surface like a coat of wax. In our UV index, those sealers yellow, peel, and flake off within a year. When learning how to choose concrete sealer for driveway protection, the first rule is:

Look for penetration, not just a coating.
