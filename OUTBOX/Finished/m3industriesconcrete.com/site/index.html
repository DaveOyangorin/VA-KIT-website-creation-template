What’s under the surface

A slab is five layers. Four of them you’ll never see.

Layer 1 of 5 · Native ground

Red dirt, graded

Every project starts with proper grading, compaction, and base preparation.

Layer 2 of 5 · Compacted base

Where jobs are won

We don’t cut corners underground. That’s what separates a 5-year concrete job from a 30-year one.

Layer 3 of 5 · Steel reinforcement

The backbone

We use rebar and wire mesh where needed to give your concrete the structural backbone to resist cracking, shifting, and heavy loads.

Layer 4 of 5 · The pour

Quality mix, honest pour

We use the right concrete mix for the application. No watering down, no shortcuts. Every yard of concrete is placed with care.

Layer 5 of 5 · Finish & seal

Straight edges, proper joints

Straight edges, consistent textures, and proper control joints. The finished look matters just as much as what’s underneath.

What we do

Every phase, new pours to protective coatings.

From new pours to protective coatings, M3 Industries handles every phase of your concrete project. On time and on budget.
