Skylight Installation in Salt Lake City

Utah One Roofing installs Velux skylights in Salt Lake City homes: ventilated skylights for kitchens and bathrooms, fixed skylights for high ceilings, and tubular skylights for small rooms, flashed into the roof by the same crew that roofs it.

Natural light, energy efficiency, and added home value

One Roofing LLC, we proudly serve Salt Lake, Davis, Utah, Summit, and Tooele Counties with high-quality roofing and skylight installation services.

Benefits of Adding Skylights

Lower Energy Bills

Natural light reduces your need for electric lighting.

Eco-Friendly

Use less power and reduce your carbon footprint.

Brighter, Open Spaces

Skylights make rooms feel larger and more inviting.

Some models may qualify for energy-efficiency credits.

Increased Home Value

Boost curb appeal and resale value.

Types of Skylights We Install

Ventilated Skylights

Among the most popular, these skylights offer both natural light and airflow—ideal for kitchens and bathrooms where moisture needs to escape.

Fixed Skylights

Perfect for high ceilings or hard-to-reach spaces. They provide beautiful lighting and views but don’t open for ventilation.

Tubular Skylights

Great for low-ceiling areas or smaller rooms where traditional skylights won’t fit. They use reflective tubes to direct sunlight indoors—efficient lighting without needing extra space.
