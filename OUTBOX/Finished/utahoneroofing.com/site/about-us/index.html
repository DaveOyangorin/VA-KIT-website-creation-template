About Utah One Roofing, Salt Lake City

A veteran-owned roofing company serving seven Utah counties.

Free Estimate (801) 928-9570

At One Roofing LLC we specialize in reliable solutions for roof repair, replacement, insurance restoration, flat and metal roofing, and gutter installation, serving Salt Lake, Utah, Davis, Summit, Tooele, Weber and Wasatch counties.

Our team is committed to high-quality service, focusing not only on the technical side of the job but on an exceptional customer experience. With integrity, honesty and professionalism, we make sure every project meets the highest standards.

Veteran-owned

As veterans, we bring our commitment beyond roofing, with a service rooted in values such as loyalty and teamwork. Just as we’ve served our country, we stand by our work and are committed to serving you.
