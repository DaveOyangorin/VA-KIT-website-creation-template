How to Know If You Need Roof Repair

Watch for these common signs of roof damage—addressing them early can save you time, money, and stress.

  • Leaks or Stains
  • Missing or Damaged Shingles
  • Granules in Gutters
  • Mold or Moss
  • Sagging Roof Deck
  • Pipe Flashing Issues

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I think my roof has been damaged after a storm?

Schedule a professional inspection right away. We’ll evaluate your roof, document the damage, and help you with your insurance claim.

Will my insurance company cover the full cost of a new roof?

That depends on your policy and the extent of the damage. We’ll work to make sure you get every dollar you’re entitled to.

How long does the insurance claim process take?

It varies by provider and damage severity. Once filed, we’ll help push the process along and keep you updated at every step.

How do I know if my roof damage is covered by insurance?

Most policies cover storm-related issues like wind or hail. We’ll review your policy with you and explain your coverage.

Do I need to pay anything upfront?

Generally, you’re only responsible for your policy deductible. We’ll go over all expected costs before we begin any work.
