Signs You May Need a New Roof

Roof damage should never be ignored. Here are some signs it might be time for a new roof:

  • Missing or damaged shingles
  • Recurring leaks or water damage
  • Sagging roof structure
  • Granules in the gutters
  • Roof over 20 years old
  • Moss or algae buildup

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most common residential roofing type in Utah?

Asphalt shingles are the most common choice due to their affordability, reliability, and variety of design options.

How long do roofs typically last in Utah?

Asphalt shingles generally last 20–25 years. Metal roofs can last 40–50 years or more. Regular maintenance extends your roof’s life.
