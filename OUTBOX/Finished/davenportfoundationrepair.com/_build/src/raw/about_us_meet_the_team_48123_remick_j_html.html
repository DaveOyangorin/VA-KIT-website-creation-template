                                  Remick J.

                                  Davenport Foundation Repair

                                  Title: Co-Foreman
                                  Hometown: West Jordan, UT

                                  Remick has been with us for just over a year, bringing a wealth of experience and dedication to his role. He is married and a proud father of a 1-year-old son. Remick's career journey began as a cabinet builder, where he honed his skills and developed a keen eye for detail and craftsmanship. This foundation has greatly contributed to his meticulous and thorough approach in his current position.

                                   

                                  A passionate fan of classic rock, Remick's love for music is evident in his diverse playlist that often features legendary bands and timeless hits. When it comes to food, Remick has a particular fondness for hamburgers, enjoying the variety and creativity that goes into crafting the perfect burger. His favorite movie is "Donnie Darko," a film that resonates with his taste for intriguing and thought-provoking cinema.

                                   

                                  Growing up in West Jordan, UT, Remick has deep roots in the community and a strong connection to the local culture. His household is a lively one, shared with three beloved pets named Floyd, Vera, and Marley. Each pet adds its own unique charm to the family dynamic.

                                   

                                  In his personal life, Remick enjoys spending quality time with his son, often binge-watching the delightful series "Bluey," which has become a favorite bonding activity. Beyond his family and pets, Remick harbors dreams of becoming an engineer, a field that aligns with his passion for building and innovation.

                                   

                                  Travel is another of Remick's aspirations, with Switzerland topping his list of dream destinations. The allure of its stunning landscapes, rich history, and cultural experiences is something he hopes to explore one day.

                                   

                                  Remick's dedication, combined with his diverse interests and strong family values, makes him a valued member of our team. His journey from cabinet building to his current role exemplifies his adaptability and commitment to personal and professional growth. We look forward to seeing him achieve his future dreams and continue to contribute positively to our organization.
