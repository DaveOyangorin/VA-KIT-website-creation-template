                                  About Davenport Foundation Repair

                                  Three Decades of Service

                                  For decades, we at Davenport Foundation Repair have focused on helping homeowners solve foundation problems the right way. From the beginning, our goal has been simple: provide reliable solutions, quality workmanship, and clear communication on every project.

                                  That commitment continues to guide how we operate today. While we use proven repair methods and industry-tested products, what matters most to us is the trust our customers place in our work and the confidence they have in the solutions we provide.

                                  From foundation stabilization and concrete leveling to basement waterproofing and crawl space repair, we work to match each homeowner with the right solution for their specific situation.

                                  Led by owner Ron Davenport, we're committed to raising the standard for foundation repair in Utah. That's why we're proud to be a certified Foundation Supportworks dealer, with access to professionally engineered products, ongoing training, and proven systems designed to deliver long-term results for homeowners.

                                  We provide foundation repair services throughout Utah, including Salt Lake City, West Valley City, Provo, and surrounding communities.

                                  Part of a Knowledgeable and Experienced Network

                                  Davenport Foundation Repair is part of the country's largest foundation repair contractor network, Supportworks, specializing in foundation repair solutions that are designed and engineered to fix any structural problem your home may be experiencing. With their headquarters located in Omaha, Nebraska, Supportworks provides professional training, continuous industry research, and some of best industry practices to each and every Supportworks dealer within their network. As a trained dealer of Supportworks, you can be sure that your foundation is in good hands when Davenport Foundation Repair is called in for the job.

                                  How We Do It

                                  Licensed Contractors

                                  Our experienced concrete foundation repair contractors utilize patented and state-of-the-art solutions to permanently repair cracked foundation walls and slab. All of our contractors are licensed with the Contractors' State License Board as a Specialty Contractor for Concrete Related Trades. You can check our license status online at https://commerce.utah.gov/dopl/. License# 252613-5501

                                  Our Services are Designed, Engineered and Installed to Support Your Structure

                                  We can help solve all your foundation problems. Our broad foundation services allow us to virtually repair any type of foundation problem your home may be experiencing. With foundation underpinning and leveling, concrete slab raising, and helical tiebacks.

                                  We maintain a $1,000,000 per incident and $2,000,000 aggregated general liability insurance coverage for your protection. We also maintain full Worker's Compensation insurance for our valued employees. As required by law, we also maintain a Contractors Bond against our license. Each of these documents are available for your inspection at any time upon request.
