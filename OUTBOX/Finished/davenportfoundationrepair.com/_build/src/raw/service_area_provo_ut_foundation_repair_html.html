                                  Fast, Effective Concrete Leveling & Repair

                                  Concrete used for walkways and driveways looks great when it's new but over time the concrete slab will eventually start to shift as the ground does. The result can look disastrous and can create a dangerous tripping hazards as well. This far cry from the originally smoothly poured concrete often leads to frustration and a hopeless feeling. If you have had a similar experience with your home know that you’re not alone and that you are in the right place! Davenport Foundation Repair has dedicated teams of knowledgeable and experienced professionals that use our patented leveling product simply known as PolyLevel®. This revolutionary concrete lifting & leveling product is capable of lifting, leveling and stabilizing concrete slab in an extremely efficient and cost-effective way. The process for concrete lifting and leveling begins by drilling a few small holes in the slab and then pouring the PolyLevel polymer into the hole. The polymer will mix and expand beneath the slab, raising it to the desired height and stabilizing it in the process. PolyLevel only takes about 15 minutes to dry and the product is environmentally friendly.

                                  Concrete Walkway & Driveway Repair in Provo, UT

                                  Location: Provo, Utah
                                  Services Provided: PolyLevel® Concrete Lifting, NexusPro® Joint Sealant, SealantPro™ Concrete Protection System
                                  Products Used:

                                  • 77.10 lbs of PolyLevel®

                                  • 245 ft of NexusPro®

                                  • 965 sq ft of SealantPro™
                                    Duration: 1 Day
                                    Customer Satisfaction: ✅ Very happy with the results

                                  The Problem

                                  A homeowner in Provo, UT, noticed a growing void beneath their front walkway near the home’s entry. The sidewalk section adjacent to the foundation had visibly settled, creating a potential tripping hazard and an unsightly gap. The hollow space beneath the slab raised concerns about further concrete cracking, water pooling near the foundation, and damage spreading to the connected driveway slab.

                                  Utah’s seasonal moisture changes and soil settlement patterns often lead to these types of voids, especially in walkways that aren’t properly compacted during construction.

                                  The Solution

                                  Our crew performed a full evaluation and recommended a 3-part Concrete Protection System designed to stabilize, protect, and extend the life of the affected concrete areas:

                                  • PolyLevel® Injection:
                                    We used 77.10 lbs of PolyLevel® to fill the void beneath the concrete walkway. This high-density polyurethane foam expands immediately upon injection, lifting and leveling the slab with precision. Unlike mudjacking, PolyLevel doesn’t add weight to the soil, making it ideal for long-term stabilization.

                                  • NexusPro® Joint & Crack Sealant:
                                    Once the slab was leveled, we sealed 245 linear feet of joints and cracks using NexusPro®. This flexible, UV-resistant sealant keeps out moisture and debris—preventing further erosion and joint widening.

                                  • SealantPro™ Protective Coating:
                                    Finally, we applied SealantPro™ across 965 sq ft of concrete surface. This hydrophobic coating bonds with the concrete at a molecular level, offering permanent protection from water, road salts, and stains. It helps reduce long-term wear, especially in Utah’s freeze-thaw cycles.

                                  The Result

                                  In just one day, the walkway and driveway were fully restored—with no demolition, no replacement, and no extended downtime. The void was eliminated, the slab leveled, and the surface sealed and protected.

                                   

                                  The homeowner was thrilled with the outcome, noting the visual improvement and peace of mind knowing their entryway was now safe and secure.
