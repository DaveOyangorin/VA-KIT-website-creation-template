In Willard, UT, a homeowner began noticing unsettling changes around their property. Cracks were forming across their concrete sidewalk, the driveway was showing signs of sinking, and gaps were appearing between the slabs. Initially, they dismissed these as minor cosmetic issues. However, as the weeks passed, the cracks widened, making the surface uneven and hazardous.

Not only did this impact the home’s curb appeal, but it also raised safety concerns—especially during Utah’s harsh winters, where ice and water could seep into the cracks, making the damage even worse. The homeowner worried about tripping hazards, further deterioration, and the potential for costly long-term repairs if left unaddressed.

The Search for a Solution: Why DIY Wasn’t Enough

Determined to fix the problem before it worsened, the homeowner explored DIY options. They considered using standard urethane caulk from a home improvement store, but research revealed that these solutions were temporary at best. Many sealants tend to crack, bubble, and dry out over time, especially when exposed to Utah’s extreme temperature fluctuations.

The homeowner also reached out to a few general contractors, but none offered a long-term solution tailored specifically to concrete settlement and cracking. That’s when they realized they needed a specialist—someone with expertise in foundation and concrete repair.

Why Davenport Foundation Repair?

After searching for a reliable concrete repair company in Willard, UT, the homeowner came across Davenport Foundation Repair. They were impressed by the company’s strong reputation, positive customer reviews, and commitment to using advanced repair techniques.

What stood out the most was Davenport’s free inspection and consultation. Unlike other contractors who provided vague estimates, Davenport sent a knowledgeable specialist to assess the damage in person. The expert explained that the cracks were caused by soil compaction and washout, which had created voids beneath the concrete. If left unchecked, the sinking could continue, leading to even larger fractures and potential structural issues.

The homeowner appreciated the transparency of the process. Rather than pushing a costly, unnecessary replacement, Davenport recommended the NexusPro solution, a cutting-edge silicone-based sealant designed for long-lasting flexibility and durability.

The Repair Process: A Fast and Effective Solution

Davenport’s crew arrived on time and immediately got to work. Their process was thorough yet efficient, ensuring minimal disruption to the homeowner’s daily routine.

Step 1: Crack and Joint Preparation

  • The team first cleaned and prepared the cracked sections, removing debris and smoothing the affected areas.
  • A non-absorbent backer rod filler was placed inside the cracks to prevent moisture penetration and provide a stable base for the sealant.

Step 2: NexusPro Application

  • Using professional-grade equipment, the crew applied NexusPro, a UV-resistant, silicone-based sealant that remains flexible even in extreme temperatures.
  • Unlike traditional polyurethane caulk, NexusPro resists cracking, bubbling, and drying out, ensuring long-term protection.

Step 3: Finishing Touches

  • To create a seamless look, the team applied a thin layer of native sand over the sealant, blending it with the surrounding concrete for a clean, natural finish.
  • The entire process was completed within a few hours, and the NexusPro sealant cured in just 60 minutes—far faster than conventional urethane-based products, which can take up to 72 hours.

The results were immediate and striking. The once-gaping cracks were now seamlessly sealed, eliminating safety hazards and preventing further damage. The homeowner no longer had to worry about water infiltration, shifting slabs, or costly future repairs.

Beyond the structural benefits, the aesthetic improvement was significant. The repaired sidewalk and driveway blended seamlessly with the surrounding concrete, restoring the home’s curb appeal.
