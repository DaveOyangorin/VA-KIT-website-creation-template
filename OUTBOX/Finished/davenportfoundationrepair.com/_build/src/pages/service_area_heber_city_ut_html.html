Concrete Patio and Walkway Lifted and Sealed in Heber City, UT

Location: Heber City, UT
Date of Completion: June 26, 2025
Products Used: PolyLevel® (434.20 lbs), NexusPro® Crack Repair (175 ft), NexusPro® Joint Sealant (279 ft)
Service Provided By: Davenport Foundation Repair
Job Type: Concrete lifting, crack repair, joint sealing, and protection

The Challenge

A homeowner on Winterton Road in Heber City, Utah contacted our team after noticing uneven sections and visible cracks across their backyard patio and walkway. Over time, the concrete had settled due to shifting soils beneath the slab. The resulting trip hazards and widening joints not only disrupted the look of the outdoor space but also created safety concerns for family and visitors.

Utah's freeze-thaw cycles and soil movement are notorious for destabilizing concrete, and this property was no exception. Left unaddressed, the damage would have worsened, eventually requiring complete replacement.

Inspection & Diagnosis

Upon inspection, our specialists confirmed that the concrete slabs had settled unevenly in several spots. Cracks had formed near control joints, and the walkways showed signs of water infiltration due to failing seals. The primary cause was subgrade erosion and voids forming beneath the surface — common in areas with sandy or poorly compacted soils like those found throughout Wasatch County.

The Solution

To restore the patio and walkways without the mess and cost of concrete replacement, we implemented our Concrete Protection System using a two-part approach:

1. PolyLevel® Injection (434.20 lbs)

We injected high-density polyurethane foam beneath the sunken slabs. As the foam expanded, it gently lifted the concrete back to its original level. The quick-curing foam provided immediate support, minimizing disruption to the homeowner.

2. NexusPro® Crack & Joint Sealing

Once the slabs were stable, we sealed 175 feet of cracks and 279 feet of joints using our UV-resistant NexusPro® sealant. This long-lasting product prevents water from penetrating the concrete, stopping erosion at its source and protecting the repair for years to come.

Results & Customer Outcome

The results were dramatic. The lifted slabs are now level, cracks are fully sealed, and the entire area looks clean and professional. More importantly, the homeowner has peace of mind knowing their concrete is protected against future soil movement and weather-related damage.

 

This Heber City concrete repair project is a great example of how our advanced materials and process can preserve outdoor living spaces — quickly, cleanly, and affordably.

Waterproofing a Cabin in Heber City, Utah

Kathy D. was referred to us by her son, who met our Marketing Manager at a local networking meeting. Kathy and her husband own a lovely mountain cabin in Heber City, Utah and were having issues with water coming into the cabin as the snow melt occurred. This year was an exceptionally high pack snow year, so as the temperatures increased, the amount of water from snow melt did too.

After taking out the bottom half of the sheetrock and flooring, they discovered several cracks in the walls and floor of the cabin. Davenport Foundation Repair sent a foundation specialist to see how we could provide permanent, cost-effective solutions.

We were able to remove the existing framing to access the cabin’s foundation, chisel out concrete and install WaterGuard, a sub-floor drainage system that intercepts water at the perimeter of the basement and drains it unseen safely away. In addition, we encapsulated the foundation with Cleanspace vapor barrier, a product that is 100% waterproof, has anti-microbial protection, is durable, washable and prevents humidity from making its way through your concrete walls and into the basement. We also installed a SuperSump pump system to pump water out of the basement to a discharge line that was set up to drain 30 feet away from structure to an out-let area. This process ensures the water will be sent away from the home, preventing water in the basement. Finally we installed an Ice Guard to prevent the sump pumps discharge line from freezing in the wintertime.

There is no doubt that Kathy D. and her family can enjoy their beautiful mountain cabin all year long with the installation of their new waterproofing system from Davenport Foundation Repair. We are happy we could resolve Kathy’s water issues by providing permanent, cost-effective solutions for her cabin in Heber City, Utah.
