Fixing a Cracked Foundation in Clearfield, UT - Davenport Foundation Repair Restores Safety & Stability

The Breaking Point: A Sinking Concrete Slab Causes Concern

A homeowner in Clearfield, UT, noticed a growing problem in their backyard patio—large cracks running across the concrete surface. Over time, sections of the slab had shifted and sunk unevenly, creating trip hazards and allowing water to pool in low spots. During winter, ice formed in the cracks, making the area even more dangerous. Concerned about property value, safety risks, and further damage, the homeowner knew they needed a permanent solution before the problem worsened.

The Search for a Solution: Temporary Fixes Weren’t Enough

At first, the homeowner attempted quick fixes like applying crack sealant and patching the affected areas with concrete filler. However, these temporary repairs failed to address the real issue—the unstable soil beneath the concrete. As the soil continued to shift, the cracks widened, and the slab became even more uneven. Realizing that a more effective solution was necessary, the homeowner decided to seek professional help.

Why Davenport Foundation Repair? A Reputation for Excellence

After researching concrete leveling specialists in Clearfield, UT, the homeowner found Davenport Foundation Repair. The company’s positive reviews, expertise, and transparent approach stood out. After scheduling a free inspection, Davenport’s team assessed the damage and determined that soil erosion had caused voids beneath the concrete, leading to settlement and cracking. The recommended solution? PolyLevel® injection foam, a high-performance material designed to lift and stabilize sunken concrete slabs with precision and durability.

The Repair Process: Lifting and Stabilizing the Concrete

Davenport Foundation Repair’s experts got to work using PolyLevel® injection foam, a cutting-edge technology designed for concrete lifting, leveling, and stabilization. The process involved:

  1. Drilling small holes into the affected concrete slab.
  2. Injecting PolyLevel® foam beneath the slab, filling voids and lifting the sunken sections back into place.
  3. Sealing the holes and cracks to ensure long-lasting stability.
  4. Final inspection to confirm the patio was now level, safe, and secure.

Unlike traditional methods like mudjacking, PolyLevel® is lightweight, waterproof, and sets in just 15 minutes, allowing the homeowner to use their patio the same day.

Transformation: A Safer, More Stable Home

Once the repair was complete, the difference was immediately noticeable. The previously cracked and uneven slab was now level and stable, eliminating tripping hazards and preventing further damage. The homeowner no longer had to worry about water pooling or additional cracks forming. With Davenport Foundation Repair’s expertise, the patio was restored to its original condition, boosting both safety and property value.

The Importance of Acting Early

Ignoring foundation and concrete issues can lead to costly repairs down the road. Soil erosion and concrete settlement will only get worse over time, increasing the risk of severe structural problems. By choosing Davenport Foundation Repair, this homeowner secured a long-term solution that would protect their home for years to come.

When a homeowner in Layton, Utah noticed a large 15-foot crack forming near the pool area in their side yard, they knew something below the surface wasn’t right. The cause turned out to be a slow leak in the pool skimmer, which had gone unnoticed long enough to wash away the supporting soil beneath the concrete slab—creating a substantial void and a growing hazard.

Concerned about safety and further damage, the homeowner contacted Davenport Foundation Repair, a trusted name in Utah for concrete leveling and foundation solutions. On June 20, 2025, our crew, led by Jake and Rafael, arrived on-site to assess the problem and implement a long-lasting fix using the PolyLevel® concrete leveling system.

The Challenge

  • A hidden leak from the skimmer line had caused soil erosion beneath the slab.

  • The result: a long, unsightly crack and a noticeable void threatening the structural integrity of the concrete.

  • Due to the proximity to the pool, any repair needed to be both precise and water-resistant.

The Solution

Jake and Rafael carefully injected 238 pounds of PolyLevel® beneath the concrete slab. PolyLevel is a high-density polyurethane foam that expands beneath the surface, filling voids and lifting the slab back to its proper level. This minimally invasive process allowed the crew to:

  • Accurately lift the slab back into place

  • Eliminate the underlying void

  • Prevent future settling in that area

The Result

The slab was restored to a level position, and more importantly, the void was completely filled—ensuring long-term support and stability. The process was fast, clean, and effective.
