Why Protect Your Concrete?

Concrete sealant keeps water, oil, and gasoline from penetrating the slab.

Moisture is concrete's strongest enemy. It seeps into the pores of untreated concrete, and when temperatures drop, especially during freeze-thaw cycles, that trapped moisture expands as it turns to ice. This expansion destroys the pores and weakens the concrete.

Without protection, your concrete will inevitably discolor, flake and crack. This not only reduces the curb appeal - and value - of your home, it can also be dangerous for your family and guests.
