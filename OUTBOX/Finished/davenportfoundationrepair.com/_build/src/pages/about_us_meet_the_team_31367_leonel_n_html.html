Title: Co-Foreman

Leonel has been a cornerstone of our team for just over 20 years, demonstrating exceptional dedication and expertise in his role. His extensive experience and commitment to excellence have made him a key contributor to the company's growth and success. Over the past two decades, Leonel has been a reliable and valued team member, known for his strong work ethic, positive attitude, and ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues.

 

Beyond his professional achievements, Leonel is a devoted family man. He is married with three children, and his family remains his greatest source of motivation and inspiration. When he's not at work, Leonel enjoys unwinding with classic rock music, indulging in his favorite cuisine—tacos—and spending quality time watching his favorite film, Titanic.

 

Leonel is also known for his vibrant personality and hidden talent as a skilled dancer, often surprising those around him with his abilities. His love for celebrating life is reflected in his favorite holiday, the 4th of July, where he enjoys the festivities with family and friends.

 

In his downtime, Leonel loves to escape to Bear Lake, his favorite vacation destination, where he can relax and recharge. His well-rounded nature, both at work and in his personal life, has made Leonel not just a respected colleague but also a cherished member of the company community.
