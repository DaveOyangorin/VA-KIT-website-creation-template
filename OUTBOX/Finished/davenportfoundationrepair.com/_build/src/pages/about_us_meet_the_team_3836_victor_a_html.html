Title: Field Supervisor
Hometown: Salt Lake City, Utah

Victor has been an invaluable part of our team for over 20 years, bringing his passion and dedication to every project. An avid fan of country music, Victor's love for this genre adds a unique touch to his work environment. His favorite movie, "Nacho Libre," reflects his fun-loving personality, and he enjoys watching boxing in his free time.

 

Hailing from a small town in Mexico, Victor’s early dreams included becoming a rancher, and though his career path took a different turn, his aspirations and work ethic remain strong. Victor's dream job is to be a lawyer, showcasing his ambition and commitment to lifelong learning.

 

A devoted family man, Victor cherishes every opportunity to visit his loved ones and maintain strong family connections. His deep-rooted values and rich personal background make him a cherished member of our team.
