Title: Warehouse Foreman/Shop Technician
Hometown: Puerto Vallarta, Mexico

Rosalio has been an invaluable part of our team as the Warehouse Foreman for just over eight months. Before joining us, he began his professional journey as a mechanic, bringing a wealth of hands-on experience and practical skills to his current role.

 

Rosalio is married with two children, and he enjoys a wide variety of music genres, including Latino music, hip-hop, rock, and even country. His favorite food is tacos, and he has a hidden talent for making a delicious pasta. An avid fan of the Fast and Furious movie franchise, Rosalio also loves soccer, with his favorite teams being FC Barcelona and Real Salt Lake.

 

Originally from Puerto Vallarta, Mexico, Rosalio now shares his home with his wife, kids, and their dog, Conelo. In his leisure time, he enjoys spending time with his family and pursuing his interests in music and soccer.

 

Rosalio is motivated by a positive work environment and setting achievable goals. His dream job is to become an architect, a goal he pursues with the same dedication and passion he brings to his current role. His favorite holiday is Christmas, and his perfect day would be spent in 80-degree weather, relaxing by the beach.

 

We are fortunate to have Rosalio on our team and look forward to his continued contributions and growth within the company.
