Basement Sump Pump Installation - Salt Lake City, UT

Before After

This before-and-after photo set showcases a sump pump installation completed in the basement cold storage area of a Salt Lake City home. The space showed signs of moisture intrusion and water collection along the basement floor, increasing the risk of water damage, mold growth, and foundation issues.

 

A professionally installed sump pump system was added to capture and redirect groundwater away from the foundation, helping keep the basement dry and protected year-round. This solution improves moisture control in below-grade spaces, safeguards stored items, and supports long-term foundation health for homes in the Salt Lake City area.

Before After

Your foundation repair problem will usually be noticed by doors or windows that no longer open and close, or drywall cracks. Incomplete or failed waterproofing around and under basement  walls and floors is a significant cause.
