We are excited to support Candy Cane Corner!

November is the month for gratitude. It is a time to focus about what is good about life and realize that no matter what, good things are happening. Sometimes helping those around us can improve our perspective. Giving back reaps rewards that are immeasurable. We believe in teamwork, honesty, integrity and dependability—we base our business on these principles.

This year our company has participated in some great community projects. We have helped local families have access to food, supported breast cancer research and assisted in making safer play areas for children. We are excited to support Sub for Santa during the upcoming holiday season.

We pride ourselves on making connections to our customers and our community. Our theme for this year has been #BETHEGOOD. Our philosophy is that we can do good things wherever we go---whether that means going the extra mile for a customer, helping our co-worker or volunteering in our communities, we can make an impact.

The team at Davenport Foundation Repair is amazing. We have been blessed to surround ourselves with talented, dependable, hardworking people. Each member of Team Davenport lends their strengths to our company on a daily basis, creating a great place to work. The enthusiasm each team member has for their job translates into our work and to our customers from Logan, UT to Salt Lake City, UT to St. George, UT.

As we continue to grow and move forward, we are looking for a few new people to join our outside sales and installation teams.  Please share these posts for Foundation Specialist or Laborer or our website with those you know who may be looking for a great job with a great company.

We look forward to a new year building and repairing literal foundations, and contribute to many community causes that strengthen metaphorical foundations as well. Most of all we are grateful to be serving our customers and the opportunity we have to provide them to solutions for their homes.
