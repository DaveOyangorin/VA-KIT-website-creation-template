Title: Regional Director of Customer Care
Hometown: Lakewood, WA

Keri Wright serves as the Regional Director of Customer Care at Davenport Foundation Repair, where she oversees our comprehensive customer service operations with strategic vision and client-focused leadership. Originally from Lakewood, Washington, Keri brings a wealth of experience to our team, having recognized the opportunity to support a growing organization in an industry that provides essential foundation services to Utah homeowners.

In her director role, Keri leads a dedicated team of customer care specialists who serve as the first point of contact for homeowners experiencing foundation concerns. She has implemented systematic approaches to ensure every client interaction reflects our commitment to excellence, from initial consultation through project completion and follow-up.

Keri's responsibilities include:

-Developing and maintaining customer service protocols that prioritize homeowner satisfaction
-Training team members on effective communication regarding complex foundation issues
-Creating streamlined processes for homeowner education about structural solutions
-Establishing quality assurance measures that maintain our reputation for excellence
-Building relationships with homeowners to ensure long-term trust and confidence

Prior to joining Davenport Foundation Repair, Keri honed her customer care expertise at Renewal by Andersen in Oregon, where she gained valuable insight into homeowner needs and concerns regarding structural home improvements. This background has positioned her perfectly to understand the critical nature of foundation repairs and the importance of clear communication with homeowners facing these challenges.


When not ensuring Utah homeowners receive exceptional service, Keri enjoys reading and watching lacrosse. As a licensed boater, she finds balance by spending time on the water—a passion that reflects her appreciation for both recreation and technical skill.


Keri's leadership in customer care reinforces our mission at Davenport Foundation Repair: protecting your home's foundation with expert guidance and personalized attention. Her commitment to excellence ensures that every homeowner receives the quality support they deserve when addressing their foundation concerns.
