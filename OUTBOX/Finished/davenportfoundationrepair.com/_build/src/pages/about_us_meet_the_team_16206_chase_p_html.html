Title: Foreman
Hometown: Grand Rapids, MI

Chase has been a dedicated member of our team for over 9 years, bringing a wealth of experience and enthusiasm to his role. His career began at the age of 14 when he started as a busser at IHop, laying the foundation for his impressive journey in the workforce.

 

Originally from Grand Rapids, MI, Chase has a deep connection to his roots and values family greatly. He is married with three children—two girls and a boy—who keep him busy and motivated. Outside of work, Chase is passionate about music, enjoying a diverse range of genres including country, rap, and rock. His hidden talent is writing and rapping his own music, showcasing his creativity and dedication to his craft.

 

Chase has a love for good food, with steak and tacos being his top choices. His favorite movie is "Edge of Tomorrow," reflecting his interest in action and science fiction. He also has a soft spot for his two cats and is an avid fan of the Detroit Lions.

 

When it comes to his dream job, Chase aspires to be involved in the music industry, a field where he feels he could truly thrive. For relaxation and enjoyment, he loves vacationing at Lake Mohave, which offers a perfect escape and a chance to recharge.
