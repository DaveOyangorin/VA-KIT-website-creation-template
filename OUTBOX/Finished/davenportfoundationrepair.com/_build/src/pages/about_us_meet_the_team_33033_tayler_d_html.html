Title: Permit/ Settlement Logistics Coordinator

Tayler has been a dedicated part of our team since 2020. Growing up in West Jordan, Tayler developed a deep love for animals and once aspired to be a veterinarian working with gorillas. This passion for animal care is evident in her hidden talent as an animal whisperer, a skill she demonstrates with her unique collection of six spiders and one scorpion.

Outside of work, Tayler is an avid music listener with a diverse taste that includes artists like Taylor Swift, Post Malone, and Lana Del Rey. She also has a strong appreciation for Mexican cuisine, with burritos being her favorite dish.

Tayler's first job was at the mall, and since then, she has grown significantly in her career, motivated by the vibrant culture at her workplace. Her love for Halloween reflects her playful and creative spirit. When she’s not at work, she enjoys watching her favorite movie, "My Best Friend’s Wedding."

Tayler brings enthusiasm, dedication, and a unique perspective to her role, making her an invaluable member of our team.
