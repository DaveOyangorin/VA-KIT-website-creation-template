Fast, Effective Concrete Leveling & Repair

Concrete used for walkways and driveways looks great when it's new but over time the concrete slab will eventually start to shift as the ground does. The result can look disastrous and can create a dangerous tripping hazards as well. This far cry from the originally smoothly poured concrete often leads to frustration and a hopeless feeling. If you have had a similar experience with your home know that you’re not alone and that you are in the right place! Davenport Foundation Repair has dedicated teams of knowledgeable and experienced professionals that use our patented leveling product simply known as PolyLevel®. This revolutionary concrete lifting & leveling product is capable of lifting, leveling and stabilizing concrete slab in an extremely efficient and cost-effective way. The process for concrete lifting and leveling begins by drilling a few small holes in the slab and then pouring the PolyLevel polymer into the hole. The polymer will mix and expand beneath the slab, raising it to the desired height and stabilizing it in the process. PolyLevel only takes about 15 minutes to dry and the product is environmentally friendly.

Driveway Repair & Concrete Stabilization in Saratoga Springs, UT

Job Story Featuring PolyLEVEL® and NexusPro® | 3-Day Project

When a homeowner in Saratoga Springs, Utah noticed cracking, surface spalling, and concrete settling near his RV pad, he knew it was time to call in professionals. The driveway not only showed visible wear but was starting to present safety concerns. He reached out to Davenport Foundation Repair with a clear goal: stop the damage from spreading and protect the concrete for the long haul.

The Problem: Spalling, Settlement, and a Sinking RV Pad

The homeowner reported signs of spalling—a common issue in Utah where freeze-thaw cycles and moisture infiltration cause the surface of the concrete to flake or peel away. Additionally, he was concerned about settling under the RV pad in the backyard, which had created uneven surfaces and small trip hazards.

Like many properties in Saratoga Springs, this home is built on soil that can shift over time, especially during dry summers or after heavy rainfall. The combination of soil movement and exposure to the elements had led to uneven concrete slabs and failing caulking between joints. Left unaddressed, the damage could worsen, leading to more costly repairs down the line.

Our Solution: PolyLEVEL® Injection & NexusPro® Joint Sealant

Davenport Foundation Repair scheduled a 3-day job to restore the driveway and RV pad. The repair process was carefully planned in phases to ensure each step provided long-term value:

Step 1: PolyLEVEL® Injection

We began by lifting and leveling the sunken concrete slabs using our high-precision PolyLEVEL® system. This two-part expanding polyurethane foam is injected beneath the concrete, gently raising the slabs back to their original position. Unlike traditional mudjacking, PolyLEVEL® is lightweight, cures quickly, and won’t wash out or add stress to the soil.

This step was critical for the RV pad, which had sunk due to poor soil compaction. PolyLEVEL® filled the voids beneath the concrete and reestablished a strong, stable base—perfect for handling heavy loads like an RV.

Step 2: Caulk Removal

Next, we removed the old, deteriorating caulk from the driveway joints and cracks. A clean surface is essential for a proper seal, so our team took extra care during this step to avoid leaving behind debris or residue.

Step 3: NexusPro® Crack and Joint Repair

Finally, we applied NexusPro®, a flexible, UV-resistant joint sealant developed to handle extreme temperature swings common in Utah’s climate. NexusPro® bonds tightly to concrete and remains flexible over time, which helps prevent water from entering the joints and causing further erosion or spalling.

The sealant was carefully smoothed for a clean, professional finish that not only looked great but offered long-term waterproofing protection.

The Outcome: Safe, Sealed, and Stable Concrete

The transformation was dramatic. What began as a spalling, uneven driveway is now a clean, level, and sealed surface that’s ready to handle daily use—and RV parking—with confidence.

The homeowner was especially pleased that the repair was minimally disruptive and completed in just three days. With PolyLEVEL® lifting the slabs and NexusPro® sealing the cracks, this Saratoga Springs home is now protected from further concrete damage, soil movement, and seasonal moisture changes.

Why It Matters: Protecting Concrete in Utah’s Climate

In Saratoga Springs and across Utah County, homeowners face unique challenges when it comes to concrete maintenance. The dry, expansive soils combined with freezing winters and hot, dry summers make it critical to maintain a stable foundation and prevent water infiltration. Products like PolyLEVEL® and NexusPro® are specifically designed for these conditions, helping extend the life of your concrete surfaces and preventing costly replacements.
