Foundation Settlement Stabilized with 7 Push Piers in Grantsville, UT

When cracks begin appearing in the exterior brick of a home, the damage may be more than a masonry problem. For a homeowner in Grantsville, Utah, significant vertical cracking and separation in the brick exterior were visible signs that the home was experiencing foundation movement.

The homeowner turned to Davenport Foundation Repair to determine what was happening beneath the home and find a solution for the settlement.

Signs of Foundation Settlement at the Grantsville Home

One of the most noticeable symptoms was a vertical crack running through several courses of exterior brick near a window. In some areas, the movement had created visible gaps between sections of the masonry.

These types of cracks can develop as portions of a foundation settle at different rates. As the foundation moves, the materials above it can be subjected to stress, resulting in cracks and separation in brick, mortar, drywall, and other parts of the home.

Rather than simply addressing the visible cracks, the Davenport Foundation Repair team evaluated the structure to determine where foundation stabilization was needed.

Identifying the Area That Needed Stabilization

The project plan documented elevation differences throughout the affected portion of the home and identified the section of the foundation requiring stabilization.

Based on the findings, a system of seven push piers was planned along the affected foundation walls. The piers were strategically positioned to support the portion of the structure experiencing settlement.

This approach addresses the underlying foundation problem instead of treating cracking as an isolated cosmetic issue.

Installing 7 Push Piers to Stabilize the Foundation

To install the push pier system, the Davenport Foundation Repair crew excavated at each designated pier location to expose the home's foundation footing.

Heavy-duty brackets were then secured at the foundation. Steel push pier sections were hydraulically advanced into the ground until they reached competent load-bearing material capable of supporting the structure.

Once the required resistance was achieved, the seven-pier system could be used to transfer the affected portion of the home's structural load away from the weaker soils immediately beneath the foundation and onto more reliable bearing material below.

After stabilization was completed, the excavated areas could be backfilled and the property restored.

Addressing the Cause Behind the Cracks

The before-and-after photos from this Grantsville project demonstrate why homeowners should pay attention when significant cracks begin developing in brick or masonry.

A crack itself may be the most obvious symptom, but repairing the masonry alone does not necessarily address why the crack developed. When foundation settlement is responsible, determining the cause of the movement and stabilizing the affected foundation can be an important part of protecting the home.

With seven push piers installed, the affected section of this Grantsville home received the structural stabilization needed to address the underlying foundation settlement.

Sunken Slab Leads to Water in Grantsville, UT Basement

Jeff H. had water leaking into his home’s basement due to an outside cement slab that had cracked and sunk up to six inches in some areas. There was also a large void under the back patio and by the garage door which caused the cement slab to become unstable and sink.

Davenport Foundation Repair was able to offer a cost-effective, permanent solution with PolyLevel injection foam. 241 pounds of PolyLevel was pumped underneath the cement slabs, filling the void and closing the entry points for the water that was coming into the basement.

Jeff H. and his family are now enjoying a dry basement. In addition the PolyLevel injection foam leveled and stabilized the home’s backyard patio so the family can enjoy a safe time outside at their home in Grantsville, UT.
