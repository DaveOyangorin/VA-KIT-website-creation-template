Stabilizing a Settling Brick Home in Salt Lake City, UT

Problem:
This red brick home in Salt Lake City had begun to show serious signs of foundation settlement. The homeowner reported visible cracks in the foundation, uneven floors, and sticking windows—all indicators of structural movement. Because the home included a basement, the risks of further damage and water intrusion were even more concerning.

Solution:
Our team conducted a full inspection and confirmed that the foundation was shifting due to unstable soil conditions beneath the structure. To stabilize the home and stop further movement, we installed 10 Push Piers beneath the foundation footing. The crew completed 6 excavations up to 5 feet deep to expose and access the foundation. Additionally, 2 areas of concrete were removed and replaced (R&R) to allow for proper installation and finish work.

The project was completed over the course of three days, from July 14th to July 16th. Push Piers were carefully installed, driven deep into stable load-bearing soil, and used to lift and permanently support the settling foundation.

 

Result:
The foundation was successfully stabilized, eliminating the risk of future settlement and restoring peace of mind for the homeowner. With the structure now supported by steel piers, the cracks are no longer progressing, and the home is protected against further damage. This Salt Lake City home is now safer, stronger, and better equipped to handle Utah’s shifting soil conditions.
