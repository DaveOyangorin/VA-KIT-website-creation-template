Sump Pump Installation in Riverton, UT

Homeowner Challenge

A homeowner in Riverton, Utah noticed persistent dampness and water buildup in one corner of their basement—an area that previously housed a built-in floor safe. Over time, this section of the concrete slab had become a low point where moisture collected, creating an ideal location for a sump pump installation. The homeowner wanted a permanent waterproofing solution to protect their finished basement and prevent long-term foundation damage.

Inspection and Plan

After a detailed inspection, our team identified that the safest and most efficient way to manage groundwater in this basement was to install a sump pump system directly where the old safe was located. The existing pit offered a natural recess for housing the sump basin, minimizing disruption to the surrounding floor and reducing excavation time.

The plan included:

  • Removing the old safe and preparing the concrete floor.

  • Installing a high-performance sump pump with a sealed lid for safety and odor control.

  • Connecting discharge lines to direct water away from the home’s foundation.

  • Finishing with a clean concrete pour for a seamless, professional look.

Installation Process

Our crew began by carefully removing the concrete around the safe and extracting it from the floor cavity. Once cleared, we excavated the area to fit the sump pump basin. After placing and securing the pump, we connected the discharge system and electrical components to ensure reliable operation. Finally, the surrounding area was sealed with fresh concrete for a watertight and polished finish.

Results

The result is a fully functional sump pump system that automatically removes groundwater and protects the basement from flooding and moisture damage. What was once an unused, problematic spot in the floor is now a crucial line of defense against water intrusion.

 

With the new system in place, the homeowner can enjoy peace of mind knowing their basement is dry, safe, and structurally protected — especially during Utah’s heavy rain and snowmelt seasons.

Concrete Patio Repair in Riverton, UT A One-Day Concrete Leveling Project Using PolyLevel®

A homeowner in Riverton, Utah reached out to our team after noticing their backyard patio had developed a significant slope. The once-flat surface had settled unevenly, leaving noticeable gaps between slabs and creating a visible trip hazard. Not only did it become a safety concern, but the sloping patio was also becoming an eyesore that detracted from the home’s outdoor appeal.

The Problem: Sunken Patio Slabs

Over time, six concrete slab sections that made up the patio had gradually shifted and settled. Utah’s expansive clay soils—common in Riverton and throughout Salt Lake County—can shrink and swell due to moisture changes, often causing slabs to sink. The unlevel concrete surface created an unsafe and unwelcoming outdoor space, especially near the transition points between the home and the yard.

The Solution: Precision Concrete Leveling with PolyLevel®

To fix the issue, our crew used PolyLevel®, a high-density polyurethane foam injection system designed specifically for lifting and stabilizing sunken concrete. A total of 233 pounds of PolyLevel was injected beneath the slabs to gently raise and re-level the entire patio surface. Because of PolyLevel’s fast curing time and lightweight properties, it provided a stable, long-lasting solution without adding stress to the underlying soils.

  • Total slabs lifted: 6

  • Product used: PolyLevel® (233 lbs)

  • Project duration: 1 day

  • Date of repair: July 21, 2025

The Result: Safe, Stable, and Level

By the end of the day, the patio was fully leveled—restoring both safety and visual appeal. The trip hazards were eliminated, making the area safe for foot traffic once again. This concrete leveling job not only resolved a structural concern but also significantly improved the patio's functionality for everyday use, entertaining, and outdoor living.

Why This Matters for Homeowners in Riverton

In areas like Riverton, UT, shifting soils and inconsistent moisture levels can make concrete repair a recurring necessity. Using modern solutions like PolyLevel allows homeowners to fix problems quickly and affordably—without the mess or expense of full replacement. If you’re dealing with sinking slabs, uneven patios, or cracking sidewalks, concrete patio repair and leveling is a smart investment in your home’s safety and value.

Waterproofing an Unfinished Basement in Herriman, Utah

In the quiet, fast-growing suburb of Herriman, Utah, one homeowner faced a frustrating and potentially costly problem: water was leaking into their unfinished basement. With exposed framing and bare concrete floors, the space was vulnerable to moisture damage—and without a solution, that dampness could lead to mold, structural issues, and unusable living space.

Fortunately, the homeowner reached out to us before the problem worsened. Our expert crew, led by Tony Johnston, was dispatched to evaluate and repair the issue. After a thorough inspection on June 27th, 2025, the team determined that groundwater was intruding at the base of the foundation walls—a common issue in basements throughout Utah, particularly during the wet season or after heavy irrigation.

Identifying the Problem

The initial photos taken by the homeowner showed clear signs of moisture along the base of the wall—insulation showing signs of dampness and personal items pushed aside in an effort to protect them from water. With the space unfinished, there was a clear opportunity to waterproof properly before drywall or flooring was ever added.

Tony and the team identified the entry points along the perimeter of the foundation, particularly where hydrostatic pressure was pushing water through cold joints and potential wall cracks. This type of issue is often worsened by poor exterior drainage, making it essential to address both interior and exterior factors.

Custom Waterproofing Solution

To protect the basement from future water intrusion, Tony implemented a comprehensive waterproofing system using industry-leading products from Supportworks. Here's what the solution included:

1. CleanSpace Wall System

A durable, vapor-barrier liner was installed along the basement walls. This white liner, known as CleanSpace, seals out water vapor and helps direct any wall seepage down into the drainage system below. It also improves the overall appearance of the basement, providing a clean, bright surface ready for future finishing.

2. WaterGuard Interior Drain System

Beneath the perimeter of the concrete slab, our crew installed the WaterGuard drainage channel—a patented system designed to capture any water that seeps through walls or the cove joint and redirect it to a sump pump. Unlike traditional French drains, WaterGuard sits atop the footing to prevent clogging and ensures a long-lasting, effective barrier against basement flooding.

3. SuperSump Pump System

Collected water is then expelled by the SuperSump, a powerful and reliable sump pump system built to handle large volumes of water with efficiency. The unit includes an airtight lid (preventing odors and evaporation), a cast-iron pump, and a built-in alarm to alert the homeowner of high water levels.

4. Buried Downspout Extensions

To protect the foundation from exterior water sources, we also installed buried downspout extensions. These pipes channel roof runoff far away from the home, preventing re-saturation of the soil next to the basement walls. It’s a crucial step many homeowners overlook, but it significantly reduces the chance of recurring leaks.

Before & After: A Big Transformation

The transformation of the space was immediate and visible. The “before” photo shows an exposed wall with hanging insulation, cluttered storage, and visible moisture risk. The “after” photo reveals a clean, sealed, and protected wall lined with CleanSpace and a freshly poured concrete trench covering the newly installed WaterGuard system.

This basement is now fully prepared for finishing—or for safe, dry storage. Most importantly, the homeowner no longer needs to worry about water intrusion.

Why Waterproofing Matters in Herriman, UT

Homes in Herriman face unique challenges due to expansive soils, seasonal storms, and the rapid pace of new construction. Many homes are built with unfinished basements, which are prone to leaks at cold joints, wall cracks, and pipe penetrations.

By installing a full waterproofing system with CleanSpace, WaterGuard, a SuperSump, and exterior drainage improvements, homeowners are investing in long-term peace of mind. Whether the basement is used for storage, a home gym, or future living space, it deserves the same protection as the rest of the home.

Trust Your Basement to the Experts

At the end of the day, no homeowner should feel helpless against water intrusion. Tony Johnston and our team have the training, tools, and materials to restore and protect any basement in Utah.

 

If you're noticing water in your basement—or simply want to protect your space before finishing it—contact us today for a free inspection. We'll provide a custom solution tailored to your home, using proven systems and expert craftsmanship.
