Cement lifting, also called concrete lifting or slab jacking, is a cost-effective solution to restore sunken or uneven concrete without full replacement. In Utah, where freeze-thaw cycles and expansive clay soils are common, this service can protect your home, improve safety, and boost curb appeal—all in one simple procedure.

Why Does Cement Settle in Utah?

Utah’s soil and climate conditions are unique. The most common causes of cement settlement here include:

  • Clay-Rich Soil: Much of Utah, especially along the Wasatch Front, has expansive soils that swell when wet and shrink when dry. This expansion and contraction creates voids under your concrete, leading to uneven surfaces.

  • Freeze-Thaw Cycles: Utah winters can wreak havoc on your concrete. When water gets under a slab and freezes, it expands. As it melts, the ground can settle or shift—causing cracks and misalignment.

  • Poor Compaction or Erosion: If the soil under your concrete wasn't properly compacted during construction or has washed away over time due to rain or irrigation, the concrete slab above may sink.

These factors make cement lifting in Utah not just a convenience, but a necessity for maintaining your home.

What Is Cement Lifting?

Cement lifting is the process of raising a sunken concrete slab back to its original position. Modern technology allows for precise lifting using PolyLevel®, a high-density polyurethane foam. This minimally invasive method replaces outdated techniques like mudjacking and comes with longer-lasting results.

How PolyLevel Works:

  1. Assessment: A technician evaluates your concrete and underlying soil.

  2. Drill & Inject: Small holes (about the size of a dime) are drilled into the slab.

  3. Foam Injection: PolyLevel foam is injected beneath the concrete. As it expands, it lifts the slab evenly.

  4. Seal & Clean: Injection holes are patched, and the area is cleaned—usually within a few hours.

The entire process is fast, clean, and environmentally friendly. It’s ideal for driveways, patios, pool decks, garage floors, sidewalks, and steps.

Benefits of Cement Lifting in Utah

Choosing to lift instead of replace your concrete comes with several advantages:

  • Save Time & Money: Lifting is typically 30–50% cheaper than replacement and can be done in a day.

  • Minimally Invasive: No need to tear out old concrete or wait days for curing.

  • Long-Lasting Results: PolyLevel is water-resistant, durable, and won’t erode like traditional mudjacking.

  • Preserves Landscaping: Because it's a targeted solution, your yard, plants, and surrounding features remain undisturbed.

  • Boosts Safety: Eliminates trip hazards and improves drainage immediately.

Especially in Utah, where weather extremes can speed up concrete damage, cement lifting is a smart preventative investment.
