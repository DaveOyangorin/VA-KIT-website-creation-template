Everett Jake has been a dedicated member of our team for over 10 years. With a strong background in construction, Jake has made significant contributions to our projects, reflecting his extensive experience and commitment to the industry.

Married and a proud father of two daughters, Jake balances his professional responsibilities with a fulfilling family life. His passion for music includes country, rock, and reggae, while his culinary preferences lean towards beef, pork, and chicken.

Jake's favorite movie is "Gladiator," and he enjoys the company of his two pets, Lucky and Skoal. As a fan of sports, he supports the Utah Jazz and the Denver Broncos with enthusiasm.

Jake's dream job would be to own his own business, a testament to his entrepreneurial spirit. For vacations, he favors destinations in Arizona or Alaska, reflecting his love for diverse landscapes and adventures.
