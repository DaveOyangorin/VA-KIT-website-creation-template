We Know You Have Choices...So Choose The Best

Denise Hassett · October 17, 2017

Davenport Foundation Repair is built upon integrity, honesty and responsibility. For the last 35 years we have worked diligently to serve our customers to the best of our ability. We use patented products that have proven to provide permanent, cost-effective solutions for home and business owners across the state.

Davenport Foundation Repair, began as a footings and foundation business in 1976 known as Davenport Construction.  While Davenport Construction still thrives as new foundation business, Davenport Foundation Repair opened in 1982 and has a reputation of the elite foundation repair, concrete lifting and basement waterproofing company throughout Utah, and parts of Idaho and Wyoming.

President and owner Ron Davenport is recognized as an authority in the application and installation of foundation repair systems state wide. His direction and forward-thinking approach helps keep Davenport Foundation Repair at the cutting edge of the industry. Being on the cutting edge of technology allows us to serve our customers better. One example of this is our revolutionary 3/8 inch PolyLevel injection ports. These are the ports we use that attach to our pumping gun, pumping the foam under concrete, allowing it to lift. We are the only dealer who has this small of a port, which means the holes drilled into the concrete are much less noticeable than other dealers who use much larger ports.

We train our crews to provide the best service possible to our customers. This means they explain, ask questions and communicate with our customers so that our customers understand the process needed to repair their home. We bring integrity to structures that have been compromised. We specialize in new home and commercial footings and foundations with our guaranteed method for stabilizing a sinking or settling foundation. We offer warranties on many of our products because we want our customers to know we will take care of them and ensure the job and materials are superior.

We invest in our community. We support many great causes including breast cancer awareness, families and children in need, local education and Sub for Santa projects. Each of our staff is involved in our community commitment. This gives us an opportunity to work toward a common goal, and build relationships that foster teamwork. We are grateful to be part of an amazing group of staff, and a part of a generous community.

So why choose Davenport? We care about our community and our customers. We are committed to providing real solutions to our customers. We stand being our work and our products. Don't take our word for it--we are also Gephardt Approved and have A ratings on Angie's List, BBB and Home Advisor. We offer free estimates, so call us today and find out why Davenport Foundation Repair is

your

local expert.
