Its basements range from 1950s spaces with low ceilings to hillside walk-outs, and we finish basements across 84010.

Serving all of Bountiful: ZIP codes 84010, 84011.

Building in Bountiful

Bountiful’s housing tells its age in the basements. Mid-century homes, and there are a lot of them here, often have 7 to 7.5 foot ceilings, older wiring, and finishes from a previous generation that need to come out before anything new goes in. The east bench is the other side of the story: sloped lots with walk-out potential and real views. We also plan around mature trees on older lots when an egress window means digging near established roots. Bountiful is the south end of our service area, and we build the drive into our scheduling rather than pretending it isn’t there.

Permits and inspections in Bountiful

Bountiful City requires a building permit for basement finishing, and bedrooms need code-compliant egress windows. Older homes sometimes surface extra items at inspection, like outdated wiring that has to be corrected, and we price and handle that work rather than leaving it between you and the inspector.

Common questions

01Our Bountiful home is from the 1950s with a low basement ceiling. Is finishing worth it?

Usually yes, with honest expectations. A 7 to 7.5 foot ceiling can still make a comfortable family room or office if the lighting and soffits are planned well. We’ll tell you during the estimate what the space can realistically become and what it can’t.

02Can you finish a walk-out basement on the Bountiful bench?

Yes. Bench lots in east Bountiful often have walk-out or daylight basements, and the natural light makes them some of the best finished spaces in the county. The downhill wall gets treated like main-floor construction, with insulation and windows to match.

03Do you really serve Bountiful from Clearfield?

Yes. Bountiful is about 25 minutes from our shop down I-15, the south end of our service area. We schedule Bountiful projects with that drive built in, so crews arrive when we say they will and the timeline you get in writing is the real one.

04Do I need a permit to finish my basement in Bountiful?

Yes. Bountiful City requires a building permit, and bedrooms need egress windows that meet code. In older homes, inspections sometimes surface wiring that has to be brought up to code, and we handle that as part of the job.

05How much does it cost to finish a basement in Bountiful?

Older homes that need tear-out or wiring updates land differently than a newer open basement, so the honest answer comes from measuring your space. Estimates are free, written, and fixed before work starts. See typical basement finishing costs in Utah
