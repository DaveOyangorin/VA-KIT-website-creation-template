Common questions

01Do you take small carpentry jobs?

Yes. Not every job is a full basement. We hang single doors, replace trim in one room, and build one-off built-ins. Small jobs get slotted between bigger projects, so tell us your timeline and we’ll be straight about ours.

02Can you match the existing trim in my house?

Usually, yes. Most trim profiles in Davis and Weber County homes are still milled today, or close enough to blend after caulk and paint. If your profile is discontinued, we’ll show you the closest match before committing.

03Are built-ins painted on site or pre-finished?

Built in place, then caulked, primed, and painted on site. That’s how the piece ends up reading as part of the wall instead of furniture pushed against it. You pick the color, and we spray or brush depending on the finish.
