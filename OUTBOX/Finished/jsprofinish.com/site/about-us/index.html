Pablo J. Padilla, owner

Pablo started JS ProFinish in Clearfield in 2019 and still runs the crew on every job. The company holds Utah contractor license #11152584-5501 for carpentry, flooring, drywall, paint, and plastering, which you can verify on the Utah DOPL licensee lookup.

JS ProFinish is a local, owner-operated shop. Not a franchise, not a lead-reselling service. When you call, you talk to the people who will actually be in your basement. When we give you a price, it’s the person accountable for the work standing behind it.

Basement specialists, licensed tradesmen

Basement finishing is what we’re known for. It’s most of what we do and what we’ve built our reputation on. Behind that specialty is a Utah contractor licensed in carpentry, flooring, drywall, paint, and plastering. Those are our own trades, not subcontracted work. That’s why one crew can take your basement from bare studs to finished space under one contract, and why we can also help with projects beyond the basement.

Where we’re headed

We want to be the first name people think of when they need basement work in Northern Utah. Not because we’re the biggest, but because we do honest work at fair prices. We build our reputation one basement at a time.
