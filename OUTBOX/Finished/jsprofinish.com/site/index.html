Common questions

Good to know.

01How much does it cost to finish a basement?

It depends on the size of your basement and what you want in it. A simple rec room costs a lot less than a full suite with a bathroom. We don’t do ballpark guesses. We come out, measure, and give you a written estimate for free, with the price locked in before work starts. See typical basement finishing costs in Utah.

02How long does a basement finish take?

Timeline depends on the scope of the project, and we’ll give you an honest schedule with your estimate before any work begins. Once we start, our crew shows up and keeps the project moving until it’s done.

03Do you handle permits and inspections?

Yes. We handle the necessary permits and inspections so your finished basement meets local building codes in Davis and Weber Counties.

04Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. JS ProFinish is a licensed and insured Utah contractor (UT Lic. #11152584-5501), specializing in basement finishing throughout Davis and Weber Counties. You can verify our license on the Utah DOPL licensee lookup.
