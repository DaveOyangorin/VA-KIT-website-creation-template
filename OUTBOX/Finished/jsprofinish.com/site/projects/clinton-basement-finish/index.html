About this job

The house is a few minutes from our Clearfield shop. The finished lower level runs a two-tone scheme: warm gray walls against white ceilings, trim, and doors, with matte black hardware on every door. The flooring in the finished photos was installed by others, so we don’t take credit for it.

Every photo on this page was taken on site by our crew. Nothing is staged and nothing is a rendering.

What we did

  1. Framed the family room, bedroom, closets, laundry, and bathroom walls
  2. Drywall hung on walls and ceilings, including the stairwell and under-stair storage
  3. Seams taped and floated, screw holes filled, full sanding
  4. Sprayed ceiling texture
  5. Primer and two-tone paint: gray walls with white ceilings, trim, and doors
  6. Two-panel interior doors hung and cased
  7. Bi-fold closet doors with a shelf and hanging rod inside each closet
  8. Baseboard throughout and a new stair handrail

Drywall hung

Our crew framed the space first (no photos of that stage, a lesson for the next job), then boarded walls and ceilings in the family room, bedroom, laundry, bathroom, and stairwell. Screws set and seams ready for tape. This is the first point where the layout is readable in person.

Taped, mudded, and textured

Seams taped and floated, screw holes filled and sanded, ceilings sprayed with texture. This stage decides whether the walls read flat under paint, so it gets the time it needs before primer goes on.
