Clinton Basement Finish: 900 Square Feet, Framed to Finished

A 900 square foot basement finish in Clinton, Davis County: an open family room, a bedroom with closets, a laundry area, and a bathroom with a shower. We framed it, hung and finished the drywall, ran the doors and trim, painted it, and set the hardware. Then we photographed the same rooms at three points so you can see what each stage looks like.

About this job

The house is a few minutes from our Clearfield shop. The finished lower level runs a two-tone scheme: warm gray walls against white ceilings, trim, and doors, with matte black hardware on every door. The flooring in the finished photos was installed by others, so we don't take credit for it.

Every photo below was taken on site by our crew. Nothing is staged and nothing is a rendering.

Read more about our framing, drywall, carpentry & trim, and painting services.

What we did

  1. 01Framed the family room, bedroom, closets, laundry, and bathroom walls
  2. 02Drywall hung on walls and ceilings, including the stairwell and under-stair storage
  3. 03Seams taped and floated, screw holes filled, full sanding
  4. 04Sprayed ceiling texture
  5. 05Primer and two-tone paint: gray walls with white ceilings, trim, and doors
  6. 06Two-panel interior doors hung and cased
  7. 07Bi-fold closet doors with a shelf and hanging rod inside each closet
  8. 08Baseboard throughout and a new stair handrail
  9. 09Matte black hardware on every door

Stage by stage

3 visits, July to August 2026.

Every photo was taken on site by our crew. Tap any one to see it larger.

01

July 2026

Drywall hung

Our crew framed the space first (no photos of that stage, a lesson for the next job), then boarded walls and ceilings in the family room, bedroom, laundry, bathroom, and stairwell. Screws set and seams ready for tape. This is the first point where the layout is readable in person.

7 photos

02

Late July 2026

Taped, mudded, and textured

Seams taped and floated, screw holes filled and sanded, ceilings sprayed with texture. This stage decides whether the walls read flat under paint, so it gets the time it needs before primer goes on.

6 photos

03

August 2026

Paint, trim, doors, and hardware

Primer, then two-tone paint: warm gray walls against white ceilings, trim, and doors. Two-panel doors hung and cased, baseboard run, bi-fold closet doors with a shelf and rod inside, and matte black hardware on every door. The flooring was installed by others.
