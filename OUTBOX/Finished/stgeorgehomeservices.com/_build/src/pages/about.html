The electrical work

We don't do the electrical ourselves, and we won't pretend to. EV charger installations are performed by our licensed electrical partner, under their own electrical license.

One company manages the whole job. Where it needs a licensed electrician, we bring one in, and we tell you who, before he arrives. The permit, the panel check, and the scheduling are ours. The wire is his.
