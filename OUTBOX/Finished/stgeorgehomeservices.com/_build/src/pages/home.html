Something wrong right now?

We’ll tell you honestly whether this can wait until morning.

If it can wait until 8 a.m., we’ll say so on the phone, and you’ll pay the daytime rate. We charge more at night, and you’ll be told exactly how much more before anyone is dispatched. It doesn’t change based on how bad it looks.

Before you call anyone, shut the water off. The main valve is usually on the garage wall, outside near the front hose bib, or in the water heater closet. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility.

Where we work

Eight towns, and the rules change at every city line.

The plumbing code is the same across Utah. The lawn cap, the watering hours, the lighting rule, the power company and what is in the water are not. Each town has its own page, and every fact on it names the document it came from.
