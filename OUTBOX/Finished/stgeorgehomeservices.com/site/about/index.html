How to check us

Utah does not require a contractor to display a license number in advertising, only to provide it on request. We display it anyway.

Ask us for the license number, then check it yourself on the state's public lookup before you call us back. We send it the same day you ask.

Ask us for the license number. Ask us for the insurance certificate. Ask for both. We'll send them the same day.

Your water runs 18 to 22 grains. That's hard enough to void a warranty. Every tankless manufacturer publishes a hardness limit. We can tell you all of them, and where your water sits. We'll tell you what usually fails on this model, and at roughly what age.

The electrical work

We don't do the electrical ourselves, and we won't pretend to. EV charger installations are performed by our licensed electrical partner, under their own electrical license.

One company manages the whole job. Where it needs a licensed electrician, we bring one in, and we tell you who, before he arrives. The permit, the panel check, and the scheduling are ours. The wire is his.

What we won't do

Certificates belong to the people who earned them. When we name one, we name the person who holds it.
