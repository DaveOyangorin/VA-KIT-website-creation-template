Questions

Good to know.

01Can it wait until morning?

Most things can, and the daytime rate is lower. Emergency calls are answered any hour, every day, and what we will tell you on that call is whether the thing you have can wait.

02What does a visit cost?

One flat fee to find the problem: $150 during standard hours, $50 more in the evening, at weekends and on holidays. If you hire us, that fee comes off the job. You’re told which rate applies on the phone, before anyone is dispatched.

03Who will come to the house?

Before your appointment you’ll get a message with the name and photo of the technician coming, and the time window. He’ll be in a marked truck and a company shirt.

04How do I check you?

Ask for the license number and the insurance certificate. Both come the same day, and you can check the license yourself on the state’s public lookup. How to check a Utah contractor.
