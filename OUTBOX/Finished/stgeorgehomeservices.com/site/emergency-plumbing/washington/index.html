Emergency plumbing

Washington City publishes its water. Part of it is over the warranty limit.

Most towns around here publish nothing useful about their water. Washington City publishes total dissolved solids — 210 to 656 parts per million — and the top of that range is above what Rinnai and Rheem warrant a tankless heater to run on. If your water heater has just failed, that is probably not a coincidence.

Right now

Three things, before you call anyone.

  1. Shut the water off. Garage wall, outside by the front hose bib, or the water heater closet. If the valve will not turn, use the curb stop at the meter box rather than forcing a valve that is already the weak point.
  2. Do not leave a leak running until Monday. Washington City’s residential rate is tiered and it roughly doubles across the tiers, from $2.41 to $5.03. A leak does not cost you the cheap rate — it pushes your whole month up the ladder. That is a real reason to fix a slow leak this week rather than next month.
  3. Then call. A person answers at any hour, and will tell you honestly whether it can wait until morning. Most things can, and the daytime rate is lower.

Washington City, specifically

210 to 656 ppm, and why your tankless heater cares.

Washington City’s 2025 water quality report gives total dissolved solids as 210 to 656 ppm, and sulfate as 32 to 198 ppm. That is a genuine range, not a single number, because the city draws from more than one place.

Now the other half. Rinnai and Rheem both cap total dissolved solids at 500 mg/L in their warranty and installation documents, alongside a 200 mg/L hardness cap. Those are the manufacturers’ own figures, published in their own paperwork.

656 is more than 500. So at the top of this city’s published range, the water is outside what the manufacturer warranted the appliance to run on. That does not mean your heater will fail tomorrow, and it does not mean a warranty claim gets denied automatically. It means the question is worth asking before you buy a tankless unit here, and it is worth knowing if one has just failed on you.

Nobody in this market will tell you that, because it costs a tankless sale. We would rather you bought the right appliance and stayed a customer. Ask us what your treatment options are, and ask us to show you the manufacturer’s own hardness and TDS page before you sign anything.

One more thing that is specific to this city and catches people out: the electric utility is split. Washington City Power serves north of the Virgin River and Dixie Power serves the rest of the city limits. Rocky Mountain Power serves no part of Washington City at all. When an electric water heater has no power, the side of the river you are on decides who you ring.

And the housing stock here is two different eras with two different pipe problems. Green Springs went up mainly through the 1990s and 2000s, which overlaps the tail of the polybutylene period — gray pipe, stamped PB2110, that fails from the inside. Long Valley Trails is current D.R. Horton new construction, where the failures are fittings and workmanship rather than material. Knowing which one you are standing in changes what we look at first.

What is written down about Washington City.

  • Washington City’s 2025 water quality report shows total dissolved solids ranging from 210 to 656 ppm.Environment, water and ground research, §1.2, published water chemistry
  • Rinnai and Rheem both cap total dissolved solids at 500 mg/L and total hardness at 200 mg/L in their warranty and installation documents. Noritz excludes damage above 12 grains per gallon.Plumbing research, §1.1, the manufacturer warranty finding
  • The same report shows sulfate from 32 to 198 ppm, arsenic 1.3 to 3.8 ppb against a federal limit of 10, and total trihalomethanes 5 to 48 ppb against a limit of 80. The city is in compliance; these are the numbers behind that.Environment, water and ground research, §1.2
  • Washington City’s residential water rate roughly doubles from tier 1 at $2.41 to tier 9 at $5.03. A leak climbs the tiers.Washington County playbook, §3, the city layer
  • A water softener does nothing to arsenic. Cation exchange does not remove it. If somebody sells you a softener as an arsenic fix, they are selling you the wrong box.Environment, water and ground research, §1.2
  • Electric service is split at the Virgin River — Washington City Power to the north, Dixie Power for the rest. Rocky Mountain Power is not the utility for any part of the city.Utility and EV research, §3.1
  • Green Springs was developed mainly through the 1990s and 2000s. Polybutylene supply pipe went into U.S. homes from roughly 1978 to the mid-1990s — gray, stamped PB2110, and it fails from the inside.HOA research, §6.2, and housing research
  • The plumbing code here is the same as St. George’s — Utah adopts construction code statewide under Title 15A. What differs by city is permits, fees and process, and those are Washington City’s own.Ordinance research, Utah Title 15A
