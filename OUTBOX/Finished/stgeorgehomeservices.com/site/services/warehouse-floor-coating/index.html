What the work is

A warehouse slab is ground with a walk-behind or ride-on planetary machine running to a dust extractor. On a floor of any size that is days of work before anything is applied, and it is where the cost sits. A quote that is cheap on a big slab is usually cheap because it skips this.

Joints are the thing to get right. A large slab has saw cuts and construction joints that move, and they are treated as joints rather than coated over. Filled to the wrong specification they fail at the edges under wheel traffic within a year.

The finish is usually a solid color or a grind and seal rather than flake, because what is wanted is a floor that cleans, reflects light and takes traffic, not a pattern. Line marking goes on as its own step.

We will tell you what your slab will and will not support. A warehouse floor with a failing surface needs repair before a finish, and a coating over it is money spent twice.

What is included

  • Planetary grinding with dust extraction
  • Moisture testing across the slab, not at one point
  • Joints treated as joints, to a named specification
  • Repair of spalled and worn areas as its own quoted step
  • Line marking and demarcation, quoted separately
  • A phased program where the operation cannot stop

What is not

  • Coating over joints as if they were floor. They move and it fails
  • Racking, stock movement or equipment relocation
  • A single-visit price on a floor that needs a phased program. We will quote the phases
