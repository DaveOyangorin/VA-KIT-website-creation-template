Solid color floor in St. George

One color, no chips. It is the plainest finish available and the most honest about the slab underneath it, because there is no pattern to hide anything in.

(435) 850-0053Ask for a visit

What the work is

A solid color floor is a pigmented resin applied over a prepared slab and sealed. It reads as one clean surface, which is why it is the usual choice for a warehouse, a showroom or anywhere a floor is meant to be a background rather than a feature.

Be clear-eyed about what it does not do. Every repaired crack, every patch, every low spot and every bit of settled dust is visible on a solid color floor, and none of it is on a flake floor. If the slab has a history, a solid color will publish it.

It is also the finish where the choice of product matters most visually, because there is no pattern to distract from a color change over time. Ultraviolet stability is a published property of the specific product, printed on its data sheet. Ask which product, and ask to see the sheet.

We will steer you off a solid color onto flake where the slab does not deserve it. That is a smaller job for us and the right answer for you.

What is included

  • The color agreed from a physical sample
  • Pigmented resin over a prepared, tested slab
  • Clear or pigmented top coat as the system requires
  • A plain account, before you commit, of what the finished floor will show

What is not

  • Hiding an unsound slab. A solid color is the wrong finish for that and we will say so
  • Color matching to anything but a physical sample
  • Touch-up to an area after cure that blends invisibly. It will not

The sun question, answered properly

A garage door open through a Washington County summer puts direct sun on the first several feet of floor, every day, for months. Whether a coating holds its color under that is a property of the chemistry, and manufacturers publish it.

Polyaspartic products are described by their makers as ultraviolet-resistant and color-stable; aliphatic polyaspartic products state ultraviolet stability by name on the data sheet. That is checkable, which means its absence is checkable too. So ask which product is going down and ask to read the sheet — of us and of anybody else quoting you.
