What the work is

The trip from bed to bathroom in the dark is a route people take every night without thinking about it. Lighting it at a low level, on motion, means no fumbling for a switch and no wide-awake overhead light at three in the morning.

Done well it is invisible in daylight and never bright enough to wake anyone up. Done badly it is a row of glare at ankle height.

Where the fittings need new wiring, that is our licensed electrical partner's work and it is quoted in. Plenty of good solutions do not need it.

What is included

  • Walking the actual route with you, at night if that helps
  • Fittings placed and aimed so the floor is lit and the eye is not
  • Motion sensing and timing set with you
  • A written note of the settings

What is not

  • New circuits, switches or hard wiring, which is our licensed electrical partner's work
  • Anything that needs a wall opened, unless we have quoted it
  • Smart-home integration

Aim matters more than brightness

The goal is to see the floor and the doorway, not to light the room. A fitting that shines toward the eye at that hour is worse than no fitting at all.

What we can show you in writing

  • The same list asks for switches at both ends of a long hall and at the top and bottom of the stairs, and names motion-activated lights that plug into an outlet as a way to light a hallway without wiring. Most of that you can buy this afternoon and install yourself.1
