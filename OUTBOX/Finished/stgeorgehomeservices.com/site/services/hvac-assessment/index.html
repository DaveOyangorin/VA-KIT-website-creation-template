Heating and air

HVAC energy and comfort assessment in St. George

Why one room is ten degrees off the rest, written down, with what each fix costs.

(435) 850-0053Ask for a visit

What the work is

A walk of the house with instruments: register temperatures and airflow room by room, static pressure across the system, the ducts looked at where they can be reached, the insulation and the glass noted, the equipment's readings taken.

You get a written list, ranked now, soon and later, with a cost band against each line. Some of it will be sealing and balancing rather than equipment, and where that is true we will say so even though it is the smaller job.

The fee is credited against the work if you go ahead with us, and the list is yours either way. Take it to anyone.

What it is not: a sales call with a clipboard. The output is a document, the fee is credited if you go ahead with us, and the document is yours to take to somebody else if you would rather.

The most common finding is not equipment. It is a duct fault, a return that is too small, a thermostat in a bad location reading a temperature the rest of the house never sees, or a room with more glass than the system was ever asked to handle. Those cost a fraction of a system and we will tell you so even where the bigger job is the one we would rather sell.

What is included

  • Room-by-room register temperature and airflow
  • Static pressure across the system
  • The ducts inspected where they can be reached
  • A written, ranked list with a cost band on each line, yours to keep
  • The thermostat's location assessed, which is a common and cheap fault

What is not

  • A blower door test or thermal imaging, which are separate specialist services
  • A report that only recommends buying equipment from us

What we can show you in writing

Every line traces to a document we read in full. The number after a sentence points to the research below.

  • Washington County is the only county in Utah that the Department of Energy does not classify as Cold. It is the state's only county in IECC Climate Zone 3, Building America Hot-Dry. That is published federal data, by county, and it takes about ninety seconds to check.1
  • Manual J is ANSI/ACCA 2 Manual J - 2016, eighth edition, the ANSI-recognized national standard for residential load calculation. It is published by somebody else, and it is what a real sizing calculation follows.1
  • Utah's H-100 HVAC license is a general classification. The state asks its holder for a written trade exam, a natural gas technician certification and 4,000 documented hours of HVAC employment in the past five years.1
  • The state's licensee lookup returns every license held under a contractor's core number, and the Construction Business Registry is a second public check. Use both on anyone you are about to let into the house.1
