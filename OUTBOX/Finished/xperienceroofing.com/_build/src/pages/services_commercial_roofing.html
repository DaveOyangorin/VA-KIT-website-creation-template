Commercial Roofing

Flat roofs fail at the seams. Ours are welded, tested, and maintained.

The Service

How we approach it

Low-slope commercial roofs live a hard life in Utah — ponding snowmelt, freeze-thaw cycles, and intense high-altitude UV. We install TPO and EPDM membrane systems engineered for it: heat-welded seams that form a monolithic surface, proper drainage detailing, and water testing before we sign off. Then we keep the roof performing with maintenance programs, because a commercial roof is an asset that pays back the attention it gets.

The Difference

Why it's done this way

01

Heat-welded seams

TPO seams are hot-air welded into a single continuous membrane — no adhesive seams to fail under freeze-thaw stress.

02

Built for UV at altitude

Reflective TPO handles the valley's intense high-elevation sun, reducing membrane degradation and summer cooling load.

03

Water-tested before handoff

We verify the system holds water out before we call it done — not after the first storm does the testing for us.

04

Minimal business disruption

Staging, scheduling, and site control planned around your operations, so the building keeps working while we do.

FAQs

Common questions

TPO or EPDM — which is right for my building?+

TPO's reflective white surface and welded seams make it the default choice for most Utah low-slope roofs, especially where summer heat matters. EPDM earns its place on certain budgets and building types. We assess the building and recommend based on the roof, not on what's on the truck.

How long does a commercial membrane roof last?+

Properly installed and maintained, TPO and EPDM systems typically deliver 20 to 30 years of service. Maintenance is the variable owners control — cleared drains and small repairs done early are what get a roof to the far end of that range.

Can you re-roof over our existing membrane?+

Sometimes — code allows a recover in certain conditions, and it can save cost. But it depends on what's under the existing roof, especially wet insulation. Our assessment tells us whether a recover is sound or a tear-off is the honest answer.

Will roofing work shut down our business?+

Almost never. We plan staging, material loading, and noisy work around your operating hours, and we coordinate with your facility contact daily so there are no surprises for your tenants or staff.

What does a maintenance program include?+

Scheduled inspections, drain and scupper clearing, seam checks, sealant touch-ups, and documentation after every visit. It's the difference between finding a small membrane cut in spring and finding wet insulation in fall.
