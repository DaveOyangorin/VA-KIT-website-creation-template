Surface Layer

The surface is the only layer anyone sees, and the first line against Utah sun, hail, and snow. We install three surface systems — shingle, metal, and tile — each matched to the home's architecture, the roof's pitch, and how long the owner intends to stay.

01

Architectural Shingle

3-tab is gone. Architectural (laminate) shingles give depth, dimension, and 25–50 year performance.

02

Metal Panels

Steel or aluminum over a solid deck. The premium surface choice for longevity.

03

Tile

Clay and concrete — Class A fire rated, heavy, and permanent when installed correctly.
