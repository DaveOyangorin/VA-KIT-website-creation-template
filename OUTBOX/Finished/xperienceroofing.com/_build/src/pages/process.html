How We Work

Our Process

Six steps, the same on every roof — from the first photograph on the deck to the warranty in your hand. Documented, unhurried, and checked at every stage.

Our Process · Step by Step

01

Inspection

Free, thorough, documented. We get on the roof and into the attic, photograph what we find, and walk you through it — deck, flashing, ventilation, everything. You keep the photos either way.

Storm Damage & Insurance

We document. You decide.

If the inspection turns up hail or wind damage, we record it the way insurance claims require — dated photos, measurements, and a written scope. The claim is yours to file; the documentation comes from us.

02

Estimate

A written scope with system options and real numbers. Take it home, compare it, sleep on it. No pressure, and no expiring “today only” pricing.

03

Materials

Your system, color, and components confirmed — surface, underlayment, flashing, ventilation — then ordered and staged so install day starts at the deck, not at the supply house.

04

Installation

Most residential roofs take one to two days. The deck is repaired and dried in first, then every layer goes down in order per The Installer's Standard — and every course is checked before the next one starts.

05

Walkthrough

We inspect the finished roof together. Flashing, ridge lines, cleanup, the magnetic nail sweep — nothing is done until it's right, and you've seen it yourself.

06

Warranty

A manufacturer warranty on the system and a workmanship warranty on the install — both in writing, both explained before we leave the driveway.

The Installer’s Standard

Six principles, Every roof

Every step above is measured against the six principles we install by. They’re not a slogan — they’re the checklist on the roof.

01 /

Preparation

The deck is inspected and repaired before anything goes down on it.

02 /

Flashing

New metal at every wall, valley, and penetration — never reused, never caulked over.

03 /

Ventilation

Intake and exhaust balanced to the attic, not to the brochure.

04 /

Layering

Each course overlaps the one below it, the way water expects.

05 /

Precision

Straight lines, correct nail placement, manufacturer specs on every course.

06 /

Clean Site

Tarped landscaping, daily pickup, and a magnetic sweep before we leave.
