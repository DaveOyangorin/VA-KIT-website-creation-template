How we build · Six principles

The Installer’s Standard.

Every roof we install is held to the same six principles: the parts of the job you’ll never see, done as carefully as the parts you will.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    A roof is only as good as what’s under it. Every deck is inspected, repaired and dried in before any surface material goes down.

  2. 02

    Flashing

    Water finds every gap. Valleys, penetrations, step flashing, drip edge: this is where most roof failures begin.

  3. 03

    Ventilation

    Trapped heat and moisture destroy roofs from the inside. Intake and exhaust are worked out before the first shingle is laid.

  4. 04

    Layering

    Ice-and-water shield, synthetic underlayment, proper overlap: the layers that are invisible once we’re done matter most.

  5. 05

    Precision

    Nail placement, exposure, alignment. A roof installed by hand deserves hand-level attention. Every course is checked.

  6. 06

    Clean site

    The job isn’t done until your property looks like we were never there. Materials staged, debris removed.

Our process · Six steps

From the first photograph to the warranty in your hand.

  1. Step 01

    Inspection

    Free, thorough, documented. We get on the roof and into the attic, photograph what we find and walk you through it.

  2. Step 02

    Estimate

    A written scope with system options and real numbers. No pressure, and no expiring “today only” pricing.

  3. Step 03

    Materials

    Your system, color and components confirmed, then ordered and staged so install day starts at the deck.

  4. Step 04

    Installation

    Most residential roofs take one to two days. Deck first, then every layer in order, every course checked.

  5. Step 05

    Walkthrough

    We inspect the finished roof together: flashing, ridge lines, cleanup, the magnetic nail sweep.

  6. Step 06

    Warranty

    A manufacturer warranty on the system and a workmanship warranty on the install, both in writing and explained.

Before you call.

From the journalHow to Read a Roofing Estimate: The Line Items That MatterRead it
01Is the roof inspection free?

Yes. The inspection is free, the photos are yours to keep either way, and the estimate arrives in writing.

02Do you help with storm damage and insurance?

If the inspection turns up hail or wind damage, we record it the way insurance claims require: dated photos, measurements and a written scope. The claim is yours to file; the documentation comes from us.

03How long does a new roof take?

Most residential roofs take one to two days. The deck is repaired and dried in first, then every layer goes down in order.

04Can you re-roof under solar panels?

Yes. A solar detach and reset removes the panels and racking, rebuilds the roof, flashes the mounts into the new roof and reinstalls the array, coordinated with your solar installer.

05Where do you work?

From our shop in Midvale, across the Salt Lake Valley: Midvale, Sandy, Draper, Murray, South Jordan, West Jordan, Riverton, Holladay, Cottonwood Heights, Salt Lake City.
