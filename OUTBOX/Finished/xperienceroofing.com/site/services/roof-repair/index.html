Roof Repair in Midvale, UT

Free Estimate (385) 402-6364

Xperience Roofing provides roof repair in Midvale and across the Salt Lake Valley, from our shop on State Street. Every job starts with a free, documented inspection and a written estimate.

Find the real source. Fix it once. That's the whole job.

How we approach it

Most roof leaks don't start where the water shows up. We diagnose first — tracing the leak to its actual source before anything gets touched — then repair it properly. Wind-lifted shingles off the canyon gusts, flashing failures around chimneys and vents, summer hail damage, ice-dam wear at the eaves: the Salt Lake Valley throws all of it at a roof, and we fix all of it.

Why it's done this way

Diagnosis before repair

We trace the leak to its source instead of patching where the stain appears, so the same problem doesn't come back next storm.

Stops small problems early

A lifted shingle or failed pipe boot costs little to fix now and a lot to fix after a season of water intrusion.

Extends your roof's life

Timely, correct repairs add years to a roof that doesn't need replacing yet — and we'll tell you honestly when it does.

Storm-response experience

Canyon winds and summer hail are routine here. We know what they do to a roof and how to document it for insurance.

What's included

Leak tracing

We follow the water to its entry point — attic, deck, flashing — before quoting the fix.

Shingle repair & replacement

Wind-lifted, cracked, or missing shingles replaced and sealed, matched as closely as possible.

Flashing repair

Chimney, valley, step, and vent flashing resealed or replaced — the most common leak source on valley homes.

Pipe boot & vent replacement

Cracked rubber boots and damaged vents swapped before they let water in.

Hail & wind damage repair

Storm damage repaired and documented with photos suitable for an insurance claim.

Ice-dam damage repair

Eave and gutter-line damage from winter ice backup repaired, with prevention recommendations.

Repair report & photos

Before-and-after photos and a written summary of what was wrong and what we did.

Common questions

Water is coming through my ceiling — how fast can you come?

Active leaks get priority scheduling. Call us, and in the meantime put a container under the drip and move belongings clear — we'll get the roof protected and then properly repaired.

Why does my roof only leak during certain storms?

Wind direction, rain intensity, and snowmelt each stress different parts of the roof. A flashing gap might only leak in wind-driven rain from the south; ice dams only show up in freeze-thaw weather. This is exactly why we diagnose before we repair.

Is it worth repairing an older roof, or should I just replace it?

It depends on the roof's overall condition, not just its age. If the system is sound and the damage is localized, a repair is the right call. If failures are spreading, we'll show you why replacement is the better investment — with photos, not pressure.

Can hail damage be invisible from the ground?

Yes. Hail bruises shingles and knocks off granules in ways you often can't see from the driveway, but that shorten the roof's life. After a hailstorm it's worth a free inspection — and if there's claimable damage, we document it properly.

Do you repair roofs in winter?

Yes. Leaks don't wait for spring, and neither do we. We work in dry weather windows and use cold-rated materials and methods so winter repairs hold just as well.
