About Xperience Roofing, Midvale

Free Estimate (385) 402-6364

Xperience Roofing is a family-run roofing contractor based in Midvale, Utah, installing, repairing and inspecting roofs across the Salt Lake Valley since 2019.

There’s a moment on every project where the homeowner steps back, looks up at their finished roof, and something shifts. Not just relief that it’s done — pride. That their house looks the way it was always supposed to look.

That moment is why we do this. Every deck we inspect, every piece of flashing we fit, every nail line we set is in service of it — a roof that protects the house for decades and looks right from the curb on day one.

Roofs built to last. Service built on trust.

Residential and commercial roofing across the Salt Lake Valley — installed by the Xperience family in Midvale, Utah.

The Installer’s Standard

Six principles every crew follows on every roof — the difference between an installation and a shortcut. The full standard, step by step, is on our process page.

Preparation

The deck gets inspected and repaired before anything goes on top of it. A roof is only as sound as what it's nailed to.

Flashing

Every wall, valley, chimney, and penetration gets metal — fitted piece by piece, not a bead of sealant and a hope.

Ventilation

Intake and exhaust balanced to the attic, so heat and moisture leave the way they should and the roof lasts its full life.

Layering

Ice and water shield, underlayment, surface — in order, to manufacturer spec, with no skipped steps under the part you can see.

Precision

Straight courses, correct nailing patterns, clean cuts. The details nobody photographs are the ones that decide how a roof ages.

Clean Site

Landscaping protected, debris contained, magnet sweeps for nails. We leave the property the way we found it — minus the old roof.

What we Believe

Quality over volume

We would rather install fewer roofs correctly than chase numbers. Every project gets the time it actually needs.

Documentation

Photos of what we find, written estimates with clear line items, and straight answers — so you always know what you're paying for and why.

Clean sites

Your home is not a job site to us. Tarps down, materials staged, nails swept — every day of the project, not just the last one.

The Xperience Family

Xperience Roofing is a family-run contractor based in Midvale, Utah. We’ve been installing, repairing, and inspecting roofs across the Salt Lake Valley since 2019 — long enough to know what Utah weather does to a roof, and recent enough to still treat every job like it matters.

We’re part of the Xperience family of Utah contractors, alongside Xperience Landscaping. Different trades, same standard: do the work right, document it, and leave the property better than we found it.

When you call, you reach a real person at our Midvale office — not a call center. Inspections are free, estimates are written, and nobody on our team is paid to pressure you.
