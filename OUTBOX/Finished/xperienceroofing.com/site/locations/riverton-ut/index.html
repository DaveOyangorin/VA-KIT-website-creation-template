Roofing Questions in Riverton

My home was built in 2004 and the roof has never been replaced. Should I be worried?

You should be informed, not worried. At twenty-plus years, a builder-grade shingle in Utah is at or near the end of its realistic life, but actual condition varies with slope orientation, ventilation, and luck with hail. A free inspection tells you precisely where yours stands, with photos, so you can plan on your schedule rather than react to a leak.

What are the early warning signs I can check myself?

From the ground: curled or lifted shingle edges, dark streaks or bald patches, and granules accumulating in gutters and downspout splash areas. Inside: any ceiling stain, especially after wind-driven rain or snowmelt. Any of these justifies a professional look — small problems caught early are repairs, not replacements.

Does hail in Riverton mean an automatic insurance claim?

No, and beware of anyone who says otherwise. Hail damage has to meet a real threshold before a claim makes sense, and a claim for trivial damage can cost you more than it returns. We document the actual condition and give you an honest read on whether what we see warrants involving your insurer.

Can you replace our roof without disturbing the solar panels for weeks?

Yes — solar detach and reset is one of our core services, performed in-house and sequenced with the roofing crew. The panels come down immediately before tear-off and go back up promptly after the new roof is complete, typically keeping the array offline for days rather than weeks.
