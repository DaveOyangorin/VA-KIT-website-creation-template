## PART A — Ask Maps (Gemini in Google Maps)

### What it is — CONFIRMED
"Ask Maps" is a Gemini-powered **conversational layer inside Google Maps**. Users tap an "Ask Maps" button and ask complex natural-language place questions ("Where can I charge my phone without waiting in a long coffee line?" / "Is there a lit public tennis court I can play at tonight?"); Maps returns a conversational answer plus a customized map of options, with actions like directions, save, share, and booking.
- Source (primary): Google blog, "Ask Maps and Immersive Navigation," 2026-03-12 — https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-immersive-navigation/
- Source: Search Engine Land — https://searchengineland.com/google-maps-turns-exploration-into-a-conversation-with-ask-maps-471491

### What powers the answers — CONFIRMED (data sources) / OBSERVED (ranking)
Google states Ask Maps draws on its **places database (300M+ places)**, **500M+ community contributors' reviews and photos**, and **Gemini models**, and that results are **personalized** "based on things like places you've searched for or saved in Maps." (Primary: Google blog above.)
- **CONFIRMED inputs:** places data, user reviews, user photos, Gemini, personalization from the asking user's search/save history.
- **Google did NOT publish a ranking/selection algorithm for Ask Maps.** Exactly how one business is chosen over another inside an Ask Maps answer is **not documented**. Treat any "ranking factor" claim from blogs as OBSERVED/SPECULATIVE.

### Rollout status - CONFIRMED as of 2026-08-25 (rewritten; supersedes the 2026-06-06 status)
- Announced 2026-03-12; described by Google as its "biggest navigation upgrade in over a decade" (bundled with Immersive Navigation). Live US + India on Android/iOS by ~end of March 2026 (9to5Google, 2026-03-31).
- **2026-08-06 [CONF] - major expansion.** Primary source: Google blog, "Ask Maps gets more helpful with food ordering and more," Miriam Daniel (VP & GM, Google Maps) - https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/order-food-in-ask-maps/
  - **Geography:** now rolling out in **Australia, Brazil, Canada, Indonesia, Japan and Mexico, plus 150+ countries and territories in English.** The old "US and India only" line is DEAD - do not repeat it to a client.
  - **Desktop:** STILL no confirmed date. Unchanged WATCH item.
- **Relevant to us:** all three managed clients are US local businesses -> Ask Maps is live in their market and has been since March.

### A.5 Agentic Ask Maps - CONFIRMED 2026-08-06 (NEW SECTION)
Ask Maps stopped being purely an answer layer. It now **completes multi-step tasks**. All items below are from the Google primary source above [CONF]:
- **Food ordering (agentic).** Ask Maps finds open restaurants **along the user's route**, honours **saved places and dietary needs**, and **adds the dish directly to the cart** for review/checkout. Live with **Square** and **Toast**; **Uber Eats "coming soon."** Google states it is co-developing a **"Universal Commerce Protocol for Food"** with partners.
- **Hotels.** Compares **real-time prices and checks availability** against a natural-language brief (price band, rating, vibe, walking distance to gym/restaurants).
- **Events.** Surfaces local events (concerts, comedy) **with ticket links**.
- **Personal Intelligence.** Users may connect **Gmail** - **opt-in, OFF by default**; **Calendar "soon"** - so answers factor in the user's existing flights and reservations without being told. Conversation memory persists when history settings are on ("remind me what activities did you suggest for my trip to Seattle").
- **Real-time transit widget** - live bus/train/subway/ferry delays inside the Ask Maps answer.
- **Conversational contributions** - users can suggest place edits and upload photos from inside Ask Maps and the Contribute tab. **This makes the GBP data layer easier for third parties to change**, which cuts both ways: faster correction of bad data, and a lower-friction path for a competitor or a stranger to submit an edit. Monitor client profiles for unexpected edits.

**What this changes for our clients [OBS - Google published no ranking doc]:**
1. **The transactable surface is the appointment slot.** Restaurants got ordering; for a plumber/chiropractor/auto shop the equivalent endpoint is **online booking**. This converges with the Sterling Sky finding already on the WATCH list (agentic local query wants an available slot back in ~1 second, "call for availability" loses). **Booking link present, resolving, and fast is now a first-class Ask Maps lever, not a conversion nicety.**
2. **Route context matters.** "Along my way home" is an explicit selection input for food. If Google extends route-context selection to services, **service-area accuracy and real address/geo data** get more weight, not less.
3. **Personalisation deepens (Gmail/Calendar).** More of the answer is decided by the asking user's own context, which is not optimisable. Fight for the part that is: complete, corroborated GBP + extractable site content.
4. **Do NOT claim any of this is a documented ranking factor.** Google confirmed the CAPABILITIES, not the selection logic. Ranking claims stay [OBS].

### How a business gets surfaced/cited in Ask Maps — what we can act on
Because Google confirmed the *inputs* (GBP/places data, reviews, photos) but not the *ranking*, the optimization levers below are the **same signals Google confirmed feed the system**, applied as ordinary local SEO. Confidence tags: [CONF] = Google-confirmed input; [OBS] = observed pattern in third-party coverage; [STD] = standard Google local-ranking guidance (relevance/distance/prominence — support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

1. **Google Business Profile completeness & accuracy** [CONF input + STD]. Fully populated profiles are the substrate Gemini summarizes. Fill every field: primary + secondary categories, services (with descriptions), attributes, hours, description, booking link, photos.
2. **Review volume, recency, and — critically — review TEXT** [CONF input + OBS]. Review *language* is machine-readable evidence of "what you're good for." Ask Maps answers reflect themes like responsiveness, honesty, professionalism, clear communication. Detailed, specific reviews ("fixed a basement drain backup same-day and explained the cause") outperform generic "great service." → the backlink team/owner: solicit reviews that describe the *situation, action, outcome*, not just stars.
3. **Photos** [CONF input]. User + owner photos are an explicit data source. Keep a current, categorized photo set.
4. **Categories + attributes** [STD + OBS]. Specific categories and identity/service attributes help Gemini match situational queries ("emergency," "same-day," "older-home repairs," wheelchair-accessible, women-owned, etc.).
5. **Services as "jobs-to-be-done," not just service names** [OBS]. Frame services around the *situation that triggers the call* (e.g. plumbing → "emergency calls, same-day response, older-home repairs"), in both GBP services and on the website.
6. **Website content that answers situational/symptom/decision questions** [OBS + aligns with official "unique, helpful, people-first"]. Pages that cover "symptoms → what it means → what to do → what to expect" give Gemini extractable, quotable passages.
7. **Consistency across third-party sources** [CONF for AI-local generally + OBS for Ask Maps]. AI-local answers cross-reference Yelp, directories, Reddit, etc. Consistent NAP + brand description across sources raises trust. (Ties to the backlink team brand-mention work, files dated 2026-06-03/04.)
8. **Standard local trio still applies** [STD]: Relevance, Distance (proximity to searcher), Prominence (real-world + online popularity, incl. reviews). Distance is user-side and not optimizable; relevance + prominence are.

## PART B — AI Citation (AI Overviews / AI Mode / ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini)

### The non-negotiable entry ticket — CONFIRMED
From the official Google guide: to appear in any generative AI feature on Search, a page must be **indexed and eligible to be shown with a snippet**. No `noindex`, no `nosnippet`, no robots block, no JS-hidden main content. This is the hard gate — do this before anything else.
- **Do NOT use `nosnippet` to "control" AI** on money pages: it also kills your normal search snippet and AI impressions. (See GSC gen-AI report note below.)

### What makes content citation-eligible vs invisible
- **Unique, non-commodity perspective** [CONF]. AI grounds answers in pages that add something — first-hand experience, specific local detail, original data — not recycled common knowledge.
- **People-first, well-structured content** [CONF]. Clear headings, short paragraphs, direct answers near the top → passage-level extractability. (This aligns with — and is the legitimate version of — "quotable formatting"; it is NOT the debunked "chunking.")
- **Answer the question explicitly and early.** A self-contained 2-4 sentence answer under a question-style H2/H3 is the most "liftable" unit for an AI summary. [OBS, consistent with official "audience-first organization."]
- **E-E-A-T / source authority** [CONF, via helpful-content + QRG]: named author with real credentials, "reviewed by" for YMYL, About/contact transparency, citations to authoritative sources. For the health client (YMYL health) this is mandatory, not optional.
- **Brand mentions + reviews + third-party citations** [study-backed, see internal notes]. NP Digital's LLM-citation factor study ranked: (1) reviews, (2) years in business, (3) brand mentions, (4) citations, (5) content creation, (6) multi-channel distribution. Unlinked brand mentions count, not just dofollow links. Reddit standing now feeds both rankings and AI answers (internal notes).

### Structured data — the honest position (resolves a real tension)
- **Official Google line [CONF]:** structured data is **helpful but NOT required** for AI features; do not over-focus on it. (`ai-optimization-generative-search.md`.)
- **Third-party/industry consensus [OBS]:** JSON-LD schema improves entity clarity and extractability for AI engines generally (incl. non-Google ones like Perplexity/ChatGPT). The schema types that earn local AI citations in 2026: **LocalBusiness (with type, priceRange, openingHoursSpecification, geo, address) + Service + FAQPage + Article + BreadcrumbList**, with Organization at parent level. Generic, attribute-less LocalBusiness is de-prioritized.
- **Our policy (reconciled):** schema is a **low-risk multiplier, not the lever**. Implement a clean, accurate, complete schema stack because it helps non-Google AI engines and rich results — but never treat it as a substitute for unique content, GBP completeness, and reviews. Schema MUST match visible content (mismatch = spam risk, now also an AI-citation blocker per the 2026-06-05 spam-policy clarification). Note: 7 schema types are being phased out (FAQ rich results retiring; CourseInfo/ClaimReview/EstimatedSalary/LearningVideo/SpecialAnnouncement/VehicleListing/PracticeProblem) — strip dead markup, keep Article/LocalBusiness/Service/Breadcrumb/Review. (internal notes)
  - **Caveat:** FAQPage *markup* no longer drives a rich result, but on-page Q&A *content* still helps extractability — keep the Q&A, the schema is now optional cosmetics.

### Spam cleanliness is now an AI-citation gate — CONFIRMED
Per the 2026-06-05 changelog clarification, Web Search spam policies also govern AI Overviews / AI Mode. A spam violation (cloaking, doorway/scaled content, keyword stuffing, misleading review markup) now blocks AI citation, not just rankings. All three clients passed MAMMATA's 2026-06-03 spam re-audit → currently AI-citation-eligible.

### Measuring AI citation — CONFIRMED, with limits
- **GLOBAL AS OF 2026-08-31 [CONF, Google primary].** Google's Search Console Help doc now states verbatim: *"As of August 31, 2026, we've rolled out this control to all websites worldwide."* The rollout question is **CLOSED** — every property Novarte touches has both the report and the control. Source: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024 (reported: https://searchengineland.com/google-search-console-ai-performance-reports-and-search-generative-ai-control-rolling-out-globally-486269)
- **GSC Generative AI performance reports** (Search Central blog, 2026-06-03; logged 2026-06-04/05): **impressions only** for AI Overviews + AI Mode + Discover gen-AI, split by page/country/device/date (hourly→monthly). **No clicks, no CTR, no position, no query data.** Availability: **all sites worldwide as of 2026-08-31.** Source: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports
- **Action:** when a client's property gets the report, **baseline AI impressions immediately**; track week-over-week. Until then, proxy by manually querying AI Overviews / AI Mode / Ask Maps / Perplexity / ChatGPT for the client's money queries and logging whether the brand is named/cited.
- **Never flip the GSC "block content from AI features" toggle** without written client sign-off — it zeroes AI-surface presence. CONFIRMED live: the control + gen-AI reports are no longer just announced — Mueller's Q2'26 Search News video (2026-06-18) restated them as shipped (toggle effective 2026-06-17), **global to all sites 2026-08-31**. Opting out is NOT a ranking signal for non-AI search (Google blog 2026-06-03; restated in the help doc 2026-08-31).
- **INHERITANCE TRAP [CONF, help doc 2026-08-31]:** the control **inherits from the closest parent property that stopped inheriting**; a property whose parents are all unconfigured inherits from the **top-level domain property**. One opt-out set on a domain property therefore silently propagates to every URL-prefix child. **Audit check: verify the control at the DOMAIN-property level, not just the child.** Changes take **1–2 days** to take effect, longer with caching/propagation. The control is separate from AI training (that is `Google-Extended`) and does not override Merchant Center / Google Ads participation.
- **New AI-surface placements to monitor (CONF, 2026-06-18 Mueller video):** (a) **Preferred sources** now expanded into AI Overviews + AI Mode — users adding a site makes it more likely to appear AND get labeled in those features; add the "add as preferred source" link/button to client pages. (b) **More inline links in AI Overviews** + new in-depth-article + forum/firsthand-advice sections = more citation surface area for well-structured content. (c) **Search profiles** (new) — US creators/publishers with a sizeable following on ≥1 platform get a followable Google profile aggregating cross-platform content; relevant if a client builds a creator presence.
