## What it says
- **What the QRG is:** the guidelines Google's external search raters use to evaluate the performance of Google's ranking systems. **Ratings do not directly influence ranking** — raters do not penalize or promote individual pages. The guidelines are also "useful to creators seeking to understand how to self-assess their own content."
- **E-E-A-T (announced Dec 2022):** E-A-T (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) gained a second E — **Experience**. Google asks whether content "was produced with some degree of experience, such as with actual use of a product, having actually visited a place or communicating what a person experienced."
  - Example from Google: for filling out tax returns you want an accounting expert; for reviews of tax-prep software you may want first-hand user experience (e.g., a forum discussion).
  - The Dec 2022 update also added clearer guidance that content should be original and helpful for people, and that helpful information comes in varied formats and from a range of sources.
- **Current version (corroborated):** Search Engine Land reports the QRG was updated **September 11, 2025**, a minor update that added **AI Overview examples**, updated **YMYL definitions**, and fixed typos. Google stated this "makes no change to our rating guidance" — clarifications and examples only. The prior update was January 23, 2025.
- **Version-size note (per task brief, not independently verified in this scrape):** the public PDF is described as a ~36-page summary, while the full in-force QRG is the Sept 11 2025 / ~182-page document. The exact page counts were NOT confirmed in the sources fetched — treat as "could not verify" until the PDF itself is checked.

## What MAMMATA must do
- Use the QRG as a self-assessment lens, not a ranking lever — never tell a client "raters will downrank you"; ratings are diagnostic for Google's systems only.
- Audit for the Experience signal: does content show first-hand use/visitation (real photos, original observations, practitioner detail) vs. generic rewrites?
- Apply heightened YMYL scrutiny to the health client (health) — Trust, accuracy, and credentialed authorship are decisive for YMYL pages per QRG.
- Map any AI Overview-eligible queries for clients and ensure pages meet E-E-A-T so they are quality candidates for AI surfaces (ties to ai-optimization-generative-search.md).
- When citing the QRG to the client, reference the September 11, 2025 version as current.
