## Query-Fan Out + December Core Update

🎥 [Loom](https://youtu.be/rszIeAbuZHA) · 10:05

Below is in addition to the above video. If you haven't yet,[ check out the module on Query-Fan Out.](https://www.skool.com/rank-expand-academy/extract-query-fan-out-from-chatgpt-1-minute-setup)

To start: Ranking is no longer just about pages. It’s about presence.

AI systems (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, other LLMs) don’t “think” like classic Google.

They fan out queries, scan the web, and pull from *trusted, repeatable signal sources*.

Your job is to show up where AI is already looking.

## Step 1: Accept the New Reality (Quick Mindset Shift)

AI tools:

- Run multiple related searches per query (fan-out queries)
- Pull from authoritative platforms, not just ranking pages
- Care more about topic coverage + consistency than one keyword win

👉 If you only publish blog posts, you are under-signaling. This is what will make you a dinosaur. Do more.

## Step 2: Focus on the 3 Platforms AI Clearly Trusts

These are non-negotiable going forward:

### 1. Wikipedia (Authority & Entity Signal)

- AI uses Wikipedia to:

- Understand entities
- Confirm brand legitimacy
- Validate factual consistency

What to do (practical):

- Make sure your brand, product category, or niche terminology:

- Exists on Wikipedia indirectly (citations, mentions, sources)
- Use Wikipedia to:

- Identify canonical phrasing
- Mirror definitions and structure in your own content

***This is a very hard thing to do if you are not a real brand. I've seen it happen with very niche edits for websites that are lesser "brands". Quick sanity check: if your business deserves a Wiki page, make sure it has one. If not, can do some edit requests to include your website/brand?

> You don’t need your own page to benefit. You need alignment.

### 2. Reddit (Real-World Intent & Language)

Reddit is gold for AI because it shows:

- How real people ask questions
- How problems are described naturally
- What gets upvoted as “helpful”

What to do:

- Find 3–5 relevant subreddits in your niche
- Look for:

- “Best X for Y”
- “Anyone recommend…”
- “What should I avoid when…”
- Use that language:

- In FAQs
- In comparison sections
- In supporting articles

And you don't need to link to your website necessarily. You can do a brand mention (without links) to do well here. There are platforms that allow you to purchase comments/upvotes for Reddit.

> AI trusts Reddit because humans trust Reddit.

### 3. YouTube (Topical Depth & Coverage)

YouTube is no longer optional.

AI uses YouTube to:

- Understand topic breadth
- Confirm consumer interest
- Validate recurring subtopics

What to do (low effort):

- Create basic videos that answer:

- Buyer questions
- Comparison questions
- Setup / expectations / mistakes
- You don’t need high production
- You DO need consistency

For Local SEO, this is less important when compared to ECOM/other branded websites - but it works for all websites. Make sure to treat your YT channel as a complimentary source of info for LLMs.

> A weak YouTube channel beats no channel.

## Step 3: Map Fan-Out Queries (Don’t Guess)

Use fan-out tools (like the bookmarklet) to see:

- What AI *actually* searches
- Which platforms it pulls from
- Which queries repeat across answers

Then do this:

- One main page (core topic)
- Multiple supporting pages:

- Comparisons
- “Best for X”
- “Cost / Reviews / Problems”
- Mirror AI’s phrasing exactly where possible

> Don’t invent keywords. Take them from the model.

## Step 4: Reinforce the Same Topic Everywhere

This is where most SEOs fail.

Pick one topic and reinforce it across:

- Your website
- Reddit discussions
- YouTube videos
- Citations / mentions
- External list articles

AI rewards pattern repetition, not cleverness.

## Step 5: Think Like a Brand, Not a Page

Post-December update winners:

- Look like real businesses
- Publish across platforms
- Cover topics deeply instead of chasing volume

Old SEO still works but only as one piece of the system.

## The Simple Rule Going Forward

> If AI fan-outs a query and searches the web…
you should already be there.

Not once.
Not accidentally.
Consistently.

## DEEPR Method to Niche Selection

🎥 [Loom](https://youtu.be/uCzazSrtWzw) · 11:56

**The DEEPER method is a framework **used to understand the five main motivations driving nearly every Google search. It helps identify which segments to focus on for financial success.

The acronym DEEPER stands for the following five core principles or "avatars":

• Decision-making

• Entertainment

• Education

• Problem-solving

• Research

The priority (nowadays especially) is to prioritize your website to focus on these three: 

## Decision-making, Education, and Research.

If you are beginning a website, or are considering which direction to go for your current website, then use this as a quick worksheet:

**1. List Your Deep Expertise and Passion:** Begin by writing down 5 to 10 topics that you know extensively, inside and out, or care deeply and passionately about.

**2. Make it Hard for AI: **The key is to choose topics that are difficult for Artificial Intelligence to provide a generic, one-size-fits-all answer for. The more hands-on, nuanced, or experience-driven a topic is, the harder it will be for AI to compete effectively. This strategy aims to avoid direct competition with AI when unnecessary.

**3. Identify Underserved Micro Communities: **Within your broad topics of expertise and passion, you need to "zoom in" and pinpoint specific, underserved micro-communities or queries where a generic AI response would be "half-baked" or insufficient. This is where people will actively seek more in-depth, human-created content. 

**4. Consider the "Money Behind the Term":** It's not enough to be passionate. There must be a market for your chosen topic. 

You need to ensure there's "money behind the term," meaning people are willing to pay for information, products, or services related to it. 

For instance, "parenting neurodiverse toddlers" and "travel to Iceland" are identified as topics with significant financial intent because people in these niches are often "voracious" and willing to spend money to solve their problems or fulfill their desires.

**5. Focus on Purchase Intent and Deeper Engagement: **This strategic selection ties directly into the "Deeper Method," particularly the Decision-Making, Education, and Research avatars. 

People searching in these areas (e.g., comparing "best laptops," learning a new skill, or conducting in-depth research) are more likely to look beyond initial AI overviews.

## Next Steps?

Here are ten practical, “no-tool” questions you can ask yourself (and test in your browser) before you launch a new site. This will help to guide you toward a "good" niche, content angles, and monetization from day one and avoid years of spinning your wheels:

1. **What 5–10 topics do I know so well (or care about so deeply) that I could write off the top of my head?**
*(This helps you pick something hands-on and experience-driven.)*
2. **Which of those topics have very specific sub-niches or micro-communities that AI would struggle to cover in depth?**
*(E.g. not “road trips” but “overlanding routes through Utah’s red-rock canyons”.)*
3. **When I type one of those micro-niche phrases into Google, what autocomplete suggestions pop up?**
*(Those are real queries people are already asking.)*
4. **What “best X under Y” or “vs.” queries come up in Google’s “People also ask”?**
*(Decision-making queries indicate strong purchase intent.)*
5. **What are the top 3 “People also ask” questions on Google for my broad topic—and how could I answer them more comprehensively than the AI snippet?**
6. **Who exactly is my target reader/customer (age, background, budget), and what problem or desire drives them to Google in this niche?**
*(The more precise your avatar, the better you’ll tailor content.)*
7. **What relatable personal stories, case studies, or hands-on “war stories” can only *****I***** provide in this niche?**
*(This is what makes your content hard for AI to replicate.)*
8. **What keywords or phrases do I see repeated in forum titles, YouTube comments, or subreddits around my niche?**
*(Scan organically—no fancy tools needed.)*
9. **What is the simplest “tripwire” or low-cost offer I could create to test willingness to pay?**
*(E.g. a $7 guide or mini-course—helps validate your market.)*
10. **How will I structure my first 5 pieces of content to cover Decision-Making, Research, and Education angles—so I capture different buyer mindsets from the start?**
*(Plan one “compare X vs Y,” one “deep research report,” and one “how-to” tutorial, etc.)*

## Microsite Basics for Success

🎥 [Loom](https://youtu.be/_1Vc-UBGa7g) · 9:40

This framework shows how to combine AI + SEO to build microsite “cash machines” that generate leads on autopilot. The video outlines the basics just to get "ideation mode" started for you.

## The 3-Part System

1. **Pick Your Money Niche**

- Don’t chase obvious “red ocean” services like roofing or plumbing.
- Look for high-ticket, ignored niches where demand is strong but competition is lazy (e.g., epoxy flooring, tree removal, niche medical services, metal carports).
- Key: Make sure the average job value is high enough that business owners are willing to pay handsomely for leads.
2. **Spin Up Your First Machine**

- Use AI to generate:
• Site structure (clean, SEO-friendly)
• Service pages, FAQs, product descriptions
- Build fast with low-code tools (WordPress, Replit, or headless CMS).
- Focus: **One website = One niche + One location** (don’t dilute).
- Forget “pretty design”—your edge is **specificity + scale**.
3. **Automate the Lead Flow**

- AI agents answer calls and qualify leads (no more voicemail dead ends).
- Form fills → instant AI/automation responses → deliver qualified leads to you or the client.
- Every click = potential dollars without babysitting.
- Option A: Sell leads individually.
- Option B: Package entire sites/systems as assets to local businesses.

## Why It Works

- **AI at Scale** → Content, structure, and automation handled instantly.
- **Microsite Multiplication** → 1 site = 1 fishing line; 50 sites = 50 lines catching unique searches.
- **Asset-Based Approach** → Each site becomes a machine that appreciates in value.
- **Hands-Off Income** → Systems replace manual labor, freeing you to focus on relationships and deal-making.

## Using AI Without Getting Flagged by Google

🎥 [Loom](https://youtu.be/cJkVrKglbf8) · 10:51

Disclaimer: Google is always changing its algo and it is not officially against "AI CONTENT". But it is against non-useful content. Proceed with caution....

TLDR: I scaled a website from nothing to hundreds of thousands of monthly visits in record time. And then Google banned my website.

One morning, everything was gone. Traffic wiped out. Rankings gone. The site, worthless.
Why? Because I leaned too hard on AI.

### What Went Wrong

- I took the shortcut: 1,000s of blog posts with no revision process.
- They looked fine on the surface but they carried the fingerprints of generic AI.
- Phrases, sentence patterns, shallow content made the content was obvious to both algorithms and human reviewers.

Google banned the website from the index (the website will never rank again on Google).

### What You Need to Know

The problem wasn’t AI itself. It was **how I used it.**

AI is a tool. If you hand over the wheel, it will drive you off a cliff.

But if you learn to control it, edit it, and release content with strategy, you can scale faster than ever.

NOTE: I use AI content every single day, for every single client, for every single one of my websites. It works. And this module is focused on avoiding the things that bit me in the butt.

---

### The Simple Three-Step Fix

**Step 1: Control the Input**
Don’t let AI run on autopilot. Feed it your own expertise, data, and frameworks so the content carries your unique voice. Drafts are fine, but never hit publish without shaping them.

**Step 2: Humanize and Layer Value**
Pure AI content feels shallow because it is. Readers sense it. Google’s reviewers do too. Add your own insights, case studies, even imperfections in tone and style. At least a quarter of every piece should be edited by a human, and the first third of the article is the most important.

**Step 3: Publish with Purpose and Pacing**
Dumping 100 articles at once screams “automation.” Release content in measured waves, interlink it, and align it with topical maps. Watch how Google indexes your posts and learn the natural rhythm before accelerating.

## NEXT STEPS:

### Step 1: Pick One Existing Article

- Go into your site and choose a blog post that was generated mostly by AI.
- If you don’t have one yet, create a quick AI draft (500–800 words). You can use ChatGPT, or any AI article writer.

### Step 2: Humanize the First 30%

- Rewrite the opening in your own words and make a point to not have it perfect! AI does perfect grammatical structuring and it's becoming more and more obvious. Imperfection nowadays = perfection for SEO.
- Add one story, insight, or mistake that only *you* could provide.
- Tighten the sentences (remove the “let’s dive in” or overlong AI phrasing).
