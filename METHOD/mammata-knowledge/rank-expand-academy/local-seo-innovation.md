## How AI Changes Local Search Behavior

**Goal:** Understand how AI Overviews reshape local intent, visibility, and clicks.

### **The Shift: What’s Really Changing**

- AI Overviews now appear above the map pack at times stealing clicks before users scroll.
- Local intent queries (“roofers near me,” “best plumber in DC”) can trigger AI summaries showing 3–5 local businesses.
- Fewer clicks → more zero-click conversions (calls, directions, messages straight from SERP).
- Reviews and sentiment are being summarized, not just listed.
- AI evaluates context, not just keywords (your entity strength, trust signals, and topical authority now matter more.)

### **How AI Alters Local Intent**

- Users ask questions, not keywords:
 → “Who’s the most trusted roofer near me?” vs “roofers Baltimore MD”
- AI interprets trust and authority from reviews, citations, and consistency.
- Searcher behavior shifts from “browse and compare” → “trust and act.”
- Local search becomes conversational — more about recommendations than rankings.

### **What Happens to Map Packs and Reviews**

- Map Packs shrink or move lower. AI Overview pre-filters which 2–3 listings appear.
- Star ratings are less visible. Review language and sentiment are quoted instead.
- Volume still matters, but now velocity (recent reviews) and context (mentions of service/location) carry extra weight.
- Businesses with rich, structured data and review freshness dominate the AI layer.

### **How to Appear in AI-Summarized Local Results**

- Optimize your Google Business Profile like a “data source,” not a brochure.
- Build review velocity + sentiment (recent, descriptive, location-rich feedback.)
- Add LocalBusiness + Service schema to all location pages
- Publish FAQ and proof content AI can cite (short, factual, scannable).
- Ensure NAP consistency across every citation.
- Strengthen entity connections (mentions in local news, directories, associations).

## Local AI SEO Checklist

### Checklist for Local SEO (AI Overview Readiness)

## 

**Goal:** Ensure your business data ecosystem is robust enough to be *included* in Google’s AI Overviews (not just to rank in Maps or organic.)

### 1️⃣ Core Business Data (NAP Consistency)

**Objective:** Your business information must match *everywhere* AI pulls from.

- ✅ Business Name, Address, and Phone Number are identical across:

- Google Business Profile (GBP)
- Website footer & contact page
- Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, Facebook, and local directories
- ✅ Primary category in GBP reflects your main service (e.g., *Roofing Contractor*).
- ✅ Secondary categories cover other key services (e.g., *Siding Contractor*, *Gutter Installation*).
- ✅ Hours of operation, service areas, and contact info are up to date.

### 2️⃣ Google Business Profile Optimization

**Objective:** Make your GBP a “data source” for AI summaries.

- ✅ Business description includes key services + location phrases naturally.
- ✅ Every service is listed individually under *Services* tab.
- ✅ Add service area ZIP codes or neighborhoods.
- ✅ Upload 20+ high-quality photos (before/after, team, trucks, etc.).
- ✅ Weekly post schedule (project highlights, reviews, promos).
- ✅ Q&A section pre-filled with common customer questions and answers.

### 3️⃣ Review Profile & Sentiment Signals

**Objective:** Build review volume, velocity, and sentiment that AI models trust.

- ✅ Maintain 4.7+ average rating across major platforms.
- ✅ 1–2 new Google reviews weekly.
- ✅ All reviews get a short, personal response within 48 hours.
- ✅ Negative reviews addressed professionally (shows accountability).
- ✅ Encourage customers to mention service type + location in their review (helps AI link contextually).

### 4️⃣ Structured Data (Schema)

**Objective:** Help AI understand who you are, what you do, and where.

- ✅ Add `LocalBusiness` schema with:

- Name, address, phone
- `@type` (e.g., `RoofingContractor`)
- Opening hours
- Service area (`geoCoverage`, `areaServed`)
- Aggregate rating (from reviews)
- ✅ Include `Service` schema for each core offering (Roof Repair, Gutter Cleaning, etc.).
- ✅ Validate all markup using Google’s Rich Results Test.

### 5️⃣ Content & Entity Signals

**Objective:** Prove local relevance through content AI can reference.

- ✅ Each location/service area has its own optimized landing page.
- ✅ Add “proof” content — project case studies, testimonials, and gallery images with location captions.
- ✅ Embed a Google Map and internal link to the GBP.
- ✅ Blog about local events, weather damage trends, or seasonal roofing issues in your region.
- ✅ Mention nearby landmarks, ZIP codes, or towns to strengthen entity-based associations.

### 6️⃣ Authority & Citations

**Objective:** Reinforce legitimacy across the web.

- ✅ Audit all citations quarterly using tools like BrightLocal or Whitespark.
- ✅ Fix duplicates or outdated listings.
- ✅ Build niche citations (RoofersGuild, NRCA, Home Services directories).
- ✅ Secure local backlinks (sponsorships, chamber of commerce, community events).

### 7️⃣ Monitoring & Reporting

**Objective:** Track visibility as a new KPI.

- ✅ Use GSC and incognito mode to monitor rankings.
- ✅ Track impressions vs clicks to spot AI-caused CTR drops.
- ✅ Log new reviews, Q&A activity, and post performance monthly.
- ✅ Adjust on-page and GBP strategy every 90 days based on data.

### Next Steps for Local AI SEO

## **🟢 BEGINNER (New to Local SEO)**

**Goal:** Build a solid foundation so AI Overviews can even recognize your business.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Run a NAP audit** – Use Google Sheets to list every place your Name, Address, and Phone appear. Fix mismatches manually.

In Google, run these **exact searches**:

- "Your Business Name" + "Address"
- "Your Business Name" + "Phone"
- "Your Business Name" + City"
- "Your Business Name" + "ZIP"
- "Your Phone Number" (in multiple formats, e.g., (410) 555-8721, 410-55-8721, 4105558721

1. **Claim or update your GBP** – Add all services, categories, and 20+ photos.
2. **Ask for your first 10 reviews** – Text happy customers directly after jobs.
3. **Add LocalBusiness schema**
4. **Track baseline visibility** – Record rank, review count, GBP insights before/after.
5. **Post weekly to GBP** – Highlight recent projects and reviews.

## **🟡 INTERMEDIATE (Already Ranking Locally)**

**Goal:** Strengthen signals that make your business “trustworthy” in AI’s knowledge graph.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Audit structured data** – Add Service schema for each offering. Validate it.
2. **Expand citations** – Build 10–20 niche citations (NRCA, local chambers, directories).
3. **Entity-content push** – Publish 1–3 blog posts quarterly referencing local events, towns, or case studies.
4. **Optimize review sentiment** – Reply to all reviews within 48 hours. Encourage reviews mentioning service type + location.
5. **Improve GBP engagement** – Add Q&A and 1 new GBP post weekly.
6. **Integrate monitoring sheet** – Track GSC impressions, GBP calls, and review velocity.
7. **Fix duplicate or outdated citations quarterly.**

## **🔵 ADVANCED (Established SEO / Agencies)**

**Goal:** Future-proof your ecosystem for AI-driven retrieval and dynamic search changes.

**Next Steps:**

1. **Build multi-entity connections** – Interlink your GBP, site schema, YouTube, and social profiles using sameAs schema.
2. **Implement structured reporting** – Use Looker Studio or Sheets to visualize AI Overview visibility changes (CTR drops, impressions, snippets).
3. **Deploy content clusters** – Create a semantic tree (Service → Subservice → Location → Proof assets).
4. **Automate monitoring** – Use DataForSEO or n8n to track reviews, post performance, and rank changes.
5. **Conduct a quarterly “AI Inclusion Audit.”** Check which GBP pages and site URLs are surfaced in AI summaries or map snippets.
6. **Earn backlinks from authoritative local or trade sources.**
7. **Refine tone for AI summaries** – Update business descriptions and schema text to sound concise, factual, and entity-rich (like a Wikipedia snippet).
