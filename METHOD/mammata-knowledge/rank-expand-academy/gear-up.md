## Automate Your Keyword Research

**A keyword research workflow that runs from Google Sheets. One click, full output.**

Input target keywords in a Sheet, trigger an n8n workflow, get back SERP data, search intent, and content opportunities automatically. Replaces hours of manual research with a button.

![](https://assets.skool.com/f/e0c04038afe74a28a97dccc4b03256df/3c67c08e36b7499289a9c2b033a95b5be35661adb2ab471cbb938341f9ab6ee5-md.png)

**What it does:**

- Pulls SERP data for every keyword you input
- Maps search intent and clusters related terms
- Surfaces content opportunities you can act on

**Setup is more involved than the SEO Research Assistant** (it's an n8n workflow, not a custom GPT), so block out an hour. Worth it: you'll run this every time you scope a new niche, plan content, or build a microsite.

## Start Warming Up Email Domains

**Cold outreach is a path to landing clients fast ... but only if your emails actually land in inboxes.**

> **Skip this if cold outreach isn't part of your plan**. But most members end up needing it eventually, and the ROI math works at a single client landed.

New domains take 2-4 weeks to warm up. If you wait until you're ready to send, you've already lost a month.

**What to do now:** Register a burner domain and start the warmup process. Full setup walkthrough lives in the Outreach at Scale module — work through the *Preparing Your Inbox* lessons.

**What it costs:** Roughly $50–80/month all-in once running — burner domain (~$12/year), Google Workspace ($6/user/month × however many inboxes you run), Instantly (~$37/month entry tier). Setup time: about an hour. Warmup then runs in the background for 2–4 weeks.
