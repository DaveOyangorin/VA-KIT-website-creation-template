## Internal Linking Challenge

🎥 [Loom](https://www.loom.com/share/e3398ffa749f47c49dd94cfd2a4a24cb) · 15:42

**1. Run a quick audit**

→ Google your site with site:[yourdomain.com](http://yourdomain.com)

See which pages show up first.

If your *privacy policy* or *terms* outrank your money pages, that’s a red flag.

**2. Crawl your site**

→ Use **Screaming Frog** (or a similar crawler).

→ Export all internal links.

→ Count how many links point to each page.

**3. Identify your top 20 money pages**

→ These are your main revenue or conversion drivers.

→ Map them against your internal link counts — are they underlinked?

**4. Clean up your footers and sidebars**

→ Remove 80% of site-wide links.

→ Keep only 3–5 focused, valuable footer links.

→ Replace “Recent Posts” or “Tag Clouds” with contextual, conversion-oriented links.

**5. Redistribute PageRank**

→ Add **3–5 contextual in-content links** to your money pages from every blog post.

→ Focus on relevant anchor text — no “click here” or “read more.”

→ Link *upward* (supporting → topic → money page).

**6. Monitor your results**

→ Note your baseline traffic and rankings now.

→ Recheck in 30 days.

→ Share your findings in the discussion thread.
