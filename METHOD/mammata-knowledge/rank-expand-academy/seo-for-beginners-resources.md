## Beginner SEO Glossary

*A simple glossary to remove 90% of early confusion.*

### **SEO Terms You Should Know (Plain English)**

1. **Crawl** — Googlebot visiting your site to discover pages.
2. **Index** — Google saving a page in its searchable database.
3. **Ranking** — Where your page appears in Google results.
4. **SERP** — The Google results page.
5. **Keyword** — A word or phrase people search for.
6. **Search Intent** — What users *really* want when they search.
7. **Title Tag** — The clickable headline in Google.
8. **H1 / H2** — Headings that structure your page.
9. **Meta Description** — The summary under the title (helps CTR).
10. **Internal Link** — A link between two pages on your own site.
11. **Backlink** — A link from another site to yours.
12. **Canonical** — Tells Google the “main” version of a page.
13. **E-E-A-T** — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust.
14. **Crawl Budget** — How many pages Google crawls per day.
15. **Schema / Structured Data** — Code that helps Google understand your content.
16. **Rich Result / SERP Feature** — Any enhanced search result (FAQ, stars, images, AI Overviews).
17. **Keyword Difficulty (KD)** — How hard it is to rank for a keyword.

## Beginner Checklists

### Week 1 Checklist

**Goal:** Make sure Google can *see* your site.

## **Checklist:**

- Submit site to Google Search Console
- Verify domain
- Submit sitemap
- Check if key URLs are indexed
- Run first crawl (Screaming Frog / Ahrefs / Sitebulb / Free tools)
- Identify major crawl errors
- Check robots.txt for mistakes
- Check for accidental noindex tags
- Make sure site loads on mobile
- Test core pages with URL Inspection

### Week 2 Checklist

**Goal:** Know what to rank for + optimize one page correctly.

## **Checklist:**

- Pick a topic and gather 5–15 keywords
- Identify search intent for each keyword
- Map one primary keyword per page
- Write/verify your H1 (matches topic, not keyword stuffed)
- Add 3–6 H2s that match subtopics
- Add 1–3 internal links from relevant pages
- Add 1 outbound link to a credible source
- Add a compelling title tag
- Write a helpful meta description
- Ensure content satisfies search intent (informational vs. commercial)

### Week 3 Checklist

**Goal:** Remove ranking blockers.

## **Checklist:**

- Fix broken links
- Fix duplicate titles/H1s
- Compress and resize images
- Ensure pages load <3 seconds
- Add canonical tags where needed
- Fix redirect chains
- Remove soft 404s
- Check mobile usability
- Ensure HTTPS everywhere
- Verify structured data (FAQ, Article, LocalBusiness, etc.)

### Week 4 Checklist

**Goal:** Publish good content, get your first links, and measure success.

## **Checklist:**

### **Content:**

- Publish 1 new optimized page/post
- Add supporting internal links
- Add images with descriptive alt text
- Add a short FAQ section

### **Links:**

- Claim social profiles
- Claim business directories if applicable
- Create 1–2 guest posts or mentions
- Link between your related articles

### **Measurement:**

- Set up GSC performance filters
- Track key queries
- Track impressions + clicks improvements
- Track new rankings using your tool of choice
- Review “pages with highest growth” weekly

### Beginner Mistakes To Avoid

New SEOs often slow themselves down by focusing on the wrong things. Avoid these early traps:

## **Strategic Mistakes**

❌ Learning endlessly instead of implementing

❌ Thinking SEO = keywords (it’s actually intent + structure)

❌ Ignoring search intent

❌ Optimizing pages without research

❌ Copying competitors without understanding SERP features

❌ Chasing high-difficulty keywords

❌ Focusing on backlinks before content quality

## **Execution Mistakes**

❌ Publishing content without internal links

❌ Using AI to rewrite existing articles instead of adding new insights

❌ Publishing thin or low-value content

❌ Changing URLs for no reason

❌ Creating pages Google can’t index

❌ Ignoring page speed

❌ Not checking what already ranks

## **Tooling Mistakes**

❌ Not using Google Search Console

❌ Trying advanced technical fixes too early

## **Stick to the fundamentals first.**

Crawlability → Intent → Content → Links → Measurement.

Master these, and everything else becomes easier.
