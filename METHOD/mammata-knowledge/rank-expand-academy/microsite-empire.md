### GMB Verification

The goal of this module is to document what we have done to get auto-verified with Google's Business Listings. 

**Why does this matter?**

Because if you can get your microsite to have a Google Business Listing, your microsite can show up in the map pack as well, and will perform better overall (much better) based upon our experience. 

**Some nuance: **

When working through the acceptance/request process, we purposefully are very very slow to click buttons, type in words, etc. It is likely that Google monitors keystrokes, speed, oddities, to detect unusual activities. We try to emulate what a non-SEO business owner would do throughout this process. 

Also, we purposefully let our microsites "cook" for 3-6 months before doing this verification process. So the cadence is: build microsite, have consistent NAP across the entire website, purchase citations to build those mentions of NAP, and wait 3-6 months. Then go through this process below.
