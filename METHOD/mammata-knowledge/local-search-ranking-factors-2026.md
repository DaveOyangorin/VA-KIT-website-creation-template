# Case Study: The 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors — What Changed and What to Sell

**Sources:** Whitespark 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors Report (Darren Shaw) · Birdeye State of Google Business Profile 2026 (April 2026) · Near Media "The Near Memo" EP 248 with Adam Dorfman
**Type:** Industry data breakdown → client playbook
**Filed:** 2026-08-19
**Niche:** Local service businesses (all Novarte local clients)

---

## The Headline

Two numbers, from Birdeye's 2026 Google Business Profile study:

- **Impressions per location: down 53.8%**
- **Customer actions (calls, directions, website clicks): down about 5%**

Views collapsed. Sales did not.

**What it means:** Google and AI answers now filter out the browsers before they ever see the profile. The people who still arrive are the ones ready to buy. The funnel got shorter, not weaker.

**What it means for us:** any client report built on "impressions" or "profile views" will show a scary red number this year that has nothing to do with their revenue. If we do not change the report first, the client changes agencies.

---

## The Survey Behind It

Whitespark's 2026 report is the biggest recurring study in local SEO.

- **47 local search experts** surveyed
- **187 ranking factors** scored
- **47 factors are new** this edition
- Every factor scored in **four separate buckets**: map pack impact, local organic impact, conversion impact, and — new for 2026 — **AI search visibility impact**

The four-bucket scoring is the useful part. A factor can be worthless for map pack rank and still be the thing that gets a client quoted inside an AI answer.

## 2. The Big Movers

### Business hours are now a top-5 ranking factor

Being **open right now** is the #5 map pack factor. Google would rather show a business that can answer the phone than the most optimised profile in town.

**Do this on every client:**
- Audit hours on day one. Wrong hours are now a ranking bug, not a cosmetic one.
- Set holiday hours in advance. A profile flagged as closed on a holiday loses the pack that day.
- Where the client genuinely can take calls later, widen the hours. Do not lie about it — a "closed on arrival" complaint costs more than the rank.
- Related factor: **proper hours set on GBP** jumped 64 places, from #85 to #21.

### Review recency joined the top tier

**Recency of reviews is now #11.** In the 2023 survey it sat around #20. Total review count matters less than it used to. A steady drip beats a big old pile.

This matches the garage door case study in this folder — see `seo-garage-door-case-study.md`, review velocity section. Two independent sources now say the same thing.

### GBP services went from myth to confirmed factor

In 2023, filling in Google's predefined services was widely called a waste of time.

In 2026:
- **Keywords in GBP services jumped 75 places**, from #110 to #35
- Adding Google's predefined services moved from around #81 to #22

**Do this:** add every predefined service Google offers for the client's category, then add custom services with the location keyword in them. It takes 20 minutes and it is now a real lever.

### Hiding the address costs you

**Address showing on the GBP is the #7 factor.** Service area businesses that hide the address pay a real ranking price. Weigh it deliberately per client instead of hiding by default.

### What lost value

- **Internal links from the GBP landing page**: fell 65 places, #45 → #110
- **Linking out to authority sites**: fell 64 places, #113 → #177
- **Volume of content on service pages**: fell about 40 places

Stop selling word counts. Long service pages are no longer the win they were.

**#1 is the whole strategy.** One page per service, and a version per city served. Everything below it supports that.

## 4. AI Search Visibility — The New Section

This is the part with no equivalent in any earlier survey.

1. Being on expert-curated "best of" lists
2. A dedicated page for each service
3. Prominence on key industry-relevant domains
4. Quality and authority of unstructured citations
5. Authority of third-party review sites
6. Geographic keyword relevance
7. Quantity of unstructured citations
8. High Google ratings
9. Niche focus of the website
10. Scannable content structure across the site

**Citations are back.** Three of the top five AI visibility factors are citation or third-party-mention factors. The thing every agency declared dead in 2020 is now how a business gets quoted by an AI.

**Unstructured citation** = the business named and described in ordinary web text — a roundup post, a local news piece, a forum answer — not a directory listing with a NAP form.

**This is a sellable service.** It maps onto the citation and backlink workflow we already run: find where a client should be mentioned, then claim or build the listing there. Add "get on the best-of lists" as the tier above it.

One caution from the report: AI search visibility still has a **smaller impact on local outcomes than plain Google search**. Sell it as insurance and upside, not as a replacement.

**Two of the eight are AI-generated content.** Text and images both. This is a direct constraint on how we produce client assets: AI-generated GBP photos are now a scored negative, so client GBP photo sets stay real.

## 6. What This Changes at Novarte

### Change the report before the client asks

Stop leading with impressions and profile views. Lead with:
- Calls
- Direction requests
- Website clicks from the profile
- Booked jobs, where the client will share the number

Add one line to every report explaining the industry-wide 54% impressions drop, so the client hears it from us first and hears it as context, not as an excuse.

### New GBP audit checklist

1. Hours correct, holiday hours loaded 90 days out
2. Every predefined Google service added
3. Custom services added with location keywords
4. Address visible unless there is a specific reason to hide it
5. Primary category matched against the top 3 competitors in the pack
6. Additional categories filled
7. Map pin placement verified
8. Review recency: is there at least one new review this month?
9. NAP on site matches NAP on profile, character for character
10. Zero AI-generated photos in the profile
11. No keyword stuffing in the description or in Posts
12. Service areas inside a 2-hour drive, no overlap across locations

### New service to package

**AI Visibility / Citation Building.** Unstructured citations plus best-of list placement. Price this as an add-on tier — the delivery workflow already exists, so this is packaging, not new build.

---

## Key Mindset Shifts

1. **Views are down for everyone. Actions are the metric now.** Change the report or lose the client.
2. **Being open beats being optimised.** Hours are a top-5 factor.
3. **Fresh reviews beat many reviews.** Velocity again, from a second source.
4. **GBP services are real.** The 2023 advice was wrong; 20 minutes of work moved 75 places.
5. **Citations are back, for AI, not for NAP.** Get mentioned in real text, not in more directories.
6. **Longer pages are not the win.** Content volume on service pages lost about 40 places.
7. **AI-generated media is now a scored negative.** Keep client GBP photos real.
