==============================================================
## CORE THESIS
==============================================================

- **Google is not a content appreciation engine.** It doesn't read pages like a human and award
  points for craftsmanship. It ranks by UTILITY, measured through external signals — links,
  clicks, repeat interactions. (YouTube analogy: Google doesn't watch videos to judge them; it
  watches user behavior.)
- **Clicks generate authority, just like links do.** "A click is a vote with time and attention
  attached." A link says "this is worth directing others to." BOTH increase PageRank; BOTH
  increase topical authority. So a page that ranks and gets clicks is *manufacturing its own
  authority* — no backlinks needed.
- **PageRank is a PAGE system, not a domain system.** Pages accumulate their own authority,
  relevancy, and signal graph. That's why canonicals and cannibalization exist. There is no
  "domain ranks for X" — the domain only provides a baseline; the page competes.
- **Every keyphrase you get clicks for IS topical authority.** Google sees clicks in a topic →
  you gain credibility in that topic → expanding the topic footprint expands the authority
  footprint. It's mechanical, not mystical.
- **Topical bridges:** topics aren't clean silos, they're overlapping circles. If people who
  search one query also commonly search another, a bridge exists. Once you get clicks in one
  topic, you can stretch into adjacent ones by linking and writing in the direction you want
  to expand.
- **Don't over-romanticize DR/DA/backlink counts.** These metrics are tool approximations of
  PageRank + relevancy + behavioral hints. You can LOSE backlinks while shipping content in a
  tight topical band and still see traffic (and even DA) go UP.

==============================================================
## THE COLONY STRATEGY — STEP BY STEP (the ~5:29 core)
==============================================================

**Step 1 — Target a colony of easy, non-competitive keywords that still get some traffic
in your space.** "Non-competitive" = no other site is putting the keyword in their page title,
URL slug, H1, and beginning of the first sentence.

**Step 2 — On-page placement (the most important places for the keyword):**
1. Page title  ← most important
2. URL slug    ← most important
3. H1
4. Beginning of the first sentence

**Step 3 — Find the keywords with the People Also Ask trick:**
- Go to **alsoask.com**, enter your niche → get actual Google "People Also Ask" questions.
- Feed the questions to Perplexity with the prompt: *"Write around 120-word plain text answers
  for each question, returned inside a single code block, with no citations."*
- Edit for brand voice + accuracy; strip AI giveaways (em dashes etc.).

**Step 4 — Publish structure:**
- Create a subfolder: `/<niche>/faq/`
- One page per question; the **question is the URL slug**; the question is repeated in the answer.
- PAA questions are easy keywords → these pages start ranking and getting clicks →
  each page begins generating its own authority.

**Step 5 — Daisy-chain the authority via internal links (monthly cadence):**
- Page A ranks and gets clicks → publish Page B (easy keyword) → internal link A → B →
  B ranks → publish Page C → link from B (and/or A+B) → C ranks… repeat.
- Every month, direct accumulated authority at ONE new page you want to rank.
- More competitive keyword (more sites targeting it / higher authorities ranking)? Point MORE
  of your own ranking pages at it.
- Assuming clicks send positive user signals, every new ranking page becomes another authority
  asset to redirect. **You are creating your own authority to direct through internal links —
  no external backlinks required.**

==============================================================
## CONVERTING AUTHORITY INTO MONEY (bottom-of-funnel pages)
==============================================================

- End game: internal-link the colony pages into pages targeting **bottom-of-funnel keywords** —
  searches where people know what they want and are searching to become a customer / book a
  discovery call; they just don't know the brand yet.
- BOFU keywords are usually **non-competitive themselves**: lower volume (fewer buyers than
  browsers), so most marketers skip them for the "sexy" high-volume keywords. Volume ≠ value.
- Same on-page formula: keyword in page title, URL slug, H1, first sentence.
- Use a **LANDING PAGE, not a blog post** — landing pages are built for conversion.
- These BOFU landing pages then get satisfied clicks themselves and can, like the PAA pages,
  internal-link authority onward to the next target.
- Proof point cited: course student ranked #1 (per Moz) for "couples therapy in [her town]" —
  a solo local-service practitioner, fast.

==============================================================
## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR SMALL LOCAL-SERVICE SITES (our clients)
==============================================================

Cleaners, chiropractors, plumbers = exactly the "backlink acquisition is very difficult" case
this is built for.

- Build a `/faq/` colony per client: alsoask.com on "house cleaning [city]", "chiropractor
  sciatica", "water heater repair" etc. → 120-word answer pages, question as slug.
- Local PAA/question keywords are almost always non-competitive by his definition (nobody puts
  them in title + slug + H1 + first sentence).
- Monthly ritual per client: pick ONE money page (service or service+city landing page) and
  aim internal links at it from whichever colony pages are ranking/getting clicks (GSC tells
  us which).
- Money pages = BOFU landing pages ("emergency plumber [city]", "same-day chiropractic
  appointment [city]", "move-out cleaning quote [city]") — conversion layout, not blog layout.
- Topical bridges: a cleaning client getting clicks on "how often should you deep clean" can
  stretch into adjacent commercial topics ("move-out cleaning checklist" → move-out cleaning
  service page) by writing/linking in that direction.

## HOW THE FLEET APPLIES THIS
==============================================================

- **MAMMATA**: add a "colony audit" lens — for each client site check: (1) does a /faq/ colony
  exist, (2) keyword in title/slug/H1/first-sentence on every page, (3) cannibalization (pages
  from one domain blocking each other on the same keyword), (4) are ranking pages internal-
  linking to a designated money page. Use DataForSEO PAA data as the alsoask.com equivalent.
- **MAMMATA (keyword research)**: prioritize BOFU low-volume high-intent keywords over volume;
  flag "non-competitive" by checking whether SERP competitors use the keyword in title+slug.
- **the backlink team**: reframe — backlinks still matter (real-world endorsements), but for clients
  where outreach stalls, the colony is the parallel track that doesn't wait on anyone.
- **FRAVATA/GSC loop**: monthly, read GSC per client → identify which colony pages get clicks
  → those are the authority donors for next month's target page.
- **Cadence**: publish colony pages in batches; ONE new authority-directed money-page push per
  client per month.
