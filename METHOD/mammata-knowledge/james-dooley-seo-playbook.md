==============================================================
## CORE PHILOSOPHY
==============================================================

- **"Big net, not a fishing rod."** Catch every lead; let the CRM segment quality. SEO is one
  large part of holistic marketing, not the whole game.
- **SEO = compounding 1% wins** across many channels → omnipresence/branding wins long-term.
- **Marketer > SEO.** Branding, reputation, and sales funnels beat technical tricks.
- **Test everything, every ~3 months** (some tactics weekly). Most "experts" repeat 2+ year-old,
  untested advice from stage. Trust your own current test data.

==============================================================
## NICHE & KEYWORD SELECTION
==============================================================

- Go **broad AND deep**. The obvious industries (roofing, plumbing, electricians, carpet
  cleaning) are hardest/most competitive. Smaller sub-niches rank easier and pay 3–4× more.
- Dig into **where the money actually is** + the **modifiers**: a plumber's money may be
  *boilers* → *air-source heat pumps*; and *install vs repair vs service/clean*. Servicing can
  out-profit installs (one engineer services 7–8/day vs one install).
- **Profitable low-volume keywords are worth dedicated pages.** 3 searches/month for "metal
  roofing Kansas City" is worth it if one job = $300k. Volume ≠ value.

==============================================================
## SOCIAL-MEDIA PARASITE SEO  (the "new listicle" — biggest 2026 lever)
==============================================================

Facebook & Instagram are now indexed well by Google (they weren't before). This is the headline
tactic of the whole interview.

**What ranks:** Facebook **Pages** and **Groups** (NOT personal profiles). LinkedIn, Twitter/X,
Instagram statuses also rank. Rule of thumb: a FB asset that can run ads = a Page (indexable); if
it can't run ads = a profile (not indexable).

**Mechanics that matter:**
- **First 7–12 words of the post become the title tag.** Front-load the exact target keyword.
- **Start with the ENTITY name, never "Our story" / "I" / "We".** e.g. not "Our story…" but
  "What type of roofs do Eastern Roofing install?" → page break → "Eastern Roofing install slate
  roofing, tile roofing, flat roofing…"
- **Write in semantic triples** (subject–predicate–object / entity–attribute–value). e.g.
  "James Dooley is 23 years old." This is how Google parses meaning and how LLMs absorb it.
- **The "who is the best" listicle**, now done on social (not just press releases/guest posts):
  `Who is the best roofing company in Kansas City?` → page break → `1. [Client], 2. …, 3. …`
  then explain *why* (USPs, stats, awards). Comments/likes are NOT required to rank — only the
  status text gets pulled into LLMs; comments do not. Back claims with data.
- **Force-index it:** grab the post URL and load into an indexing tool (social posts have no GSC).
  Typically indexed in **3–5 hours**, then fed to LLMs ("LLM seeding"). Gemini & Claude pick it up
  fast; ChatGPT takes ~weeks.

**LinkedIn nuance:** do **NOT** use hashtags if you want the post to rank in Google — the hashtag
hijacks the title tag. On Twitter/Instagram/Facebook, hashtags **and** links are fine and still
pull through correctly.

**Images:** put the testimonial/review/case-study text directly **on the image** → it can rank in
Google Images for those review queries.

**Above-board:** none of this requires bought likes/comments. List yourself #1 with a real,
USP/stats-backed reason. (Watch industry regulations — e.g. US lawyers often can't self-list as
"best.")

==============================================================
## REPUTATION = QUERY FAN-OUT  (LLM citation driver)
==============================================================

To get cited in LLMs, reputation is core. Reputation = **testimonials, reviews, case studies,
awards.** Won a local award? Make an image, post the trophy photo on IG/LinkedIn/Twitter with a
semantic triple naming the company + the award in one sentence → AI indexes and uses it.

==============================================================
## INDEXING
==============================================================

- **Money site:** don't use spammy indexing tools. Use GSC fetch/render; then share the new
  article on socials and link back. **Twitter is the #1 way to get money-site pages indexed** if
  not picked up naturally; Google crawls Twitter & LinkedIn constantly.
- **Social/off-site URLs:** use an indexing tool (no GSC available). Aim to land in Google's top
  20 → it then feeds the LLMs.

==============================================================
## AI / LLM REALITY (lead data)
==============================================================

- **Leads DOWN, sales UP.** Top-of-funnel keywords now get ~zero clicks (AI Overviews answer
  them). But **branded search is up** and converts phenomenally — buyers research via AI, then
  search the brand and convert as bottom-of-funnel, fully informed ("ChatGPT / Google recommended
  you"). AI acts as **third-party validation, like a referral from a friend.**
- **Direct LLM leads are tiny:** ~0.6% → ~1.3–1.4% of total and rising slowly. Counting
  AI-driven branded search, more like **15–20%** — still small vs Google organic + GBP.
- **Trend:** ChatGPT leads dropping; **Claude rising fast** (mirrors ChatGPT's emergence ~a year
  prior). ChatGPT won't get near 10% of Google search volume.
- **Engine behavior:** Claude ≈ Gemini (both lean on Google search). ChatGPT uses Bing (slower
  indexing), is weak at local (seen sourcing MapQuest). **Gemini wins long-term** — Google's
  knowledge vault/graph + Maps data (foot traffic, directions, entities) is unmatched; no need to
  hit live search. Optimize for Gemini/Claude first.

==============================================================
## ON-SITE: URLS, CITY PAGES, SERVICE-AREA PAGES
==============================================================

**URL structure:** include the main entity + attributes (service + city), keep it **short**,
don't repeat a word already in the brand/path. Don't stuff 10 words. He likes `/near-me/` as a
breadcrumb hub linking out to all locations, e.g. `dudleysroofing.co.uk/flat/near-me/{county}/{town}`.

**When to build a city/service page:** driven by profitability/search volume. Check the SERP —
- competitor already has "metal roofing {city}" → you should build it too;
- nobody has it → opportunity, OR rank it **off-site** (guest post / press release / parasite)
  that links back, to **save crawl budget** on your own domain. Topical maps aren't only about
  on-site content — use third-party corroboration to rank without bloating your site.

**Service-area page structure:** order H2s by what the buyer cares about (the attributes), so they
never need to leave the page before converting:
- Most niches → **cost first**, then spec, then funding/benefits/FAQs.
- **Locksmith / emergency** → **speed first** ("24/7, with you within the hour"), then cost.
- Ask AI "what attributes does a buyer of {service} in {city} care about?" to build the H2 order.

==============================================================
## DUPLICATE CONTENT MYTH  (high-conviction, tested both ways)
==============================================================

- For the **same service in different areas, duplicate content BEATS spun/reworded content** in
  testing. Different geo = different intent = not a duplicate-content problem.
- **Spinning makes pages worse** (sounds garbage, loses semantic relevance) AND raises risk: a UK
  lead-gen site with **3,000 hand-reworded** pages got a **manual penalty**; a law firm that
  reworded ~200→400 service-area pages got them all de-indexed.
- Joy's internal test (inspired by this): took clients' uniquely-written SAPs, replaced them all
  with a copy of the **best-performing** page → **all improved.**
- **Requirements for duplicate SAPs to work:** brand trust + enough crawl budget (a brand-new,
  trustless domain won't get 10k dupes indexed — that's a *crawl* problem, not a content problem)
  + good UX / nav-boost. Match searcher intent ("roofing New York" → a New York page, not a
  Florida page that mentions NY at the bottom).
- Pizza analogy: you can't meaningfully reword "we deliver pepperoni pizza" 500 times — don't try.

==============================================================
## CORE UPDATES 2025–2026 — WHAT MOVED
==============================================================

- **Brand signals are now key.** EMDs/PMDs slipping slightly (still work) vs branded sites —
  partly an AI-Overview artifact (an EMD cited in AIO loses the click if it doesn't rank #1 for
  its own exact-match term, which reads like a brand search).
- **Social media growing** (FB/IG now indexed). **Reddit, Quora, forums/discussions, FB Groups
  rising in local**, month over month.
- **Video is a top-1 strategy for 2026.** YouTube "working like gangbusters"; splice into Shorts;
  video carousels (incl. short-video carousels) increasingly shown.
- Adapt fast; don't get demoralized — the algorithm landscape is in flux.

==============================================================
## LINK BUILDING — DIMINISHING; REVIEWS REPLACE IT
==============================================================

- Link building **declines in value every year** (he owns 2 link agencies, says it against his own
  pocket). Traditional PageRank-syndication value is largely gone.
- **Reviews are the new link building.** What still matters is **third-party corroboration** —
  pages/listicles elsewhere saying who you are and why you're great — plus reviews.
- **Stop chasing DR50 do-follow links.** DR is the most manipulated metric there is. Toxic/PBN
  links tank sites — often years later (Google catches up slowly; many SMBs getting "nailed now"
  for links bought ~4 years ago). Risk now outweighs the (already-falling) reward.
- **Link quality test:** a link is only good if it can **index and rank** (ideally top 10, at
  least top 100). If it can't rank, forget the DR and the do-follow — it's not a good link. Most
  bought links don't even index.

==============================================================
## PROACTIVE REPUTATION MANAGEMENT
==============================================================

Control the narrative or a competitor will. When a buyer narrows to you vs a same-priced
competitor, they ask AI "who's better, X or Y?" If you haven't published third-party
listicles/comparisons stating your USPs and why you're best, you lose — or the buyer takes the
third option: **no action at all** (the most common outcome when it's inconclusive). Shout about
awards, case studies, recoveries, and do-it-all-white-hat positioning.

==============================================================
## TRAFFIC & USER SIGNALS — "#1 RANKING FACTOR"
==============================================================

- In his opinion **traffic is the #1 ranking factor.** User signals / **nav boost** / dwell time /
  virality drive rankings.
- **Do NOT do CTR manipulation** — Google detects proxies; it's black hat. Get **real** traffic.
- **Dollar-a-day strategy (credit Dennis Yu):** create a genuinely strong article/data study,
  then boost it for ~$1–a-few-$/day on LinkedIn/Twitter/Facebook to earn real clicks, likes,
  comments, and virality back to the site.
- **Paid ads indirectly lift SEO** via user signals — across PPC, Twitter ads, FB ads. **Stop the
  ads and rankings drop.** Spread small spend across many sources (LinkedIn, Pinterest, Reddit,
  Quora) but target real prospects, not junk traffic.

==============================================================
## PPC ↔ SEO MISTAKE
==============================================================

Most PPC sends traffic to a **separate landing page / subdomain / different domain** → those
valuable user signals never reach the money site you want to rank. **Send PPC to the money page**
and improve that page to convert (Google now parses content behind accordions/tabs fine, so you
can keep it attractive). Huge, common, under-discussed mistake.

==============================================================
## AD PLATFORMS & CREATIVE
==============================================================

- **Test all with small budgets, measure with UTM tags, narrow down.** One $5 LinkedIn conversion
  can justify scaling that channel.
- **Top 3:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram) + Twitter. **LinkedIn is cheap on weekends** (everyone
  runs Mon–Fri). Pick by niche/demographic.
- **Creative:** mix it up; **giveaways / free offers beat award-bragging.** Case study: a kitchen-
  remodel client ran a **"free kitchen design"** offer to the homepage at ~£3.5k/$5k/mo, rotated
  the kitchen images, ran video ads + retargeting (50%+ video viewers) — **did zero SEO for 6
  months and all rankings + LLM citations went up.** "Let it rip" — let Meta find the audience
  (don't hard-set it); even Messenger mentions train targeting. Lawyers → matter-of-fact ("looking
  for a divorce lawyer in {area}?"), still cheaper per lead than PPC.

==============================================================
## NEW-SITE RANKING TIMELINE & LAUNCH CHECKLIST
==============================================================

- **Smaller niches** (roofing/plastering/plumbing): leads in **a few weeks**. **Local law firms:
  4–6 months.** Aged domains can rank in 24h but vet the prior link profile carefully (toxic =
  risk; aggressive link building ranks fast but not durably).
- **Launch signals:** build the site + location pages + ~a dozen most-relevant blog posts; build
  socials; run the dollar-a-day boosts; do the core basics — **citations / business listings, NAP
  consistency** (name-address-phone repeated across many sources to raise referring-domain count),
  **nofollow links to mix anchor text**, and **press releases** (new office/service) purely for
  **brand mentions** (he cares about mentions, not the links).

==============================================================
## LEAD HANDLING (sales > SEO)
==============================================================

- **Speed to lead:** respond in **under a minute**. His finance call center (112 agents) is KPI'd
  at **23 seconds lead-to-name.** ~**30% of web-form leads are never reachable** — a live call
  beats a cold web lead.
- **Niche-dependent urgency:** mortgages/locksmiths = respond instantly or the job's gone;
  architect/extension = slower is fine.
- **Local pack often drives better leads than organic** because they're calls (person on the
  line). Otherwise it's the keyword intent, not map-vs-website, that decides quality.
- **WhatsApp is huge in the UK** (> email). Integrate WhatsApp + SMS + live chat into the CRM
  (HighLevel) to auto-engage instantly ("got photos of the roof? when do you want it done?").
- Big net + CRM segmentation; love the funnel — cross-sell / down-sell / monetize every lead.

## MAMMATA + FRAVATA APPLICATION RULES
==============================================================

Apply this knowledge in audits, briefs, and builds:

1. **Audits (MAMMATA):** check for FB **Page/Group** presence, video presence, review/reputation
   assets, NAP/citation consistency, and whether service-area pages are needlessly "uniquified."
   Flag DR-chasing / PBN exposure as a *risk*, not a win.
2. **Duplicate SAPs:** recommend the **best-page-copied-across-areas** approach (given brand trust
   + crawl budget) — do NOT recommend spinning/rewording. Cite the manual-penalty precedent.
3. **Keyword strategy:** dig to the profitable sub-niche + modifier; greenlight low-volume
   high-value pages; plan **text + video + social-listicle** coverage per keyword.
4. **AEO / LLM:** prioritize Gemini & Claude; build reputation (reviews, awards, case studies,
   "who's best" social listicles with semantic triples); seed via Twitter + indexing tools.
5. **On-page:** short entity+attribute URLs; H2 order by buyer attributes (cost-first, or
   speed-first for emergency niches).
6. **Off-site:** reviews + third-party corroboration over do-follow link chasing; rank some
   targets off-site (guest post/PR/parasite) to protect crawl budget.
7. **Traffic/CRO:** recommend dollar-a-day social boosts and sending PPC to the money page;
   never CTR manipulation.
8. **FRAVATA briefs:** when these tactics show up in the news/transcripts, cross-reference this
   file and the [[edward-sturm-seo-playbook]]; both treat **video + social parasite SEO + reputation
   for LLMs** as the 2026 core. This knowledge is PERMANENT — it informs every audit and strategy.
