==============================================================
CORE FRAMEWORK: 2026 SEO PLAYBOOK — MAXIMIZE COVERAGE
==============================================================

The winning strategy in 2026 is maximum keyword coverage across platforms:

  1 website page/post targeting a keyword
  + multiple short videos targeting the SAME keyword
  = maximum SERP coverage (text + video results)

Videos should be:
- Created in minutes (casual UGC style, NOT polished production)
- Distributed to: YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, TikTok
- Linked back to the website page (Facebook and YouTube allow clickable links)

Google admitted "the open web is in rapid decline" and needs stronger link signals.
Video description links provide this value — treat them as backlinks.

==============================================================
VIDEO SEO — EXACT OPTIMIZATION FORMULA
==============================================================

Title format:
  [Target Keyword] | [Benefit or Searcher's Goal] | [Brand Name]

Description formula:
  Line 1: Target keyword (exact match)
  Lines 2+: Full edited transcript of the video

Additional:
  - Use 3 hashtags
  - Video duration: 18-43 seconds (most effective range)
  - Under 60 seconds always

Why short video beats long articles:
  - Users prefer quick answers
  - Recent content gets prioritized
  - Short videos rank faster than written content
  - Multiple videos can rank simultaneously for same keyword
  - 5-10 videos can be made in the time it takes to write 1 article

Key result: Videos can rank within 8 hours of publishing.

==============================================================
BRAND REVIEW KEYWORD STRATEGY (Video UGC Tactic)
==============================================================

Target these review-intent keywords with video:
  - Is [brand] legit?
  - [Brand] review
  - Is [brand] a scam?
  - Does [brand] really work?
  - [Brand] 2025/2026 review
  - [Brand] worth it?
  - [Brand] alternative

How to execute:
  - Repurpose video testimonials into dedicated keyword-targeted videos
  - Convert written testimonials into video with screenshots
  - Distribute across all video platforms
  - These rank extremely well in Google traditional search (UGC platforms dominate)

==============================================================
BACKLINKS — RESEARCH REPORTS AS LINKABLE ASSETS
==============================================================

Original research still generates massive backlinks:
  Example: Originality.ai → 78,000 backlinks from 7,100 domains via research reports

Steps:
  1. Conduct industry research using your own tools/data
  2. Write up findings as a shareable report
  3. Build direct journalist relationships (NOT PR agencies)
  4. Amplify through those relationships

Key quote: "I burned so much company money trying to outsource digital PR"
— cultivate direct journalist relationships instead.

==============================================================
PRESS RELEASES FOR AI INFLUENCE (AEO TACTIC)
==============================================================

Use affordable press releases to influence AI search recommendations:
  - LLMs learn from major news sites (PR Newswire, Yahoo News, etc.)
  - Syndicated press releases appear on these sites
  - LLMs cannot filter them without losing valuable data
  - This means press releases = AI training signal

Best use: Trending niches + interesting/counterintuitive angles

E-E-A-T SIGNALS THAT ACTUALLY MOVE THE NEEDLE (2026)
==============================================================

From collaboration with Shaun Anderson (Hobo Web):
  - Experience > Expertise in 2026 (personal firsthand > credentials)
  - Author schema with @id pointing to an about page is critical

  - Every post must have one original insight not findable on competing pages

==============================================================
KEYWORD ANGLE STRATEGY
==============================================================

Do NOT try to cover all keyword angles in a single piece.
Instead: target each video/post from ONE angle.

Example: For "technical SEO audit"
  - Video 1: "how to run a technical SEO audit in 10 minutes"
  - Video 2: "technical SEO audit checklist for small businesses"
  - Video 3: "technical SEO audit mistakes most people make"
  - Article: "Technical SEO Audit: The Complete Guide [2026]"

Each piece covers one angle = multiple rankings for same keyword cluster.

For top-of-funnel keywords, always connect to your business:
  "Our SEO clients noticed..." or "When we audited X..."
