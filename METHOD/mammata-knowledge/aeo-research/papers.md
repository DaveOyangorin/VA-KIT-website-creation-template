## Three of these are padding

`2202.03629` (hallucination survey), `2312.10997` (RAG survey) and `1706.04902` (cross-lingual
word embeddings) carry the huge citation counts — 5,542, 3,901 and 608 — but they are general
ML surveys, not AEO research. They inflate the section's apparent authority. **Do not cite them
as evidence for anything AEO-specific.** The real AEO evidence base is everything from
`2311.09735` down, and most of it is 2026 work with single-digit citations. That is a young
field, not a weak list — but it means findings are provisional and should be described that way
to clients.

## Novarte relevance flags

- **`2601.17109` — Authority Signals in AI Cited Health Sources.** Directly applicable to
  **a chiropractic client's site**. Four authority domains ChatGPT appears to weight: author credentials,
  institutional affiliation, quality assurance, digital authority. If a chiropractic page has no
  named credentialed author, that is a citation problem, not a style problem.
- **`2606.20065` — GEO at Scale.** The brand-stature ladder: global household names appear in
  ~73% of relevant AI answers on the first run; mid-market and regional brands far lower. Every
  Novarte client sits at the bottom of that ladder. Use it to set honest expectations — a local
  client will not reach a Stripe-like citation rate, and the paper says so.
- **`2506.11097` — C-SEO Bench.** Tests what happens when *multiple* competitors adopt the same
  AEO tactics. Gains erode under multi-actor adoption. The honest counterweight to "do this and
  you'll get cited".
